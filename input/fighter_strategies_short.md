# Fighter strategies (short)

A short version of `fighter_strategies.md`: how each Unmatched fighter wins and loses, the key cards and a few tips. The sources and Further reading links for each fighter are in `fighter_strategies.md`.

Fighters are in the same order as the site. Each entry starts with an `<!-- id: ... -->` line holding the fighter's ID from `input/fighters.json`. Card names match `input/heroes.json`.

## King Arthur

**In short:** A slow, tanky swordsman who lets Merlin work from range, then finishes with a boosted Excalibur.

### Plan A: Merlin leads, Arthur shields
**Goal:** Keep Merlin alive and attacking · **How:** Merlin attacks from a multi-zone space with Any cards like Momentous Shift while Arthur stands in the way · **Watch out:** without Merlin, Arthur loses his draw and tricks.

### Plan B: The boosted Excalibur
**Goal:** Land a 9-damage Excalibur · **How:** bait out the opponent's Feints first, then boost with a 3-value card · **Watch out:** a Feint wastes the boost, so only boost attacks worth 4+.

### How they lose
- Lots of Feints cancel his boosts.
- Merlin dies early and Arthur gets kited.

### Key cards
- **Excalibur**: his finisher.
- **The Lady of the Lake**: use it after Excalibur is discarded for a second swing.

### Tips
- Keep 5+ cards in hand.
- Let Arthur's 18 health soak hits while Merlin attacks.
- Count the opponent's Feints.

## Alice

**In short:** An aggressive melee fighter who hits hard while BIG, shrinks to defend, and sends the Jabberwock after heroes.

### Plan A: Stay BIG and keep the pressure on
**Goal:** Attack with +2 every turn · **How:** start BIG, and use The Other Side of the Mushroom or I'm Late, I'm Late to change size and position · **Watch out:** 2 movement means she can be outrun.

### Plan B: Let the Jabberwock hunt
**Goal:** Big hits on the enemy hero · **How:** Claws That Catch is worth 5 against heroes; mix in Any cards so it's not predictable · **Watch out:** only 4 Jabberwock cards.

### How they lose
- The Jabberwock dies early and Alice runs out of pressure.
- Low card draw leaves her with the wrong hand.

### Key cards
- **Snicker-Snack**: makes them discard a card of your choice.
- **Looking Glass**: draw, heal or reposition; pick two.

### Tips
- A Feint doesn't cancel the BIG bonus.
- Be careful with Manxome Foe; it can discard your best cards.
- Play the hand you have.

## Medusa

**In short:** A ranged fighter who controls the board, strips your hand and finishes you with Gaze of Stone.

### Plan A: Hold a strong spot
**Goal:** Hit from range while they can't reach her · **How:** stand on a multi-zone space and block the paths with Harpies · **Watch out:** area damage against bunched-up Harpies.

### Plan B: Empty their hand, then Gaze of Stone
**Goal:** Win a combat with Gaze of Stone for 8 extra damage · **How:** use discard effects first; until then, use Gaze as the boost for Second Shot (7 value) · **Watch out:** it's only worth 2 against a full hand.

### How they lose
- The Harpies die fast and she loses control.
- A melee fighter reaches her: 16 health, no healing.

### Key cards
- **Gaze of Stone**: finisher, or a 4-value boost.
- **Hiss and Slither**: her best defense.

### Tips
- Use her ability every turn.
- Never Feint to save a Harpy.
- Keep your hand size up.

## Sinbad

**In short:** Starts weak, plays his Voyage cards to build up, then becomes fast and hits very hard.

### Plan A: Survive and build up
**Goal:** Get Voyages into your discard pile · **How:** keep away early and attack with Voyages whose effects you don't need; let the Porter block · **Watch out:** he can draw himself out of cards.

### Plan B: The late-game rush
**Goal:** Fast, big Voyage attacks · **How:** with 3–4 Voyages discarded he moves 5+ and hits 5+; bait Feints first · **Watch out:** a Feint cancels any Voyage.

### How they lose
- Rushed early before his Voyages are ready.
- Feints saved for his big Voyages and Voyage Home.

### Key cards
- **Voyage Home**: returns all your Voyages. Don't save it for last.
- **Leap Away**: defend, then jump 4 spaces away.

### Tips
- Keep Voyages in their own discard pile.
- Don't overuse Riches Beyond Compare.
- Opponents rarely Feint the Porter's draw attack.

## Robin Hood

**In short:** A fragile archer who shoots, hops back behind his Outlaws and wears you down from range.

### Plan A: Hit and hop back
**Goal:** Attack without getting hit back · **How:** shoot, then use his ability to move 2 spaces back behind the Outlaws; A Hunter's Eye can't be Feinted · **Watch out:** he draws a lot and can run out of cards.

### Plan B: Highway Robbery pressure
**Goal:** Damage they can't block · **How:** Highway Robbery ignores defense; once they fear it, attack with other Outlaw cards · **Watch out:** a Feint still cancels it.

### How they lose
- Enemies break through the Outlaws; he has 13 health.
- He runs out of cards.

### Key cards
- **A Hunter's Eye**: a 5-value attack with nothing to Feint.
- **Highway Robbery**: unblockable 2 damage.

### Tips
- Use the 2-space hop after every attack.
- Let some Outlaws die instead of defending them.

## Bigfoot

**In short:** Simple and powerful: big attacks, lots of movement and extra Feints.

### Plan A: Hit hard and slip away
**Goal:** Big hits, then escape · **How:** Larger Than Life (6, can't be Feinted) and Savagery, then move 5 with Crash Through the Trees or Hoax · **Watch out:** small maps limit his escapes.

### Plan B: Draw and wear them down
**Goal:** Always have one more big card · **How:** end your turn alone in his zone to draw; Jackalope Horns adds 2 damage three times · **Watch out:** busy free-for-alls.

### How they lose
- The Jackalope dies early.
- Constant pressure runs him out of good defenses.

### Key cards
- **Larger Than Life**: 6 value, no effect to cancel.
- **It's Just Your Imagination**: a defense that doubles as a Feint.

### Tips
- End your turn alone whenever you can.
- Use Hoax on defense to reach ranged fighters.

## Bruce Lee

**In short:** A solo fighter who chains Jeet Kune Do attacks for extra actions and can win in one big turn.

### Plan A: The combo turn
**Goal:** Knock them out in one turn · **How:** each Jeet Kune Do attack gains an action; set up with Bring It On and Nunchaku, then strike when their hand is low · **Watch out:** a Feint breaks the chain.

### Plan B: Keep the pressure on
**Goal:** Steady damage · **How:** use Jeet Kune Do cards often; Be Like Water brings them back · **Watch out:** ranged fighters can stay out of reach.

### How they lose
- Feints and cancel effects break his combos.
- 14 health and no healing.

### Key cards
- **Jeet Kune Do cards**: 3-value attacks that each gain an action.
- **Little Dragon**: worth 6 with an empty hand.

### Tips
- Use his end-of-turn move to step away from melee fighters.
- Go all in when their hand is low.

## Robert Muldoon

**In short:** A ranged hunter who controls the map with traps and Workers and shoots you as you approach.

### Plan A: Zone them with traps
**Goal:** Make every approach costly · **How:** traps on choke points; Rending Shot pushes enemies out of range or into traps · **Watch out:** keep 3–4 traps out, not all 8.

### Plan B: Punish them near traps
**Goal:** Extra damage from traps · **How:** They Should All Be Destroyed gets +1 per adjacent trap; Shoot Her! is 5 as a first action · **Watch out:** fighters who ignore zones, like Bullseye.

### How they lose
- He runs out of his few good defenses.
- Scheme damage and long-range attacks get past his traps.

### Key cards
- **Call for Backup**: traps, Workers back, or cards.
- **I've Hunted Most Things That Can Hunt You**: his only pure defense.

### Tips
- Play the long game.
- Don't end your turn next to your own traps.

## Raptors

**In short:** Three fast, fragile hunters who swarm one target and tear it apart.

### Plan A: Surround and shred
**Goal:** Pin the enemy hero with 2–3 Raptors · **How:** each adjacent Raptor adds +1; Pack Hunters can deal 9 on a pinned target · **Watch out:** movement defenses break the pin.

### Plan B: Strike and run
**Goal:** Choose when fights happen · **How:** wait 3 spaces away, strike, then Disengage · **Watch out:** they get weaker with every Raptor lost.

### How they lose
- A long game lets the opponent pick off a lone Raptor.
- They have very few defenses.

### Key cards
- **Pack Hunters**: up to 9 damage.
- **Decoy**: their only defense; it pulls another Raptor in.

### Tips
- Attack first and often.
- Boost movement to finish a surround.

## Dracula

**In short:** A stalking vampire who chips you down, plays with your hand, then ends it with Beastform.

### Plan A: Stalk and chip
**Goal:** Wear them down safely · **How:** ability damage each turn, Ravening Seduction, and Do My Bidding to pick the card they play · **Watch out:** 13 health and few defenses.

### Plan B: Beastform for the kill
**Goal:** One-hit KO · **How:** Beastform is 6 +1 per card discarded; Mistform in with a full hand · **Watch out:** a Feint wastes it.

### How they lose
- He goes in too early and gets worn down.
- The Sisters die before they're useful.

### Key cards
- **Beastform**: up to 14 damage.
- **Mistform**: appear anywhere and gain an action.

### Tips
- Keep your hand full.
- Use Beastform early against hand-readers like Sherlock.

## The Invisible Man

**In short:** A slippery fighter who jumps between fog tokens, chips with schemes and makes you run out of cards.

### Plan A: Run them out of cards
**Goal:** Survive while they chase · **How:** spread the fog, stay on it (+1 defense) and Vanish to waste their actions · **Watch out:** ranged fighters don't need to chase.

### Plan B: Scheme damage from the fog
**Goal:** Finish a weakened opponent · **How:** Reign of Terror (2) and Step Lightly (3) while on fog · **Watch out:** his attacks alone won't win.

### How they lose
- No Feints: scheme damage and healers outlast him.
- Small maps leave no room to run.

### Key cards
- **Vanish**: disappear until your next turn.
- **Impossible to See**: sets their card to 0.

### Tips
- Spread your fog tokens early.
- Make them come to you.

## Jekyll & Hyde

**In short:** Jekyll defends and sets up; Hyde comes out for brutal attacks, then turns back.

### Plan A: Jekyll by default, Hyde to strike
**Goal:** Attack only when ready · **How:** defend and heal as Jekyll, switch to Hyde, then turn back with Recoiling Blow or Madness Relents · **Watch out:** Hyde takes damage when moving and has few defenses.

### Plan B: Forever Hyde
**Goal:** One massive hit · **How:** discard Jekyll cards for +2 each · **Watch out:** discarding too early leaves you defenseless.

### How they lose
- Early pressure burns Jekyll's defenses.
- Caught as Hyde with no defense.

### Key cards
- **Forever Hyde**: his finisher.
- **Duality of Man**: worth 6 on Jekyll defense or Hyde attack.

### Tips
- Use Pure Evil to move Hyde without taking damage.
- Keep an emergency defense for Hyde.

## Sherlock Holmes

**In short:** Studies your hand, then strips your best cards and calls your bluffs.

### Plan A: Observe, then scheme
**Goal:** Learn and punish their hand · **How:** Eliminate the Impossible to see their hand, then Confirm Suspicion for forced discards and damage · **Watch out:** weak card draw against fast aggression.

### Plan B: Read their attacks
**Goal:** Perfect defense · **How:** Elementary cancels an attack if you guess its value; his own card effects can't be canceled · **Watch out:** Any cards can still be canceled.

### How they lose
- Early, relentless aggression.
- Dr. Watson dies early.

### Key cards
- **Eliminate the Impossible**: see their hand and discard a card.
- **Elementary**: cancels an attack you predict.

### Tips
- Learn your opponents' decks.
- Keep Watson close to Holmes for healing.

## Buffy

**In short:** A mobile slayer who moves through enemies, strikes and slips away, with Giles or Xander at her side.

### Plan A: Hit and slip away
**Goal:** Strike, then end your turn out of reach · **How:** defend with Cartwheel Kick, attack with Mr. Pointy, then push enemies off with Slayer's Strength · **Watch out:** opponents who cancel after-combat effects.

### Plan B: Pick the right sidekick
**Goal:** Fit the matchup · **How:** Giles for hand knowledge and draw, Xander for a 6-value Right-hand Man · **Watch out:** both have only 6 health.

### How they lose
- 14 health and little healing.
- Pinned down when her movement is canceled.

### Key cards
- **Mr. Pointy**: 5 value, no effect to Feint.
- **Slayer's Strength**: pushes enemies away.

### Tips
- Don't end your turn next to the enemy.
- Keep Xander next to Buffy.

## Spike

**In short:** Covers the map in shadow tokens and becomes deadly when the fight happens inside them.

### Plan A: Cover the map in shadows
**Goal:** Force them into your shadows · **How:** a token every turn, plus Seek the Shadows from the best spot · **Watch out:** he is weak early.

### Plan B: Fight in the shadows
**Goal:** Cash in on shadows · **How:** The Rush becomes 5, Bloody Hell! scales, The Sight discards 2 · **Watch out:** bait their Feint first.

### How they lose
- Early aggression before the shadows are set.
- Ranged foes avoid the shadows.

### Key cards
- **Seek the Shadows**: spreads tokens and draws 2.
- **The Sight**: hand reading and discards.

### Tips
- Make them come to you.
- Push enemies into shadows with Leap Away.

## Willow

**In short:** A ranged witch who fights from behind Tara and manages her risky Dark Willow side.

### Plan A: Poke from behind Tara
**Goal:** Ranged pressure · **How:** keep Tara in front; Rending Shot and Swift Strike keep your distance · **Watch out:** she has only 2 movement.

### Plan B: Use Dark Willow when it happens
**Goal:** Big blind-boosted hits · **How:** Flayed Alive and Black Magic as Dark Willow; turn back next to Tara or with Meditation · **Watch out:** never play Love and Loss as Dark Willow.

### How they lose
- Tara dies and Willow is stuck as Dark Willow.
- Fast melee fighters reach her.

### Key cards
- **Meditation**: a 5-value defense that turns her back.
- **Resurrect**: brings Tara back.

### Tips
- End your turn next to Tara.
- She shines in 2v2.

## Angel

**In short:** A no-frills fighter with big attacks who works with Faith and gains from losing fights.

### Plan A: Hit hard, stay out of reach
**Goal:** Big hits without getting hit back · **How:** Five by Five, Disengage and Killer of the Dead; punish Feints with Angelus · **Watch out:** he has few defenses.

### Plan B: Plan to lose
**Goal:** Draw from lost fights · **How:** losing after attacking draws a card; bait with Regroup and Brooding · **Watch out:** mix in real threats.

### How they lose
- Ranged fighters keep him away.
- Big hitters punish his weak defenses.

### Key cards
- **Angelus, Scourge of Europe**: 5 value, no effect.
- **Five by Five**: Faith hits for 5 and moves 5.

### Tips
- Save versatile cards for defense.
- Position so they need a maneuver to reach you.

## Little Red Riding Hood

**In short:** A tricky fighter who plans each card around her "basket" symbol and deals damage on attack and defense.

### Plan A: Match the basket
**Goal:** Trigger bonus effects every turn · **How:** sequence cards so the top of your discard pile matches the next one; What's That In My Basket? is wild · **Watch out:** discards and Feints scramble the basket.

### Plan B: Punish them on defense
**Goal:** Make attacking her hurt · **How:** What a Terrible Big Mouth You Have hits back for their printed value; heal with A Grimm Tale · **Watch out:** 14 health.

### How they lose
- Lots of Feints or cancels.
- No matching symbols in hand.

### Key cards
- **What Large Hands You Have**: an attack that comes back to your hand.
- **Never Leave the Path**: teleport, area damage or draw.

### Tips
- Keep the Huntsman alive until A Grimm Tale is played.
- Plan two cards ahead.

## Beowulf

**In short:** A huge-health warrior who turns damage into Rage and spends it on brutal hits and punishing defenses.

### Plan A: Bank Rage, then spend it
**Goal:** Cash in Rage at the right time · **How:** The Ancient Heirloom (can't be canceled, up to 5 plus a boost) and The War-King (+2 per Rage) · **Watch out:** keep 2 Rage for defense.

### Plan B: The long game
**Goal:** Outlast them · **How:** make them come to you, take some hits, and use schemes as passes · **Watch out:** don't overdraw.

### How they lose
- Kited by faster or ranged fighters.
- Out of Rage with no defenses.

### Key cards
- **The Equal of Grendel**: hits back for their printed value.
- **The Ancient Heirloom**: an attack that can't be canceled.

### Tips
- Let some hits through to gain Rage.
- Save Skirmish for defense.

## Deadpool

**In short:** An unpredictable joke fighter who heals every time he attacks.

### Plan A: Attack, heal, repeat
**Goal:** Keep swinging · **How:** +1 health after every attack, plus lots of healing cards · **Watch out:** very few defenses.

### Plan B: Be the wild card
**Goal:** Keep them guessing · **How:** single-copy cards with odd bonuses (food on the table, unsleeved cards) · **Watch out:** unreliable card draw.

### How they lose
- Burst down from 10 health once his defenses run out.
- Random, weak hands.

### Key cards
- **Super Feint**: a cancel that can't be canceled.
- **Underrated Super Heroes**: 6 value, no effect.

### Tips
- Save versatile cards for defense.
- Best in chaotic multiplayer games.

## Achilles

**In short:** Fights alongside Patroclus, then becomes a +2, card-drawing monster once Patroclus falls.

### Plan A: The brothers fight together
**Goal:** Value from Patroclus · **How:** Brothers In Arms for extra actions, Under Achilles' Helm to swap him in on defense · **Watch out:** little card draw early.

### Plan B: The rage of Achilles
**Goal:** Kill Patroclus when it suits you · **How:** Battle Frenzy and The Day of Your Doom; then +2 attacks and draw on wins · **Watch out:** 2 random discards when he dies.

### How they lose
- Patroclus dies at the wrong moment.
- Kited on open maps until he runs out of cards.

### Key cards
- **Brothers In Arms**: an attack that gains an action.
- **Battle Hardened**: recycles key cards.

### Tips
- Keep spare cards in hand before Patroclus falls.
- End the game fast once enraged.

## Bloody Mary

**In short:** An aggressive ghost who wants exactly 3 cards in hand to gain a third action and unleash her best effects.

### Plan A: The three-action turn
**Goal:** Hit hard on the third action · **How:** maneuver first, attack with Bloody Requiem or Jump Scare, then finish with Speak Three Times (7) · **Watch out:** they save Feints for your third action.

### Plan B: Relentless pressure
**Goal:** Keep both players low on cards · **How:** 3 movement, constant attacks and discard effects · **Watch out:** no sidekick, so she's predictable.

### How they lose
- Left defenseless after an all-in turn.
- Her third action gets read and canceled.

### Key cards
- **Speak Three Times**: 7 on the third action.
- **Mirror Image**: copies their printed value on defense.

### Tips
- End turns with 3–4 cards.
- Use Closer Than She Appears for a third action with a big hand.

## Sun Wukong

**In short:** A trickster whose Clones play his whole deck while he wins the guessing game with big attacks and defenses.

### Plan A: Fight through the Clones
**Goal:** Clones fight, the real Wukong stays safe · **How:** summon Clones for 1 health; Sly Monkey and Infinite Strikes · **Watch out:** every Clone costs health.

### Plan B: The trickster's mind game
**Goal:** Make them guess wrong · **How:** Ox Form (7, can't be canceled) versus Ruyi Jingo Bang (0, but a 4-value bonus attack if they defend) · **Watch out:** predictable plays get countered.

### How they lose
- Area damage wipes the Clones.
- No cancels and low card draw.

### Key cards
- **Ox Form**: 7 value; recur it with 72 Transformations.
- **Golden Chain Mail**: reflects combat damage.

### Tips
- Summon Clones with a purpose.
- Save Bewilderment for the real Wukong.

## Yennenga

**In short:** A tanky ranged leader who passes damage to her Archers and grinds out value.

### Plan A: Shield with Archers
**Goal:** Trade efficiently · **How:** redirect damage to Archers in her zone; One With The Land heals all three · **Watch out:** zone-wide area damage.

### Plan B: Chain actions
**Goal:** Burst through their hand · **How:** Rain of Arrows (two attacks) and Master of the Hunt (2 extra actions) · **Watch out:** she has no Feints.

### How they lose
- No cancels, so tricks go through.
- Area damage strips her Archers.

### Key cards
- **Stallion Charge**: a big repositioning move, best on defense.
- **Jaws of the Beast**: +1 per zone the enemy is in.

### Tips
- Keep the Archers at 1 health as a wall.
- Keep your hand big.

## Luke Cage

**In short:** A bulletproof brawler who takes 2 less combat damage and hits back hard, with Misty Knight's support.

### Plan A: Tank and punish
**Goal:** Make attacking him a bad deal · **How:** -2 combat damage; Trash Talk ends their turn; Skin Like Titanium reflects damage · **Watch out:** effect damage still hurts, and he has 13 health.

### Plan B: Hit like a truck
**Goal:** Land big hits · **How:** Sweet Christmas! (6, no effect), Get Paid, and Where's My Money? to close in · **Watch out:** 2 movement.

### How they lose
- Scheme and effect damage.
- Misty dies early and he gets kited.

### Key cards
- **Trash Talk**: a Feint that can end their turn.
- **Sweet Christmas!**: 6 value, no effect.

### Tips
- Keep Misty alive.
- Recycle Trash Talk with Still Standing.

## Ghost Rider

**In short:** Spends Hellfire to run over opponents for damage they can't block, then finishes with a big burst.

### Plan A: Run them over
**Goal:** Chip damage they can't block · **How:** spend 1 Hellfire to move 4 through enemies, dealing 1 to each; refill with schemes · **Watch out:** keep at least 2 Hellfire.

### Plan B: Spirit Of Vengeance finish
**Goal:** Burst a weakened foe · **How:** Blaze of Glory (zone damage) or Penance Stare (adds their boost) · **Watch out:** don't waste Hellfire on extra actions.

### How they lose
- Out of Hellfire with weak defenses.
- Mobile, tanky fighters blunt the run-overs.

### Key cards
- **Control The Demon**: +1 per Hellfire, for defense.
- **Chains of Hellfire**: back to 5 Hellfire.

### Tips
- Maneuver over enemies every turn.
- Finish with Blaze of Glory at 5 health or less.

## Moon Knight

**In short:** Rotates every turn between Moon Knight (movement), Khonshu (+2 attack) and Mr. Knight (+1 defense).

### Plan A: Ride the rotation
**Goal:** Use each identity well · **How:** move as Moon Knight, hit as Khonshu (immune to non-combat damage), defend as Mr. Knight · **Watch out:** opponents see the rotation coming.

### Plan B: The "Perfect Knight" turn
**Goal:** All three identities in one turn · **How:** I'm Not Real to switch early, a second attack as Khonshu, end as Mr. Knight · **Watch out:** a Feint breaks it.

### How they lose
- Hit as Moon Knight, avoided as Khonshu.
- Weak defenses outside Mr. Knight.

### Key cards
- **I'm Not Real**: switch identity mid-turn.
- **A Totally Sane Thing To Do**: Khonshu only.

### Tips
- Plan two turns ahead.
- Khonshu ignores scheme and effect damage.

## Daredevil

**In short:** A tough solo brawler who recycles his best cards so he never runs out, then outlasts you or stays in your face.

### Plan A: Keep the engine running
**Goal:** Never run out of cards · **How:** play Devil of Hell's Kitchen early and often to shuffle it and your last four discards back in, with Take A Knee and Breather among them · **Watch out:** a Feint cancels the reshuffle, so hold a Breather to get it back.

### Plan B: Play without fear
**Goal:** Get damage through with cards worth 2 or 3 · **How:** with 2 or fewer cards in hand, blind boost; Man Without Fear can be boosted twice · **Watch out:** blind boosts can discard your key cards.

### Plan C: Get in their face
**Goal:** Pressure fighters who need setup · **How:** Grappling Hook and Through Adversity get him in and out while he heals with Take A Knee · **Watch out:** as a solo fighter he's easy to corner.

### How they lose
- Lots of Feints or discard effects break his recycling.
- Opponents read his discard pile and attack while his hand is small.

### Key cards
- **Devil of Hell's Kitchen**: his engine. Don't save it for the empty-deck 8; it's predictable.
- **Breather**: 3 copies to get back a lost Devil of Hell's Kitchen.

### Tips
- Plan the four cards that will be shuffled back.
- Keep a bigger hand against combo fighters.
- Step out with Through Adversity to redraw, then come back in.

## Elektra

**In short:** A two-life ninja who stalls in her first life, then returns with a reshuffled deck and a lethal combo.

### Plan A: First life: stall and dig
**Goal:** Survive while going through your deck · **How:** hide behind the Hand; defend with Sai and Snakeroot Clan · **Watch out:** 7 health; dying early is a disaster.

### Plan B: Second life: the combo
**Goal:** A winning turn after she resurrects · **How:** plan your hand before death, then Mesmerize twice (discards) and Mystic Assassin with no self-damage · **Watch out:** a full hand means fewer cards left to draw.

### How they lose
- Early death before digging through the deck.
- The Hand gets wiped out.

### Key cards
- **Mesmerize**: hand-reading and discards after resurrection.
- **Mystic Assassin**: 6 value, safe after resurrection.

### Tips
- Don't play Mystic Assassin in her first life.
- Die on your terms, with the combo in hand.

## Bullseye

**In short:** A fragile sniper who hits from 5 spaces away and wins combats to unlock deadly follow-ups.

### Plan A: Win a combat, then cash in
**Goal:** Double attacks · **How:** win first (Arrogant But Effective), then World's Greatest Assassin or I'm Better And I'll Prove It · **Watch out:** all or nothing.

### Plan B: Shoot and stay away
**Goal:** Hit from range · **How:** attack from 5 away; escape with Tactical Retreat or Master Strategist · **Watch out:** fighters who close the gap fast.

### How they lose
- 14 health and weak defenses.
- No first win means dead combo cards.

### Key cards
- **Study The Target**: draw and an extra action after a win.
- **Arrogant But Effective**: an automatic combat win.

### Tips
- Go first.
- Either maneuver twice or attack twice.

## Dr. Sattler

**In short:** A planner who seeds insight tokens as she moves, then cashes them in for big hits, area damage, healing and escapes.

### Plan A: Seed insight, then cash it in
**Goal:** One big play · **How:** You Never Had Control, That's the Illusion (2 +1 per token, up to 7); Violently, If Necessary for area damage · **Watch out:** slow and needs setup.

### Plan B: Heal, escape and reset
