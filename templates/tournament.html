Edit

Matches already played never change. In edit mode every match not played yet shows tools for its slots: change who's in a slot, ⇄ swap two slots, or + play-in to make whoever is in a slot win an extra match first (handy for adding new fighters).
Drag and drop (mouse or finger, by the ⠿ handles): a match onto another round or before another match; a fighter onto another slot to swap them, or onto the bench; a bench fighter onto a slot (empty: they go in; filled: replace or play-in) or onto a round to start a new match. Every change is saved as a version you can go back to (🕘 History).

Let the bench fight each other

Makes the fairest matches among the benched fighters. Their winners can then join the bracket with "Slot them in" below, or by hand. To pick the pairs yourself, use + match on a round instead.

Slot them into the bracket

Each benched fighter, and each winner that doesn't go anywhere yet (like a qualifier's), gets a play-in against someone already in the round you pick: whoever has played the fewest matches (the ones who had byes), fairest pairings first. You can still change anything afterwards, or do it by hand with + play-in on any slot.
