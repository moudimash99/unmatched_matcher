## Titania

**In short:** A fragile ranged hero supported by Oberon who cycles through a separate deck of active glamour abilities. She combines low base card values with powerful effect synergies, devastating scheme combos, and late-game exhaustion immunity.

### Plan A: Cycle glamours and scale your attacks
**Goal:** Balance keeping a useful glamour active on the board with discarding them to fuel card effects and power up your late-game strikes.
**How to do it:** At the start of your turn, if no glamour is face-up, flip the top card of your glamour deck. Each glamour grants an ongoing effect, such as drawing cards or moving enemies. Use cards like As Wise As Beautiful and Protection Of The Fairy Woods to discard your active glamour when you need extra card draw or effect cancels. Discarding glamours builds up your glamour discard pile, which directly increases the attack value of Queen Of The Fairies. When you need a specific effect back, play The Moon Looks Down after combat to return a discarded glamour to the bottom of your glamour deck.
**Watch out for:** Once your glamour deck is completely empty and no cards remain to be recycled, you lose access to hero abilities entirely. 

### Plan B: Unleash the scheme combo engine
**Goal:** Generate free actions using Glamour of Rhyme to rip cards from the opponent's hand and deal heavy damage.
**How to do it:** When Glamour of Rhyme is face-up, playing any scheme grants you 1 action. This allows you to chain your schemes together without losing tempo. Start your turn by playing Gift Of The Fair Folk to draw cards, heal, or reposition. Follow it with What Fools These Mortals Be to look at the opponent's hand and force them to shuffle a card back into their deck. With your actions preserved, you can immediately follow up with A Momentary Glance to deal direct damage or strike with Oberon's Met By Moonlight.
**Watch out for:** This combo relies heavily on drawing Glamour of Rhyme and multiple schemes in the same window, making it inconsistent if forced prematurely.

### Plan C: Sleep through the exhaustion clock
**Goal:** Outlast the opponent in the late game by negating exhaustion damage entirely.
**How to do it:** Use The Moon Looks Down to place Glamour of Sleep at the bottom of your glamour deck so it is the last one you reveal. Glamour of Sleep ensures your fighters take no damage from anything other than combat damage. Once your deck runs dry and you hit exhaustion, you can safely double maneuver every turn without taking standard non-combat exhaustion damage, while your opponent suffers fatal exhaustion damage.
**Watch out for:** Titania's low 12 hero health makes her vulnerable to being rushed down and defeated before you can reach the safety of the late game.

### How they lose
- Aggressive fighters with high damage outputs easily overwhelm her low 12 hero health.
- Heavy card discard or hand disruption strips away vital options and breaks your planned sequences.
- Losing Oberon early eliminates valuable melee pressure, flanking options, and body-blocking protection.
- Failing to draw the right glamour for the matchup leaves her relying on weak base values without matching the opponent's pressure.

### Key cards
- **Queen Of The Fairies**: Increases its attack value by +1 for each glamour in your glamour discard pile.
- **But A Dream**: Immediately cancels opponent effects, ignores their card value, returns the card to their hand, and draws 1 card.
- **Met By Moonlight**: Places Oberon in a space adjacent to Titania after combat.
- **Gift Of The Fair Folk**: Lets you choose two different effects, providing healing, card draw, or tactical movement.
- **The Moon Looks Down**: Returns a discarded glamour to the bottom of your glamour deck after combat.

### Tips
- Keep Oberon alive during the early turns to absorb incoming pressure and deliver 5-value attacks with Met By Moonlight.
- Do not hoard a glamour if its effect does nothing useful against your current opponent; discard it to fuel your card effects and clear your deck.
- Use Glamour of Love during maneuvers to pull opposing fighters closer or clear out annoying sidekick body blocks.
- Remember that canceling opponent card effects with Protection Of The Fairy Woods requires discarding your active glamour immediately.
- Titania holds a massive advantage against Sherlock Holmes because Glamour of Sleep completely ignores his non-combat damage effects.
