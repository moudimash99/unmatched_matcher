## Annie Christmas

**In short:** A hybrid hero paired with a ranged sidekick, Charlie. She spikes in power when her health drops below her opponent's, using self-inflicted damage and careful positioning to leverage her special ability while keeping herself alive at low health.

### Plan A: Trigger Necklace of Pearls
**Goal:** Keep your health lower than your opponent's to add +2 to the value of Annie's attacks.
**How to do it:** Annie starts with 14 health, so most opponents begin the match with equal or higher health. Use Lagniappe to deal up to 2 damage to yourself immediately and draw cards. Because the damage happens before combat, it can drop your health just in time to turn on Necklace of Pearls and boost the attack. Keep your health close to your opponent's rather than deliberately tanking it all the way down, using Better Together to recover health for both Annie and Charlie when needed.
**Watch out for:** Staying at very low health makes you vulnerable to finishing blows if you miscalculate your defenses.

### Plan B: Position Charlie and swap threats
**Goal:** Use Charlie to control the board, attack from range, and take heat off Annie.
**How to do it:** Charlie has 8 health and a ranged attack. Because Charlie does not benefit from Necklace of Pearls, opponents may ignore him. Use Slick Talker to swap spaces between Annie and Charlie so your other fighter becomes the defender, pulling Annie out of danger or bringing her in to strike. Bottom Dealing uses Charlie to reveal the bottom card of your deck, increasing Charlie's attack by the boost value. Long Shot becomes a 5-value attack or defense when not adjacent, making it ideal for Charlie or for defending against ranged attackers.
**Watch out for:** Letting Charlie get isolated or focused down too early leaves Annie without her primary utility and swap targets.

### How they lose
- Ranged fighters with high mobility keep her at a distance and prevent her from closing in for melee attacks.
- Opponents who manage their own health carefully can avoid letting Annie stay below them, denying her special ability bonus.
- Heavy pressure can overwhelm her defenses before she can set up her low-health plays.

### Key cards
- **Mississippi Queen**: a defense that prevents damage from reducing Annie's health below 1 for the entire turn, stopping both combat damage and effects.
- **Lagniappe**: an attack that deals up to 2 damage to Annie to draw cards and trigger her ability.
- **Slick Talker**: a defense that swaps Annie and Charlie's spaces to change the defender.
- **Quite a Pair**: a versatile that cancels all effects on the opponent's card if you reveal two cards with the same name from your hand.
- **A Few More Pearls**: a scheme that deals 2 damage to each opposing fighter adjacent to Annie.

### Tips
- Use Mississippi Queen on an opponent's turn with multiple actions or bonus attacks to shut down their entire push, since it protects her for the whole turn.
- Quite a Pair does not discard your matching cards; it only requires you to reveal them from your hand.
- Captain's Orders places Annie in any space in her zone, places another friendly fighter in her zone, and grants an extra action, helping with positioning and surprise attacks.
