## Luke Cage

**In short:** A simple, high-damage brawler who tanks hits and hits back hard with his unbreakable skin and ranged partner, Misty Knight. He relies on heavy attacks, defensive damage reduction, and turn-ending cancels.

### Plan A: Tank and counter with heavy damage
**Goal:** Wear down the opponent by absorbing their attacks and punishing them with massive offensive outputs.
**How to do it:** Use Luke's special ability to take 2 less combat damage from attacks, making his low 13 health stretch much further than it appears. Stand in the center of the board or close the distance with Where's My Money? to force your opponent to engage you. Once they attack, block with cards like Power Man or Trash Talk, which effectively turn into high-value defenses due to his ability, and then retaliate with printed 6-value attacks like Sweet Christmas! or boosted Hero for Hire.
**Watch out for:** Effect damage and auto-damage from abilities or schemes bypass his combat damage reduction entirely, meaning fighters with persistent pings or direct damage will whittle him down quickly.

### Plan B: Control the pace and recycle resources
**Goal:** Maintain offensive pressure and loop your best cards to exhaust or overwhelm the enemy.
**How to do it:** Use Misty Knight's ranged attacks and support cards, such as Daughter of the Dragon (which becomes a 6-value attack or defense when adjacent) and Got My Back? to swap spaces with Luke and set up double-attack turns. Play Still Standing early to shuffle your strongest cards back into your deck, creating a reliable recursion loop that denies exhaustion and keeps your high-value attacks coming.
**Watch out for:** Misty Knight has low health and acts as a primary target for opponents looking to strip away your board control and ranged options.

### How they lose
- Effect-heavy fighters and auto-damage sources ignore his damage reduction and bypass his defensive strengths.
- Highly mobile or ranged characters kite him across large maps, exploiting his low movement and lack of mobility boosts.
- Opponents who manage their defenses well can starve him of the combat wins required to trigger extra card draw and recursion.

### Key cards
- **Sweet Christmas!**: A blank 6-value attack that provides reliable, high-damage output and heavily punishes Feints.
- **Trash Talk**: A 2-value defense that cancels opponent effects immediately and ends their turn if you win combat.
- **Still Standing**: A 4-value attack that lets you shuffle two cards from your discard pile back into your deck, enabling recursion loops.
- **Power Man**: A 2-value defense that moves the opponent, pushes them through fighters, and deals ping damage.
- **Where's My Money?**: A scheme that teleports Luke adjacent to the nearest opposing fighter and grants an extra action.

### Tips
- Keep Misty Knight positioned safely at range to support Luke, using her presence to create mix-ups and tactical openings.
- Use Regroup and Pushback as boost sources when you need to cover distance on larger maps, but avoid burning them all too early.
- Save Skin Like Titanium for big opposing attacks when you have enough health to safely reflect substantial damage back at the attacker.
