## Ghost Rider

**In short:** A slow melee solo fighter who manages a pool of 5 Hellfire to power his cards and fund his special ability. He wins by running over opponents with his bike for unblocked chip damage, locking down 1-health sidekicks, and surviving until he can close out the game.

### Plan A: Vroom vroom chip damage and sidekick control
**Goal:** Wear down the opponent and clear fragile sidekicks without engaging in risky combat exchanges.
**How to do it:** Spend 1 Hellfire when you maneuver to increase Ghost Rider's move to 4, move through opposing fighters, and deal 1 damage to each fighter you passed through. Because the movement is set to 4, you cannot boost it higher. This passive damage ignores card values and destroys 1-health sidekicks (Robin Hood, Sun Wukong, Medusa) instantly without attacking them. Use Hell Rides with Me to reposition and gain 2 Hellfire and an action. Keep the opponent coming to you while you control the pace.
**Watch out for:** Spamming the maneuver ability blindly will drain your Hellfire and leave you with weak defensive options. Avoid characters like Bigfoot, Achilles, or Muldoon who handle mobility well or punish passive play.

### Plan B: The Blaze of Glory finish
**Goal:** Deliver a massive, unblockable final blow to end the game on your terms.
**How to do it:** Bring the opponent down to 5 health or less, make sure they are in Ghost Rider's zone, and play Blaze of Glory. Spend your remaining Hellfire to deal 1 unblocked damage per Hellfire to every fighter in his zone, including Ghost Rider himself. If both fighters die simultaneously, you win because you are the active player. Replenish your resources beforehand with Chains of Hellfire to reset your Hellfire to 5.
**Watch out for:** Feints cancel Blaze of Glory entirely, wasting your Hellfire and leaving you vulnerable. Smart opponents will hold their Feints or stay out of your zone when they are low on health.

### How they lose
- They get outpaced by aggressive, action-heavy fighters or overwhelmed by ranged attackers who keep their distance.
- Direct damage and heavy hitters (Sherlock Holmes, Little Red Riding Hood) bypass his defenses and punish his lack of healing.
- Opponents track his Hellfire pool, applying maximum pressure when his resources are low and he is forced to recharge.
- Random discard effects strip away key schemes like Chains of Hellfire before they can be played.

### Key cards
- **Control The Demon**: a 0-value versatile that gains +1 value for each Hellfire you have, peaking at 5. Your most reliable high-value defense when fully loaded.
- **Penance Stare**: a 3-value versatile that adds the opponent's boost value to its own, with an option to repeat it by spending 2 Hellfire. Devastating against high-boost cards.
- **Chains of Hellfire**: a scheme that instantly resets your Hellfire to 5, fueling your next wave of attacks or defenses.
- **Stoke The Flames**: a 2-value defense that grants Hellfire equal to the combat damage you took if you lose the combat.

### Tips
- Never let your Hellfire drop to zero unless you have a scheme or card effect ready to replenish it immediately.
- Use Control The Demon and Deal With The Devil as your primary defensive anchors when you have the resources to support them.
- Position yourself outside of the opponent's immediate attack range after maneuvering so they are forced to spend an action to approach you.
- Remember that moving through an opposing fighter multiple times during a single maneuver still only deals 1 damage to them.
