## Squirrel Girl

**In short:** A melee fighter who relies on small squirrel sidekicks to swarm the board, unlock massive attack values, and trigger auto-damage or hand disruption. She starts slow while building her squirrel army, but once they are in place, her positioning and offensive output become terrifying.

### Plan A: Build the swarm and strike with numbers
**Goal:** Fill the board with squirrels, keep them spread out to avoid area-of-damage wipes, and cash them in for high-value attacks.
**How to do it:** Your special ability summons a squirrel adjacent to you at the start of your turn. Because you start with no squirrels on the board, your early game is slow, so prioritize playing cards that accelerate your setup. Get 'Em Tippy-Toe! summons two squirrels in any space after combat, and Eat Nuts recovers health while bringing in another squirrel. Spread your squirrels across the battlefield rather than stacking them, since any damage dealt to a squirrel in a shared space wipes out every squirrel in that same space. Position them to meet the four-squirrel thresholds required for your strongest effects: Bite of Steel and Call of the Mild, and to supercharge Kick Butts, which gains plus one value for each squirrel adjacent to the target, reaching up to nine.
**Watch out for:** Opponents with area-of-damage attacks can clear your entire squirrel population in a single strike if you clump them together too tightly. 

### Plan B: Control and disrupt with squirrel synergy
**Goal:** Strip your opponent of resources and shut down their best cards using your unique squirrel-powered effects.
**How to do it:** Once you have established your board presence, use Horde of Squirrels to deal direct auto-damage to a fighter near four or more squirrels. Follow up with Nutwork of Spies to look at your opponent's hand and force them to discard two cards instead of one if their fighter is adjacent to four or more squirrels. Keep Unbeatable Squirrel Girl ready to cancel card effects and ignore values when you have four or more squirrels adjacent to the opposing fighter. 
**Watch out for:** Relying entirely on your four-squirrel requirements leaves your big turns vulnerable if your opponent eliminates your swarm right before you can execute your combo.

### How they lose
- Area damage and move-through effects clear your squirrels cheaply before you can group them for your big effects.
- She has low printed defensive values (mostly 3s) and only two Feints, leaving her squishy if she gets caught without defensive resources.
- A slow early game allows aggressive fighters to rush her down before she can build her squirrel count.
- She has low base card boosts (1 or 2), making exhaustion a real danger if the game goes long and she has to burn cards to move.

### Key cards
- **Kick Butts**: a 1-value attack that scales up dramatically with adjacent squirrels, reaching a maximum value of 9.
- **Get 'Em Tippy-Toe!**: a 3-value versatile that summons two squirrels anywhere after combat, serving as the anchor of your setup.
- **Unbeatable Squirrel Girl**: a 4-value versatile that cancels effects and ignores values when four squirrels are adjacent to the target.
- **Squirrel Agility**: a 3-value defense that lets her move up to 3 spaces after combat, moving through sidekicks without counting movement for spaces containing them.
- **Nutwork of Spies**: a scheme that makes the opponent discard two cards if they stand near four or more squirrels.

### Tips
- Use Squirrel Agility and Dash to cross the map quickly, taking advantage of the rule that lets you ignore movement counts for spaces containing sidekicks.
- Save your squirrel-only attack and block cards (Bite of Steel and Call of the Mild) for attacks to maximize your offensive options.
- Remember that squirrels are small fighters who can share spaces with other fighters and move through opponents.
