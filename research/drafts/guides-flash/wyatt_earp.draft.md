## Wyatt Earp

**In short:** A fast, aggressive ranged fighter backed by Doc Holliday. He builds heavy pressure with high-value attacks and his special ability, but his weak defenses and lack of healing leave him vulnerable if his attacks are stopped or his sidekick falls early.

### Plan A: Full aggression and ability combos
**Goal:** Overwhelm the opponent by chaining attacks and forcing them to burn through defensive cards.
**How to do it:** Both Wyatt Earp and Doc Holliday are ranged, allowing them to control space and prevent opponents from hiding. Use attacks to win combat and trigger his special ability, choosing either to draw a card, gain a free action to attack, or move up to 1 space. You cannot choose the same effect twice in a turn. Mulligan for Doc Holliday cards, especially In Vino Veritas, to get Doc into the action early and keep your offensive momentum going.
**Watch out for:** Aggressive fighters can outpace him, and if his quick shots get stopped by defensive cards or cancels, he struggles to keep up.

### Plan B: Shift to a value game
**Goal:** Outlast the opponent and push for exhaustion if they overreact and overdraw.
**How to do it:** When opponents become paranoid about his pressure and start double-maneuvering to build a full hand, you can shift from an aggressive approach to a value game. Use cards like Fan The Hammer to recycle after defeating small sidekicks, or play schemes like In Vino Veritas and You Die First as functional passes when you need to bide your time and let the exhaustion clock work in your favor.
**Watch out for:** Switching gears too early can leave you vulnerable if the opponent wasn't actually running low on options.

### How they lose
- Fast aggressive fighters outpace his low defenses and lack of healing.
- Doc Holliday dies early, denying you crucial damage and card effects before his cards can be played.
- Opponents reliably stop his attacks with high-value defenses or cancels, shutting down his ability triggers.
- He runs out of cards quickly if you overcommit to full aggression without managing your deck.

### Key cards
- **You Just Gonna Stand There And Bleed?**: Wyatt's only 5-value attack and his main source of reliable damage to win combats.
- **I Have Two Guns, One For Each Of You**: Doc Holliday's 4-value attack that deals damage to a different opposing fighter in his zone equal to the printed value of the card your opponent played.
- **You're A Daisy If You Do**: A 4-value versatile card that draws 2 cards after combat, keeping your hand full whether you win or lose the exchange.
- **In Vino Veritas**: A scheme that draws cards until your hand has four and grants a free attack action, serving as a vital engine for pressure or a pass.
- **Bring 'Em To Justice**: Wyatt's only defense card; it starts at 0 but adds twice the boost value of the top card of your opponent's deck.

### Tips
- Protect Doc Holliday just enough to get his cards played, but don't waste Wyatt's turns trying to keep him hidden since Wyatt's 15 health cannot hold up alone.
- Use You Die First both offensively to pull fighters in for an attack and defensively to push blockers away from secret passages on maps like the White House.
- Keep track of your opponent's deck when using Better Swear Me In or Bring 'Em To Justice to maximize your defense values.
