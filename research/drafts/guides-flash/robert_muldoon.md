## Robert Muldoon

**In short:** A ranged zoner who maintains distance and controls the board using InGen Workers and trap tokens that stop enemy movement, deal damage, and draw cards. He relies on recycling sidekicks and punishing approaching enemies, but suffers from a very thin defensive package.

### Plan A: Control choke points with traps
**Goal:** Restrict enemy movement, force bad positioning, and safely chip away at approaching fighters.
**How to do it:** Place a trap at the start of your turn in unoccupied spaces within Muldoon's zone. Position your InGen Workers and traps to form a defensive wall that melee fighters cannot cross without stopping and taking damage. Use Rending Shot to push opposing fighters up to 3 spaces away or directly into a trap. When an enemy steps on a trap, it deals 1 damage, returns to the box, and forces you to draw a card.
**Watch out for:** Do not dump all 8 traps onto the board immediately. Keeping 2 to 3 traps active at once prevents you from running out of traps in a pinch and protects you from accidentally drawing too many cards, which speeds up your exhaustion clock. Certain fighters with move-through abilities can pass through your workers on traps, taking the damage and triggering the card draw without stopping their movement.

### Plan B: Recycle the squad with backup
**Goal:** Maintain constant body shields and hand options by bringing back defeated InGen Workers.
**How to do it:** Send your 1-health InGen Workers out to attack and absorb hits, treating them as expendable units. When they fall, play Call for Backup, which lets you choose two different effects: placing up to 3 traps, placing all defeated workers in Muldoon's zone, or drawing 2 cards. Choose the worker revival option alongside trap placement or card draw to instantly refill your board presence.
**Watch out for:** Hand disruption and discard effects can strip Call for Backup from your hand before you can play it. Ensure there are enough empty spaces in Muldoon's zone to accommodate all returning workers, or they cannot be placed.

### Plan C: Punish with heavy attacks and repositioning
**Goal:** Capitalize on trapped opponents and escape danger when cornered.
**How to do it:** 
- Play They Should All Be Destroyed when opposing fighters are surrounded by traps, gaining +1 to the attack value for each adjacent trap token.
- Defend with I've Hunted Most Things That Can Hunt You to move each of your fighters up to 5 spaces through opposing fighters, breaking pins and resetting your formation.
**Watch out for:** Muldoon has very few reliable defensive cards. Feints cancel your attack effects and reduce defensive value, leaving you vulnerable to heavy hitters if your few strong blocks are gone.

### How they lose
- Ranged fighters who ignore zones or outrange him bypass his worker screens and traps entirely.
- Highly mobile fighters or those with move-through effects slip past his board control.
- Running out of defensive cards forces him to take clean hits, and his low 14 health pool means he collapses quickly once cornered.
- Overdrawing cards from rapid trap triggers or schemes pushes him into early exhaustion.

### Key cards
- **Call for Backup**: Revives all defeated InGen Workers at once and provides secondary utility like trap placement or card draw.
- **I've Hunted Most Things That Can Hunt You**: A 4-value defense that relocates your entire team up to 5 spaces through opposing fighters.
- **They Should All Be Destroyed**: A 4-value attack that scales up with adjacent traps to punish enemies who try to avoid them.
- **Rending Shot**: A 3-value attack that moves the opponent up to 3 spaces to maintain distance or trigger traps.
- **Leap Away**: A 4-value versatile card that moves a fighter up to 4 spaces after winning combat.

### Tips
- The "up to 3 traps" option on Call for Backup includes zero, allowing you to revive workers and draw cards without placing unneeded traps.
- Remote Detonation can be played as a pass when no traps or workers are available, helping you manage hand size without penalty.
- Use your 3-movement value to kite melee opponents and stay safely behind your InGen Worker screen.
