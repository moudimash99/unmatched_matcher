## Bloody Mary

**In short:** An aggressive solo melee fighter who leverages high movement and low hand management to trigger her special ability for extra actions. She relies on punishing opponents who overcommit, setting up explosive third-action attacks, and forcing awkward defensive choices.

### Plan A: Trigger the third-action spike
**Goal:** Reach a 3-card hand at the start of your turn to gain an extra action and power up key attacks.
**How to do it:** Intentionally manage your hand size by attacking and maneuvering without over-drawing, keeping your hand at 3 cards so her ability fires at the start of your turn. With the extra action, deploy heavy hitters like Speak Three Times, which jumps to a 7-value attack. Use Closer Than She Appears to manufacture a third action on demand when your hand size doesn't line up naturally. 
**Watch out for:** Opponents holding Feints to cancel your third-action effects, reducing Speak Three Times to a plain 3 value and wasting your setup.

### Plan B: Relentless pressure and card manipulation
**Goal:** Deplete the opponent's defensive options and force them into dead-end choices while maintaining board control.
**How to do it:** Push forward aggressively with her 3-movement, using Broken Glass on offense to match values, deal direct damage, and draw cards. Play Stolen Memories to look at your opponent's hand and force them to either discard a high-boost key card or take heavy damage equal to its boost value. If managing the 3-card ability proves too risky or restrictive in certain matchups, play a big-hand aggressive style, ignoring the passive to keep the pressure high.
**Watch out for:** Ending your turn with too many cards, which denies your ability, or getting pinned down on the board since she has no sidekicks to absorb pressure or block paths.

### How they lose
- Opponents who carefully manage their own hand size to avoid letting you start your turn with 3 cards.
- Heavy reliance on the 3-card passive leaves her vulnerable if the opponent denies card-count triggers or cancels her third-action payoffs.
- Lack of a sidekick means she absorbs all incoming attacks and can easily get body-blocked or pinned on tight maps.
- Strong defensive cancels (Feints) cleanly shut down her high-impact third-action turns and mirroring effects.
- Ranged or highly mobile fighters who kite her and punish her when her hand is critically low.

### Key cards
- **Speak Three Times**: her main damage threat, turning into a 7-value attack on her third action.
- **Closer Than She Appears**: a scheme that moves her, draws a card, and grants an action to guarantee a third action when needed.
- **Broken Glass**: a versatile card that can match enemy values to deal direct damage and draw a card.
- **Mirror Image**: a defensive 0-value card whose value becomes the printed value of the opponent's card, punishing big printed attacks.
- **Evade**: a 3-value defense that draws a card, helping maintain the target hand size.

### Tips
- Maneuver first on your extra-action turns before attacking so you don't leave yourself stranded and defenseless.
- Use Ghostly Touch on third actions against sidekicks or when you suspect a Feint, sacrificing damage to safely recover 3 health.
- Jump Scare rewards careful positioning by hitting for 6 when she leaves her starting zone; threaten it to draw out defensive resources.
- Keep track of your opponent's remaining Feints so you know when it is safe to unleash Speak Three Times without getting cancelled.
