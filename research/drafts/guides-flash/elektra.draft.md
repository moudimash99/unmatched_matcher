## Elektra

**In short:** A unique assassin with a compact 20-card deck who stalls during her first life to burn through her cards, resurrects with increased health and a shuffled discard pile, and unleashes a devastating multi-attack combo.

### Plan A: Stall and cycle the deck
**Goal:** Survive the first life while depleting your deck to secure a massive card advantage and control the exhaustion clock.
**How to do it:** Play defensively at first. Because her deck only contains 20 cards compared to standard 30-card decks, going through as much of your deck as possible before dying ensures that shuffling your discard pile back into your deck upon resurrection gives you a commanding card advantage over your opponent. Use your four Hand sidekicks as physical shields to block incoming attacks and keep Elektra safe. Position Snakeroot Clan to swap spaces with a Hand so the sidekicks take the damage instead of your hero. Play Hands of Red to return defeated Hand ninjas back to a space in Elektra's zone, maintaining your board presence.
**Watch out for:** Dying too early before you have cleared enough of your deck leaves you with a smaller deck disparity and less advantage after resurrection.

### Plan B: The resurrection burst
**Goal:** Cripple the opponent's hand and wipe them out in a single turn right after resurrecting.
**How to do it:** Before Elektra's first death, save key cards in your hand rather than playing them: both copies of Mesmerize, at least one copy of Mystic Assassin, and a copy of The Fist. When Elektra is defeated, her current hand carries over. Upon resurrection, place Elektra adjacent to the opposing hero and set your sidekicks across the board in different zones. Play Mesmerize to look at their hand and force them to discard a card, then play the second Mesmerize to strip another card. Once their defensive options are depleted, attack with The Fist using your sidekicks, dealing damage and returning the card to your hand to gain extra actions. Finish the onslaught with Mystic Assassin as a 6-value attack that no longer deals self-damage post-resurrection.
**Watch out for:** Reviving with an empty hand means you lose your burst potential, so manage your card retention carefully before triggering her first death.

### How they lose
- The opponent rushes down her low 7 health during her first life before she can cycle her deck or establish a comfortable game state.
- Sidekicks are cleared rapidly by area damage or move-through effects, leaving Elektra exposed to focused attacks.
- Hand disruption strips her combo pieces before she can execute her resurrection turn.
- She reaches exhaustion during her second life, where she operates on only 9 maximum health and cannot reshuffle her discard pile again.

### Key cards
- **Mesmerize**: a scheme that lets you look at an opponent's hand and gain an action, adding a card discard effect after resurrection.
- **Mystic Assassin**: a 6-value attack that deals 3 damage to Elektra pre-resurrection, but deals no self-damage post-resurrection.
- **The Fist**: a 3-value Hand attack that deals 1 damage to the attacking sidekick to return to your hand and gain an action.
- **Snakeroot Clan**: a 1-value defense that swaps Elektra with a Hand so the sidekicks take hits.
- **Hands of Red**: a 4-value attack that revives a defeated Hand in her zone.
- **Intercept**: a 3-value defense with a boost of 4 that increases to 5 post-resurrection by revealing a Sai.
- **Cloaked In Shadow**: a 2-value versatile that cancels opponent effects, and moves her 3 spaces post-resurrection.

### Tips
- Keep your Hand sidekicks spread out across different zones when placing them during resurrection to satisfy board placement rules.
- Do not waste Mystic Assassin early unless you are certain your opponent will cancel it with a Feint, avoiding the self-damage.
- Use Ninjitsu to teleport your fighter to safety or reposition after combat.
- Track your card count so that your discard pile reshuffle gives you the maximum advantage over your opponent's remaining deck size.
- Use Whirlwind after resurrection to deal 1 damage to each adjacent opposing fighter and clear out pesky sidekicks.
