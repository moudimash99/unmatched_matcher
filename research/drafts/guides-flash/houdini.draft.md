## Houdini

**In short:** An explosive melee hero who deals heavy damage by boosting his attacks with cards carrying special boost effects. His escape ability lets him dictate the tempo, and Bess provides utility even after she is defeated.

### Plan A: Maximize damage with boosted attacks
**Goal:** Deliver massive strikes by pairing high-impact attacks with powerful boost effects.
**How to do it:** Use cards like The Big Reveal or An Illusion of My Own Design, and boost them with cards that have valuable boost effects, such as Misdirection or Sleight of Hand. Set the Stage allows you to look at your opponent's hand and discard their defensive option before you strike. With The Big Reveal, you can boost twice and return one of the boosted cards to your hand to reuse its effect. Avoid boosting movement with high-value boost cards, as you need them for damage output.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who save their Feints and cancels for your big attacks will shut down your burst potential.

### Plan B: Control the board and escape
**Goal:** Stay out of danger when your hand is low and dictate the pacing of the match.
**How to do it:** Take advantage of the Escape Artist special ability by boosting your maneuver to place Houdini anywhere on the map, letting you chase mobile targets or escape pressure. Smoke and Melee options like Smoke and Mirrors let you swap places with Bess so she takes the hit instead. Use Grand Escape to defend and reposition after combat if you win.
**Watch out for:** Ranged fighters or high-mobility opponents on open maps who can track you down if you spend your movement boosts defensively.

### How they lose
- He has low health (14) and limited defensive options, making him vulnerable to aggressive, multi-action attackers.
- Opponents who force discards strip away his carefully managed hand.
- Losing the mind game on Feints leaves his big attacks and boost tricks stranded.
- Getting pinned down on cramped maps neutralizes his escape ability.

### Key cards
- **The Big Reveal:** Allows two boosts in a single combat and returns one boost card to your hand for recursion.
- **Misdirection:** Acts as a cancel effect while its boost effect deals 2 auto-damage to any fighter.
- **Sleight of Hand:** Lets you switch to a different attack after seeing your opponent's play, while drawing a card on its boost.
- **Set the Stage:** Bess's scheme whose boost lets you inspect the opponent's hand and discard a card.
- **All Part of the Show:** Revives a defeated fighter to 4 health if unprevented, or heals Houdini for 2 when boosted.

### Tips
- Keep Bess alive early to utilize her positioning and swap tricks, but do not overspend resources defending her since her boost effects work even when she is gone.
- Balance your hand size carefully; a tiny hand leaves you defenseless, but you don't need to hoard cards if you can secure a kill.
- Use weaker attacks or flourish to check for Feints before committing your best resources.
- On smaller maps or against heavy pressure, rely on targeted auto-damage boosts to close out games without over-committing to risky attacks.
