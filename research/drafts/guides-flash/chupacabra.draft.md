## Chupacabra

**In short:** A solo melee fighter who thrives at 7 or less health. He maintains constant pressure by drawing a card after every attack, triggers extra actions and heavy values when injured, and uses self-damage to cross the threshold into his ideal operating range.

### Plan A: Trigger the low-health sweet spot
**Goal:** Drop to 7 or less health early to unlock powerful card effects, then finish the opponent with multi-attack turns.
**How to do it:** Intentionally take a hit or two early, or use Unsettle to deal 2 damage to yourself and force extra discards. Once at 7 or less health, play Feeding to gain an action and recover 1 health, and use Wounded beast as a 5-value attack. Chain these into Blood in the air on a second action since you already won a combat. 
**Watch out for:** Dropping your health too fast against high-damage fighters. With printed defenses capping at 3, staying at low health leaves you vulnerable to a sudden one-turn defeat.

### Plan B: Press the attack and out-draw them
**Goal:** Maintain offensive momentum without wasting actions on maneuvers.
**How to do it:** Use the ability The hunger to draw a card after every attack. Pair Traveler of the night (which grants an action and 4 movement through enemies) with your attacks to stay attached to the enemy. Keep your hand size around 5 or 6 cards to avoid overdrawing while keeping plenty of options ready.
**Watch out for:** Burning through your deck too quickly. Heavy card draw from constant attacks leads directly to the exhaustion clock.

### Plan C: Punish high values and crowd control
**Goal:** Turn the opponent's strong cards against them and sustain your health pool.
**How to do it:** 
- Play The more they struggle against high enemy values to double their printed card value.
- Use Tooth and tail to damage adjacent fighters and heal 1 health for each one hit, clearing small sidekicks while staying alive.
- Rely on Natural toughness to return to your hand if you lose a combat, ensuring you always have a defensive card available.
**Watch out for:** The more they struggle whiffs entirely if the opponent plays an unnumbered card or a zero-value defense.

### How they lose
- They run out of cards too fast due to constant drawing from attacks, dying to exhaustion.
- Opponents burst them down before they can safely drop to 7 health.
- Low printed defenses (maxing out at 3) fail against heavy hitters who punch through every time.
- Sidekicks or defensive cancels disrupt setup turns and waste key attacks.

### Key cards
- **Feeding**: An attack that grants an action and recovers 1 health when you are at 7 or less health.
- **Wounded beast**: A versatile card that becomes a 5-value attack or defense when you are at 7 or less health.
- **The more they struggle**: A 0-value attack that doubles the printed value of the opponent's card.
- **Traveler of the night**: A scheme that moves you 4 spaces through enemies and grants 1 action.
- **Natural toughness**: A 3-value defense that returns to your hand if you lose the combat.

### Tips
- Do not use Unsettle blindly; save it to force discards or to lower your own health into the 7-or-less threshold.
- Save Blood in the air for your second attack so you can meet its condition of already winning a combat this turn and boost it for massive damage.
- Watch your opponent's hand count and use Ambush to strip away their random cards while adding their boost value to your attack.
- Keep track of your deck count so you do not accidentally exhaust yourself while drawing from your attack ability.
