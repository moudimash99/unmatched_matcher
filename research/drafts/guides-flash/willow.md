## Willow

**In short:** A ranged fighter whose game revolves around triggering her transformation into Dark Willow. Once she turns, she burns through her deck with blind boosts and aggressive attacks, using Tara as a shield, a resource engine, and a returning presence via Resurrect.

### Plan A: Trigger Dark Willow and stay there
**Goal:** Capitalize on the powerful combat effects tied to Dark Willow rather than constantly fighting the transformation.
**How to do it:** Most of Willow's specific cards do nothing unless she is Dark Willow. When she or Tara takes damage, she transforms. Instead of trying to revert immediately, embrace the dark state: hit hard with Flayed Alive and Black Magic, which let you blind boost during combat. Use Swift Strike to disengage after attacking and keep your distance, or Rending Shot to push opponents away and prevent double attacks. 
**Watch out for:** Staying Dark Willow burns rapidly through your deck due to the blind boosts and random discards from cards like When Good Magic Fails and Love and Loss, putting you on a fast clock toward exhaustion.

### Plan B: Use Tara as a tactical asset and backup
**Goal:** Position Tara to protect Willow, disrupt the enemy, and make the most of her exclusive utility.
**How to do it:** Both Willow and Tara are ranged, so keep Tara stepping between Willow and the opponent. Tara carries Revoke, which functions as a 3-value versatile card to cancel opponent abilities. Knowledge of the Craft lets you pull any card back from your discard pile after a won combat, making it ideal for retrieving boosted or discarded key cards. If Tara falls in battle, use Resurrect to bring her back in Willow's zone with 3 health, effectively granting your sidekick extra lives.
**Watch out for:** Tara only has 6 health, so she will go down quickly if left exposed to heavy melee pressure.

### How they lose
- They burn through their deck too fast by staying Dark Willow and relying heavily on blind boosts and forced discards.
- Tara is permanently defeated before Resurrect can be played or after both copies are spent.
- Low movement limits their ability to kite aggressive melee fighters who manage to close the gap.

### Key cards
- **Flayed Alive**
- **Revoke**
- **Resurrect**
- **Knowledge of the Craft**
- **Meditation**

### Tips
- Do not use Love and Loss as Dark Willow unless you want to discard two cards and deal 3 damage to your own sidekick.
- Send Tara out to take damage intentionally to trigger Dark Willow when you are ready to pressure the enemy.
- In team matches, Resurrect can bring back a completely defeated allied hero, making Willow exceptionally strong in multiplayer formats.
