## Dr. Sattler

**In short:** A tactical strategist who relies on Dr. Malcolm to accelerate insight token distribution, sustains herself through substantial health recovery, and applies pressure via area damage and scaling attacks.

### Plan A: Plant insight and cash in for massive healing
**Goal:** Outlast opponents by cycling through healing effects while keeping Dr. Malcolm alive to maximize token generation.
**How to do it:** Both fighters leave an insight token behind whenever they move to a new space, making double maneuvers with Dr. Malcolm an efficient way to flood the board. Once tokens are established, play Woman Inherits the Earth on defense to draw a card and recover health scaling with the number of tokens present, resetting the board for future placement. Use Life Finds a Way to force an opponent to discard their top card, granting healing to both Dr. Sattler and Dr. Malcolm based on that card's boost value.
**Watch out for:** Opponents will target Dr. Malcolm to disrupt your setup; losing him halves your token production, severely slowing down your ability to fuel your key card effects. Avoid using your healing cards on offense, as playing them into an opponent's Feint will waste their crucial recovery effects.

### Plan B: Control space and punish with auto-damage
**Goal:** Dictate board positioning and wear down adversaries without depending solely on printed combat values.
**How to do it:** 
- Violently, If Necessary deals direct damage after combat to all opposing fighters on or adjacent to an insight token, making it particularly effective against multiple targets. Following up placement cards like Chaotician or Must Go Faster with this card helps set up reliable damage.
- Lock The Doors! deals direct damage after combat and repositions your fighter.
- The Concept of Attraction lets you teleport directly to an insight token to evade danger.
- Unleash You Never Had Control, That's the Illusion when your token count peaks to deliver a powerful attack before clearing the board.
**Watch out for:** Timing is critical when cashing in your tokens, as defensive Feints can completely negate your high-value attack scaling or recovery.

### How they lose
- Dr. Malcolm is eliminated early, leaving Dr. Sattler to struggle with single-fighter token generation.
- Heavy reliance on card effects leaves her vulnerable when opponents time their Feints to blank recovery and damage triggers.
- Low base defense values make her susceptible to aggressive rush-down strategies before her healing engine stabilizes.

### Key cards
- **Woman Inherits the Earth**: Draws a card, restores health based on active insight tokens, and clears them from the board.
- **You Never Had Control, That's the Illusion**: Scales its attack value based on current insight tokens before removing them.
- **Life Finds a Way**: Forces a top-deck discard and heals both fighters using the discarded boost value.
- **Violently, If Necessary**: Delivers after-combat area damage to enemies positioned near insight tokens.
- **The Concept of Attraction**: Teleports your fighter to any insight token to escape pressure.

### Tips
- Place your insight tokens with purpose rather than scattering them blindly, as positioning directly influences your defensive escapes and area damage options.
- Maintain a full hand of cards to maximize the combat value of I Think We're Back in Business.
- Utilize Sexism in Survival Situations against opposing heroes to boost your defense value and execute a helpful repositioning step.
