## Sherlock Holmes

**In short:** A methodical hybrid fighter who relies on hand knowledge, devastating schemes, and Dr. Watson as a protective shield. He wins by disrupting the opponent's hand, bypassing card cancels with his special ability, and punishing attackers with efficient damage and defense.

### Plan A: Investigate and maintain distance
**Goal:** Avoid reckless attacks, draw cards through double maneuvering, and build complete knowledge of the opponent's hand.
**How to do it:** Sherlock only has four 5-value attacks in his entire deck, meaning constant attacking will quickly drain your resources. Instead, spend many of your turns double maneuvering to stay away from the enemy while drawing cards and building a hand full of defenses and schemes. Use Eliminate the Impossible to look at your opponent's hand and strip away a key card. If you win combats defensively, Study Methods lets you peek at their hand further. When opponents approach, play Elementary face up on defense; by correctly predicting their printed attack value, you cancel all their card effects and ignore its attack value entirely. Thanks to your special ability, your Holmes and Watson card effects cannot be canceled by Feints.
**Watch out for:** Aggressive opponents who swarm you early will force you to burn through your 3-value defense cards faster than you can draw them.

### Plan B: Force discards and dictate terms
**Goal:** Dismantle the opponent's strategy by turning their own hand against them with targeted scheme damage.
**How to do it:** Once you know what your opponent is holding, play Confirm Suspicion to force them to discard a matching card, dealing their hero direct damage equal to the discarded card's boost value. Combine this with Eliminate the Impossible beforehand to guarantee they have no safe options. Use Master of Disguise to swap spaces with an opposing hero, dealing 1 damage while escaping pins or repositioning for Watson's ranged attacks. When melee attackers press the attack, rely on Counterpunch to deal 2 direct damage after combat if Holmes is adjacent.
**Watch out for:** Confirm Suspicion cannot target scheme cards, so use your forced discards on attacks and defenses while saving Eliminate the Impossible to strip away troublesome schemes.

### How they lose
- He has extremely poor card draw, relying entirely on conditional effects like Education Never Ends or Administer Aid, making him vulnerable to being out-paced.
- Relentless early aggression and high-volume attacks exhaust his 15 available defense cards, leaving him defenseless.
- Ranged fighters keep their distance, nullifying Counterpunch and making it harder to protect Watson or leverage adjacency.
- Opponents who empty their hands or keep very low card counts minimize the effectiveness of hand-peeking and discard schemes.
- Sources disagree on whether Watson should always be targeted first: some recommend eliminating him early to remove Sherlock's ranged attacks and healing, while others advise ignoring him depending on the matchup.

### Key cards
- **Elementary**: a 3-value defense played face up. Predict the opponent's printed attack value to cancel their effects and ignore their attack value completely.
- **Confirm Suspicion**: a scheme that names a value, forcing the opponent to discard a matching card and take damage equal to its boost value.
- **Eliminate the Impossible**: a scheme that lets you look at the opponent's hand and choose 1 card for them to discard.
- **The Game is Afoot**: a 5-value attack for Holmes that lets you move up to 3 spaces after combat.
- **Counterpunch**: a 3-value versatile that deals 2 damage after combat if Holmes is adjacent.
- **Service Revolver**: a 5-value ranged attack for Dr. Watson with no effect.

### Tips
- Always keep Dr. Watson alive if possible; he acts as a body block, provides ranged pressure, and fuels healing through Administer Aid and Fixed Point in a Changing Age.
- Treat most versatile cards as defensive options, since your deck is heavily saturated with schemes and you need reliable blocks.
- When using Deduce Strategy, you can optionally lower an opponent's card value to its boost value during combat, but remember it is optional so you never accidentally boost their attack.
- Master of Disguise lets you swap spaces with the opposing hero to break pins or set up unexpected ranged lines of sight for Watson.
- Use Education Never Ends defensively when you need to draw two cards after losing a combat.
