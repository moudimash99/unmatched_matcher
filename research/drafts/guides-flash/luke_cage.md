## Luke Cage

**In short:** A straightforward brawler who mitigates incoming damage with his special ability and counterattacks with high-value strikes while leveraging his ranged sidekick, Misty Knight.

### Plan A: Tank and counter with heavy damage
**Goal:** Wear down opponents by absorbing attacks and retaliating with massive offensive output.
**How to do it:** Luke's special ability reduces all incoming combat damage from attacks by 2, making his health pool last longer. Position him centrally or close distance using Where's My Money? to force engagement. When attacked, defend with Power Man or Trash Talk, which function as higher-value defenses due to his damage reduction, then strike back with printed 6-value attacks like Sweet Christmas! or boosted Hero For Hire.
**Watch out for:** Effect damage and auto-damage from abilities or schemes bypass his combat reduction entirely, meaning persistent ping effects or direct damage will wear him down rapidly.

### Plan B: Control the pace and recycle resources
**Goal:** Maintain offensive pressure and loop key resources to overwhelm the enemy over the course of the match.
**How to do it:** Utilize Misty Knight's ranged attacks and support cards, such as Daughter of the Dragon—which reaches a high value when she is adjacent—and Got My Back? to swap spaces with Luke and set up coordinated turns. Play Still Standing early to shuffle vital cards back into your deck, establishing a recursion loop that prevents exhaustion and continuously supplies your heaviest attacks.
**Watch out for:** Misty Knight has low health and serves as a primary target for opponents seeking to strip away your board control and ranged options.

### How they lose
- Effect-heavy fighters and auto-damage sources ignore his damage reduction and exploit his low health.
- Highly mobile or ranged characters kite him across larger maps, exploiting his low movement and limited mobility boosts.
- Opponents who manage their defenses well can starve him of the combat wins required to trigger extra card draw and recursion.

### Key cards
- **Sweet Christmas!**: A blank attack that provides reliable, high-damage output and punishes Feints.
- **Trash Talk**: A defense that cancels opponent effects immediately and ends their turn if you win combat.
- **Still Standing**: An attack that lets you shuffle two cards from your discard pile back into your deck, enabling recursion loops.
- **Power Man**: A defense that moves the opposing fighter, pushes them through fighters, and deals ping damage.
- **Where's My Money?**: A scheme that teleports Luke adjacent to the nearest opposing fighter and grants an extra action.

### Tips
- Keep Misty Knight positioned safely at range to support Luke, using her presence to create mix-ups and tactical openings.
- Use Regroup and Pushback as boost sources when you need to cover distance on larger maps, but avoid burning them all too early.
- Save Skin Like Titanium for big opposing attacks when you have enough health to safely reflect substantial damage back at the attacker.
