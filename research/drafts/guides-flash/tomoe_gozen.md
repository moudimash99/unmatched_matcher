## Tomoe Gozen

**In short:** A ranged solo fighter who plays like a melee aggressor. She punishes enemies for leaving her zone with her special ability, closes gaps with movement effects, and deals massive damage up close while staying surprisingly tanky.

### Plan A: Control the board with proximity and pings
**Goal:** Force the opponent into awkward positioning where running away or moving away costs them health.
**How to do it:** Stay close to opposing heroes even though you are a ranged fighter. Your special ability, Attack of Opportunity, deals 1 damage whenever an opposing hero leaves your zone for any reason—by maneuvering, schemes, or after-combat effects. Use A Warrior's Way to push an enemy hero up to 3 spaces out of your zone (triggering the ping) and then teleport Tomoe into a space adjacent to them. Lord Kiso's Final Stand lets you move 3 spaces through sidekicks and either recover 2 health or gain an action, letting you break pins or close in for an attack.
**Watch out for:** The special ability only triggers on heroes, not sidekicks, so 1-health or annoying sidekicks can swarm and harass you without triggering pings.

### Plan B: Land high-value adjacent attacks
**Goal:** Drop heavy hits that opponents cannot easily block or survive.
**How to do it:** 
- Witness My Last Battle must be played face up against an adjacent fighter, and ends your turn after combat. Because it is printed so high, opponents are forced to drop their best defenses, but be mindful of cards like Mary's Mirror Image that copy values or cancels.
- Five Against Thousands lets you discard a card after combat if you are adjacent to put it back into your hand for another swing.
- A Worthy Opponent becomes 5 against a hero, serving as a reliable attack or a hefty 5-value block.

**Watch out for:** Ending your turn adjacent to a surviving hero after playing Witness My Last Battle leaves you exposed to a guaranteed double attack if you lack movement or defense.

### How they lose
- Multi-hero or multi-sidekick decks (such as Medusa, Robin Hood, or Raptors) clog the board, ignore your zone pings, and prevent you from reaching the hero.
- Fighters who cancel card effects strip away the utility of your defensive and offensive tools.
- Large maps with sprawling zones (like Heian-kyo or Mount Rushmore) let opponents move around freely without ever leaving your zones, denying your special ability entirely.
- Getting bogged down against fighters who out-value or out-draw you in a straight exchange without letting you dictate the pressure.

### Key cards
- **Witness My Last Battle**: a 7-value adjacent attack played face up that ends the turn. Your ultimate closer or heavy hitter.
- **A Warrior's Way**: a 2-value attack that pushes an enemy 3 spaces and teleports Tomoe adjacent to them, reliably triggering your special ability.
- **Refuse to Retreat**: a 3-value defense that keeps Tomoe in place and forces adjacent opponents to discard a card.
- **Lord Kiso's Final Stand**: a scheme that moves Tomoe 3 spaces through sidekicks and grants either 2 healing or 1 action.

### Tips
- Favor maps with tight movement pockets, chokepoints, and hard zone cutoffs (like Soho, Raptor Paddock, or Hell's Kitchen) where opponents are forced to cross zone borders and take ping damage.
- Use Deeds of Valor specifically against massive attacks like 6-values or signature finishers, since it caps combat damage at 2.
- Flash of Steel cancels hero card effects immediately and deals 1 damage if adjacent, but does nothing against sidekick effects.
- Treat Lord Kiso's Final Stand as a vital healing tool to bump your effective health pool past 14, while using action-gain options only when you need to press immediate pressure.
