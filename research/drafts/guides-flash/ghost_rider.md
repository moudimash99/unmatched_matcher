## Ghost Rider

**In short:** A slow melee solo fighter who manages 5 Hellfire tokens to power his cards and fund his special ability. He wins by riding over opponents for unblocked chip damage, neutralizing low-health sidekicks, and surviving until the late game.

### Plan A: Vroom vroom chip damage and sidekick control
**Goal:** Wear down opponents and clear fragile sidekicks safely.
**How to do it:** Spend 1 Hellfire when maneuvering to set Ghost Rider's movement to 4 and pass through opposing fighters, dealing 1 damage to each one crossed. Because movement becomes locked at 4, it cannot be increased further with card boosts. This passive damage ignores card values entirely, instantly destroying 1-health sidekicks like Robin Hood, Sun Wukong, and Medusa without initiating combat. Use Hell Rides with Me to reposition, gain 2 Hellfire, and earn an extra action. Let the opponent approach you while maintaining control of the match's tempo.
**Watch out for:** Blindly spamming the maneuver ability exhausts your Hellfire pool and leaves you defenseless. Avoid characters like Bigfoot, Achilles, or Muldoon who handle mobility exceptionally well or punish passive play styles.

### Plan B: The Blaze of Glory finish
**Goal:** Deliver a decisive, unblockable final blow.
**How to do it:** Reduce the opponent to 5 health or lower, ensure they share Ghost Rider's zone, and play Blaze of Glory. Spend any remaining Hellfire to deal 1 unblocked damage per token to every fighter in the zone, including Ghost Rider. If both fighters hit zero health simultaneously, the active player wins. Reset your Hellfire to 5 beforehand using Chains of Hellfire.
**Watch out for:** Feints completely negate Blaze of Glory, wasting your resources and leaving you vulnerable. Prudent opponents will hold their Feints or stay out of your zone when low on health.

### How they lose
- They are outpaced by aggressive, action-heavy fighters or overwhelmed by ranged attackers who maintain their distance.
- Direct damage and heavy hitters like Sherlock Holmes bypass his defenses and exploit his total lack of healing.
- Opponents track his Hellfire pool, applying maximum pressure when his reserves are depleted and he is forced to recharge.
- Random discard effects strip away essential schemes like Chains of Hellfire before they can be played.

### Key cards
- **Control The Demon**: a versatile card that scales with your Hellfire, reaching maximum value at 5 tokens. It serves as your most reliable high-value defense when fully loaded.
- **Penance Stare**: a versatile card that adds the opponent's boost value to its own, with an option to repeat the process by spending 2 Hellfire. It is devastating against high-boost threats.
- **Chains of Hellfire**: a scheme that instantly restores your Hellfire to 5.
- **Stoke The Flames**: a defense card that grants Hellfire equal to the combat damage you sustained if you lose the combat.

### Tips
- Never let your Hellfire drop to zero unless you have a scheme or card effect ready to replenish it immediately.
- Use Control The Demon and Deal With The Devil as your primary defensive anchors when funded by sufficient resources.
- Position yourself outside of the opponent's immediate attack range after maneuvering so they are forced to spend an action approaching you.
- Remember that moving through an opposing fighter multiple times during a single maneuver still only deals 1 damage to them.
