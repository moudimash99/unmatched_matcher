## Buffy

**In short:** A flexible melee fighter with high movement who can pass through opposing fighters. She chooses one of two sidekicks before the match: Giles for card draw and hand control, or Xander for extra attacks and heavy hits.

### Plan A: Control combat with after-effect positioning
**Goal:** Punish enemies who attack you and control positioning with movement card effects.
**How to do it:** Start your turn close to the opponent so they have to engage. On defense, play Cartwheel Kick to move away and deal damage to adjacent fighters. On your turn, use Mr. Pointy to attack safely without an effect to absorb enemy Feints, then follow up with Slayer's Strength to move adjacent fighters and deal damage. Use her special ability to move freely through opposing fighters so you cannot be trapped.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who frequently cancel after-combat effects, such as Bigfoot or Black Widow.

### Plan B: Choose your sidekick for the matchup
**Goal:** Adapt her deck to the opponent using either Giles or Xander.
**How to do it:** 
- Pick Giles for strong scheme control: Insight lets you view an opponent's hand and force a discard, while Training draws three cards to make up for her low natural card draw. 
- Pick Xander for offensive pressure: Military Knowledge provides an attack with card draw, and Right-hand Man turns into a 6-value card when Xander is adjacent to Buffy.
**Watch out for:** Xander and Giles have low health, so they can become liability targets if left exposed.

### How they lose
- Opponents cancel her after-combat effects before they trigger.
- Her health pool is low, leaving little room for error against heavy hitters.
- Choosing Giles lacks the extra heavy attacks that Xander provides, while choosing Xander reduces your scheme card utility and card draw.

### Key cards
- **Mr. Pointy**: a 5-value attack with no effect, ideal for safely drawing out Feints.
- **Cartwheel Kick**: a 2-value defense that moves Buffy and damages adjacent opposing fighters.
- **Slayer's Strength**: a 4-value versatile that moves adjacent fighters and damages them.
- **Insight**: a Giles scheme to inspect an enemy hand and force a discard.
- **Right-hand Man**: a Xander versatile that reaches a value of 6 when he is adjacent to Buffy.

### Tips
- Use her ability to slip past bottlenecks and enemy sidekicks instead of wasting movement.
- Keep Xander close to Buffy so Right-hand Man can reach its full value.
- Protect Giles to ensure you can play all of his scheme cards.
