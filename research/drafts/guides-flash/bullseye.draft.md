## Bullseye

**In short:** A high-pressure, long-range combo assassin with 14 health and no healing. He uses his unique 5-space attack range and post-combat movement to punish opponents, relying on a sequence of combo starters, extenders, and heavy finishers.

### Plan A: Attack twice and execute your combo chain
**Goal:** Melt the opponent's health before they can break through your low defenses, using multi-attack turns to fuel your strongest effects.
**How to do it:** Structure every aggressive turn as a 3-step sequence. First, play a starter to win combat: **I Never Miss** (4 copies) can be boosted or draw a card if unboosted, **I Planned To Be Here** turns into a 5 if you stay put, or **Arrogant But Effective** guarantees a combat win via its after-combat effect. Second, play an extender or scheme like **For My Next Trick** or **Study the Target** (2 copies) to gain actions and draw cards. Third, drop your finisher: **World's Greatest Assassin** (2 copies) ignores their defense value after a won combat, or **I'm Better And I'll Prove It** scales to a 6 value. 
**Watch out for:** Feints completely shut down your combat-winning effects and starters like **I Planned To Be Here**. Bait them out with weaker attacks or use **I Never Miss**, which beats 2-value cancels on base value alone.

### Plan B: Kite and slip away
**Goal:** Stay just out of reach of melee fighters while raining down attacks from up to 5 spaces away.
**How to do it:** Exploit your special ability to attack from 5 spaces away ignoring zones, keeping slow melee heroes constantly scrambling. When an opponent manages to close the distance and threaten a double attack, use **Tactical Retreat** to teleport to a zone with no shared spaces, or **Master Strategist** to move exactly 4 spaces through opposing fighters.
**Watch out for:** Standard movement is only 2, so getting cornered on small maps or pinned down by aggressive multi-action fighters leaves you vulnerable due to your low defensive values (topping out at 3).

### How they lose
- They get rushed down and overwhelmed before your combos can stack up, as your highest defense value is only 3 and you have zero healing.
- Heavy Feint users and cancels strip away your crucial combat-winning triggers.
- You get pinned or cornered in 2v2 matches where two opponents can pressure your low health pool simultaneously.
- You burn through your hand too fast without securing card draw through **Study the Target** or unboosted **I Never Miss** plays.

### Key cards
- **Study the Target**: The most crucial card in your deck. Draws 2 cards, or 3 cards and grants an extra action if you already won a combat this turn.
- **I Never Miss**: A 3-value attack that can be boosted or draw a card if unboosted. Great for beating 2-value Feints and maintaining hand advantage.
- **World's Greatest Assassin**: A 4-value attack that ignores the opponent's card value if you already won a combat this turn.
- **Arrogant But Effective**: A 2-value versatile that guarantees a combat win through its after-combat effect, enabling your "win a combat" card conditions.
- **Tactical Retreat**: A 3-value defense that instantly teleports Bullseye to a space sharing no zones with his current one to escape double attacks.

### Tips
- Mulligan aggressively for **Study the Target**; drawing it early makes or breaks your game.
- Do not play **Arrogant But Effective** or **I'm Better And I'll Prove It** on defense unless you are forced to, as you need their offensive triggers.
- Use **Ricochet** against sidekicks to poke down 1-health targets or finish off weakened heroes from a distance.
- Avoid playing **Right Between The Eyes** as an attack unless you have already secured a combat win; otherwise, treat it as a reliable 3-value boost or a basic defense.
- Keep track of opponent Feints so your high-value combo finishers don't get blanked.
