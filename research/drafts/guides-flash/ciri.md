## Ciri

**In short:** A flexible melee fighter who builds power over time by stocking source cards in her discard pile. She survives early pressure with her unicorn and strong defensive skirmishes, then finishes games with uncancelable effects and heavy-hitting attacks.

### Plan A: Ramp and strike
**Goal:** Build your source count safely while keeping your key offensive and defensive options ready for the late game.
**How to do it:** Maneuver and boost early to fill your discard pile with source cards. Your main power comes from Lion cub of Cintra hitting for 7 once you reach five source, and Zireael forcing a random discard. Use Pushed to the brink early to pick up cheap ping damage and get source cards into your discard pile before you need them. Hold onto your Parry cards to shut down opponent effects when they try to crack your defenses.
**Watch out for:** Opponents will try to rush you down before your uncontainable power kicks in at seven source. Don't waste your strongest source cards on early attacks unless you need immediate pressure.

### Plan B: Control exhaustion with the unicorn and recursion
**Goal:** Outlast your opponent by managing your deck count and recycling key resources.
**How to do it:** Use Ihuarraquax as a body block in the early game to absorb attacks while Ciri builds up. Deploy Unicorn ally to recover health and draw cards, but drop it when you have five or more cards in hand if you want to avoid unwanted card draw and deck exhaustion. Use The lady of space and time at three source to return vital cards to the top of your deck, creating safe passes or looping your best resources. 
**Watch out for:** Keeping Unicorn ally active too long can force you into deck exhaustion faster than you can handle. At eight source, The lady of space and time returns cards directly to your hand and draws a card, which shrinks your deck instead of adding to it.

### How they lose
- Aggressive fighters pressure her before her uncontainable power and high-value source cards come online.
- Heavy early damage kills the unicorn and forces Ciri to spend her defensive cards too fast.
- Characters with hand disruption or strong early pressure interfere with her ability to stack source cards.

### Key cards
- **Zireael**: a versatile card that scales up with source, eventually giving you a max-value block and forcing a random discard.
- **The lady of space and time**: a versatile card that returns a card from your discard pile to the top of your deck at three source.
- **Lion cub of Cintra**: an attack that grows from a modest opener into a heavy 7-value strike.
- **Parry**: a 3-value defense that cancels all effects on the opponent's card.
- **Unicorn ally**: a scheme that heals Ciri and sets up ongoing card draw after combat.

### Tips
- Keep your discard pile organized so you and your opponent can track your source count easily.
- Use Channel the source early if you need to dump unwanted source cards into your discard pile quickly to jumpstart your abilities.
- Do not expect Searching strike to win combats often; treat its effect as a bonus when it triggers.
- Take advantage of Blink to escape pins or close the distance against ranged attackers.
