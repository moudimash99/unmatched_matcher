## Beowulf

**In short:** A bulky melee fighter with 17 health and sidekick Wiglaf, who turns damage taken into Rage tokens to fuel devastating attacks and defenses. He uses non-drawing schemes to pass turns, leaning into a value and fatigue play pattern while forcing opponents to approach him.

### Plan A: Control Rage and force them to come to you
**Goal:** Maintain 2 Rage to keep your powerful options active while mitigating low mobility.
**How to do it:** Since Beowulf lacks movement options, make the enemy come to you by utilizing your 7 scheme passes that do not draw cards. When defending, try to take 1 or 2 damage instead of boosting your defense so high that you take zero, because you need the pain to turn into Rage. Always aim to end your turn with 1 or 2 Rage so you are never caught empty-handed, and use Vigor and Courage to strip a card and grab Rage.
**Watch out for:** Hitting Beowulf with tiny, constant attacks gives him free Rage, but heavy single attacks can overwhelm his limited defenses if he isn't careful.

### Plan B: Spend Rage for high-impact strikes
**Goal:** Crush defenses and punish attackers with scaled attacks and immediate counters.
**How to do it:** 
- The Ancient Heirloom has an effect that cannot be canceled. Spend 1 Rage to boost it, or spend 2 Rage to set its base value to 5 and boost it. 
- The Equal of Grendel is your only defense card; immediately spend 2 Rage to deal damage equal to the printed value of the opponent's card. 
- The War-King scales its 1 value by adding +2 for every Rage spent, acting as an adaptable offensive or defensive tool.
- Epic Poem gives 1 Rage immediately and adds +1 value for each Rage you have, making it a highly economic strike when you start the turn with 2 Rage.
**Watch out for:** Spending all your Rage recklessly leaves you vulnerable on defense and strips your attacks of their bite. Avoid spending 3 Rage on The Ancient Heirloom unless you can afford it and know the opponent isn't defending.

### How they lose
- Ranged fighters keep their distance and chip away without giving him the Rage he needs.
- Opponents use high individual attacks that bypass his weak printed defenses when he is low on Rage.
- Wiglaf is isolated and eliminated too early, stripping Beowulf of his body blocker and extra scheme passes.
- Getting pinned or out-maneuvered due to low movement and few escape options.

### Key cards
- **The Ancient Heirloom**: a 3-value attack that cannot be canceled; boost it with 1 or 2 Rage for massive damage.
- **The Equal of Grendel**: a 3-value defense that lets you spend 2 Rage immediately to deal damage equal to the opponent's printed card value.
- **The War-King**: a 1-value versatile that gains +2 value for each Rage spent.
- **Epic Poem**: a 2-value attack that immediately grants 1 Rage and scales +1 per Rage you hold.
- **Golden Drinking Horn**: a scheme that lets you spend Rage for different effects, including recovering health or drawing cards.

### Tips
- Use Wiglaf as a body blocker to soak hits and protect Beowulf, and use Remnant of Valor even when missing the 1 damage just to pass a turn.
- Golden Drinking Horn can only use each effect once per card play (you cannot stack three heals for 6 health); the sources confirm this is the intended rule.
- Do not use No Contest Expecteth to try and defeat sidekicks; its 3 Rage cost is rarely worth it, so save those copies as boost fuel for The Ancient Heirloom instead.
- Hot for the Battle allows Wiglaf to swap spaces with Beowulf after combat to reposition safely.
