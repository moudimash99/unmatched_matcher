## Eredin Bréacc Glas

**In short:** A melee fighter backed by four fragile Red Rider sidekicks. He starts defensively by redirecting damage and recycling cards, then kills off his riders to become Enraged, gaining increased movement and boosted values across his entire deck.

### Plan A: Clear the riders and go Enraged
**Goal:** Strip away the Red Riders as quickly as possible to unlock Eredin's Enraged state, turning his attacks and defenses into heavy threats.
**How to do it:** Most sources agree that you should get rid of your Red Riders immediately because they have low health and their revival cards are rarely worth the tempo loss. Once all four are defeated, Eredin gets Enraged, adding +1 to all combat cards and increasing his movement to 3. This transforms your kit: Backhand forces discards instead of deck returns, Wild Hunt deals 2 damage to adjacent enemies, and Implacable can be played as an attack. Use Might of the Aen Elle to draw a card, gain an action, and protect yourself from non-combat damage, ensuring you attack every turn to keep the ongoing effect active.
**Watch out for:** Killing your riders too recklessly without extracting value can leave your hand bricked with Portal Defense, which requires an adjacent friendly fighter to function.

### Plan B: Tactical setup with the Red Riders
**Goal:** Use the sidekicks for early shielding and card utility before transitioning into aggressive combat.
**How to doita:** In specific matchups where an immediate rush fails, you can use Foul Purpose by defeating a Red Rider to return the card to your hand. Unyielding Hordes can bring a defeated rider back while adding value when other friendly fighters are adjacent to the target. Portal Defense lets Eredin swap spaces with an adjacent rider to make them take the hit instead. Once you have worn down the opponent's defenses using these tactical plays, finish them off by triggering your Enraged state and unleashing high-value attacks.
**Watch out for:** Sources disagree on how long to keep riders alive; while most advise killing them immediately, one notes that keeping them longer depends on the matchup and helps avoid dead defensive cards.

### How they lose
* Ranged fighters on open maps kite him easily before he can close the distance.
* Opponents who deny the setup by ignoring or efficiently clearing the Red Riders leave him with awkward hands.
* Running out of tricks in the early game if resources are wasted against healing or hand-discarding opponents.

### Key cards
* **Backhand**: a strong attack that forces the opponent to put a random card on top of their deck, upgrading to a full discard when Enraged.
* **Wild Hunt**: a versatile card that deals area damage to adjacent enemies, doubled when Enraged.
* **Foul Purpose**: an attack that can defeat a Red Rider to return to your hand, or target any space on the map when Enraged.
* **Might of the Aen Elle**: a scheme that grants card draw, an extra action, and protection from non-combat damage as long as you attack.
* **Implacable**: a solid defense that can also be deployed as an attack once Eredin is Enraged.

### Tips
* Keep Portal Defense in hand for a late-game push if you are Enraged, as it draws two cards after combat at the cost of a small amount of damage.
* Always make sure you attack on the turn you play Might of the Aen Elle, or it gets discarded at the end of your turn.
* Close for the Kill lets all your fighters move through opposing fighters, providing essential mobility to reposition or escape pins.
* Use Skirmish to reposition fighters after winning a combat exchange.
