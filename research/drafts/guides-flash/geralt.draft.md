## Geralt of Rivia

**In short:** A versatile hybrid fighter who configures his custom gear at the start of the match and leverages heavy-hitting attacks, hand knowledge, and an annoying bard sidekick to control the pace.

### Plan A: Tailor your gear and burst with Sword of Steel
**Goal:** Outpace the opponent with extra actions and aggressive pressure.
**How to do it:** Select the Sword of Steel for your starting gear so you gain actions on attack—two actions if the opposing fighter has 8 or more health—which lets you press the offensive early while they are healthy. Pair it with Tawny Owl for consistent card draw and an extra action. Use Witcher Senses to pull your gear cards straight from your deck or discard pile back into your hand, keeping your strongest tools available. Rend lets you discard to boost its value on attack after your opponent commits, making it a reliable finisher when combined with your high-value gear.
**Watch out for:** Sword of Steel requires aggressive positioning to keep attacking every turn so you don't have to discard Tawny Owl at the end of your turn.

### Plan B: Control distance and disrupt with Dandelion
**Goal:** Keep enemies pinned or pushed away while stripping their hand.
**How to do it:** Use Dandelion's Annoying tune to shove the opposing fighter up to 4 spaces, creating breathing room or keeping them out of melee range. Play Plot Twist to peek at your opponent's hand, force them to discard a card, and draw a card to maintain your hand size. Yrden prevents the opposing fighter from leaving their space, letting you either trap them to approach safely or freeze them in place to run away.
**Watch out for:** Dandelion has only 5 health and no generic sidekick defense cards, making him a fragile target that opponents will rush down early.

### How they lose
- Dandelion dies fast because he lacks generic defense cards, stripping you of his disruption and Plot Twist pressure.
- Forcing you to play your high-value defenses on offense leaves you unprotected against counter-attacks.
- Relying on heavy boosting to make the most of Rend drains your hand when you struggle to maintain card advantage.
- The sources disagree on his overall tier, with some citing his massive 6-value attacks and powerful gear as top-tier power creep, while others argue his lack of consistent mobility and card draw leave him vulnerable in competitive play.

### Key cards
- **Gear: Sword of steel**: a 4-value attack that grants extra actions depending on enemy health.
- **Witcher Senses**: a scheme that searches your deck or discard pile for a gear card.
- **Damn, you're ugly**: a 5-value versatile with a 4 boost.
- **Annoying tune**: a 3-value versatile that pushes the opposing fighter up to 4 spaces.
- **Plot Twist**: a Dandelion versatile that lets you inspect their hand, discard a card, and draw one.

### Tips
- Pick your starting gear carefully before shuffling based on the matchup, as choosing the wrong weapon or scheme can limit your options against specific fighters.
- Use Igni to deal 2 damage to your target and 1 damage to every other adjacent fighter, but watch out for positioning so you don't clip Dandelion.
- Keep Tawny Owl active by ensuring you attack every turn, preventing opponents from stripping cards from your hand.
- Use Wolf Medallion against ranged fighters to cancel card effects and ignore their attack value entirely when they aren't adjacent.
