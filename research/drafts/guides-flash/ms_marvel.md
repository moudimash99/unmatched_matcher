## Ms. Marvel

**In short:** A flexible melee fighter who uses her stretching ability to attack from a distance, kite opponents, and constantly trigger positional card effects. She excels on maps with hard zone cutoffs and small zones.

### Plan A: Control positioning and kite with stretchy attacks
**Goal:** Attack from a safe distance, force your opponent to constantly chase you, and trigger zone-based effects without taking return damage.
**How to do it:** Use her special ability at the start of your turn to move 1 space away or step closer to set up an attack. Because she can attack from up to 2 spaces away without sharing zones on certain spaces, you can strike without letting melee opponents hit you back. Play Momentous Shift as an upgraded attack by utilizing your starting movement change. Look for maps with small zones or abrupt zone cutoffs (like Globe Theater or Raptor Paddock) where your opponent cannot easily hide or retaliate.
**Watch out for:** Map dependency is a major weakness. On boards with wide dual or triple zones (like Navy Pier), opponents can easily stay in your zone and deny your non-sharing effects. Smart opponents will also position themselves to prevent you from triggering your positional requirements.

### Plan B: Keep a high hand count for explosive hits
**Goal:** Maintain card advantage to fuel massive attack values and card-dependent secondary effects.
**How to do it:** Make Fangirl a constant threat by keeping a large hand size to scale its strength. Use I'm Not Touching You to draw cards after combat when sharing no zones. Play Easy Peasy to cycle cards and deal damage to an adjacent fighter when you hold 4 or more cards. Save the 4-boost Friends and Family scheme to either draw up to 7 cards against aggressive counter-pressure decks or use it entirely as boost fuel to make Big Wind Up hit for massive damage.
**Watch out for:** Overdrawing or letting your hand dwindle leaves your attacks weak and strips you of defensive options, exposing your low printed defenses.

### How they lose
- Maps with large zones or few cutoffs block her ability to avoid sharing zones, rendering her key attack and defense effects useless.
- Heavy pressure from fast, aggressive fighters or ranged attackers who outmaneuver her and punish low printed defenses.
- Opponents who carefully track her position and force her into shared zones every turn.
- Running low on cards, which lowers damage output and leaves her vulnerable to exhaustion.

### Key cards
- **Big Wind Up**: an attack that can be boosted if you share no zones with the opposing fighter, allowing for heavy damage.
- **Embiggen**: a versatile card that becomes a 6 value if Ms. Marvel is in more zones than the opposing fighter, serving as a rare high-value defense or attack.
- **Fangirl**: an attack whose strength matches the number of cards in your hand, punishing opponents who let your hand size grow.
- **Shrink! Shrink! Shrink!**: a versatile card that lets you force a discard and move the enemy up to 3 spaces if you share no zones.
- **Slingshot**: a defense that lets her reposition anywhere in her zone after combat to set up her next turn.

### Tips
- Save Friends and Family as boost fuel for Big Wind Up rather than playing it as a scheme, unless you desperately need to refill your hand against aggressive opponents.
- Use your start-of-turn 1-space movement to slip away from adjacent melee fighters, forcing them to waste an action maneuvering just to reach you.
- Play Easy Peasy on defense to take advantage of the card draw and guarantee extra damage if your hand count is high enough.
- Pair her with maps that feature tight bottlenecks and hard zone boundaries to maximize her non-sharing card triggers.
