## The Golden Bat

**In short:** A sturdy melee hero with high mobility and strong base stats who relies on schemes and non-maneuver positioning to trigger massive attack bonuses, grinding opponents down through card recycling and value efficiency.

### Plan A: Strike without maneuvering
**Goal:** Maximize damage output by utilizing your special ability on every possible offensive turn.
**How to do it:** The special ability adds plus two to your attacks as long as you have not taken a Maneuver action that turn. Avoid walking into position normally. Instead, play Alpine Fortress to move up to four spaces and draw cards, or use Arrive Just in Time to place yourself directly next to an opponent. This sets up heavy strikes like Super Strength without sacrificing your bonus. Use Terrifying Roar to trap enemies in their spaces so they cannot escape your reach on the following turn.
**Watch out for:** Ranged fighters who stay out of your movement range, forcing you to spend your first action on a Maneuver and losing the attack bonus.

### Plan B: Outvalue and exhaust the opponent
**Goal:** Maintain card advantage and wear down the enemy deck while keeping your own options open.
**How to do it:** Alpine Fortress lets you choose a card from your discard pile, shuffle it into your deck, draw two cards, and move. You can loop this scheme to continuously pull back key resources or repeat the scheme itself. When holding low-impact cards like A Punch to Shake the Earth or Sight Beyond Sight, do not force weak attacks. Instead, double maneuver away, discard them, and force opponents to overdraw or burn movement boosts. Like a Flash of Golden Light provides massive mobility, allowing you to cross the board and reposition safely.
**Watch out for:** Highly aggressive fighters who apply relentless pressure and deny you the breathing room needed to cycle your deck.

### How they lose
- Aggressive, mobile fighters with strong action economy (such as Raptors, Houdini, or Sinbad) constantly pressure his defenses and exploit middling card draw.
- Getting pinned down in tight spaces without adequate defensive cards in hand.
- Heavy pressure that prevents setting up non-maneuver attack turns.

### Key cards
- **Alpine Fortress**: The cornerstone of your consistency, offering movement, card draw, and deck recycling.
- **Super Strength**: A heavy attack that turns into a devastating strike when paired with your special ability.
- **He Laughs at Your Feebleness**: A reliable, high-value defense against heavy incoming attacks.
- **Imposing Presence**: A defensive card that cancels all effects on the opponent's card.
- **Like a Flash of Golden Light**: Grants exceptional movement to escape danger or close distance unexpectedly.

### Tips
- Treat weak or situational cards as discard fodder during double maneuvers rather than wasting actions on ineffective attacks.
- Mix up your movement methods between schemes and defensive repositioning so opponents cannot easily predict your attack turns.
- Use Vaporizing Eyebeams to force random discards from the opponent's hand, disrupting their planned combos.
- Take advantage of your high health pool to absorb trades when you need to set up a counter-attack.
