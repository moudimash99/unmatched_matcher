## Winter Soldier

**In short:** A ranged solo fighter who hits with massive values and an ability that prevents effects from being canceled, but suffers from self-inflicted drawbacks on his best attacks. He relies on recycling his deck to outlast opponents or overwhelm them with heavy pressure.

### Plan A: Curate and cycle the deck
**Goal:** Burn through bad cards early, then use infinite recursion to loop your best assets.
**How to do it:** Winter Soldier's deck contains several low-impact cards and self-damaging effects that you should discard or boost away immediately. Use Reprogram to pull powerful cards—like your 6-value attacks and Complete the Mission—back into your deck. Because your effects cannot be canceled, your recycling loop is entirely safe from Feints or counterplay, letting you outlast opponents in the late game.
**Watch out for:** Low movement and a lack of reliable card draw outside of Reflex Memories can leave you vulnerable if you burn through your hand too fast.

### Plan B: Unleash uncancelable power
**Goal:** Deal massive damage from range or in melee before the opponent can stabilize.
**How to do it:** Without Remorse provides a safe 6-value ranged attack at the cost of giving the opponent a card. Bionic Arm turns into a devastating 6-value attack when adjacent, though its drawback forces the opponent's fighters to move. Time these strikes carefully, or pair them with A Boy Named Bucky to ignore their negative star effects entirely and gain an extra action for a follow-up.
**Watch out for:** Program to Kill forces you to take 2 damage if you win the combat, making it a severe liability unless its effect is ignored.

### How they lose
* Hyper-aggressive fighters (Houdini, Raptors, Bruce Lee) swarm him before he can set up his recursion loop or stabilize his hand.
* Hand-disruption effects and heavy pressure deny him the breathing room needed to play defensive setups or curate his deck.
* Low mobility makes it easy for opponents to pin him down, especially when adverse card effects grant the enemy extra movement.

### Key cards
* **Complete the Mission:** A 3-value defense that lets you boost the card and deal damage back equal to the combat damage you took.
* **Without Remorse:** A 6-value ranged attack that lets the opponent draw a card.
* **Reprogram:** Returns three cards from your discard pile to your deck, enabling infinite loops.
* **Reflex Memories:** A 5-value versatile card that forces you to discard two random cards and draw two, best used when your hand is empty.

### Tips
* Play A Boy Named Bucky as your first action to bypass negative star effects and secure an extra action for a heavy attack.
* Use Complete the Mission as a flexible defensive tool: boost it with weak cards to mitigate damage, or let damage through to reflect it back.
* Save Reflex Memories for when you have zero or one card in hand to avoid discarding crucial resources.
* Take advantage of item token maps or Wily Fighting to deal with pesky 1-health sidekicks.
