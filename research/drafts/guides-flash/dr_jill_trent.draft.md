## Dr. Jill Trent

**In short:** A versatile melee duo with 13 health on Jill and 6 on Daisy. She alternates between two gadgets at the start of each turn to buff attacks or heal, relies on high-value defense cards and Daisy's life span, and wins through value or exhaustion.

### Plan A: Control the value game with Daisy
**Goal:** Keep Daisy alive to pressure the board and recycle key cards.
**How to do it:** Daisy carries high-value combat cards like Ace Fighter (5 copies, value 5) and can use Jill's versatile tools. Protect her behind Jill or on maps with hiding spots, such as Soho or King's Mountain, to prevent early losses. Play Helpful Assistant (2 copies) to shuffle up to three Jill Trent cards from your discard pile back into your deck, netting extra cards and extending your deck size past the opponent's for an exhaustion win.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who focus down Daisy early deny you your recursion and hand advantage, making exhaustion much harder to reach.

### Plan B: Mind games and damage through Hypnoray Blaster
**Goal:** Out-value opponents in combat using hidden gadget timing and deck knowledge.
**How to do it:** Start your turn on Hypnoray Blaster as your default gadget. When attacking with low-value cards like Laser Pen (3 copies, value 2) or Battle of Wits (3 copies, value 2), Hypnoray Blaster reveals the top card of the opponent's deck and adds its boost to your attack if your printed value is lower. Use Insightful Deduction (2 copies) beforehand to see their top cards, bury a dangerous card on the bottom of their deck, or stack a high boost on top to guarantee that Battle of Wits deals its 3 bonus damage or Hypnoray Blaster hits hard.
**Watch out for:** Feints cancel the combat math, preventing Hypnoray Blaster from adding boost values.

### Plan C: Pivot to Ultrabiotic Tonic for late-game healing
**Goal:** Recover health when the opponent's deck runs thin and high-value blocks are gone.
**How to do it:** Switch to Ultrabiotic Tonic when you plan to attack with a high printed value, such as Energizing Spray (2 copies, value 5), ensuring Jill recovers 1 health after combat when her printed value beats the defender's. This is most reliable in the late game when you know the remaining cards in your opponent's hand and discard pile.
**Watch out for:** While Ultrabiotic Tonic is active, your Hypnotist defenses lose their cancel effect.

### How they lose
- Daisy dies early, depriving you of her heavy attacks, high defense values, and Helpful Assistant recursion.
- Aggressive fighters with high-attack pressure or ranged mobility outpace your movement and crack your defenses.
- Hand disruption or heavy cancel usage strips away your Hypnotist cancels and stops your gadget setups.

### Key cards
- **Helpful Assistant**: Recycles three Jill Trent cards from the discard pile, driving your exhaustion win condition.
- **Indestructible Cloth**: A 5-value defense with an immediate effect preventing non-combat damage, acting as one of the strongest defensive stops in the game.
- **Ace Fighter**: A 5-value versatile card for Daisy that draws a card, giving her immense combat threat and survivability.
- **Insightful Deduction**: Reveals the top three cards of the enemy deck, letting you manipulate the order for your gadgets and bury key threats.
- **Hypnotist**: A 3-value defense that cancels opponent effects immediately, provided Hypnoray Blaster is active.

### Tips
- Use Stasis Diffuser to lock an opponent's card to its printed value, shutting down sudden combat boosts like Momentous Shift.
- Energizing Spray gains an action after combat, letting you chain attacks or hit and run.
- Keep track of the cards revealed by Hypnoray Blaster to gain precise hand knowledge of what your opponent is holding.
- Use Gyroscopic Jetpack (3 copies, value 4) to move through opposing fighters and escape pins safely.
