## Sun Wukong

**In short:** A high-health melee trickster who builds a defensive clone wall at the cost of his own life. He forces devastating mind games with massive attacks like Ox Form, protects himself with incredible anti-damage defenses, and loops his best cards with 72 Transformations.

### Plan A: Build the clone wall and pressure the opponent
**Goal:** Keep the real Sun Wukong safe behind clones while forcing your opponent into losing value trades.
**How to do it:** Use his ability at the start of your turn to take 1 damage and summon a clone adjacent to him. Position your clones to block opposing movement and absorb attacks, ensuring the enemy spends more damage and cards to clear them than it cost you to make them. Because all 30 cards in your deck are any-fighter cards, your clones can attack and defend with the exact same power as Sun Wukong. Keep the pressure high, and use Infinite Strikes with adjacent clones to gain an extra action and scale its value up to 5.
**Watch out for:** Grouping your clones too tightly makes you vulnerable to area-of-effect damage, such as Wily Fighting or zone-clearing effects, which can wipe out your entire wall at once and cost you heavily.

### Plan B: Force impossible mind games
**Goal:** Punish your opponent with unblockable pressure or massive damage depending on whether they choose to defend.
**How to do it:** Mix up your attacks so your opponent never knows what is coming. Ruyi Jingo Bang starts at 0, but if they play a defense card, it triggers a second attack called Tricked You for 4 damage, forcing them to burn two defensive resources on a single action. Conversely, Ox Form is a printed 7-value attack whose effect cannot be canceled; if they do not defend, they take 7 clean damage, but if they do, they must boost their defense. Taunting Laughter forces them to discard a card or let you ignore their defense value entirely. Use Fiery Eyes That See to look at their hand and bury a key defensive card on the bottom of their deck.
**Watch out for:** Playing both copies of Ox Form or Ruyi Jingo Bang too early ruins the threat of the mind game, letting your opponent easily gauge when to block.

### Plan C: Loop your forms and sustain your health
**Goal:** Outlast your opponent by recycling your best cards and recovering lost health.
**How to do it:** Phoenix Form lets Sun Wukong recover 1 health for each clone on the board, helping recoup the health you spent summoning them. Use 72 Transformations after combat to pull Ox Form, Tortoise Form, or Phoenix Form straight from your discard pile back into your hand, letting you repeat your strongest plays multiple times per match.
**Watch out for:** Letting 72 Transformations get canceled by a Feint, which permanently stops you from recycling your key shape-shifting cards.

### How they lose
- Area damage clears his clones cheaply without wasting individual card resources.
- Ranged fighters ignore his clone wall by shooting straight at the real Sun Wukong from a distance.
- He runs low on card draw, making it difficult to keep up pressure or find defensive options.
- His health dwindles too fast if he spams clones against an opponent who consistently wipes them out.
- High-value attack cards bypass weak defensive setups if his heavy blocks are exhausted.

### Key cards
- **Ox Form**: A 7-value attack that cannot be canceled, keeping your opponent constantly guessing and forcing awkward defense boosts.
- **Ruyi Jingo Bang**: A 0-value attack that triggers a 4-value follow-up attack if the opponent defends.
- **Golden Chainmail**: A 4-value defense that negates all combat damage and reflects it directly back at the opposing fighter.
- **Bewilderment**: A 0-value defense that prevents all combat damage and lets you teleport anywhere on the board.
- **72 Transformations**: A versatile card that returns an Ox Form, Tortoise Form, or Phoenix Form from your discard pile to your hand.

### Tips
- Going first in the match lets you establish a clone early before your opponent can close the distance and pressure you.
- Save your heavy defenses like Golden Chainmail and Bewilderment specifically for the real Sun Wukong rather than wasting them on disposable clones.
- Keep your discard pile organized so you know exactly which form to pull back with 72 Transformations.
- Avoid using your ability to summon clones if your health is critically low and you are facing a high-damage finisher.
- Use Sly Monkey to let the real Sun Wukong escape a dangerous position while simultaneously dropping a free clone in his place.
