## George Washington

**In short:** A tricky spymaster who controls the board with three 1-health Culper Spies and uses ruse tokens to force mind games. Opponents must guess whether an attack carries a real ruse effect or a bluff, risking a random card discard to remove the token before choosing defense.

### Plan A: Play the ruse mind game
**Goal:** Force your opponent into bad defensive choices and strip cards from their hand.
**How to do it:** You start with four ruse tokens. Before you attack, place a token next to your card, prompting your opponent to decide whether to discard a random card to remove it before they choose their defense. To keep opponents guessing, occasionally bluff with cards that don't need tokens or use a ruse token without a true ruse effect. Save your actual ruse tokens for high-impact turns, such as placing a ruse on Lead Astray as your last action: if they feint, they take damage, and if they don't, you can reposition with movement. Use Misinformation to regain ruse tokens after winning combats.
**Watch out for:** Relying entirely on token generation can leave you stranded if you draw poor card combinations, though mixing in bluffs keeps the psychological pressure high regardless of your hand.

### Plan B: Keep the Culper Spies alive
**Goal:** Maximize network bonuses and map control while protecting your fragile sidekicks.
**How to do it:** Spread your three 1-health spies across different zones to protect them from area damage. Keep them alive because you only have two cards that bring them back. Use Network Of Spies to add +1 to its value for each other friendly fighter in Washington's zone, and use Sympathetic Stain to look at an opponent's hand and force a discard when a spy is adjacent to their fighter. Delay using the Undercover Agent defense swap early in the game if you still need your spies positioned safely across the board.
**Watch out for:** Area-of-effect attacks easily wipe out 1-health sidekicks if they are clustered together.

### How they lose
- You fail to land key interactions consistently, such as getting Circumvent to land into high blocks or high attacks into feints, leaving you short on damage.
- You are forced into a fatigue game against opponents stronger in exhaustion, particularly when you have to waste actions chasing them down.
- Your spies are eliminated early, depriving you of zone bonuses and reliable spy-based schemes.

### Key cards
- **Circumvent**
- **Lead Astray**
- **Agent 711**
- **Gather Information**
- **Recruit To The Ring**

### Tips
- Mix up your attacks so opponents cannot safely ignore your ruse tokens or save all their feints for obvious moments.
- Against fighters stronger in exhaustion, be mindful that George functions mostly as a defensive fighter with limited passes.
- Do not waste your limited spy-recovery cards when spies can be safely repositioned or kept out of harm's way.
