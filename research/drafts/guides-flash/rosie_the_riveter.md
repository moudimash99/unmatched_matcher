## Rosie the Riveter

**In short:** A progressive melee fighter who builds momentum by activating her four mech suit upgrades over time, transforming into a tougher and more versatile threat as the game goes on, backed by her sidekick Wendy The Welder.

### Plan A: Scale up the mech suit
**Goal:** Stack and cycle upgrades to feed your powerful card effects and pressure the opponent.
**How to do it:** You begin the match with all four upgrades inactive, activating one at the start of each turn or through card effects like We Can Do It! or Arc Welding. When all four are active, they automatically reset at the end of your turn. Order your upgrades strategically depending on the matchup: while many players put card draw first, placing +1 damage or ranged attacks at the front helps against opponents where you want to avoid exhaustion, whereas early draw helps when you need resources immediately. Use Technological Superiority to cancel opponent effects when you have three or more active upgrades, and let Full Metal gain massive value from each active upgrade.
**Watch out for:** Reaching full capacity resets all upgrades at the end of your turn, so time your explosive turns carefully rather than letting them fizzle out accidentally.

### Plan B: Wendy and scheme tempo
**Goal:** Control positioning and overwhelm the board with coordinated actions from Rosie and Wendy.
**How to do it:** Wendy The Welder supports Rosie with ranged attacks and utility using Arc Welding. Schemes like "E" Award and Rapid Development let you reposition both fighters across the board by up to 3 spaces, manipulate your upgrades, draw cards, and gain free attack actions. Use Overrun to clear distance, move through opposing fighters, and chip away with damage along your path. 
**Watch out for:** Leaving Wendy isolated makes her an easy target for enemy aggression, robbing you of crucial board control and card flexibility.

### How they lose
- Rushing straight to exhaustion without managing your upgrade order leaves you under-resourced against efficient opponents.
- Opponents who shut down your active upgrades or ignore your positioning can disrupt your scaling curve.
- Heavy pressure before your mech suit is fully online can overwhelm your defensive options.

### Key cards
- **D-Day:** Activates all upgrades instantly, granting a free attack action and providing recovery plus damage if they were already active.
- **Hasty Repairs:** Deactivates an upgrade when you have active ones to recover 3 health for Rosie.
- **Momentous Shift:** A versatile card that jumps from 3 to 5 value if your fighter started the turn in a different space.
- **Loose Lips Sink Ships!:** Forces the opponent to reveal their top card, reducing their card value by that card's boost value.

### Tips
- Keep track of your upgrade order carefully, as deactivating them individually or all at once affects how quickly your preferred bonuses come back online.
- Save your biggest "turn on all upgrades and gain an extra action" scheme for late-game swings.
- Adjust your upgrade priority based on whether the matchup demands faster card draw, extra damage, or board control.
