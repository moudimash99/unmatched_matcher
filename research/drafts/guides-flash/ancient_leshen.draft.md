## Ancient Leshen

**In short:** A slow ranged fighter with 13 health and a base movement of 1 who relies on two fast, highly mobile Wolves (move 3) to control the board, block paths, and enable constant pressure. He heals steadily with ongoing schemes and threatens massive damage when his ability triggers on a second attack.

### Plan A: Harass with the Wolves and control positioning
**Goal:** Keep the opponent at bay, block bottlenecks, and chip away with constant attacks while protecting the Leshen.
**How to do it:** Send your Wolves in using their 3 movement to surround opponents and block choke points. Use disturbing howls to reposition enemies and gain an action, or harrying strike, which adds +2 for each fighter adjacent to the target. Keep the Leshen safely behind the Wolves so enemies are forced to chew through the sidekicks first. 
**Watch out for:** Letting the Wolves die carelessly without a way to bring them back, which shuts down your healing and board control.

### Plan B: The healing and exhaustion game
**Goal:** Outlast the opponent by recovering health every turn and controlling the clock.
**How to do it:** Play Strength of the pack to summon a Wolf and recover 1 health at the start of your turn, keeping it active by maintaining a Wolf in the Leshen's zone. Use Primeval slam to resummon defeated Wolves and reposition them across the board. In the late game, vanish into murder can act as a double pass to push advantage or safely reposition while drawing a card.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who can rush through your Wolves and burst down your low 13 health before your healing adds up.

### Plan C: Threaten the double-attack finish
**Goal:** Close out the game with a devastating second attack boosted by Heart of the Forest.
**How to do it:** His ability adds +3 to the Leshen's attacks if it already attacked this turn. Do not try to force this early, as opponents will run away or block. Instead, use it as a late-game win condition. Pair flock of birds (which lets you teleport anywhere after combat) or vanish into murder to reposition right next to an escaping enemy and land a surprise high-value second strike.
**Watch out for:** Overcommitting early to force the ability and leaving yourself stranded in the open with no defenses.

### How they lose
- Fast, aggressive fighters run him down before his wolves and healing can establish control.
- He has only 9 personal defenses in his deck, leaving him vulnerable if enemies punch through the Wolves.
- Primeval guardian forces the opponent to discard and add its boost value to their card, which can backfire against fighters with huge boosts.
- Ranged fighters who don't need to approach him can whittle him down.
- Getting pinned with no movement left, since his base move is only 1.

### Key cards
- **Vanish into murder**: deals damage in his zone, removes him from the board, places him anywhere next turn, and draws a card. His strongest scheme.
- **Strength of the pack**: summons a Wolf and gives ongoing healing of 1 health at the start of your turn as long as a Wolf stays in his zone.
- **Flock of birds**: a 5-value attack that lets you place the Leshen in any space after combat, securing movement or setting up a second attack.
- **Primeval guardian**: a 5-value defense that makes the opponent discard and add their top deck's boost value, while moving each Wolf 3 spaces.
- **Harrying strike**: a 2-value Wolf versatile that gains +2 for each fighter adjacent to the target, scaling up to a heavy block or attack.

### Tips
- Mulligan aggressively for attacks; you don't want a starting hand full of versatile cards or defenses.
- Use Wily Fighting on defense with the Leshen to deal 1 auto-damage to adjacent enemies.
- Remember that Primeval slam summons a Wolf and moves all Wolves up to 3 spaces, bringing back defeated sidekicks.
- Don't camp in the back trying to force your ability all game; use standard pressure and keep your hand size high with Planted feet or Nature abounds.
- Token and gimmick maps suit his low movement and token interactions well.
