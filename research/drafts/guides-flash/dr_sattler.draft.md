## Dr. Sattler

**In short:** A technical strategist who relies on Dr. Malcolm to spread insight tokens, outlasts opponents with massive healing, and strikes back with automatic damage and scaled attacks.

### Plan A: Plant insight and cash in for massive healing
**Goal:** Outlast your opponent by cycling through your high-value healing cards while keeping Dr. Malcolm alive to accelerate token placement.
**How to do it:** Both Dr. Sattler and Dr. Malcolm place an insight token on their new space whenever they move. Double maneuvering with Dr. Malcolm alive lets you quickly fill the board with tokens. Once your tokens are out, use Woman Inherits the Earth on defense to immediately draw a card and recover health equal to the number of tokens on the board, clearing them so you can start placing them again. Use Life Finds a Way to force a discard and heal both fighters based on the discarded card's boost value.
**Watch out for:** Opponents will prioritize hunting down Dr. Malcolm because his survival is crucial for fast token generation; without him, your insight production is cut in half, making your token-dependent cards much slower to set up.

### Plan B: Control space and punish with auto-damage
**Goal:** Control board positioning and wear down enemies without relying solely on combat values.
**How to do it:** 
- Violently, If Necessary deals 2 damage after combat to every opposing fighter on or adjacent to an insight token, making it a strong tool against heroes with multiple sidekicks.
- Lock The Doors! deals 2 damage and lets you move up to 2 spaces.
- The Concept of Attraction lets you teleport your fighter to any insight token to escape pressure.
- When your token count is maxed out, spend them all on You Never Had Control, That's the Illusion to land a heavy attack.
**Watch out for:** Feints will cancel your attack and defense effects, so time your big token payoffs carefully and avoid walking into predictable cancels.

### How they lose
- Dr. Malcolm dies early, leaving Dr. Sattler to struggle with slow, single-fighter insight generation.
- Heavy Feint users shut down key card effects, blanking your healing and auto-damage.
- Low printed base defense values leave her vulnerable to being rushed down before her healing engine comes online.

### Key cards
- **Woman Inherits the Earth**: instantly heals up to 5 health based on your insight tokens and replaces itself in your hand.
- **You Never Had Control, That's the Illusion**: scales up to a 7-value attack based on your tokens before clearing them.
- **Life Finds a Way**: forces a top-deck discard and heals both Dr. Sattler and Dr. Malcolm.
- **Violently, If Necessary**: deals area damage after combat to enemies near insight tokens.
- **The Concept of Attraction**: teleports your fighter to an insight token to escape danger.

### Tips
- Position your insight tokens deliberately rather than dropping them randomly, as placement matters for both defense and area damage.
- Keep a full hand when possible to maximize the value of I Think We're Back in Business.
- Use Sexism in Survival Situations against opposing heroes for a 4-value defense and a repositioning step.
