## T. Rex

**In short:** A relentless, aggressive melee heavy-hitter with a massive 27 health pool and an extended base. She draws a card at the end of every turn, keeping her hand full at the cost of racing toward exhaustion, and deals self-inflicted damage to fuel heavy offensive pressure.

### Plan A: Go full throttle at the hero
**Goal:** Overwhelm the opposing hero with high-value attacks and constant pressure before your self-damage and card draw catch up to you.
**How to do it:** Move in and attack every turn, as any turn not spent attacking is wasted. Because you draw a card at the end of every turn, you naturally maintain card advantage. Use your schemes like Closer Than She Appears and Ripples in the Water to close distance. When you need to cross the board, boost your maneuvers with high-value boost cards like Reckless Lunge (boost 4) or 65 Million Years of Gut Instinct (boost 3). 
**Watch out for:** Ranged fighters and careful opponents who try to kite you and force a prolonged game. If they make you chase them across large maps while avoiding engagements, your mandatory card draw will push you straight into a fatal exhaustion clock.

### Plan B: Exploit large-fighter reach and board control
**Goal:** Bypass obstacles, control positioning, and lock enemies into your attack zone.
**How to do it:** As a large fighter, you occupy up to two spaces and can attack up to two spaces away, even over fighters blocking those spaces. Ignore map one-way arrows, and use Terrifying Roar on defense or when adjacent to freeze enemies in their spaces, preventing movement abilities and setting up a brutal double attack on your next turn. Use Thrash defensively to clear out bothersome sidekicks in your zone when winning combat.
**Watch out for:** Large fighters cannot use secret passages, making certain maps like Baskerville Manor a major movement chore if you are forced to boost constantly around long paths.

### How they lose
- Ranged fighters (like Muldoon or Nikola Tesla) and mobile characters keep their distance, kite her around large maps, and let her die to exhaustion.
- Opponents avoid engagement or hide behind sidekicks and obstacles, forcing her to burn through her own health and deck.
- Her low defense card count (only 10 cards) leaves her vulnerable if she cannot end the game quickly with sheer aggression.

### Key cards
- **Reckless Lunge**: an attack that deals 3 damage to the opponent and 3 to yourself, with a massive boost value of 4. Best saved for movement boosting unless you are going for the kill.
- **15,000 Pounds of Muscle**: an attack that ignores the opponent's card value while dealing 2 damage to yourself after combat. Ideal for punching through strong value blocks.
- **When Dinosaurs Ruled the Earth**: an attack you can boost; if you win, you draw a card, gain an action, and take 2 damage.
- **Ripples in the Water**: places T. Rex anywhere in her zone and forces adjacent enemies to discard a random card.
- **Terrifying Roar**: a versatile card that immediately stops fighters from leaving their spaces for the rest of the turn.

### Tips
- Do not double maneuver, as drawing extra cards on top of your mandatory end-of-turn draw accelerates your exhaustion.
- Use your massive health pool to absorb damage trades; do not worry about preserving health at the expense of pressure.
- Mix in 65 Million Years of Gut Instinct to pull key utility or attack cards back from your discard pile.
