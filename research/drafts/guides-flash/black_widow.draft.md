## Black Widow

**In short:** A versatile, double-ranged spy who dictates the tempo and controls the board alongside Maria Hill, converting specialized mission schemes and heavy discards into overwhelming value.

### Plan A: Execute missions and recur pressure
**Goal:** Outpace the opponent through repeatable auto-damage, hand disruption, and relentless scheme cycling.
**How to do it:** Begin by utilizing her extra starting card, The Moscow Protocol, to kick off your mission chain. Position Maria Hill to survive and play Acting Director of S.H.I.E.L.D., which moves both fighters through enemies and shuffles a played mission scheme from your discard pile back into your deck. Select the mission that fits the matchup: deploy The Kinshasa Directive to strip cards from your opponent's hand, or drop The Madripoor Sanction and The Firenze Agenda to wipe out sidekicks and deal direct damage across zones.
**Watch out for:** Revealing your deck contents while searching for missions gives observant opponents a clear look at your remaining resources and defense counts.

### Plan B: Control the tempo and drain their hand
**Goal:** Keep your opponent defensive while managing your hand size to swing momentum entirely in your favor.
**How to do it:** Use her 5-value Widow's Sting and 4-value Widow's Bite to pressure opposing fighters, forcing discards whenever you win combat. Play Widow's Kiss to lock aggressive melee fighters into their space and set up devastating follow-up turns. When your hand dwindles, trigger The Budapest Gambit to draw five cards and force every opponent to discard a random card, creating an immense resource swing.
**Watch out for:** Over-relying on card draw without monitoring your deck count can push you dangerously close to exhaustion.

### How they lose
- Aggressive, high-pressure fighters (such as Ghost Rider or T-Rex) run her down before her complex mission engine can take over.
- Opponents who prioritize eliminating Maria Hill early strip Black Widow of her crucial scheme recursion.
- Heavy defense and clever positioning minimize the impact of her zone-wide mission damage.
- Low-value printed defenses make her squishy 13-health pool vulnerable to big attacks.

### Key cards
- **Acting Director of S.H.I.E.L.D.**: A versatile 4-value attack/defense that repositions your fighters and recycles mission schemes.
- **The Budapest Gambit**: A scheme that refills your hand and forces random discards when you are low on options.
- **Widow's Sting**: A 5-value ranged attack with after-combat movement to help you kite and position.
- **Double Identity**: A 2-value defense that lets Black Widow and Maria Hill swap spaces and change the active defender.
- **Life Model Decoy**: A 0-value defense that resurrects a defeated Maria Hill adjacent to Black Widow with 3 health.

### Tips
- Protect Maria Hill carefully; her presence is essential for recurring your most powerful mission schemes.
- Keep track of which missions are in your discard pile so Acting Director of S.H.I.E.L.D. always has a prime target to shuffle back.
- Use Fake Out to bait out strong defensive cards, trading the lost combat for a card draw and an extra action.
- Mix in Caught in a Web and standard Feints to disrupt opponent after-combat effects and card setups.
- Use Widow's Line to shift enemies out of position and clear the way for mission execution.
