## Jekyll & Hyde

**In short:** A form-shifting solo fighter who operates primarily as Dr. Jekyll to defend, draw cards, and recover health, then transforms into Mr. Hyde to deliver devastating attacks with Forever Hyde before reverting to safety.

### Plan A: Build and release Forever Hyde
**Goal:** Close out the game suddenly with an overwhelming attack that bypasses normal defense limits.
**How to do it:** Retain your Dr. Jekyll cards in hand rather than spending them on early defense. When you have accumulated enough of them, transform into Mr. Hyde at the start of your turn and play Forever Hyde, discarding those Jekyll cards during combat to add +2 to the attack value for each one. Stock your hand beforehand using Scientific Method, which draws cards equal to the damage you were dealt, or Calming Research. 
**Watch out for:** If an opponent plays a Feint against Forever Hyde, it cancels the card's effect. You still deal base damage, but your discarded Jekyll cards are wasted, leaving you vulnerable.

### Plan B: Let them come to you
**Goal:** Attack as Mr. Hyde without bleeding health from movement penalties.
**How to do it:** While you are Mr. Hyde, maneuvering costs you 1 damage. Avoid taking this penalty by making your opponent come to you and end their turn adjacent to your miniature. Alternatively, use non-maneuver movement effects to position yourself. With Haste! moves Jekyll up to 4 spaces after combat, setting you up to start your next turn right next to the enemy as Hyde. Pure Evil lets Hyde jump anywhere in his zone and deal area damage to adjacent fighters. Use Recoiling Blow or Madness Relents to safely transform back to Jekyll after striking.
**Watch out for:** Ranged fighters or mobile opponents who simply run away when you become Hyde, forcing you to maneuver and damage yourself just to reach them.

### How they lose
* Early aggression forces you to burn your defensive Jekyll cards early, destroying the discard fodder you need for Forever Hyde.
* Mr. Hyde has poor defensive options. If you get stuck as Hyde without Madness Relents or Skirmish, you will take heavy damage.
* The deck is split strictly between defensive Jekyll cards and offensive Hyde cards, making your turns predictable to experienced opponents.
* As a true solo hero with no sidekicks, you lack body-blocks and have a low total health pool relative to fighters with support units.

### Key cards
* **Forever Hyde**: A high-value attack that scales massively by discarding Jekyll cards. Your primary win condition.
* **Duality of Man**: A versatile Any card that becomes a 6-value defense when played by Jekyll or a 6-value attack when played by Hyde.
* **With Haste!**: A 4-value Jekyll defense that moves you up to 4 spaces after combat, perfect for closing gaps.
* **Scientific Method**: A 2-value Jekyll defense that draws cards equal to the damage you took, loading your hand for Forever Hyde.
* **Madness Relents**: A 4-value Hyde defense that transforms you back into Jekyll after combat to escape danger.

### Tips
* Stay as Dr. Jekyll for roughly three-quarters of your turns, treating him as your default state for survival and setup.
* Be cautious when drawing three cards with Calming Research; putting the unchosen cards on the bottom of your deck can bury your best combo pieces.
* Save your Feints and Skirmishes to protect Mr. Hyde since his native defense pool is very thin.
* Use Strange Case to deal damage equal to a revealed boost value while drawing a card, maintaining hand size while pressuring the enemy.
* Avoid using Succumb to Compulsion to attack; save its movement and transformation effect or feed it to Forever Hyde.
