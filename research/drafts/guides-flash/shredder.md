## Shredder

**In short:** A melee fighter who controls the board by spawning Foot soldiers and attacking through them. He relies on Bebop & Rocksteady to body block and can unleash massive multi-action turns using scheme cards when surrounded.

### Plan A: Camp, build, and burst
**Goal:** Accumulate Foot soldiers around Shredder on a high-connection space, then cash in with heavy attacks and action-gain schemes.
**How to do it:** Pick a map space with many connections, move there, and camp. Use Bebop & Rocksteady to body block and apply pressure. Your special ability deploys a Foot soldier to a path adjacent to a friendly fighter at the start of your turn, letting you fill the surrounding spaces. Once your network is established, play Obedient Subjects to gain extra actions based on adjacent Foot soldiers, then attack with Swarming Strike, whose value increases by 1 for each adjacent Foot soldier.
**Watch out for:** If Bebop & Rocksteady are defeated early and you lack the Back to work! scheme to revive them, Shredder loses his main support and gets pressed hard by aggressive opponents.

### Plan B: Hit and run with reach
**Goal:** Attack from a distance safely using Foot soldiers to bypass melee range limitations.
**How to do it:** Instead of permanent camping, place a Foot soldier first, maneuver, and attack an enemy adjacent to that soldier without closing in directly. Gruff Escort deploys two Foot soldiers anywhere and moves Shredder up to two spaces to help set up these angles. Long Shot also becomes a 5-value attack when the opposing fighter is not adjacent to you.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who can push Shredder off his camping spot—such as Leonardo—or boost through his Foot soldiers to clear them out will dismantle your network.

### How they lose
- High-tempo fighters pressure him hard because his practical defense counts are low and he relies on Masterful Defense to recover health.
- Opponents with board mobility or displacement effects push Shredder away from his clustered positions.
- Opponents can boost through his Foot soldiers during a maneuver to remove them from the board.

### Key cards
- **Swarming Strike**
- **Obedient Subjects**
- **Perplexing Tactics**
- **Masterful Defense**
- **Back to work!**

### Tips
- Use extra Foot soldier tokens not currently on the board as physical action counters when Obedient Subjects grants you a large number of actions.
- Watch out for Savagery: since it deals damage to each fighter adjacent to your fighter, make sure you aren't standing next to your own fighters when playing it.
- Disrupting Strike locks an opponent's card to its printed value, stopping floating modifiers.
