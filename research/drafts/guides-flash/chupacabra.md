## Chupacabra

**In short:** A solo melee fighter with 14 health and a move of 3 who leverages an attack-triggered card draw ability to maintain aggressive pressure. He unlocks powerful effects when dropping to 7 or less health, utilizing self-damage and combat positioning to operate effectively in his preferred health threshold.

### Plan A: Trigger the low-health sweet spot
**Goal:** Drop to 7 or less health safely to activate high-impact card effects, then overwhelm opponents with multi-attack sequences.
**How to do it:** Intentionally absorb early damage or use Unsettle to deal 2 damage to yourself while forcing extra discards. Once at 7 or less health, play Feeding to gain an action and recover 1 health, and use Wounded beast as a 5-value attack. Chain these into Blood in the air on a subsequent action after securing a combat win. 
**Watch out for:** Transitioning to low health too early against high-burst characters, as low printed defense values leave you vulnerable to swift elimination.

### Plan B: Press the attack and out-draw them
**Goal:** Maintain relentless offensive pressure by capitalizing on your special ability to draw a card after every attack.
**How to do it:** Use the ability The hunger to replace your attacking cards immediately. Pair Traveler of the night—which grants an action and 4 movement through opposing fighters—with your attacks to stay glued to the enemy. Balance your card count around a comfortable threshold to avoid overdrawing while preserving options.
**Watch out for:** Burning through your deck too rapidly from constant attack draws, which accelerates the exhaustion clock.

### Plan C: Punish high values and crowd control
**Goal:** Turn strong enemy cards against them and sustain your health pool.
**How to do it:** 
- Play The more they struggle against high enemy values to double their printed card value.
- Use Tooth and tail to damage adjacent fighters and heal 1 health for each one hit, clearing sidekicks while staying alive.
- Rely on Natural toughness to return to your hand if you lose a combat, ensuring defensive readiness.
**Watch out for:** The more they struggle failing entirely if the opponent plays an unnumbered card or a zero-value defense.

### How they lose
- They suffer defeat from exhaustion caused by excessive card draw from constant attacks.
- Opponents burst them down before they can safely utilize their low-health threshold.
- Low printed defenses fail against heavy-hitting attackers.
- Defensive cancels disrupt setup turns and waste key high-value attacks.

### Key cards
- **Feeding**: An attack that grants an action and recovers 1 health when you are at 7 or less health.
- **Wounded beast**: A versatile card that becomes a 5-value attack or defense when you are at 7 or less health.
- **The more they struggle**: A 0-value attack that doubles the printed value of the opponent's card.
- **Traveler of the night**: A scheme that moves you 4 spaces through enemies and grants 1 action.
- **Natural toughness**: A 3-value defense that returns to your hand if you lose the combat.

### Tips
- Save Unsettle to force discards or to intentionally lower your health into the 7-or-less threshold when needed.
- Time Blood in the air for your second action so you can fulfill its requirement of already winning a combat this turn.
- Watch your opponent's hand count and use Ambush to strip away a random card while adding its boost value to your attack.
- Monitor your deck count carefully to avoid accidentally exhausting yourself due to constant attack draws.
