## Nikola Tesla

**In short:** A high-output ranged combo fighter who manages a fluid resource of two coil charges to unleash heavy damage, extra actions, and control effects. He relies on pressure and adaptability, but has thin defensive options and a low health pool.

### Plan A: Combo aggression with coil management
**Goal:** Overwhelm the opponent by chaining high-value attacks and extra actions before they can stabilize.
**How to do it:** Start your turn with both coils charged to trigger his special ability, automatically dealing 1 damage to each adjacent opposing fighter and pushing them up to 1 space. Use 7 Hertz (3 copies, value 4) to discharge coils for extra actions and card draw, maintaining relentless offensive tempo. Spend Full Charged (2 copies, scheme) and Kinetic Induction (3 copies, value 2) to quickly replenish your charges between assaults. When you need to finish a sequence, Death Ray (3 copies, value 3) can reach up to 7 when discharging two coils.
**Watch out for:** Feints completely deny your coil discharges and disrupt your combos, leaving you vulnerable to counter-attacks due to his weak baseline defensive cards.

### Plan B: Disruption and board control
**Goal:** Control enemy positioning and strip key options directly from the opponent's hand and deck.
**How to do it:** Use X-Ray Radiation (3 copies, value 4) to inspect the top card of the opponent's deck, discarding it and potentially adding its boost value to your attack or defense. Play Lightning Storm (3 copies, value 3) defensively or offensively to clear out low-health sidekicks with area-of-effect damage. Deploy Remote Control (2 copies, scheme) to reposition opposing fighters into your zones, setting them up for punishing strikes or isolating them from allies. Repulsion Blast (3 copies, value 2) helps you escape early pins or force random discards.
**Watch out for:** Over-discharging your coils for minor effects leaves you dry when you need an immediate cancel or a massive damage spike.

### How they lose
- Feints and cancel effects shut down his coil-dependent effects and ruin his turn momentum.
- High-aggression fighters with multiple actions out-tempo him before he can set up his coils.
- His low health pool of 14 with no sidekicks leaves him exposed to big attacks, and he has no passes to dig deeper into his deck without burning actions.
- Running low on cards pushes him toward exhaustion, where his lack of built-in recursion hurts.

### Key cards
- **7 Hertz**: A 4-value attack that fuels your action economy and card draw by discharging coils.
- **Polyphase Coils**: A 3-value versatile card that cancels opponent effects and can ignore their card value entirely.
- **X-Ray Radiation**: A 4-value versatile that provides crucial deck knowledge, mills enemy cards, and scales up damage.
- **The Alternating Current**: A 5-value attack that either charges both coils or discharges them to recover 2 health.
- **Intense Experimentation**: A 3-value defense card that draws cards and restores health when discharging coils.

### Tips
- Always monitor your coil count at the end of your turn to ensure you trigger your automatic adjacency damage and push at the start of your next turn.
- Mulligan aggressively for key setup and card draw tools like Intense Experimentation or X-Ray Radiation to jumpstart your offense.
- Prioritize small maps where your range and mobility outpace opponents who try to kite you.
- Avoid discharging coils blindly against opponents holding unspent Feints.
