## Achilles

**In short:** A high-health melee hero who operates with his sidekick Patroclus until triggering an explosive enrage ability. Once Patroclus falls, Achilles gains bonus attack power and card draw on combat wins, turning him into an aggressive powerhouse racing against his own exhaustion clock.

### Plan A: Control the fall of Patroclus
**Goal:** Trigger Achilles' enrage ability on your own terms while maintaining a healthy hand of cards.
**How to do it:** Do not let Patroclus die by accident early in the game. Use his own damaging cards like Battle Frenzy and The Day of Your Doom to drain his 6 health deliberately. Defend with Under Achilles' Helm to swap Achilles out and force Patroclus to take incoming damage. Ensure you have a large hand when Patroclus finally dies so you do not lose critical cards to the random two-card discard penalty. 
**Watch out for:** Killing Patroclus with an empty or tiny hand leaves Achilles defenseless and wastes vital resources. Opponents will also try to snipe Patroclus early to disrupt your setup.

### Plan B: Press the enrage advantage
**Goal:** Overwhelm the opponent with massive attacks and sustained card draw before exhaustion catches you.
**How to do it:** Once Patroclus is defeated, every attack gains a plus-two damage boost, turning versatile cards like Skirmish into 6-value threats and Brothers in Arms into a 6-value log. Winning any combat draws a card, keeping your hand full while you maintain relentless pressure. Use Test for Weakness to bait defenses and follow up with Relentless Assault. On spacious maps, use your high-boost cards like Under Achilles' Helm to maneuver and chase down opponents trying to kite away.
**Watch out for:** Because you draw a card every time you win a combat, you will tear through your 30-card deck rapidly. Dragging the match out leads to a fast and fatal exhaustion clock.

### Plan C: Recycle your key tools
**Goal:** Keep your best options looping back into your hand.
**How to do it:** Use Battle Hardened to pull vital cards back from your discard pile. Target your unblockable Spear Throw for consistent auto-damage outside of combat, or recycle Feints and attack cards to sustain your offensive push. 
**Watch out for:** Losing your recursion pieces to random discards when Patroclus dies. Time your plays carefully to preserve your options.

### How they lose
- They run out of cards too quickly due to the enraged card draw, falling to exhaustion before finishing the opponent.
- Ranged fighters and high-mobility heroes kite Achilles across large maps, forcing him to waste actions and boosts.
- Opponents clear Patroclus early while Achilles holds a small hand, stripping him of key defenses through the random discard penalty.
- Sidekick-heavy rosters block his path and drain his resources before he can reach the enemy hero.

### Key cards
- **Under Achilles' Helm**: A 2-value defense that swaps Achilles with Patroclus, soaking damage and setting up your enrage, backed by a massive 4-boost value.
- **Brothers In Arms**: A 4-value attack that grants an action while Patroclus lives, or hits for 6 after his death.
- **Spear Throw**: A scheme that deals 2 unblockable damage to an opposing fighter in your zone, serving as a vital pass that doesn't draw cards.
- **Battle Hardened**: A versatile card that returns any card from your discard pile to your hand for endless utility.

### Tips
- Use Day of Your Doom before Battle Frenzy early on to draw out opponent Feints and secure your self-inflicted damage.
- Keep your discard pile organized and protect your crucial cards from the random discard when Patroclus falls.
- On smaller maps like the Raptor Paddock, Achilles dominates by cutting off escape routes completely.
- Against fighters who rely on card effects, keep Feints in reserve to shut down their counter-plays.
