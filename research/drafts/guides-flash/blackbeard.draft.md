## Blackbeard

**In short:** A ranged value and control fighter who manages doubloons to deny his opponents resources and power his own high-end effects. He relies on his Sea Dogs to body block and wear down enemy hands, forcing opponents into sub-optimal ransom choices.

### Plan A: Control the treasury and force sub-optimal ransom choices
**Goal:** Deplete the treasury so your opponent has no doubloons to cancel your effects or buy their way out of your pressure.
**How to do it:** Use Plunder early and often to steal doubloons from the treasury and move your fighters up to 3 spaces through opposing fighters. This sets up your board positioning while keeping the economy starved. When you attack, pick moments when the opponent has 0 or 1 doubloons so they cannot afford the ransom costs on cards like Avast Ye! or Fearsome and calculating. 
**Watch out for:** Taking combat damage gives a doubloon back to the treasury and hands resources right back to your opponent. Avoid getting hit by positioning your Sea Dogs as protective body blocks.

### Plan B: Lock down the game with hand knowledge and disruption
**Goal:** Rip key attacks and defenses out of your opponent's hand before they can use them.
**How to do it:** Play Fearsome and calculating exclusively on defense when the effect is guaranteed. Looking at your opponent's hand and forcing a chosen discard strips away their heavy hitters and defensive counters, leaving them with nothing to punch through your defenses or heal effects. Pair this control with Parlay to shut down opposing card effects completely.
**Watch out for:** Playing your chosen discard attacks on offense, which gives your opponent a chance to react or move away before you can fully capitalize on the hand knowledge.

### How they lose
- High-damage aggressive fighters with explosive output and too many high-value attacks (like T-Rex) overwhelm his defenses and push damage through.
- Losing the Sea Dogs too early turns Blackbeard into an exposed target, forcing you to waste movement and actions repositioning or burning boosts.
- Letting the opponent stockpile doubloons lets them easily pay ransoms to ignore your most disruptive effects.
- Direct damage and area attacks clear out the Sea Dogs cheaply without triggering your defenses.

### Key cards
- **Plunder**: a 0-value scheme that moves your fighters 3 spaces through enemies and steals a doubloon. The absolute core of your economy and setup.
- **Fearsome and calculating**: a 3-value versatile with an immediate effect that lets you look at the opponent's hand and discard a card unless they pay a doubloon. Your best defensive disruption.
- **Queen Anne's Revenge**: a 7-value versatile attack or defense whose effects cannot be canceled. Use it as a massive attack against stasis effects or as a heavy defense when you need to stop major damage.
- **Intimidating visage**: a 4-value defense that recovers 2 health if Blackbeard is adjacent to an opposing fighter, effectively turning melee attacks into net healing.
- **Give no quarter**: a 3-value attack that becomes a 5 when another friendly fighter is adjacent, adding action-gain pressure.

### Tips
- Mulligan aggressively for Plunder at the start of the game so you can strip the treasury immediately.
- Use your Sea Dogs as 1-health shields to absorb attacks, waste opponent resources, and keep Blackbeard safe from taking combat damage.
- Save your ability's extra action gain mostly for late-game finishers or guaranteed lethal turns, since spending a doubloon hands a resource directly to your opponent.
- Defend with Intimidating visage against melee attackers to shut down their damage and heal back up.
- Keep Light the fuse in mind when facing 1-health sidekicks like Wukong's clones to clear them out with area damage.
