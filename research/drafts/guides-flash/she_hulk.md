## She-Hulk

**In short:** A resilient melee bruiser with high health who converts cards into unblockable ability damage and sets up devastating single-card attacks while pressuring opponents.

### Plan A: Control the zone and pressure with Just Throw Something
**Goal:** Deal steady unblockable damage from range and whittle down your opponent without spending actions.
**How to do it:** At the start of your turn, you may discard a card to deal damage equal to its boost value to a fighter in your zone. Force opponents to come to you by staying back, or let them approach while you manage your hand. When they try to run or kite you, use **Leap Toward** to move up to four spaces through opposing fighters and gain an action if adjacent, or **Jennifer Walters, Esq.** to place her in a starting space, draw a card, and gain an action. If they end their turn adjacent, use **Nerve Cluster Strike** to ensure the opposing fighter cannot leave their space this turn.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who stay out of your zone or use hard zone cutoffs can shut down your special ability, forcing you to chase or risk running low on cards.

### Plan B: The big finishers
**Goal:** Crack open opponent defenses with **Omega-Level Threat** and **The Savage She-Hulk**.
**How to do it:** 
- **Omega-Level Threat** deals 5 damage. If you deal 2 or more combat damage, your opponent discards 2 random cards.
- **The Savage She-Hulk** is a 3-value attack where you may spend an additional action to make its value 9 instead. Use **Leap Toward** or **Jennifer Walters, Esq.** first to gain an extra action, ensuring you can pull off the 9-value strike. 
- Use **Sensational** (value 4) to win combat and choose a card in your discard pile to shuffle into your deck.

**Watch out for:** Faints cancel the extra action effect on The Savage She-Hulk or stop your Omega-Level Threat's discard effect, so try to bait out cancels with smaller attacks or versatile cards first.

### How they lose
- Highly mobile ranged fighters and elusive opponents (such as Darkblade, Golden Bat, or Black Widow) can kite her and run her out of cards.
- Opponents who manage their positioning to avoid her zone completely negate her special ability.
- High-value defensive cancels and heavy aggressive pressure can overwhelm her lower-value defense cards.

### Key cards
- **The Savage She-Hulk**: A 3-value attack that can jump to 9 if you spend an additional action.
- **Omega-Level Threat**: A 5-value attack that forces 2 random discards if you deal at least 2 combat damage.
- **Lady Justice**: A 0-value defense that discards the top card of the opponent's deck and increases its value by twice that card's boost value.
- **Nerve Cluster Strike**: A 3-value versatile that prevents an adjacent opposing fighter from leaving their space this turn.
- **Leap Toward**: A scheme that moves her up to four spaces through opposing fighters and grants an action if she ends adjacent to an opposing fighter.

### Tips
- Use **The Defense Rests** when facing big attacks to draw cards equal to the combat damage you took, though be mindful of exhaustion.
- **Cease and Desist** cancels all effects on the opponent's card, and its value becomes 4 when attacking, but it only defends for 1.
- **Green Energy** is a reliable 4-value versatile block that deals 1 damage to each fighter adjacent to She-Hulk if you won the combat.
- **Double Jeopardy** lets you draw 2 cards and recover 2 health, pushing her effective health pool higher, but watch your card count against fatigue matchups.
