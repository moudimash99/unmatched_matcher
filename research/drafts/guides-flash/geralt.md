## Geralt of Rivia

**In short:** A versatile hybrid fighter who sets up custom gear before shuffling and relies on heavy attacks, hand disruption, and his bard sidekick to dictate the pace of the match.

### Plan A: Tailor your gear and burst with Sword of Steel
**Goal:** Outpace opponents through action economy and aggressive pressure.
**How to do it:** Select the Sword of Steel during setup to gain an extra action on attack, or two actions if the opposing fighter is at 8 or more health, enabling early aggression. Pair it with Tawny Owl to draw two cards and gain an action, protecting your hand from forced discards while keeping the pressure on. Use Witcher Senses to retrieve your chosen gear cards from your discard pile or search your deck. Rend can be boosted during combat, turning it into a heavy-hitting finisher when you time your attacks against lower defenses.
**Watch out for:** Tawny Owl and Blizzard require you to attack every turn, or you must discard them at the end of your turn.

### Plan B: Control distance and disrupt with Dandelion
**Goal:** Keep enemies out of position while stripping their resources.
**How to do it:** Use Dandelion's Annoying tune to shove the opposing fighter up to 4 spaces to create breathing room or deny melee engagement. Play Plot Twist to inspect your opponent's hand, force a card discard, and draw a card. Yrden locks the opposing fighter in their space immediately and lets Geralt reposition up to 2 spaces afterward.
**Watch out for:** Dandelion has only 5 health and lacks generic defense cards, making him an easy target for opponents looking to eliminate him early.

### How they lose
- Dandelion perishes quickly because he cannot use generic defense cards and relies entirely on his six dedicated cards.
- Forcing Geralt to spend his powerful defensive gear on offense leaves him exposed to counter-attacks, and vice versa.
- Heavy reliance on boosting Rend or moving without sufficient card draw drains your hand and leaves you vulnerable.
- Debates persist regarding his competitive standing, with some players highlighting his high-damage attacks and gear as top-tier power creep, while others argue that inconsistent card draw and mobility keep him balanced in the middle of the pack.

### Key cards
- **Gear: Sword of steel**: an attack that grants extra actions depending on enemy health.
- **Witcher Senses**: a scheme that pulls a gear card from your deck or discard pile.
- **Damn, you're ugly**: a versatile with a 4 boost.
- **Annoying tune**: a Dandelion versatile that moves the opposing fighter up to 4 spaces.
- **Plot Twist**: a Dandelion versatile that lets you view their hand, force a discard, and draw a card.

### Tips
- Choose your starting gear before shuffling based on your opponent's matchup to maximize your strategy.
- Position Igni carefully to deal 2 damage to your target and 1 damage to every other adjacent fighter, ensuring you do not clip Dandelion.
- Maintain constant attacks to keep Tawny Owl active on the board rather than discarding it prematurely.
- Utilize Wolf Medallion against ranged fighters to cancel all card effects and completely ignore their attack value when they are not adjacent.
