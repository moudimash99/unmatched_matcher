## Cloak and Dagger

**In short:** A volatile duo of melee heroes who use Cloak to set up the board and drain cards, while Dagger waits to unleash massive combo turns when the enemy's hand is low.

### Plan A: Control and set up with Cloak
**Goal:** Soften the opponent's hand size and position them into a pin before switching to Dagger.
**How to do it:** Lead with Cloak to engage. Attack with cards like Darkforce Dimension and Channel the Dark to move the opposing fighter adjacent to Dagger and force discards through Umbra. Keep Dagger safely behind while Cloak absorbs early pressure. Use Living Shadow on defense with Dagger to swap spaces and let Cloak take hits instead. Lower the opponent's hand to four or five cards before you begin your attack phase.
**Watch out for:** Both fighters only have 8 health, so letting Cloak take too much unchecked damage early on will leave you vulnerable.

### Plan B: Unleash Dagger's combo finisher
**Goal:** Run the opponent out of defenses and secure a rapid-fire sequence of attacks.
**How to do it:** Once the enemy's hand is depleted, bring Dagger forward. Use Refraction to gain an action whenever she deals at least 2 combat damage. Chain high-impact cards like Commanding Impact and Perfect Balance to keep the pressure on. Save Lightforce Barrage as an ultimate finisher when you are ready to end the game, as its self-damage drawback makes early use too risky.
**Watch out for:** Dagger can easily defeat herself if you play Lightforce Barrage too early against an opponent with plenty of health left.

### How they lose
- Hard-hitting fighters and burst damage quickly overwhelm their low 8-health pools.
- Heavy defense packages or value-swapping cards stop their critical attacks from dealing the 2 damage needed to trigger their abilities.
- Losing Dagger early ruins your kill potential, as Cloak struggles to find lethal damage on his own.
- Lack of strong boost values makes it difficult to chase down mobile opponents across larger maps.

### Key cards
- **Darkforce Dimension**: A 4-value Cloak attack that places the opponent anywhere and forces a discard.
- **Channel the Dark**: A 2-value attack that positions the enemy next to Dagger and grants an extra action.
- **Lightforce Barrage**: A 7-value Dagger attack that acts as a devastating finisher despite its self-damage.
- **Living Shadow**: A 2-value defense that lets Dagger swap places with Cloak and boosts the defense value to 4.
- **Chosen Fate**: A scheme that lets you reallocate health between your heroes and draw two cards.

### Tips
- Always keep your fighters positioned near empty spaces so you have room to maneuver opponents into a pin.
- Use Chosen Fate to shift health away from a hero in danger, or over-damage a nearly defeated fighter to fully heal the other.
- Play on smaller maps like Raptor Paddock or Hanging Gardens where their limited boost values and pinning strategies work best.
- Remember that their special abilities cannot be canceled by Feints or other effects.
