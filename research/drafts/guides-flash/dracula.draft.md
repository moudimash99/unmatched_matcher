## Dracula

**In short:** A methodical melee fighter who uses schemes and his Sisters to manipulate positioning while dealing steady chip damage. He saves his high-impact Beastform attacks for a decisive late-game finish.

### Plan A: Stalk and chip
**Goal:** Wear down the opponent's health and resources until they are in range of a lethal blow.
**How to do it:** 
- Play passively and let opponents approach you, taking advantage of your ability to deal 1 damage and draw a card at the start of your turn when adjacent.
- Use **Ravening Seduction** and **Prey Upon** for unblocked damage and healing.
- Deploy **Do My Bidding** to force opponents to play versatile cards or burn cancels, clearing the path for your big attacks.
**Watch out for:** Ranged fighters can easily avoid ending their turn adjacent to you, making your special ability much harder to trigger.

### Plan B: Unleash Beastform
**Goal:** Secure the win with a massive, hand-fueled attack.
**How to do it:** 
- Maintain a large hand size to keep opponents constantly threatened by **Beastform** (value 6, 2 copies), which gains +1 for every card you discard from your hand.
- Combine **Mistform** with **Beastform** to teleport across the map and strike unexpectedly.
**Watch out for:** If your Beastform gets Feinted, it still deals 4 base damage, but losing the extra discarded cards on a canceled attack leaves you vulnerable.

### How they lose
- Low base health (13) leaves him fragile against aggressive opponents who pressure him before he can set up his healing and finishers.
- A high concentration of schemes and low block counts can leave you holding a hand with poor defensive options.
- Ranged aggression (such as Tesla or Yennenga) denies your adjacency ability and forces you to spend valuable actions chasing them down.

### Key cards
- **Beastform**: A 6-value attack that scales up with every card you discard from your hand, serving as your primary win condition.
- **Do My Bidding**: A 3-value defense that returns the opponent's card to their hand and forces them to play an attack or versatile card you choose, perfect for stripping away cancels.
- **Mistform**: A scheme that places Dracula anywhere on the board and grants 1 action, enabling surprise engagements and closing distance.
- **Baptism of Blood**: A scheme that recovers 2 health and returns a defeated Sister to Dracula's zone.

### Tips
- Keep your Sisters alive early to maximize the damage output of **Ravening Seduction** and **Feeding Frenzy**, but don't hesitate to use **Baptism of Blood** to bring them back if they fall.
- Remember that your special ability can target any adjacent fighter, including your own Sisters, if you are desperate for a card draw.
- Watch your deck count near the end of the game; your ability forces card draws, and drawing from an empty deck triggers exhaustion damage.
