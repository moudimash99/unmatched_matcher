## Yennefer & Triss

**In short:** A versatile double-ranged spellcaster pair with 14 health and a 6-health sidekick. Before the match, you choose whether Yennefer or Triss is your hero, altering your ability while keeping your health pools and deck intact. You win by managing low-value card resources, keeping safe distance with double range, and choosing between Yennefer's immediate boost pressure or Triss's adjacency scheme damage.

### Plan A: Yennefer's uncancelable boost pressure
**Goal:** Force heavy damage or card discards with Incinerate and your unique hero ability.
**How to do it:** Choose Yennefer as your hero so her Sorceress of Vengerberg ability triggers immediately during combat: if she is attacking, you may boost her attack, and this effect cannot be canceled. Because this happens after seeing your opponent's defense, use it when you know it secures extra damage or punishes a low block. Incinerate (value 7) forces the opponent to discard two cards to ignore its value; boosting it makes the threat overwhelming. Use Advisor to the King to recover health, draw a card, and grant your boost values +1, but be sure to attack on that turn so you do not discard the ongoing scheme.
**Watch out for:** Boosting consumes cards rapidly, leaving your hand thin and stripping your deck of defenses and versatile cards like Ball Lightning or Telepathy.

### Plan B: Triss's melee control and scheme auto-damage
**Goal:** Outlast melee opponents by combining ranged kiting with guaranteed auto-damage from schemes.
**How to do it:** Choose Triss as your hero to activate Merigold the Fearless, which deals 2 damage to a fighter adjacent to Triss after she plays a scheme. Because this requires adjacency, pick Triss against melee fighters who are forced to approach you. Hold your five schemes to stack up to 10 automatic damage. Marigold's Hailstorm (value 4) is a devastating attack where the opponent must choose between discarding two cards, taking 3 hero damage, or discarding the top four cards of their deck. Play Triss against low-health melee fighters like Luke Cage or Bigfoot where her auto-damage and board presence outpace their defenses.
**Watch out for:** Triss is weak against ranged fighters who can attack from afar without triggering her adjacency ability, leaving her vulnerable and exposed in melee range.

### How they lose
- High-value attackers (Alice, Bigfoot, Achilles, She-Hulk) punch through their limited defenses and punish their 14-health hero pool.
- Aggressive pressure and high card-draw fighters (Raptors, Tesla) overwhelm their hand and exhaust their resources before they can set up combos.
- Fighting ranged opponents as Triss, since they stay out of her adjacent damage range.
- Over-boosting with Yennefer leaves you with no cards left to defend against counter-attacks.

### Key cards
- **Incinerate**: a 7-value attack that forces the opponent to discard two cards to ignore its value. Uncancelable effect.
- **Marigold's Hailstorm**: a 4-value attack that forces the opponent to choose between discarding two cards, taking 3 damage, or dumping four cards from their deck.
- **Magical Barrier**: a 4-value defense that immediately recovers 1 health before combat resolves, making it effectively a 5-value block.
- **Advisor to the King**: a scheme that heals your fighters, draws a card, and grants +1 to your boost values as long as you attack that turn.
- **Echoing blast**: a 3-value attack that returns to your hand if you win the combat.

### Tips
- Never make the mistake of playing a scheme next to Triss when only Yennefer is adjacent, or Yennefer will take the 2 damage.
- Use Portal to anywhere as a bait attack to place fighters anywhere on the board, gain an action, and set up kiting or finish off targets.
- Save Paralyzing fetters against opponents who rely on during-combat value bumps, as it locks their card to its printed value and cannot be changed.
- Item maps are exceptionally strong for Triss because map tokens count for her ability triggers against approaching melee fighters.
- Use Quick and ready to reposition in your zone and draw a replacement card to maintain hand pressure.
