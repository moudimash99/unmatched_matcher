## Bigfoot

**In short:** A powerful and mobile melee fighter who relies on high-value attacks, abundant movement, and exceptional defensive cancels. Supported by the Jackalope, he applies steady pressure while maintaining card advantage through his special ability.

### Plan A: Out-value and overwhelm
**Goal:** Crush the opponent's defenses with massive attack values and force them to burn through their best blocks early.
**How to do it:** Use Larger Than Life (3 copies, value 6, boost 3) aggressively against opposing heroes, as its high printed number forces the opponent to commit top-tier defenses or risk massive damage. Pair Momentous Shift (3 copies, value 3, boost 1) with a prior movement to hit for 5. Savagery (3 copies, value 4, boost 3) deals 1 damage to each adjacent fighter after combat if you win, making it ideal for clearing crowds of 1-health sidekicks or landing extra damage on the enemy hero.
**Watch out for:** Heroes with effects that punish high-value attacks or nullify card values (such as Beowulf, Invisible Man, or Little Red) can turn these big numbers against you.

### Plan B: The Jackalope rush and board control
**Goal:** Deliver guaranteed direct damage and keep the pressure up using the Jackalope as an extension of Bigfoot.
**How to do it:** Send the Jackalope into the fray to attack using the shared versatile cards, while saving Jackalope Horns (3 copies, boost 2) to move 5 spaces through enemies and deal 2 direct damage to an adjacent fighter. This provides 6 unblockable damage that bypasses defenses entirely. Because the Jackalope can use all of Bigfoot's versatile and defense cards, it remains durable enough to execute these strikes before enemies can take it down.
**Watch out for:** Chasing the Jackalope around the board blindly sacrifices too many resources; if your opponent dedicates heavy attacks to taking it down early, pivot to defending it with your versatile cards only when it is directly approached.

### Plan C: Kiting with advantage
**Goal:** Maintain control of the board, evade counter-attacks, and farm extra cards with Bigfoot's ability.
**How to do it:** Use Bigfoot's 3-movement combined with movement-heavy cards like Hoax (3 copies, versatile value 4, boost 2) and Crash Through the Trees (2 copies, boost 3) to move up to 5 spaces through opposing fighters. Attack and then maneuver away to an empty zone—one with no opposing fighters—so that at the end of your turn, Bigfoot's special ability triggers to draw 1 card. This builds a consistent card advantage over your opponent.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who spread out across multiple zones or use multi-sidekick formations to deny you empty zones and lock down your card draw.

### How they lose
- Swarmed by multiple 1-health sidekicks that body-block paths and prevent Bigfoot from reaching the opposing hero.
- Facing fighters who punish high-value attacks or disrupt hands before larger combos and blocks can be deployed.
- Letting the Jackalope die too early without getting value out of the horns schemes, leaving Bigfoot short on overall firepower.
- Getting pinned down on tight or narrow maps where it is impossible to find an empty zone to trigger card draw.

### Key cards
- **Larger Than Life**: Bigfoot's signature heavy hitter, providing three un-Feintable 6-value attacks.
- **It's Just Your Imagination**: A 3-value defense that immediately cancels opponent effects, effectively acting as an extra pair of Feints.
- **Jackalope Horns**: The Jackalope's scheme that grants 5 movement through enemies and 2 unblockable damage.
- **Hoax**: A versatile 4-value card that doubles as a strong defense and a 5-space movement tool to escape or reposition.
- **Savagery**: A 4-value attack that clears adjacent sidekicks with area-of-effect damage after a won combat.

### Tips
- Combine your five total cancels (three Feints and two It's Just Your Imaginations) liberally to shut down opponent tricks without fearing card scarcity.
- Use Regroup (3 copies, value 1, boost 2) to bait out strong defensive cards from your opponent before dropping a Larger Than Life.
- Keep the Jackalope alive just long enough to deploy all three horns schemes; once those are spent, its value as an aggressive tool drops significantly.
- Favor open maps with sharp zone cut-offs to make triggering Bigfoot's end-of-turn card draw reliable.
