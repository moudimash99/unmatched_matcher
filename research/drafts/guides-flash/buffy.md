## Buffy

**In short:** A flexible melee fighter with high movement who passes through opposing fighters. She selects one of two sidekicks before the match: Giles for hand control and card draw, or Xander for extra attacks and heavy-hitting options.

### Plan A: Control combat with after-effect positioning
**Goal:** Punish enemies who engage you and control the board using movement card effects.
**How to do it:** Position yourself close to the opponent to force them to approach. On defense, play Cartwheel Kick to move away and damage adjacent fighters. On your turn, initiate with Mr. Pointy to safely test for enemy Feints without risking an effect, then follow up with Slayer's Strength to reposition adjacent fighters and deal damage. Use her special ability to move freely through opposing fighters and prevent yourself from being trapped.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who frequently cancel after-combat effects, such as Bigfoot or Black Widow.

### Plan B: Choose your sidekick for the matchup
**Goal:** Adapt your strategy to the opponent by selecting either Giles or Xander.
**How to do it:** 
- Pick Giles for strong scheme control: Insight lets you view an opponent's hand and force a discard, while Training draws three cards to supplement her limited card draw. 
- Pick Xander for offensive pressure: Military Knowledge provides an attack that draws a card, and Right-hand Man reaches a value of 6 when Xander is adjacent to Buffy.
**Watch out for:** Xander and Giles have low health pools and become vulnerabilities if left exposed.

### How they lose
- Opponents cancel her after-combat effects before they trigger.
- Her health pool is low, leaving little margin for error against heavy hitters.
- Choosing Giles lacks the heavy attacks provided by Xander, whereas choosing Xander reduces scheme utility and card draw.

### Key cards
- **Mr. Pointy**: a 5-value attack with no effect, ideal for drawing out Feints.
- **Cartwheel Kick**: a 2-value defense that moves Buffy and damages adjacent opposing fighters.
- **Slayer's Strength**: a 4-value versatile that moves adjacent fighters and damages them.
- **Insight**: a Giles scheme to inspect an enemy hand and force a discard.
- **Right-hand Man**: a Xander versatile that reaches a value of 6 when he is adjacent to Buffy.

### Tips
- Use her ability to bypass bottlenecks and enemy sidekicks rather than wasting movement.
- Keep Xander adjacent to Buffy so Right-hand Man can reach its full value.
- Protect Giles to ensure you can play all of his scheme cards.
