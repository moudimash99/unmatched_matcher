## Michelangelo

**In short:** A hybrid melee wild card paired with ranged sidekick April O'Neil. He features a tiny starting and maximum hand size of three and mandatory card draws after every attack or scheme, forcing rapid card turnover and high-tempo aggression.

### Plan A: Push the offensive tempo
**Goal:** Attack continuously to trigger mandatory card draws and maintain relentless pressure without getting stalled by hand limits.
**How to do it:** Your special ability dictates that whenever you attack or play a scheme with either Michelangelo or April, you must draw a card, and your hand size cannot exceed three. Because attacks generate cards, attacking acts almost like maneuvering. Stay engaged in close quarters and strike every turn rather than fleeing. Use action-gaining cards to string multiple offensive moves together. Prioritize tighter maps where you can reach targets instantly and pin them down.
**Watch out for:** In 1v1 matchups, he is extremely swingy. If your mandatory draws miss your action-chaining options, you can stall out or burn through your deck quickly.

### Plan B: Build up to a massive combo finisher
**Goal:** Stack sequential plays and unleash a devastating final strike.
**How to do it:** Execute multiple cards in a single turn using action-gaining effects, then drop your high-scaling versatile attack, which adds value for each other card played that turn. Enhance your damage further using scheme cards that boost your attack values. Use your attack-boosting options when you can afford to invest resources for a heavy blow. 
**Watch out for:** Opponents who anticipate your combo string can ruin your turn with cancellation effects, wasting your setup and your limited hand resources.

### How they lose
- Ranged fighters or highly mobile opponents keep their distance and pick him apart while he tries to close the gap.
- Heavy defense and cancellation cards shut down his high-value combo pieces or intercept his action-gaining attacks.
- Running out of cards triggers the exhaustion clock rapidly because mandatory card draws and a small hand limit force high turnover.
- Losing April O'Neil early deprives you of her ranged pressure and multi-option utility cards that recycle discards or manage hand state.

### Key cards
- **Hi-yaaaaah!!**: A 4-value attack that deals 1 damage to an adjacent opposing fighter and allows tucking a card to the bottom of your deck to gain an action.
- **Boisterous beatdown**: A 2-value attack that grants an action, or two actions if you win the combat.
- **Cowabunga!!**: A 3-value versatile attack that scales up for every other card played earlier that turn.
- **Guaranteed delivery**: A versatile card that lets you tuck a card to cancel opponent effects immediately, drawing two cards if your hand is empty.
- **Shell insertion**: An April scheme offering versatile options including card recycling, moving fighters, forcing opponent discards, or gaining actions.

### Tips
- Start games aggressively by going first and closing the distance immediately, as he thrives on early pressure.
- Use action-gaining attacks to pick off annoying 1-health sidekicks while generating extra actions.
- Position April O'Neil carefully to support Michelangelo and control bottlenecks, but protect her health since she has only 6 health.
- Use card-recycling defenses and utility effects to shuffle key cards back into your deck before exhaustion catches up.
- Michelangelo is a party dude!! heals every friendly fighter by 1, providing a useful minor recovery when you need to stabilize.
