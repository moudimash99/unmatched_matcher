## Leonardo

**In short:** A hybrid melee fighter accompanied by Master Splinter. He leverages his opening-turn board manipulation and positional tools to secure zone advantages, pressure opponents, and dictate engagements.

### Plan A: Control the zones and deal auto-damage
**Goal:** Out-position adversaries to trigger guaranteed damage and restrict map control.
**How to do it:** Use Team Tactics at the start of your turn to relocate any fighter one space, preventing opponents from locking down choke points or securing key map elements. Position Leonardo and Splinter in superior zone counts compared to the enemy to activate Wise beyond his years and Spatial awareness for reliable auto-damage. Utilize Eat, sleep, and breath ninjutsu to scatter adjacent combatants or shift your fighters after skirmishes. Take advantage of specific boards, such as secret passages or single-path layouts, where your movement ability disrupts enemy blocking strategies.
**Watch out for:** Discard costs across your deck can drain your hand rapidly if overused, especially when fueling effects and boosting.

### Plan B: Mix high values with tactical mind games
**Goal:** Deliver heavy strikes while forcing out defensive cancels.
**How to do it:** Threaten opponents with Katana or boost Turtle power! into a formidable strike. Threaten with Wise beyond his years or Fearless leader to bait enemy faints before committing your strongest attacks. If adversaries attempt to counter your setup, I have a plan cancels their card effects and ignores its value at the cost of a discarded card. Protective father allows Leonardo and Splinter to swap spaces and heal both fighters while gaining an extra action to maintain your combined health pool.
**Watch out for:** Relying on Leonardo leads for movement in a standard 1v1 duel is inefficient; save it primarily as a four-boost option to supercharge Turtle power! or use it when playing cooperative modes.

### How they lose
- Opponents with heavy discard pressure or card-burning effects make your multiple card discard costs unsustainable.
- Ranged fighters who maintain distance and stay out of multi-zone advantages prevent your auto-damage triggers from activating.
- Aggressive attackers can punish your limited high-value defensive options, like Heroes in a half shell, if you misposition or run out of defensive cards.

### Key cards
- **Katana**: a blank six-value attack with no text, ideal for punishing low defenses.
- **Turtle power!**: a three-value attack that can be boosted during combat.
- **Wise beyond his years**: a two-value versatile that deals two damage if Leonardo occupies more zones than the target.
- **I have a plan**: a two-value versatile that discards a card to cancel opponent effects and ignore their value.
- **Eat, sleep, and breathe ninjutsu**: a three-value versatile that repositions adjacent fighters within their zone after combat.

### Tips
- Verify zone positioning before your turn ends to guarantee your auto-damage triggers.
- Reserve Leonardo leads for its four-value boost rather than wasting it as a standard movement scheme in a duel.
- Coordinate Leonardo and Splinter together carefully to maximize Splinter's nine health and healing utility.
- Remember that For sensei grants an action when you discard a card, but weigh the discard cost against your overall hand size.
