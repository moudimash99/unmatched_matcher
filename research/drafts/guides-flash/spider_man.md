## Spider-Man

**In short:** A mobile melee fighter who uses Spider-Sense to learn his opponent's printed attack values before defending, blunts massive strikes with specialized defenses, and darts across the board with movement effects, though he lacks card cancels.

### Plan A: Read the attack and deploy targeted defenses
**Goal:** Nullify heavy hits and punish high-value attacks by leveraging knowledge of your opponent's printed values.
**How to do it:** Before you play a defense card, your opponent must state the printed value of their attack card. Use this information to choose your response. When facing massive attacks like Dracula's Beastform or Bigfoot's log, play Web Shooters to cap your combat damage at 2. When the opponent attacks with a value of 4 or higher, use Counter-Attack to deal 2 damage back after combat, or play Spider-Sense Tingling! to draw 2 cards if you are willing to accept some damage for hand recovery. 
**Watch out for:** Opponent effects and modifiers can change final attack values. An adjacent Winter Soldier can turn a printed 2 into a 6, bypassing triggers that require a value of 4 or higher. Furthermore, fighters who use multiple attacks with the same or similar values (such as Black Panther) give away fewer tactical clues than fighters with widely spread values (like Wukong).

### Plan B: Acrobatic hit-and-run aggression
**Goal:** Control board positioning, strike hard, and keep opponents off balance with constant relocation.
**How to do it:** Combine a base movement of 3 with positioning cards to dictate the flow of combat. Start your turn in a different space to make Momentous Shift hit for 5 value. Use Swinging Kick to deal damage and push the opposing fighter away, creating distance or pursuing a fleeing enemy. Wall Crawler, Thwip!, and the scheme Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man let you place Spider-Man in any space in his zone to retreat, heal for 3 health, or close the gap on ranged opponents. Use Right in the Face! to immediately stop the opposing fighter from drawing cards for the rest of the turn.
**Watch out for:** Overextending your position without enough cards in hand. Without any Feints or card cancels in his deck, Spider-Man cannot blank opposing card effects, so you must rely entirely on positioning and value blocks to survive counter-pressures.

### How they lose
- He has no card cancels or Feints, leaving him vulnerable to devastating unhindered card effects.
- Opponents with uniform or repeating attack values reduce the informational advantage of Spider-Sense.
- Aggressive fighters who swarm him can deplete his hand faster than he can recover.
- Failing to hold onto Web Shooters leaves him defenseless against late-game high-value finishers.

### Key cards
- **Web Shooters**: A defense card that restricts combat damage to a maximum of 2, shutting down massive enemy attacks.
- **Counter-Attack**: A defense card that deals 2 damage to the attacker if their card value is 4 or higher.
- **Swinging Kick**: A high-value attack that pushes the opponent away based on the damage dealt.
- **Right in the Face!**: A versatile card that immediately stops the opponent from drawing cards for the term.
- **Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man**: A scheme that recovers 3 health and repositions him within his zone.

### Tips
- Study your opponent's deck in advance to help interpret what a specific printed value actually represents in context.
- Use With Great Power as an opening action when adjacent to an opponent to increase your attack values by 1, draw a card, and gain an extra action for a heavy follow-up.
- Leverage overdealing interactions: when using attacks like Disarming Shot against a 1-health sidekick, you still draw cards based on the full damage calculated even if they only have 1 health remaining.
- Conserve your high-value specialized defenses rather than spending them on minor attacks, since you lack general cancels to bail you out of bad trades.
