## Spike

**In short:** A melee fighter who relies heavily on covering the board with shadow tokens to trigger conditional bonuses and zone control. He is slow to start and depends on careful positioning and card management to trap opponents.

### Plan A: Cover the board with shadows
**Goal:** Expand your shadow network so that opponents have nowhere to move without stepping into your zone of control.
**How to do it:** Use your special ability at the start of every turn to place a shadow token adjacent to Spike or Drusilla. Play Seek the Shadows to drop tokens around you while drawing cards. Position yourself carefully to maximize coverage because your strongest card effects depend on these tokens. Force your opponent to approach you rather than chasing them into open spaces.
**Watch out for:** Ranged fighters and high-mobility opponents will stay away from your shadows and pepper you from afar, making it difficult to trigger conditional bonuses.

### Plan B: Trap them and strike hard
**Goal:** Punish opponents who are forced to engage you inside your shadow tokens using boosted attacks and forced discards.
**How to do it:** Once enemies are near your tokens, leverage cards like The Rush, which scales up in power when fighters are on a shadow token, and Let's Dance, which lets you boost during combat. Use Bloody Hell! to deal extra damage for each shadow token adjacent to the defender. Have Drusilla use The Sight to force them to discard two cards when standing in a shadow token, or Empathy to draw three cards instead of one.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who save Feints to cancel your critical combat effects, leaving you with plain numeric values.

### How they lose
- Ranged fighters kite them easily, ignoring the shadow tokens entirely.
- Opponents avoid the shadow network, rendering conditional cards like The Rush and Bloody Hell! much weaker.
- Early-game pressure catches them before the shadow tokens can cover enough ground.
- Pinning them or using high-mobility effects disrupts their slow setup.

### Key cards
- **Seek the Shadows**: your primary tool for blanketing the map in shadow tokens while drawing cards.
- **The Rush**: an attack that scales up in power when fighters are in the shadows.
- **Bloody Hell!**: a versatile card that deals stacking damage based on adjacent shadow tokens.
- **The Sight**: Drusilla's scheme that strips cards from the opponent's hand, especially punishing inside the shadows.
- **Leap Away**: a versatile used defensively to reposition fighters up to 4 spaces after winning combat.

### Tips
- Use Regroup to bait out early Feints from cautious opponents who fear your stronger shadow-enhanced cards.
- Keep Drusilla close to Spike to help spread shadow tokens faster and protect your positioning.
- Double maneuver when necessary to close the distance or reposition for an optimal Seek the Shadows play.
- Use Leap Away and Skirmish defensively to push enemies directly into your shadow tokens.
