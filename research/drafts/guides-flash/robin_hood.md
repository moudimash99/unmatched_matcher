## Robin Hood

**In short:** A ranged skirmisher with 13 health who uses his four melee Outlaws as a protective barrier and constant hit-and-run tactics. His special ability grants permanent kiting mobility, and the psych-out threat of Highway Robbery forces opponents to guess between blocking or taking unblocked damage.

### Plan A: Form the barrier and kite
**Goal:** Keep Robin Hood out of harm's reach while attacking safely from distance.
**How to do it:** Position your four Outlaws around Robin Hood to form a defensive wall. Because his special ability allows the attacking fighter to move up to 2 spaces after any attack, both Robin and the Outlaws can step in, strike, and retreat. This forces melee opponents to spend their first action maneuvering just to reach you, preventing double-attacks. Use A Hunter's Eye to pressure opponents with guaranteed damage, and rely on Piercing Shot and Disarming Shot to dig deep into your deck.
**Watch out for:** Low health and heavy card draw mean you risk running into exhaustion quickly if you over-attack.

### Plan B: The Highway Robbery mind game
**Goal:** Exploit your opponent's fear of Outlaw attacks to sneak in value or deal free damage.
**How to do it:** Highway Robbery cancels all effects and ignores defense values, dealing 2 guaranteed damage when played by an Outlaw. Since there are four copies, opponents are terrified of blocking Outlaw attacks. Use this paranoia to push through Regroup or Snark safely when they choose not to block, or draw out their Feints so your heavier attacks land cleanly.
**Watch out for:** Feints completely shut down Highway Robbery and waste the Outlaw attack.

### How they lose
- High-tier powerhouses like Bigfoot easily overwhelm his low health and 3-value maximum defenses.
- Area-of-effect damage and move-through effects clear out his Outlaws cheaply, leaving Robin exposed.
- Aggressive fighters who out-tempo him or bypass his barriers catch his fragile 13-health hero quickly.
- Ranged matchups negate the movement advantage of kiting away from melee zones.
- Heavy card draw pushes him into fatigue and self-inflicted exhaustion damage.

### Key cards
- **Highway Robbery**: An Outlaw attack that cancels card effects and ignores defense, creating a constant psychological threat.
- **A Hunter's Eye**: A 5-value attack with no text, providing reliable, un-faintable damage.
- **Defenders of Sherwood**: A 3-value defense that draws a card and revives a defeated Outlaw into Robin's zone.
- **Disarming Shot**: Draws cards equal to damage dealt, rewarding overkills on low-health sidekicks with a massive hand refill.

### Tips
- Protect your Outlaws, but accept that they will die; only use Defenders of Sherwood when you have a defeated Outlaw ready to bring back.
- Use Ambush with Outlaws or Robin to force random discards and boost your attack values.
- Wily Fighting deals 1 damage to each adjacent fighter after combat, making it great for punishing clustered enemies or clearing small sidekicks.
- Avoid holding A Hunter's Eye against opponents who run Ambush.
