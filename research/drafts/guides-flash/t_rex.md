## T. Rex

**In short:** A relentless melee brawler featuring a 27 health pool and large-fighter base mechanics. Her mandatory end-of-turn card draw forces a fast pace toward exhaustion, while her self-damaging attacks fuel immense offensive momentum.

### Plan A: Go full throttle at the hero
**Goal:** Crush the opposing hero with heavy attacks before your mandatory card draw and self-inflicted damage lead to exhaustion.
**How to do it:** Attack every turn because hesitation wastes your potential. Since you draw a card at the end of every turn, your hand naturally replenishes despite constant boosting. Use schemes like Closer Than She Appears to bridge gaps and gain actions, or Ripples in the Water to reposition and force random discards. Spend high-boost cards like Reckless Lunge or 65 Million Years of Gut Instinct to fuel your single movement value across the board. 
**Watch out for:** Ranged fighters and keep-away opponents who force a sluggish game. Chasing mobile characters on large maps while avoiding engagements causes your mandatory card draw to accelerate your defeat via exhaustion.

### Plan B: Exploit large-fighter reach and board control
**Goal:** Bypass obstacles, control positioning, and keep enemies trapped in your strike zone.
**How to do it:** As a large fighter occupying two spaces, you can attack up to two spaces away, even over obstructing fighters. Ignore map one-way arrows, but note that you cannot use secret passages, making boards like Baskerville Manor a movement hurdle. Play Terrifying Roar on defense or when adjacent to lock enemies in their spaces, preventing escape and setting up a devastating double attack. Use Thrash defensively to clear annoying sidekicks in your zone when winning combat.
**Watch out for:** Maps with restrictive secret passages that require constant movement boosting.

### How they lose
- Ranged attackers and mobile characters keep their distance, kite her across expansive maps, and let exhaustion claim the win.
- Opponents turtle or hide behind obstacles, forcing her to burn through her own health and deck.
- Her limited defensive card count (only 10 cards total that can be used to block, consisting of You're Just Making Her Angry and versatiles) leaves her exposed if she fails to close out the game quickly.

### Key cards
- **Reckless Lunge**: an attack dealing 3 damage to the opponent and 3 to yourself, boasting a top-tier boost value of 4.
- **15,000 Pounds of Muscle**: an attack that ignores the opponent's card value while inflicting 2 self-damage after combat.
- **When Dinosaurs Ruled the Earth**: an attack you can boost; winning yields a card, an action, and 2 self-damage.
- **Ripples in the Water**: places T. Rex anywhere in her zone and forces adjacent enemies to discard a random card.
- **Terrifying Roar**: a versatile card that immediately stops fighters from leaving their spaces for the rest of the turn.

### Tips
- Avoid double maneuvering, because drawing extra cards on top of your mandatory end-of-turn draw rapidly burns through your deck toward exhaustion.
- Leverage your large health pool to absorb damage trades rather than holding back for defense.
- Recycle key utility or attack cards from your discard pile using 65 Million Years of Gut Instinct.
