## Muhammad Ali

**In short:** A tactical, defensive fighter who adopts an outboxer mindset. He cycles between a mobile, defense-oriented butterfly stance and a hard-hitting bee stance, wearing opponents down through patient positioning and hand disruption before striking with calculated combinations or winning via exhaustion.

### Plan A: Play the long game for fatigue
**Goal:** Survive early aggression, preserve your health pool, and outlast the opponent until exhaustion.
**How to do it:** Avoid playing as a traditional aggressive brawler. Stay in butterfly stance during your opponent's turn to maximize your defensive options. Use Rope-a-dope to recover health when you lose a combat, and leverage Hard to be humble to inspect their hand and reposition. Recycle key defensive or high-boost cards back into your deck using Ali Shuffle so you maintain card advantage into the late game. 
**Watch out for:** Rushing in too early or lingering in bee stance, which leaves you vulnerable because your defensive text effects do not trigger.

### Plan B: Set up multi-action counterattacks
**Goal:** Deliver burst damage and strip the opponent's defenses when an opening appears.
**How to do it:** Use Answer the Bell to maneuver without spending an action, gaining the positioning and action economy needed for a multi-attack turn. Follow up with Momentous Shift, which scales in power if you started the turn elsewhere and hits even harder in bee stance. Play Jab as a low-cost probe to draw out high-value opponent blocks, or use Stronger than the skill to look at their hand and shuffle a card back into their deck. Louisville Lip rewards you after winning a combat by forcing random discards while drawing cards and healing.
**Watch out for:** Forcing the extra actions from The Greatest when opponents are holding onto premium defenses, which often wastes your best attack.

### How they lose
* Aggressive fighters with high mobility and multiple actions overwhelm him before his defensive pacing pays off.
* Getting stuck in bee stance prevents essential butterfly defensive effects from triggering, making him a sitting duck against counterattacks.
* Opponents who hold onto solid block cards easily weather his setup attacks.
* Mismanaging hand size and running out of resources against pressure fighters.

### Key cards
* **Momentous Shift**: A versatile card that increases in value if you moved before playing it, becoming a primary damage dealer in bee stance.
* **Ali Shuffle**: An attack that lets you recycle non-scheme cards from your discard pile back into your deck when you win in butterfly stance.
* **Rope-a-dope**: A defense card that recovers health if you lose the combat, serving as a core survival tool.
* **Answer The Bell**: A scheme that grants movement, health recovery, and an extra action, acting as the primary engine for multi-card turns.
* **Stronger than the skill**: An attack that reveals the opponent's hand and punishes them by shuffling a card back into their deck upon winning.

### Tips
* Keep your stance token clearly visible or place a reference card in front of you so you never lose track of whether you are in butterfly or bee stance.
* Use Close and clinch to lock elusive melee or ranged opponents down next to you so they cannot escape your counter-offensive.
* Treat Fancy footwork as a positional escape tool; if you position correctly so you share no zones with the attacker, you ignore their card value entirely.
* Save your 4-value Champion of the world cards carefully, as they double as reliable high-value attacks or critical defenses.
* Do not telegraph your big turns; use minor pings and hand checks to confirm the opponent lacks defensive resources before committing your heaviest cards.
