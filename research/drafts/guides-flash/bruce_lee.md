## Bruce Lee

**In short:** A solo melee fighter with 14 health who uses action-gaining Jeet Kune Do attacks to overwhelm opponents with high-volume flurries. His special ability lets him back away after attacking to avoid double attacks, but his low health and lack of sidekicks leave him vulnerable to exhaustion and heavy pressure.

### Plan A: Chain the Jeet Kune Do flurry
**Goal:** Attack multiple times in a single turn to drain the opponent's defenses and punch through their health.
**How to do it:** Six unique Jeet Kune Do attack cards give you an extra action after combat, making them action-neutral or free to play. Attack in small strings of two or three to bait out cancels like Feint or movement effects. Use Downward Side Kick early to strip a random card from their hand, or High Straight Lead to hit over defensive values. Play Nunchaku beforehand to add +1 to all your attacks that turn.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who save their Feints will cancel your action gains and stop your chains cold. Never bank on an endless string of attacks against experienced players.

### Plan B: Control distance with your special ability and schemes
**Goal:** Stay out of range of melee counterattacks while dragging ranged opponents into close quarters.
**How to do it:** Bruce's special ability lets him move 1 space at the end of your turn. Always use it after attacking so melee fighters can never end their turn adjacent to you, preventing standard double attacks. Against ranged fighters who try to kite you, use the Bring It On scheme to pull them from anywhere in your zone into an adjacent space, then immediately pressure them with a flurry. Skirmish can also move fighters up to 2 spaces after combat when you win.
**Watch out for:** Ranged fighters with high mobility or map layouts that allow opponents to keep their distance can nullify your end-of-turn hop.

### How they lose
- They run out of cards and take fatal exhaustion damage, since Bruce lacks healing and has only 14 health to absorb the clock.
- Hand disruption and random discards take away his key Jeet Kune Do cards before he can set up a turn.
- Defensive cancels and after-combat movement cards break up his action chains.
- Aggressive fighters with multiple actions or high-value attacks out-value his 3-value defenses and chip him down to zero.
- Without sidekicks to body block, he is easily pinned or trapped by mechanics like Muldoon's traps.

### Key cards
- **Be Like Water**: returns a Jeet Kune Do card from your discard pile to your hand. Your main tool for recycling your best attacks.
- **High Straight Lead**: becomes a 5-value attack if either fighter started the turn in a different space. Your best tool for punching through moderate blocks.
- **Downward Side Kick**: forces a random card discard, crippling the opponent's defensive options.
- **Bring It On**: pulls an opposing fighter in your zone into an adjacent space, solving his worst matchup against ranged fighters.
- **One-Inch Punch**: deals 2 damage to an adjacent fighter and returns to your hand if it defeats them, making short work of 1-health sidekicks.

### Tips
- Always keep at least one Jeet Kune Do card in your discard pile so Be Like Water and "HOO! WHAAAAAA!" have a target when played.
- Do not fall into the trap of using Little Dragon as a 6-value attack when your hand is empty; the risk is too high for a hero with low health and weak defenses.
- Use Regroup as a reliable bait attack to draw out enemy Feints before committing your stronger Jeet Kune Do cards.
- Play One-Inch Punch against sidekicks like Medusa's Harpies or Robin Hood's Outlaws to wipe them out efficiently while recycling the card.
- Keep your discard pile organized so both players can track which Jeet Kune Do cards are available for recycling.
