## Raptors

**In short:** A fast, aggressive glass cannon trio that relies on high mobility and pack tactics to swarm opponents early, dealing massive damage before their weak defense and lack of card draw catch up to them.

### Plan A: Swarm and pin early
**Goal:** Surround the enemy with all three Raptors on turn one or two to maximize attack values and deny escape routes.
**How to do it:** Use their high move of 3, or boost when necessary, to close distance immediately. Play Working Things Out to move through opposing fighters and set up surprise pins from all sides. When multiple Raptors are adjacent to the defender, your ability adds bonus damage to your attacks. Feed your attacks into this positioning: Pack Hunters deals extra damage for every adjacent Raptor, and Clever Girl and They Remember gain actions to keep the assault going. Save Eviscerate and Ambush to punish opponents when they try to break away.
**Watch out for:** If an opponent manages to pick off even a single Raptor, your offensive pressure and special ability bonus drop significantly. 

### Plan B: Hit and run to rebuild
**Goal:** Restrain your aggression when hand size runs low, retreating to safety until you can draw back up.
**How to do it:** Because they have zero card draw and only 10 defense cards in the entire deck, overextending leaves you completely defenseless. Use Disengage or movement boosts to pull low-health Raptors out of combat, hiding them behind other Raptors or at the edges of zones out of range. Double maneuver to refill your hand before charging back in for another explosive turn.
**Watch out for:** Spending too much time running away gives the opponent breathing room to recover health or set up board control, which favors them in the long run.

### How they lose
- An opponent focuses attacks on a single Raptor to take it out early, causing your damage output to snowball downward.
- Ranged fighters keep their distance and pick off the pack before they can close in.
- Fighters who move through opposing fighters easily slip out of your pins.
- The game drags on, exhausting your limited card pool and exposing your lack of healing and meager defenses.

### Key cards
- **Pack Hunters**: an attack that deals extra damage for each adjacent Raptor when you win combat.
- **Decoy**: a defense with an immediate effect that places another undefeated Raptor adjacent to the opposing fighter, instantly restoring your board presence.
- **Working Things Out**: a scheme that moves every Raptor up to 3 spaces through enemies and grants an action, perfect for setting up sudden pins.
- **Eviscerate**: a high-value attack with no text, making it great for bypassing cancel effects and baiting Feints.

### Tips
- Treat your 10 defense cards with extreme care. Save your high-value blocks for defense rather than attacking whenever possible.
- Use Decoy on the opponent's turn to drop a Raptor right into their space, setting up your flanking ability for your own turn.
- Against characters like Robert Muldoon, use They Remember to clear out low-health InGen workers and gain actions without wasting momentum.
- Never let Feints cancel your high-impact attacks like Clever Girl or Pack Hunters; use plain attacks or bait out cancels first.
