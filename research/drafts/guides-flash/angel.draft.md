## Angel

**In short:** A melee duo fighter with Faith who specializes in fundamentals and attacking. Many of his cards and his special ability reward him for losing combat, but his high attack values often make him win instead.

### Plan A: Bait and strike with Faith
**Goal:** Use Faith's strong cards to control space and punish adjacent enemies.
**How to do it:** Faith carries the deck's most threatening cards. Use Five by Five to deal heavy damage and reposition her up to 5 spaces through enemies. Play The Rogue Slayer on defense to hit all adjacent enemies, which clears out 1-health sidekicks. Follow up with the Killer of the Dead scheme to deal 2 damage to a fighter next to her. Opponents will focus their Feints and strong defenses on Faith, which can inadvertently let Angel's effects trigger more easily.
**Watch out for:** Faith only has 8 health, so opponents will try to isolate and eliminate her before she can use her best options.

### Plan B: Force the loss
**Goal:** Trigger Angel's ability and card effects that activate when you lose combat.
**How to do it:** Angel's special ability draws a card whenever Angel or Faith attacks and loses the combat. Attack with lower-value cards like Regroup or Wisdom of the Ages to bait out high-value enemy defenses, letting you lose the combat deliberately to draw cards and trigger effects. Brooding deals 1 damage to the opposing fighter on a lost combat, while Cursed with a Soul recovers 1 health. Use Disengage on either fighter to attack for 4 and escape to an empty space in their zone, preventing a retaliatory double attack.
**Watch out for:** Angel's high-value attacks like Angelus, Scourge of Europe and Momentous Shift (when moved) are so strong that they usually win combats, turning your ability off.

### How they lose
- His deck has very few straight-up defensive options, relying mostly on versatile cards.
- Ranged fighters can pepper him from a distance while he tries to close in.
- Opponents with high-value attack cards easily overpower his limited defense.
- His special ability fails to trigger consistently because his attack values are too high to lose combats reliably.

### Key cards
- **Five by Five**: Faith's 5-value attack that lets her move up to 5 spaces through opposing fighters.
- **The Rogue Slayer**: Faith's 3-value versatile defense that deals 1 damage to each adjacent enemy.
- **Disengage**: A 4-value attack for either fighter that repositions them to an empty space in their zone after combat.
- **Brooding**: A 3-value versatile for Angel that damages the opponent if he loses combat.
- **Haunted by the Faces**: Angel's only dedicated defense card, letting him place himself anywhere in his zone if he loses the combat.

### Tips
- Save versatile cards like Momentous Shift and Brooding for defense unless you specifically need an attack.
- Position your fighters carefully so opponents must spend movement or actions to reach you.
- Use Regroup as a reliable bait card to draw extra cards through your ability or winning the exchange.
- Treat Cursed with a Soul's healing as a pleasant bonus rather than a guaranteed recovery method.
