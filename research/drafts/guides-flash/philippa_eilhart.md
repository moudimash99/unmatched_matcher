## Philippa Eilhart

**In short:** A ranged sorceress and glass cannon combining low-value chip attacks with high defensive utility. Her special ability lets her draw back up to four cards at the end of every turn, fueling constant aggression or supporting a slower, stalling approach.

### Plan A: Constant aggression
**Goal:** Attack every turn and keep relentless pressure on the opponent.
**How to do it:** Leverage your ability to draw cards at the end of the turn to offset the cost of continuous attacks and boosting. Use Owlform by dumping cards from your hand to boost its value, then refill your hand using Two Steps Ahead. Play Regicide as a reliable heavy hitter. When you win the initiative and can maneuver and boost for an attack on the first turn, press the advantage immediately to start chipping away.
**Watch out for:** Fighters with strong card draw or hand-maintenance abilities (like Ciri) can outpace your aggression and leave you struggling to keep up.

### Plan B: Stall and control
**Goal:** Restrict your opponent's options using hand knowledge, discard effects, and movement control.
**How to do it:** Control the board with Dijkstra and Polymorphy. Use Spymaster's ruse to force your opponent to discard key cards, and Do my bidding to gain hand knowledge and dictate what card they must play. Cast Spellbreaker to cancel effects and end the turn. If Dijkstra dies before you can use his unique scheme, you will need to pivot to a more aggressive style.
**Watch out for:** Stalling can feel too weak against aggressive or scaling fighters who grow more dangerous the longer the game goes on.

### How they lose
- They get overwhelmed by fighters who maintain superior card draw and hand advantage.
- Philippa has many attacks and can struggle to find her defenses when she needs them.
- Her defensive cards are great for setting up counter-attacks, but weak at directly protecting her low health pool.
- Aggressive opponents can rush her down and defeat her before her chip damage and effects add up.

### Key cards
- **Owlform**: an attack that lets you discard any number of cards to add +1 to its value for each card discarded, allowing you to dump your hand and immediately draw back up.
- **Polymorphy**: a scheme that places Philippa in any space and sets her move value to 5, providing an escape tool.
- **Do my bidding**: a defense that returns the opponent's card to their hand, lets you look at their hand, and forces them to choose a card to play.
- **Paralyzing fetters**: a defense that locks the opposing card's value to its printed value, ignoring all boosts, card effects, and map modifiers.
- **Backup plan**: a scheme that lets you choose to draw three cards or reset Philippa's health to 5.

### Tips
- Use Spellbreaker as your first action of the turn to pass safely when you have a secure hand and are ahead in value.
- Remember that Two Steps Ahead is optional, which is crucial when approaching exhaustion so you do not draw unnecessary cards and take fatal damage.
- Position Dijkstra carefully so that when you use Polymorphy to escape, you remain in the opponent's zone while keeping Philippa out of easy reach.
- Use Blinding dust to punish attackers by discarding a card to deal damage equal to its boost value.
