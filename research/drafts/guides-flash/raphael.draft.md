## Raphael

**In short:** A bruising hybrid brawler who punishes the opponent for blocking. By treating early attacks as calculated losses, he triggers his ability to gain an extra action, strips the enemy's hand of defenses, and finishes them off with massive strikes.

### Plan A: Pressure with calculated losses
**Goal:** Force the opponent to burn their defensive cards while turning every combat loss into extra momentum.
**How to do it:** Use low-value or versatile cards like Let's do this! (3 copies, value 1) or Crowd control (3 copies, value 2) as your first attack of the turn. Since they have low printed values, you are likely to lose the combat, which triggers his ability, Anger Issues: the first time you lose combat on your turn, you gain 1 action. This applies to Casey Jones as well. With the extra action, follow up with a stronger strike before the enemy can recover. Keep Relentless (2 copies, value 3) in your hand for defense; it shuffles back into your deck and draws you a card, keeping your hand size healthy.
**Watch out for:** Don't throw away all your defenses blindly, and be careful not to overextend if the opponent manages to hold onto a large hand and turns the pressure back on you.

### Plan B: Unload Unbridled Rage as a finisher
**Goal:** Deliver a knockout blow once the enemy's defenses are depleted.
**How to do it:** Once the opponent is low on cards and health—around 7 health or lower—unleash Unbridled Rage (2 copies, value 3). During combat, you can discard any number of defense or versatile cards to add +2 to this card's value for each card discarded. Saving a stack of discarded or extra defense cards lets you turn this into a lethal attack that one-shots the enemy. 
**Watch out for:** Dumping your entire hand leaves you completely exposed to counter-attacks if the enemy survives the hit. Always keep a strong defense like Heroes in a half shell (2 copies, value 5) or Relentless in hand unless you are certain it is game-over.

### How they lose
- Aggressive opponents who out-tempo him or run him out of defenses before he can set up his big combos.
- Hand disruption and heavy control that strip away his resources and leave him unable to fuel Unbridled Rage.
- Ranged fighters who avoid getting pinned by Casey Jones and kite Raphael around the board.

### Key cards
- **Unbridled Rage**: his main finisher. Discard defense and versatile cards to pump its value up for massive burst damage.
- **Let's do this!**: an aggressive attack that draws cards on combat and replaces itself, feeding his action economy.
- **Relentless**: a versatile card that recycles itself back into the deck and draws a card, acting as his primary defensive engine.
- **Break something**: a 2-value versatile that cancels opponent effects or deals 2 damage to an adjacent fighter if nothing is canceled.
- **Heroes in a half shell**: a clean 5-value defense with no negative effects, vital for keeping him alive when he isn't discarding.

### Tips
- Use Casey Jones (8 health, ranged) to control space, body block bottlenecks, and force the opponent to burn cards on defense before Raph moves in.
- Mix up your first attacks; don't always play weak cards, or savvy opponents will stop blocking and let you win combats for free.
- Abuse Relentless on defense whenever possible, as it is less likely to be canceled than on attack.
- Remember that Casey's attacks (like Slapshot, value 6 when adjacent) also benefit from positioning and can punish enemies who ignore him.
