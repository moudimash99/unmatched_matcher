## John Henry

**In short:** A relentless melee fighter who lays track tokens across the board to expand his movement, build up momentum, and deliver crushing blows with heavy hammer attacks.

### Plan A: Lay track and build momentum
**Goal:** Cross the map rapidly and trigger powerful secondary effects on your hammer cards.
**How to do it:** At the start of your turn, place a track token in a space in John Henry's zone. Spaces with track tokens do not count toward your movement, allowing you to zip across the board. Several hammer cards—such as Eighteen-pound Hammer, Twelve-pound Hammer, and Nine-pound Hammer—require you to use at least 4 different paths during the turn to trigger their extra effects, like canceling opponent effects, placing fighters onto track spaces, or dealing area damage. Use schemes like Striking Fire or attacks like Hear That Cold Steel Ring to change spaces and meet these path requirements before striking.
**Watch out for:** Failing to plan your movement pathing means your hammer cards lose their bonus effects, reducing your damage output.

### Plan B: Unleash heavy strikes
**Goal:** Scale your damage output as the match progresses and sustain your health pool.
**How to do it:** Bring Down The Mountain gains value for each hammer card in your discard pile, making your attacks progressively heavier as you cycle through your deck. When you need to recover, Cool Drink Of Water restores 3 health and draws a card. Use Larger Than Life for a flat 6-value attack with a high boost of 4 when you need reliable heavy pressure.
**Watch out for:** Relying solely on raw values without setting up your movement leaves your positional tricks wasted.

### How they lose
- If opponents pin him down and prevent him from laying tracks or moving across different paths, his key hammer effects fail to trigger.
- Running out of defensive resources while trying to set up large attacks leaves his 17 health vulnerable.

### Key cards
- **Eighteen-pound Hammer**: A 6-value attack that cancels all effects on the opponent's card if you used at least 4 different paths this turn.
- **Cool Drink Of Water**: A 0-value defense that recovers 3 health and draws 1 card.
- **Bring Down The Mountain**: A versatile card that scales in value based on the number of hammer cards in your discard pile.
- **Striking Fire**: A scheme that moves John Henry up to 4 spaces and grants 1 action to help set up movement paths.

### Tips
- Remember to summon a track token at the start of every turn.
- Combine track-free movement with schemes to easily hit the 4-path requirement for your hammer cards.
- Keep track of your discarded hammer cards to maximize the value of Bring Down The Mountain.
