## The Invisible Man

**In short:** A unique solo melee fighter who relies on positional defense, board-wide scheme damage, and elusiveness to outlast his opponent in the exhaustion game.

### Plan A: Camp the fog and play for exhaustion
**Goal:** Force your opponent to chase you across the board, waste cards maneuvering, and eventually perish from exhaustion.
**How to do it:** Spread your three fog tokens out across the board using cards like Rolling Fog, Covert Preparation, and Slip Away so your opponent has to cover serious ground. Teleport between fog tokens as if they were adjacent. Stay on a fog token whenever possible to gain a plus-one buff to all your defense cards and versatile cards used on defense. Pile up un-cancellable auto damage using schemes like Reign of Terror and Step Lightly (which deals 3 damage instead of 1 when standing on fog). Finish off the game by letting fatigue deal the final blows as your opponent runs out of cards.
**Watch out for:** Playing him as a hyper-aggressive character. He lacks the offensive power to slug it out face-to-face and will quickly lose if he tries to rush the enemy down.

### Plan B: Disappear and reset with Vanish
**Goal:** Escape danger, force card overdraws, and stall the clock.
**How to do it:** Use Vanish as your first action to recover 1 health and remove The Invisible Man completely from the board until your next turn. Because he is gone, your opponent cannot attack you and will often be forced to double maneuver, speeding up the emptying of their deck for the late-game exhaustion race. Try to time Vanish when your opponent has a full hand to force painful end-of-turn discards.
**Watch out for:** Vanishing as a second action ends your turn immediately, so you must plan your first action carefully. Also, returning from Vanish means you didn't *start* your turn on a fog token, so you cannot trigger Emerge From Mist that turn.

### How they lose
- Powerful sidekicks (like Watson, Merlin, or the Huntsman) soak up damage, preventing your 10 points of scheme damage from securing a hero kill.
- Aggressive move-3 fighters, fast combo characters, and high-mobility attackers (like Bruce Lee or Sinbad) run you down before fatigue sets in.
- Discard effects (such as Ambush) strip crucial damage schemes and high-value attacks from your hand.
- Getting pinned or cut off from your fog network strips away your defense buffs and escape routes.

### Key cards
- **Surprise Attack**: a 5-value attack that cancels all effects on the opponent's card, serving as your primary offensive tool to bypass defensive triggers.
- **Step Lightly**: a scheme that deals 3 damage to an adjacent fighter when standing on fog, forming the core of your direct damage output.
- **Vanish**: removes you from the board, heals 1, and forces enemy maneuvers to accelerate exhaustion.
- **Into Thin Air**: a 4-value defense that moves you 1 space, boosted to 5 on a fog token for top-tier protection.
- **Impossible to See**: a versatile card that immediately reduces the opponent's attack or defense value to zero and prevents it from being changed by card effects.

### Tips
- Always keep your turn ending on a fog token so your defense bonus stays online for the opponent's turn.
- When playing Covert Preparation, choose to move the fog token you are currently standing on zero spaces to deny your opponent the chance to displace you.
- Use Coded Notes early in the match; holding it late into the game risks dead-card hand clogging and unwanted exhaustion damage.
- Combine Rolling Fog with Step Lightly to bridge the gap against elusive ranged opponents.
