## Ancient Leshen

**In short:** A ranged fighter with 13 health and a base movement of 1 supported by two melee Wolves with a movement of 3. He controls space by blocking choke points with his sidekicks, pressures opponents with constant attacks, sustains himself with ongoing healing, and threatens massive damage via his ability when executing a second attack in a turn.

### Plan A: Harass with the Wolves and control positioning
**Goal:** Keep opponents at a distance, restrict their movement, and chip away with steady attacks while keeping the Leshen secure.
**How to do it:** Utilize the Wolves' superior movement to surround enemy fighters and obstruct pathways. Employ disturbing howls to shift adversaries into position while gaining an action, or leverage harrying strike, which scales up based on adjacent fighters. Position the Leshen behind the Wolves so attackers must target the sidekicks first.
**Watch out for:** Losing the Wolves without retaining methods to resummon them, which halts board control and strips away healing capability.

### Plan B: The healing and exhaustion game
**Goal:** Outlive opponents by generating continuous healing each turn and managing resources.
**How to do it:** Play strength of the pack to introduce a Wolf and trigger ongoing recovery at your turn's start, provided a Wolf remains in the Leshen's zone. Use primeval slam to retrieve defeated Wolves and maneuver them across the board. In later stages, vanish into murder can function as a temporary absence or double pass while securing a card draw.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who aggressively bypass the Wolves and exploit the 13 health pool before recovery establishes momentum.

### Plan C: Threaten the double-attack finish
**Goal:** Conclude matches using a high-damage second attack amplified by heart of the forest.
**How to do it:** The special ability grants +3 to the Leshen's attacks if he already attacked during the current turn. Avoid forcing this condition early, as opponents can easily evade or block it; treat it as a late-game win condition instead. Combine flock of birds or vanish into murder to close the distance on fleeing targets and deliver a surprise second strike.
**Watch out for:** Overcommitting early to trigger the ability, which leaves the Leshen exposed without adequate defenses.

### How they lose
- Fast, aggressive fighters overwhelm him before his sidekicks and recovery establish control.
- He possesses only 9 personal defenses in his deck, leaving him vulnerable if enemies break through the Wolves.
- Primeval guardian forces an opponent discard and adds that card's boost value to their attack, which backfires against fighters with high boost values.
- Ranged fighters who can attack from afar without approaching him.
- Getting pinned with no movement options due to his base movement of 1.

### Key cards
- **Vanish into murder**: deals damage in his zone, removes him from the board, places him anywhere next turn, and draws a card.
- **Strength of the pack**: summons a Wolf and provides ongoing healing of 1 health at the start of your turn as long as a Wolf remains in his zone.
- **Flock of birds**: a 5-value attack that allows placing the Leshen in any space after combat.
- **Primeval guardian**: a 5-value defense forcing an opponent to discard their top deck card, adding its boost value to their total, and moving each Wolf up to 3 spaces.
- **Harrying strike**: a 2-value versatile card adding +2 to its value for each of your fighters adjacent to the target.

### Tips
- Mulligan aggressively for attack cards to ensure an early offensive presence.
- Use wily fighting on defense with the Leshen to retaliate with auto-damage against adjacent foes.
- Remember that primeval slam summons a Wolf in his zone and moves all Wolves up to 3 spaces, recovering defeated sidekicks.
- Avoid camping in the back trying to force the ability all game; rely on steady pressure and card draw from planted feet or nature abounds.
- Token and gimmick maps favor his low movement and token interactions.
