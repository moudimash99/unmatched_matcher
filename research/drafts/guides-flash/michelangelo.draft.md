## Michelangelo

**In short:** An agile melee wild card with a tiny hand limit of three and mandatory card draw on every attack or scheme. He moves fast on his skateboard, chains multiple actions, and throws chaotic, unpredictable strings of attacks before his hand fills up.

### Plan A: Push the offensive tempo
**Goal:** Attack constantly to trigger card draws and maintain relentless pressure without getting weighed down by your hand size.
**How to do it:** Your ability forces you to draw a card after every attack or scheme, and your maximum hand size is only three. Because you draw on attacks, attacking functions almost like a maneuver. Stay close to the fight and keep swinging every turn instead of fleeing. Use action-gaining cards like Boisterous Beatdown, Hi-yaaaaah!!, and April's Hard-hitting investigation to chain multiple strikes together. Play on tighter maps where you can reach the enemy immediately and pin them down so they cannot escape your sequence.
**Watch out for:** In a 1v1 match, he is extremely swingy. If your card draws miss your action-gaining chains, you can easily stall out or burn through your deck.

### Plan B: Build up to a massive combo finisher
**Goal:** Stack sequential bonuses and unleash a devastating final hit.
**How to do it:** Play multiple cards in a single turn using your action gains, then drop Cowabunga!!, which adds +1 value for each other card you played that turn. Enhance your damage further with Nunchaku schemes to raise your attack values. Use Turtle Power! when you can afford to boost your own attack for a heavy blow. 
**Watch out for:** Opponents who anticipate your combo string can ruin your turn with a Feint, wasting your setup and your card resources.

### How they lose
- Ranged fighters or opponents with high mobility keep their distance and pick him apart while he tries to close in.
- Heavy defense and cancel cards shut down his high-value combo pieces like Cowabunga!! or intercept his action-gaining attacks.
- Running out of cards triggers the exhaustion clock rapidly because his mandatory card draws and small hand force high turnover.
- Losing April O'Neil early deprives you of her ranged pressure and valuable utility cards like Shell insertion.

### Key cards
- **Hi-yaaaaah!!**: A 4-value attack that deals 1 damage to an adjacent fighter and lets you tuck a card to gain an action.
- **Boisterous Beatdown**: A 2-value attack that grants an action, or two actions if you win the combat. Great for picking off 1-health sidekicks.
- **Cowabunga!!**: A 3-value versatile attack that scales up for every other card you played earlier that turn.
- **Guaranteed delivery**: A versatile card that lets you tuck a card to cancel opponent effects immediately, drawing two cards if your hand is empty.
- **Shell insertion**: An April scheme offering versatile options including card recycling, moving fighters, forcing opponent discards, or gaining actions.

### Tips
- Always start your games aggressively, as he thrives on going first and getting into the opponent's face immediately.
- Use Hi-yaaaaah!! and Hard-hitting investigation to pick off annoying 1-health sidekicks while generating extra actions.
- Remember that April O'Neil is ranged; position her to support Michelangelo and control bottlenecks, but protect her health since she only has 6 health.
- Use Back for seconds and Shell insertion to shuffle key cards back into your deck before exhaustion catches up to you.
- Michelangelo is a party dude!! heals every friendly fighter by 1, which provides a useful minor recovery when you need to stabilize.
