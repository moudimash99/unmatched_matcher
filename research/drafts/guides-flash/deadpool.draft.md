## Deadpool

**In short:** A quirky solo melee fighter with a 30-card highlander deck of unique cards. He offsets his low 10 health with constant post-attack healing, high-impact bursts, and late-game deck recycling, but suffers from severe card inconsistency and limited defensive options.

### Plan A: Out-heal the damage with constant pressure
**Goal:** Stay alive by attacking every turn to trigger your healing factor while wearing down the opponent.
**How to do it:** Your special ability heals you 1 health after every attack. Lean into this by maintaining offensive momentum, attacking frequently to keep your health pool stable above danger range. Supplement this with card-based healing from Gimme Gimme Chimichanga, Non-Retinal Scan Access to Danger Room, They Have An Amazing Buffet, and schemes like Faint. Because your maximum health is strictly capped at 10, constant chip healing is your primary lifeline against getting two-shot by heavy hitters.
**Watch out for:** Over-aggression without defenses. You only have a limited pool of 15 cards capable of blocking, and low card draw means double maneuvering leaves you vulnerable. If you run out of blocks against an opponent with multi-attack capabilities, your low health makes you collapse instantly.

### Plan B: Fish early, recycle late
**Goal:** Overcome highlander deck randomness by tutoring key options early and delaying exhaustion late.
**How to do it:** Because every card in your deck is unique, drawing what you need when you need it is difficult. Use Time out time out time out! early while your deck is full to search your deck and immediately play a needed card. Conversely, save Dumpster Divin' Deadpool for the late game; playing it shuffles five cards from your discard pile back into your deck, recovers 1 health, and heavily delays exhaustion while recycling your best attacks and defenses.
**Watch out for:** Letting opponents strip your hand. Discard effects like Passwords or opponent abilities can target Dumpster Divin' Deadpool while it sits in your hand, destroying your late-game recovery plan.

### Plan C: Coordinate burst damage and hand peeking
**Goal:** Punish the opponent with massive attack values and targeted hand disruption.
**How to do it:** Pair your 1-value attack Cha-Ching! with a heavy boost card like Push to Teleport to scale your attack value up to 7. Use Non-Retinal Scan Access to Danger Room to look at the opposing hand, then immediately follow up with Holy Mackerel! to name a card in their hand, forcing them to discard all copies of it. 
**Watch out for:** Feints. Boosting an attack into a Feint wastes your boost card entirely, and getting your hand-peeking setup disrupted leaves your high-cost schemes whiffing.

### How they lose
- Low maximum health (10) leaves no margin for error once defenses are depleted.
- Highlander deck inconsistency means essential blocks or attacks can be buried at the bottom of the deck.
- Getting pinned by fighters with sidekicks, as your move value of 2 and solo melee profile give you poor board mobility and flexibility.
- Running out of cards due to poor draw support, leading to fatal exhaustion damage.
- Hand disruption stripping your critical defensive cards before you can deploy them.

### Key cards
- **Super Feint**: A 4-value versatile that cancels opponent effects, draws a card, and cannot be canceled, providing reliable defense and card cycle.
- **Dumpster Divin' Deadpool**: A scheme that shuffles five discarded cards back into your deck and heals 1 health, acting as your primary exhaustion delay and resource loop.
- **Time out time out time out!**: A 0-value versatile that searches your deck for a playable card immediately, mitigating bad opening draws.
- **Gaze of Stone**: A 2-value attack that deals 8 extra damage if you win the combat, creating massive threat potential.
- **Passwords**: A 5-value defense that blocks heavily, though it forces you to let the opponent choose a card from your hand to discard.

### Tips
- Never play They Have An Amazing Buffet when you are at 8 or more health; the post-heal damage penalty will trigger and waste your life total.
- Remember that your healing ability triggers after the combat is fully resolved, meaning after-combat effects can still defeat you before the heal applies.
- Track your discard pile closely to know which unique cards are still available in your deck versus already spent.
- Use Xavier Institute Faculty when you need to attack from a distance or trigger your healing without closing into dangerous melee range.
- Avoid wearing pants during the match—while it confers no mechanical advantage, it adds to the psychological warfare.
