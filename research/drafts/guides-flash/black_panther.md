## Black Panther

**In short:** An aggressive hybrid fighter who draws a card every time he boosts, storing up to ten stolen cards from the opponent's deck in his Vibranium Suit to use as boost fuel. He relies on Shuri's schemes to accelerate the opponent's exhaustion while managing his mandatory card draw.

### Plan A: Steal cards and press the attack
**Goal:** Deplete the opponent's deck and leverage their high-boost cards to deliver heavy blows.
**How to do it:** Mulligan for Shuri's schemes, specifically Tactical Remote Scanning, to instantly stash cards away. Protect Shuri early so she can play her schemes safely. Use Analyze and Adjust and Ancestral Insight to continuously strip cards from the opponent's deck and tuck them into your Vibranium Suit, denying them access to key resources. Spend your boosted attacks—such as Wakanda Forever! and Anti-Metal Claws—to pressure the enemy while your ability keeps your hand full.
**Watch out for:** Getting tunnel vision on the Vibranium Suit. You do not need every steal to win, and over-boosting can spiral you rapidly toward exhaustion.

### Plan B: Unleash Wakanda Forever!
**Goal:** Exploit massive multi-boost attacks to chunk down the enemy hero's health pool.
**How to do it:** Save Wakanda Forever! for critical offensive turns when you have enough cards in hand to support double-boosting. Because your special ability forces you to draw a card with every individual boost, you can pump this versatile card up multiple times without draining your hand completely. Pair your attacks with Anti-Metal Claws to scale off the opponent's revealed defense boost values.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who hold onto Feints to cancel your big offensive payouts or catch you when your hand is too small to complete a full multi-boost sequence.

### How they lose
- Shuri is hunted down and eliminated early, cutting off her schemes and forcing you to rely solely on combat steals.
- Aggressive card-draw and mandatory boosting accelerate your own exhaustion clock faster than the opponent's.
- High-tier characters like Sherlock Holmes, Doctor Strange, Bigfoot, and She-Hulk ignore or outpace your discards and pressure you down.
- Your base attack values are low, leaving you vulnerable if you fail to secure high-value boosts or if your key cards get Feinted.

### Key cards
- **Tactical Remote Scanning**: Shuri's scheme that reveals and stores two cards from the opponent's deck directly into your Vibranium Suit.
- **Wakanda Forever!**: A versatile card that can be boosted up to two times during combat, serving as your primary damage engine.
- **Analyze and Adjust**: An attack that stores the opponent's top deck card in your suit.
- **Anti-Metal Claws**: A versatile card that adds the opponent's card boost value to its own, with an option to boost further.
- **Ancestral Insight**: A versatile card that stores an opponent's card on combat victory.

### Tips
- Always keep Shuri positioned safely, as her schemes are vital for stripping cards from the opponent before they can use them.
- Use Stalking Panther to reposition both fighters across tight board spaces or set up sudden engagements without spending your maneuver actions.
- Remember that your card draw on boosting is mandatory; balance your aggression carefully to avoid burning through your deck too early against defensive opponents.
- Characters with heavy recursion, such as Elektra and Daredevil, suffer immensely when their key pieces are locked away in your Vibranium Suit.
- Small maps like the Sanctum help mitigate movement restrictions and let you leverage your mobility and schemes effectively.
