## Oda Nobunaga

**In short:** A melee fighter backed by two 6-health Honor Guards. He relies on strict board positioning to flank opponents, triggering powerful card effects while his special ability buffs allies within his zone.

### Plan A: Set up tight flanks
**Goal:** Keep your fighters surrounding the enemy to activate bonus effects on attacks and defenses.
**How to do it:** An opposing fighter is flanked when they are adjacent to two or more fighters under your control. Oda's entire strategy revolves around maintaining this formation. Keep the Honor Guards inside Oda's zone so his special ability adds +1 to the value of their played combat cards. Many of his strongest cards require a flank to work:
- Fire and Flames increases from its base value to 5 when the target is flanked.
- Lightning and Thunder cancels all effects on the opponent's card immediately if flanked.
- Sun and Moon ignores the opponent's card value entirely during a flank.
Use Battle Maneuvers to draw a card and move each fighter up to 2 spaces to reposition into a flanking setup.
**Watch out for:** Experienced opponents will actively avoid ending their turns where you can surround them, making flanks difficult to execute against mobile fighters.

### Plan B: Pressure early and draw
**Goal:** Cycle through your deck quickly to land heavy hits before your Honor Guards are defeated.
**How to do it:** Oda has a high total health pool spread across three bodies, but once his sidekicks die, his options shrink dramatically. Use Student of War to deal heavy damage and draw cards equal to the damage dealt, keeping your hand full of high-value responses. Play Reinforce to draw two cards, gain an action, or heal your fighters to sustain pressure. 
**Watch out for:** Student of War can backfire disastrously if played against a 1-health sidekick that takes unblocked damage, forcing you to draw five cards and potentially running you toward exhaustion.

### How they lose
- The Honor Guards are eliminated early, leaving Oda isolated, squishy, and stripped of his zone ability benefits since he does not benefit from it himself.
- A significant portion of his deck relies entirely on flanking; when opponents deny positioning, those cards become dead weight.
- Ranged fighters keep their distance and pick off the Honor Guards before Oda can close the gap.
- He lacks reliable defensive cancels for his Honor Guards, leaving them vulnerable to unmitigated damage and effects.

### Key cards
- **Student of War**: a 5-value attack that refills your hand based on combat damage dealt.
- **Lightning and Thunder**: an Honor Guard attack that cancels opponent effects upon a successful flank.
- **Fire and Flames**: an any-fighter attack that scales up in power when the enemy is surrounded.
- **Pragmatism**: a 3-value defense for Oda that cancels all effects on the opponent's card.
- **Reinforce**: a scheme that offers flexible utility through healing, card draw, or extra actions.

### Tips
- Keep your Honor Guards adjacent to Oda so they continuously benefit from the +1 combat card bonus on both offense and defense.
- Avoid throwing Student of War blindly against low-health targets where excessive card draw could hurt you.
- Use Spring the Trap to swap spaces with an adjacent friendly fighter, turning them into the defender while chipping the opponent for 1 damage after combat.
- Position your fighters near map bottlenecks to restrict enemy movement and force engagements on your terms.
