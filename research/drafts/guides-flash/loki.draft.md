## Loki

**In short:** A tricky melee solo fighter who clogs the opponent's hand with TRICK cards, gains movement and combat value from them, and sets up mind games to punish players who hold too many resources.

### Plan A: Clog the hand and drive initiative
**Goal:** Force the opponent to hold your TRICK cards, increasing your movement and enabling high-value attacks.
**How to do it:** Use maneuvers and schemes like TRICK: SVADILFARI'S LURE to pull opposing fighters adjacent to you and force TRICKS into their hand. Your special ability, Mischief-Monger, adds +1 to your move value for each TRICK currently in your opponents' hands. Keep your opponent's hand full so they are forced to deal with your TRICK cards or discard them, which returns those cards to your hand or the top of your deck. 
**Watch out for:** Opponents who aggressively play or discard your TRICK cards to minimize their hand size, denying your movement bonuses and weakening your card scaling effects.

### Plan B: Punish with Ragnarok and value tricks
**Goal:** Deal massive damage when the opponent holds your TRICK cards or maintains a large hand.
**How to do it:** 
- Play RAGNARÖK when your opponent holds TRICKS to add +3 to its value for each TRICK in their hand. Its effects cannot be canceled.
- Use Shapershifter to add +1 to its value for each card in your opponent's hand, or spend the action to gain an extra action when you need tempo.
- UNDERHANDED becomes a 6-value attack if your opponent has more cards in their hand than you.
- LOOKING FOR TROUBLE lets you return their card, look at their hand, and choose a card for them to play.
- GOD OF MISCHIEF ignores a TRICK card's value immediately, or cancels all effects on any other card.
- LAEVATEINN reveals a card in their hand, adds its boost value to yours, and retrieves a TRICK if you reveal one.

**Watch out for:** Ragnarok only reaching a moderate value if the opponent manages to dump your TRICKS quickly, and getting caught with lower damage outputs if the opponent plays with a small hand.

### How they lose
- Opponents who quickly discard your TRICK cards and keep their hand size small, denying your scaling bonuses.
- Fighters with low printed values or cards that change values unexpectedly, which disrupt your math and mind games.
- Running low on damage when the opponent refuses to hoard cards or TRICKS.
- Aggressive fighters who pressure your 16 health before you can set up your combos.
- Large maps, as Loki relies on small maps with tight centers (3 to 4 spaces) to control positioning and movement.

### Key cards
- **RAGNARÖK**: A 0-value attack that cannot be canceled and scales with TRICKS in the opponent's hand.
- **Shapershifter**: A 0-value attack that offers a choice between scaling damage based on hand size or gaining an action.
- **LOOKING FOR TROUBLE**: A 4-value attack that forces the opponent to replay a chosen card after viewing their hand.
- **TRICK: SINDRI'S BET**: A 3-value defense that forces the opponent to discard a card or suffer a 3-value reduction.
- **TRICK: FREYJA'S RESCUE**: A 2-value versatile that swaps card values on offense or heals Loki for 3 health when played by an opponent.

### Tips
- Mix up your attacks so the opponent never knows whether you are playing a true heavy hitter or baiting them with a TRICK.
- Use TRICK: SVADILFARI'S LURE to pull an opponent adjacent before executing Shapershifter or Ragnarok.
- Don't rely blindly on fatigue as your sole win condition; your best path to victory is dealing heavy damage through calculated card combinations and resource control.
- Pay attention to card text specifics: Ragnarok checks the individual opponent's hand, while your move speed bonus checks all opponents' hands.
