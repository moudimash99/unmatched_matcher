## Donatello

**In short:** A strategic specialist and tempo-aggro fighter who leverages a unique maneuver ability to curate his hand, sets up three permanent inventions to boost stats for both himself and Metalhead, and applies heavy pressure through card recursion and direct auto-damage.

### Plan A: Build the engine and go aggro
**Goal:** Stall until Electro Grenade is active, then shift from tempo play to aggressive pressure with enhanced attacks.
**How to do it:** While waiting for Electro Grenade, hold subpar cards like Quick Strike and Shift Focus to use as removal fuel, and keep Metalhead protected. Utilize your special ability on every maneuver to draw two cards and tuck a card from your hand onto the bottom of your deck. Once Electro Grenade is active, your attacks hit much harder. Place high-boost cards on the bottom of your deck to prepare for Metalhead's Untested Enhancements.
**Watch out for:** Rushing into combat before your inventions are active leaves you relying on weak base values that struggle to win engagements.

### Plan B: Unleash auto-damage with Party Wagon
**Goal:** Grind down opponents using reliable direct damage without relying solely on winning combats.
**How to do it:** Use Party Wagon to move a friendly fighter up to five spaces through an opposing fighter and deal 2 direct damage. Because Donatello features card recursion, you can retrieve Party Wagon using Donatello Does Machines or cycle it back into the deck with Thinking Ahead to use it multiple times. 
**Watch out for:** Party Wagon goes to the bottom of the deck when cycled with Thinking Ahead, meaning it takes time to draw back into your hand.

### How they lose
- Metalhead dying early strips Donatello of a vital body blocker and the primary damage driver for Untested Enhancements.
- Opponents leveraging heavy card-burning or shutting down Thinking Ahead with high defenses, stopping your recursion.
- Inability to stop immediate or during-combat effects, since Short Circuit only cancels after-combat effects.
- Struggling against opponents who can outpace your card draw or punish aggressive positioning.

### Key cards
- **Electro grenade**: An attack invention that removes up to 4 attack or versatile cards from your discard pile to increase its value, and permanently grants +1 to the value of all your attacks.
- **Self-defense grid**: A defense invention that removes up to 4 defense or versatile cards from your discard pile to recover health, and permanently adds +1 to the value of your defenses.
- **Party wagon!**: A scheme that moves a friendly fighter up to 5 spaces through opposing fighters and deals 2 damage to one of them.
- **Donatello does machines**: Returns an attack, versatile, or defense card from your discard pile to your hand and lets you draw a card.
- **Untested enhancements**: A Metalhead versatile attack that discards the bottom card of your deck and adds its boost value to the attack.

### Tips
- Always use your maneuver ability to draw two and tuck a card from your hand onto the bottom of your deck to maintain hand consistency.
- Consider playing Smoke Bomb as a pass without removing schemes from your discard pile so you can trigger its permanent effect on future scheme plays while keeping your removal options open.
- Use Bo Staff defensively to deal 1 chip damage to an adjacent fighter and draw a card.
- Protect Metalhead early so he remains a formidable threat with Untested Enhancements.
- Capitalize on favorable matchups where your recursion and direct damage shine, such as against fighters like Luke Cage or Nikola Tesla.
