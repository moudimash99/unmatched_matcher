## Hamlet

**In short:** A high-tempo melee fighter who cycles rapidly through two alternating stances using his special ability: drawing extra cards during To Be turns and adding flat damage to attacks during Not To Be turns. He treats his sidekick as a resource pool and uses heavy cycling and positioning tricks to overwhelm opponents.

### Plan A: Stance alternating and blitz pressure
**Goal:** Alternate between drawing extra cards and dealing massive attacks to overwhelm the opponent's defenses.
**How to do it:** Start your turn by choosing between To Be and Not To Be. On To Be turns, double maneuver or move to draw two additional cards, filling your hand to fuel your options and set up big turns. On Not To Be turns, take 2 damage on your sidekick Rosencrantz & Guildenstern to avoid hurting Hamlet, and add +2 to the value of your attacks. Time your Not To Be turns when you can boost Cruel To Be Kind for huge numbers, double attack, or capitalize on a full hand of 6 or more cards. Use The Readiness Is All or The Ghost to teleport and keep opponents locked into position so they cannot escape your heavy strikes.
**Watch out for:** This aggressive playstyle burns through your deck extremely fast, making you highly vulnerable to exhaustion if you do not manage your card counts.

### Plan B: Hand management and exhaustion control
**Goal:** Stall the exhaustion clock and keep your hand size manageable using your unique schemes.
**How to do it:** Use Method In The Madness on a To Be turn after drawing cards to place any number of cards from your hand back onto the bottom of your deck without drawing them back, gaining 1 action to keep playing. This lets you cycle through your deck, avoid overhand discards, and stack known cards at the bottom of your deck to stall against opponents trying to win via exhaustion. Use The Ghost to recover 2 health or reposition while gaining an action to maintain board control.
**Watch out for:** Relying on drawing Method In The Madness can leave you stranded if it stays buried deep in your deck early on.

### How they lose
- They burn through their 30-card deck too quickly through excessive card drawing and self-inflicted stance pressure, leading to fatal exhaustion damage.
- Opponents with reliable cancels and Feints blank your high-value boosted attacks and versatile healing effects.
- Ranged fighters or high-mobility characters keep their distance and force you to kill yourself through continuous Not To Be damage or overdrawing.
- Low-value defenses or heavy pressure overwhelm your weak printed defense stats before your combos can launch.

### Key cards
- **Method In The Madness:** A scheme that lets you put cards from your hand onto the bottom of your deck and gain an action, serving as your primary tool to prevent overdrawing and stall exhaustion.
- **Cruel To Be Kind:** A 2-value attack that you may boost, turning into a massive strike when combined with a Not To Be stance bonus.
- **The Rest Is Silence:** A 2-value defense that ends the turn immediately after combat, halting opposing after-combat effects and momentum.
- **The Play's The Thing:** A 3-value versatile that forces a random opponent discard and heals Hamlet for the boost value if you win the combat.
- **Uncertain Doom:** A 2-value attack that ignores the value of the opposing fighter's versatile card, punishing opponents who rely heavily on versatile defenses.

### Tips
- Keep your sidekick Rosencrantz & Guildenstern safely back to absorb the 2 damage from Not To Be turns, saving Hamlet's health pool for combat.
- Play The Play's The Thing as an attack rather than a defense to maximize your chances of winning combat and triggering the healing effect.
- Use Uncertain Doom against fighters who lack dedicated defense cards and rely entirely on versatile cards.
- Take advantage of Hamlet's relative obscurity in tournaments to catch opponents off guard who are unfamiliar with his stance-switching rhythm.
