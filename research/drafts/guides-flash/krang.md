## Krang

**In short:** A melee fighter whose movement and combat numbers rely on his active doomsday machines and the Die of Ultimate Destruction. He balances building mobility with managing machine deactivations for rerolls.

### Plan A: Scale your movement and push the tech
**Goal:** Increase your movement value and pressure the opponent using active machines.
**How to do it:** Krang begins with one active doomsday machine and two inactive ones, adding one to his move value for each active machine. Play Molecular Amplification Unit and Welcome to the Technodrome! to activate your machines. Use Android arms: Wings to move across the board, passing through opposing fighters and dealing damage to each one. When you need a massive reset, IQ of 968 activates all machines at once, draws cards, and recovers health based on separate die rolls.
**Watch out for:** Deactivating machines for rerolls reduces your movement back down, leaving you slow and vulnerable if you burn through them too quickly.

### Plan B: Gamble on the Die of Ultimate Destruction
**Goal:** Exploit question-mark effects to turn average cards into massive attacks and high-impact schemes.
**How to do it:** Many of Krang's cards feature a question-mark value determined by rolling a D6 where an X counts as zero. Because you can see the opponent's defense card before resolving during-combat effects on attacks like Android arms: Missiles or Molecular Amplification Unit, you can decide whether the rolled value is enough or if you need to deactivate a machine to reroll. Android arms: Powerbomb allows you to boost during combat and push the enemy spaces equal to the damage dealt.
**Watch out for:** Rolling a zero or an X wastes the card's scaling effect unless you have an active machine available to burn for a reroll.

### How they lose
- Bad luck on consecutive die rolls leaves him stuck rolling zeros or low values, rendering his variable effects weak.
- Losing all his active machines drops his movement to one, making it impossible to escape or chase agile opponents.
- Strong defensive cards and cancels block his during-combat increases and prevent him from executing his big attacks.
- Getting pinned down on the board when his mobility is low.

### Key cards
- **Android arms: Missiles**: a ranged attack that lets you see the opponent's defense card before you roll to increase its value.
- **Minimizer**: a defense card starting at zero that immediately decreases the opponent's card value by four, ending the turn afterward.
- **Welcome to the Technodrome!**: gives you the choice to either cancel all effects on the opponent's card or activate a machine.
- **Pan-dimensional Portal**: moves a fighter into any space where they are locked for the turn, followed by a hand-recycling effect.
- **Warlord of Dimension X**: compares card name lengths to determine if you draw your revealed card.

### Tips
- Count every character, letter, number, and space when evaluating card name lengths for Warlord of Dimension X; the prefix Android arms helps make your card names longer.
- Save at least one active machine when you anticipate needing a crucial reroll on defense or attack.
- Balance when to use IQ of 968 so you do not draw more cards than you can comfortably hold.
- Keep track of how many machines you have flipped, as burning all of them turns you into a slow target.
