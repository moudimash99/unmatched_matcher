## Yennenga

**In short:** A formidable double-ranged fighter who uses her Archers as a shared health pool and meat shields, overwhelming opponents with high attack volume and action chaining while lacking cancel effects.

### Plan A: Share the load with the Archers
**Goal:** Maximize your effective health pool and prevent damage from reaching Yennenga's health dial.
**How to do it:** Keep your two Archers inside Yennenga's zone. Whenever Yennenga would take damage from combat effects, schemes, or attacks, assign that damage to the Archers instead. Because each Archer starts with 2 health, you effectively add 4 health to your pool, and potential revives push your total health pool much higher. Use One With The Land (2 copies, boost 2) when your fighters are damaged to move them up to 2 spaces, recover 1 health for each fighter, and draw 1 card. Heal your Archers when they are down to 1 health to reset them, or keep them at 1 health to use as body blockers before reviving them later.
**Watch out for:** Opponents using area-of-effect (AoE) attacks like Little Red's Never Leave the Path that target multiple fighters simultaneously. Because damage cannot disappear, AoE attacks bypass your ability to selectively assign damage and can clear your Archers off the board instantly.

### Plan B: Chain actions for high-volume attacks
**Goal:** Run your opponent out of defensive cards by launching multiple attacks in a single turn.
**How to do it:** Yennenga relies on quantity over quality, using cards that generate extra actions. Master of the Hunt (2 copies, boost 3) is a scheme that gains 2 actions, allowing you to attack multiple times in a turn. Surprise Volley (3 copies, value 3, boost 3) is an attack that lets you either return a defeated Archer to the opposing fighter's zone as the new attacker or, if you don't, gain 1 action. Rain of Arrows (3 copies, value 3, boost 3) triggers a second 3-value volley attack after combat, forcing your opponent to spend two block cards to stop a single card.
**Watch out for:** Spending all your aggressive cards on a massive combo turn leaves you with weak defenses for the counter-attack. Additionally, Surprise Volley and Rain of Arrows are vulnerable to being canceled if your opponent holds answers.

### Plan C: Control positioning and punish mistakes
**Goal:** Dictate board spacing and punish clustered or poorly positioned enemies.
**How to do it:** Use Stallion Charge (3 copies, value 3, boost 3) to move Yennenga up to 5 spaces, passing through opposing fighters and dealing 1 damage to each fighter she moves through. This is devastating against heroes with 1-health sidekicks like Medusa's Harpies or Robin Hood's Outlaws. For defense, Jaws of the Beast (3 copies, value 3, boost 3) increases its value by +1 for each zone the opposing fighter is in, turning it into a 4, 5, or 6 value card. Pin the Prey (2 copies, value 1, boost 2) moves an opposing fighter up to 4 spaces and forces a card discard, disrupting their positioning and hand size.
**Watch out for:** Stallion Charge deals 1 damage after moving through fighters, but moving back and forth through the same fighter does not stack the damage.

### How they lose
- They face area-of-effect damage that clears the Archers and deals unmitigated damage to Yennenga.
- They run low on cards, as the deck features no card draw outside of One With The Land.
- Opponents exploit the complete lack of Feints or cancel effects in her deck, executing powerful card effects without interference.
- Melee fighters successfully pin Yennenga away from her Archers, breaking her special ability.

### Key cards
- **Rain of Arrows**: a 3-value attack that triggers a second 3-value attack, breaking down opponent card advantage.
- **Stallion Charge**: a versatile card that provides up to 5 spaces of movement through enemies and deals 1 damage to each.
- **Master of the Hunt**: a scheme that grants 2 actions to set up multi-attack turns.
- **Shield Formation**: a 3-value defense that forces the opponent to discard a card or let you return a defeated Archer.
- **One With The Land**: a scheme that moves fighters, heals each for 1, and draws a card.
- **Surprise Volley**: an attack that revives a defeated Archer or grants an extra action.

### Tips
- Position Yennenga in front of your Archers against melee opponents so you can choose when and where damage is distributed.
- Save Master of the Hunt until later in your turn after seeing how your opponent responds to your first action.
- Use Shield Formation when your opponent has a small hand size, making it harder for them to choose to discard.
- Remember that Divide and Conquer (Archer versatile, value 2, boost 1) becomes a 4 when the Archer is outside Yennenga's zone, giving you an incentive to separate them situationally.
- Mulligan for Skirmish (2 copies, value 4, boost 2) when playing second against fast combo fighters like Tesla to avoid heavy early damage.
