## The Genie

**In short:** A ranged solo fighter who treats cards as a disposable resource, burning through his deck to fuel extra actions, devastating wish turns, and targeted hand disruption. 

### Plan A: The wish combo
**Goal:** Assemble all three copies of Three Wishes to empty the opponent's hand and strike for massive unblocked damage.
**How to do it:** Play defensively or use card draw options to collect your schemes and keep a high hand size. When the moment is right, use your special ability to gain extra actions, play Three Wishes to force your opponent to discard two cards repeatedly until their hand is stripped bare, and then follow up with high-value attacks. Because their hand is gone, they cannot defend. Use I've Made Sultans Out of Less on defense earlier to peek at their hand and rob them of their best defensive card before you execute your turn. 
**Watch out for:** If you fail to finish the opponent during this all-in turn, you will be left with an empty deck and few resources, forcing you into a difficult fatigue race.

### Plan B: Aggressive pressure
**Goal:** Overwhelm the opponent with constant attacks and wear them down through card advantage.
**How to do it:** Use your special ability at the start of your turn, discarding a card to gain an extra action, ensuring you can attack multiple times per turn. Draw extra cards with Wishing For More Wishes or the draw effect on Three Wishes to maintain a large hand size. Force the opponent into a defensive retreat where they are constantly forced to maneuver and burn through their blocks. Use I Am Freed to teleport across the board, catch up to fleeing opponents, and deal adjacency damage to small sidekicks.
**Watch out for:** Reckless drawing will rapidly deplete your 30-card deck and trigger exhaustion damage much sooner than expected.

### Plan C: Value and fatigue
**Goal:** Outlast opponents with high health pools or heavy card draw by systematically stripping their options.
**How to do it:** Play a patient game where you use Three Wishes to force discards in the mid-to-late game once the opponent's deck is already thinning. Combine this with I've Made Sultans Out of Less to target key cards in their hand, effectively reducing their active deck size while keeping your own card count balanced. Back In The Lamp lets you recover health immediately to survive long, grindy matchups against resilient heroes.
**Watch out for:** Heroes with massive health pools or extreme aggression can punish you before your fatigue plan takes full effect.

### How they lose
* They reach exhaustion quickly due to aggressive card drawing and heavy cycling, taking fatal damage from empty-deck draws.
* Their defensive options are weak and rely on low values or conditional triggers, leaving them vulnerable to big hitters and heavy retaliations.
* Opponents who maintain high hand sizes can weather the discard effects and retain enough defenses to survive your offensive push.
* Area damage and characters who ignore positioning can pin them down, since they lack reliable defensive movement.

### Key cards
* **Three Wishes**: The central engine of the deck. Grants an action and lets you draw cards, set all card values to four for the turn, or force opponent discards.
* **Back In The Lamp**: A defense card that immediately recovers four health, functioning like an uncancelable heal before combat damage resolves.
* **I've Made Sultans Out of Less**: A versatile card that lets you inspect the opponent's hand and force a discard, disrupting their planned defenses.
* **Wishing For More Wishes**: A versatile card that gives your opponent one card while letting you draw three, keeping your hand full for future actions.
* **Prisoner's Torment**: A defense card that draws you cards equal to the combat damage you took, turning enemy attacks into reloading ammunition.

### Tips
* Keep your hand size large so you can comfortably pay the card cost for your special ability without running out of defensive options.
* Use This is No Parlor Trick carefully on attack or pair it with the value-setting wish, as using it blindly on defense can backfire if the opponent's card value scales after combat.
* Treat Back In The Lamp as a proactive heal rather than a strong block, timing it when you have taken enough damage to fully utilize the recovery without wasting excess healing capacity.
* Use I Am Freed to clear out pesky one-health sidekicks without wasting valuable attack resources.
* Do not telegraph your massive wish turns too early; keep the threat of a hand-stripping combo hanging over your opponent to make them play sub-optimally.
