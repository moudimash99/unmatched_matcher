## Leonardo

**In short:** A hybrid melee fighter with Master Splinter as his sidekick. He uses his start-of-turn movement ability and positional cards to control the board, farm auto-damage, and execute straightforward combat exchanges.

### Plan A: Control the zones and deal auto-damage
**Goal:** Out-position the opponent to trigger auto-damage and deny them map advantages.
**How to do it:** Use Leonardo's ability at the start of your turn to move any fighter one space, setting up board control and forcing opponents out of key multi-zone spaces or item locations. Position Leonardo and Splinter in more zones than the opposing fighter to trigger Wise beyond his years and Spatial awareness, dealing guaranteed auto-damage. Eat, sleep, and breathe ninjutsu lets you break up formations or reposition fighters after combat. Use the map to your advantage, especially on boards with single paths or secret passages where your ability prevents opponents from blocking choke points.
**Watch out for:** Discards are tight; you need to manage your resources carefully between boosting and paying for effects. 

### Plan B: Mix high values with tactical mind games
**Goal:** Land heavy attacks while baiting out enemy cancels.
**How to do it:** Threaten the board with Katana and your boosted Turtle power! attacks. Use Wise beyond his years or Fearless leader as bait to draw out enemy Feints or cancels before committing your biggest hits. If opponents try to counter your setup, I have a plan cancels their effects and ignores their value, though it requires discarding a card. Protective father allows Leonardo and Splinter to swap spaces and recover health while gaining an action, keeping your health pool high.
**Watch out for:** Over-relying on Leonardo leads in 1v1 matches; its movement is underwhelming in a duel, so it is often better used as a 4-boost card for Turtle power! or maneuvers.

### How they lose
- Discard-heavy opponents burn through your resources, making the heavy discard costs of cards like I have a plan and For sensei unsustainable.
- Ranged fighters who stay out of reach and avoid ending up in fewer zones make your auto-damage cards difficult to trigger.
- Fast or aggressive fighters overwhelm your limited high-value defenses (Heroes in a half shell) if you misposition or run out of cards.

### Key cards
- **Katana**: a blank 6-value attack with no text, excellent for punishing whiffs or low defenses.
- **Turtle power!**: a 3-value attack that you may boost, turning it into a heavy hit.
- **Wise beyond his years**: a 2-value versatile that deals 2 auto-damage if Leonardo is in more zones than the opponent.
- **I have a plan**: a 2-value versatile that cancels opponent effects and ignores their value, at the cost of discarding a card.
- **Eat, sleep, and breathe ninjutsu**: a 3-value versatile that repositions adjacent fighters after combat to disrupt enemy placement.

### Tips
- Always check zone counts before ending your turn to ensure Wise beyond his years or Spatial awareness can trigger.
- Save Leonardo leads primarily as a 4-value boost for Turtle power! rather than wasting it as a 1v1 movement scheme.
- Coordinate Splinter and Leonardo closely; Splinter's 9 health and healing cards provide crucial sustainability for the team.
- Remember that For sensei grants an action if you discard a card, but watch your total card count to avoid exhausting too quickly.
