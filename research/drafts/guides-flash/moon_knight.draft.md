## Moon Knight

**In short:** A solo melee burst fighter with 16 health and three rotating identities—Moon Knight, Khonshu, and Mr. Knight—that cycle at the end of every turn. He relies on high mobility to set up devastating Khonshu attacks and a strong defensive transition into Mr. Knight.

### Plan A: Set up the Khonshu strike
**Goal:** Deliver massive burst damage while bypassing non-combat and fatigue damage.
**How to do it:** Start your turn as Moon Knight, utilizing his free 2-space movement at the start of the turn to close distance. Combine this with the scheme Travelers of the Night (3-boost, move up to 4 spaces through enemies and gain an action) to maneuver deep into the enemy's sidekick line and reach their hero. Attack as Khonshu, whose ability adds +2 to all attack values. Use That's Why I Always Win (3-value attack, 3 copies) and boost it with a 3-boost card to hit for 8. Khonshu also ignores all damage from effects outside of combat, meaning you take zero damage from your own self-damaging effects or exhaustion.
**Watch out for:** Your opponent knows the fixed rotation and will try to run away or force awkward attacks during your Moon Knight or Mr. Knight turns to waste your power window.

### Plan B: Control with identity manipulation and hand disruption
**Goal:** Disrupt the opponent's deck and bypass the rigid identity cycle when needed.
**How to do it:** Use I'm Not Real (4-value attack, 3 copies) to change to your next identity after combat, letting you skip forms or trigger Khonshu early for an unexpected second strike. As Khonshu, play That's The Part I Like (3-value versatile, 2 copies); if you win the combat, look at the top 3 cards of the opponent's deck, discard one to accelerate exhaustion, and put the rest back in any order for future knowledge. Defend with Past and Present Intermingle (2-value versatile, 3 copies), adding the boost value of the top card of your discard pile to its value, giving Moon Knight reliable high-value defense.
**Watch out for:** Relying on Let Your Insanity Guide You (1-value versatile, 2 copies) is risky, as its effect forces you to discard the top card of your deck to draw cards equal to its boost value, burning through your deck too quickly.

### How they lose
- Fast, pressure-heavy fighters (Bruce Lee, Bullseye, T-Rex) exploit your lack of sidekicks and pin you down during vulnerable turns.
- Ranged meta-threats and fighters with strong defensive cancels (Sherlock, Elektra) can blunt your Khonshu burst.
- Getting caught in the wrong identity without movement or attack cards leaves your turn stalled.

### Key cards
- **Travelers of the Night**: A 3-boost scheme that moves you 4 spaces through opposing fighters and grants an action, enabling explosive double-attack setups as Khonshu.
- **That's Why I Always Win**: A 3-value attack that can be boosted during combat, reaching up to 8 damage as Khonshu.
- **That's The Part I Like**: A 3-value versatile that lets you view the top 3 cards of the opponent's deck and discard one after winning combat.
- **Past and Present Intermingle**: A 2-value versatile that adds the top discard's boost value to its total, functioning as a sturdy block or attack.
- **I'm Not Real**: A 4-value attack that lets you change to your next identity after combat to manipulate your rhythm.

### Tips
- Keep track of your discard pile order so you know exactly what bonus Past and Present Intermingle will receive.
- Use Madness Will Keep You Alive (3-boost scheme, 2 copies) to recover 2 health and gain an action if you started your turn in a different space, effectively raising your health pool to 20.
- Do not fear ending your turn adjacent to dangerous threats like Dracula or Medusa when playing as Moon Knight, because you will immediately shift to Khonshu after combat or to Mr. Knight at the end of the turn.
- Treat Let Your Insanity Guide You primarily as boost fodder rather than a card to play in combat.
