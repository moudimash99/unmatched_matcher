## Donatello

**In short:** A strategic specialist who uses his unique maneuvering ability to curate his hand, sets up three permanent inventions to boost his stats and actions, and deals heavy damage through recursion and auto-damage.

### Plan A: Build the engine and go aggro
**Goal:** Stall until you get Electro Grenade into play, then shift from tempo to aggressive pressure with enhanced attacks.
**How to do it:** While waiting for Electro Grenade, keep subpar cards like Quick Strike and Shift Focus in hand to use as removal fuel, and protect Metalhead. Use your special ability on every maneuver to draw two and tuck a card at the bottom of the deck. Once Electro Grenade is active, your attacks and Turtle Power! hit harder. Stuff your high boost cards at the bottom of the deck to prepare for Metalhead's Untested Enhancements.
**Watch out for:** Rushing into combat before your inventions are active leaves you with weak 2- and 3-value attacks that struggle to win combats.

### Plan B: Unleash auto-damage with Party Wagon
**Goal:** Grind down opponents without relying solely on winning combats.
**How to do it:** Use Party Wagon to move a friendly fighter up to 5 spaces through an opponent and deal 2 direct damage. Because Donatello can recur cards, you can bring Party Wagon back to your hand using Donatello Does Machines or shuffle it back into the deck with Thinking Ahead to use it multiple times. 
**Watch out for:** Party Wagon goes to the bottom of the deck when recurred with Thinking Ahead, so it takes time to cycle back to your hand.

### How they lose
- Metalhead dies early, stripping Donatello of a key damage source for Untested Enhancements and a vital body blocker.
- Opponents counter your after-combat effects with cancels, though Short Circuit lets you cancel theirs.
- Heavy card-burning without managing your deck leads to running low on resources, though your massive card draw makes exhaustion a danger if you are not careful.
- Opponents with high-value defenses shut down Thinking Ahead, stopping your recursion.

### Key cards
- **Electro grenade**: Donatello's primary attack invention. Removes up to 4 attack or versatile cards from your discard pile to add +1 to its value, and permanently grants +1 to the value of all your attacks.
- **Self-defense grid**: Removes up to 4 defense or versatile cards from your discard pile to recover health, and permanently adds +1 to the value of your defenses.
- **Party wagon**: A scheme that moves a friendly fighter up to 5 spaces through opposing fighters and deals 2 damage to one of them.
- **Donatello does machines**: Returns an attack, versatile, or defense card from your discard pile to your hand and lets you draw a card.
- **Untested enhancements**: A Metalhead versatile attack that discards the bottom card of your deck and adds its boost value to the attack.

### Tips
- Always use your maneuver ability to draw two and place a card on the bottom of your deck; it keeps your hand consistent and lets you curate what you need.
- Play Smoke Bomb as a pass or save it for a massive action-gain turn later when you have plenty of schemes in the discard pile.
- Use Bo Staff defensively to deal 1 chip damage to an adjacent fighter and draw a card.
- Protect Metalhead from early damage so he remains a threat with Untested Enhancements.
