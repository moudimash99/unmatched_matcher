## Deadpool

**In short:** A solo melee fighter wielding a unique 30-card highlander deck. He manages his fragile 10-health pool through consistent post-attack healing, explosive damage scaling, and late-game resource looping, though he struggles with card inconsistency and limited defensive options.

### Plan A: Out-heal the damage with constant pressure
**Goal:** Stay alive by attacking on every turn to activate your healing factor while wearing down opponents.
**How to do it:** Your special ability restores 1 health after every attack. Lean into this by keeping up offensive momentum to stabilize your health pool. Supplement this with healing from cards like Gimme Gimme Chimichanga, Non-Retinal Scan Access to Danger Room, and They Have An Amazing Buffet, alongside schemes like Faint. Because your maximum health is strictly capped at 10, consistent chip healing is your primary safeguard against heavy hitters.
**Watch out for:** Over-aggression without proper shielding. You only possess 15 cards capable of blocking, and limited card draw means heavy maneuvering leaves you vulnerable. If you run out of defensive options against an opponent with multi-attack capabilities, your low health pool causes you to collapse instantly.

### Plan B: Fish early, recycle late
**Goal:** Overcome highlander deck randomness by tutoring key options early and delaying exhaustion late.
**How to do it:** Because every card in your deck is unique, drawing specific tools on demand is difficult. Use Time out time out time out! early while your deck is fully stocked to search for a playable card immediately. Conversely, save Dumpster Divin' Deadpool for the late game; playing it shuffles five cards from your discard pile back into your deck and recovers 1 health, heavily delaying exhaustion while recycling your strongest assets.
**Watch out for:** Hand disruption effects. Opponent abilities or cards like Passwords can target Dumpster Divin' Deadpool while it sits in your hand, dismantling your late-game recovery plan.

### Plan C: Coordinate burst damage and hand peeking
**Goal:** Punish the opponent with massive attack values and targeted hand disruption.
**How to do it:** Combine your 1-value attack Cha-Ching! with a heavy boost card like Push to Teleport to scale your output up to 7. Use Non-Retinal Scan Access to Danger Room to inspect the opposing hand, then immediately follow up with Holy Mackerel! to name a card, forcing your opponent to discard all copies of it. 
**Watch out for:** Feints. Boosting an attack into a Feint wastes your boost card entirely, and disrupted hand-peeking setups will leave your high-cost schemes whiffing.

### How they lose
- Low maximum health (10) leaves zero margin for error once defenses are depleted.
- Highlander deck inconsistency means essential blocks or attacks can be buried at the bottom of the deck.
- Getting pinned by fighters with sidekicks, as your move value of 2 and solo melee profile provide poor board mobility and flexibility.
- Running out of cards due to poor draw support, leading to fatal exhaustion damage.
- Hand disruption stripping your critical defensive cards before you can deploy them.
- High-boost cards like Push to Teleport triggering massive damage vulnerabilities against cards like Sherlock Holmes's Confirmed Suspicion.

### Key cards
- **Super Feint**: A versatile card that cancels opponent effects, draws a card, and cannot be canceled.
- **Dumpster Divin' Deadpool**: A scheme that shuffles five discarded cards back into your deck and heals 1 health.
- **Time out time out time out!**: A versatile card that searches your deck for an immediate playable option.
- **Gaze of Stone**: An attack that deals 8 extra damage if you win the combat.
- **Passwords**: A heavy-value defense that forces you to let the opponent choose a card from your hand to discard.

### Tips
- Never play They Have An Amazing Buffet when you are at 8 or more health, as the post-heal damage penalty will waste your life total.
- Remember that your healing ability triggers after combat is fully resolved, meaning after-combat effects can still defeat you before the heal applies.
- Watch out for opposing characters named Logan, as your special ability grants all your attacks +5 against them.
- Avoid holding high-boost cards like Push to Teleport if you suspect an opponent has an ambush effect that punishes high boost values.
- Avoid wearing pants during the match—while it confers no mechanical advantage, it adds to the psychological warfare.
