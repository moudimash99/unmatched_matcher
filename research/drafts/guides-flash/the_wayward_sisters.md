## The Wayward Sisters

**In short:** A melee team of three 6-health heroes who control the board by pinning opponents and using their cauldron to fuel versatile spells. They manage ingredients on every attack to heal, position enemies, or deal direct damage.

### Plan A: Control the board and pin the enemy
**Goal:** Surround opposing fighters with the three sisters to restrict movement, lock down key areas, and force unfavorable combats.
**How to do it:** Spread the sisters across adjacent spaces to create a wall. Because any sister can use any card in the deck, every body on the board is equally dangerous. Use Ward to place the opposing fighter in your fighter's zone, shutting down escape routes. Position to maximize The Stars Align early while all three sisters are alive.
**Watch out for:** Area damage and multi-target effects can catch multiple sisters at once, quickly wearing down their low individual health pools.

### Plan B: Brew spells for pressure and recovery
**Goal:** Build ingredients in the cauldron to cast powerful spells after attacks and maintain momentum.
**How to do it:** Cards sent to the cauldron provide symbols (bat, snake, and lizard leg) that unlock four distinct spells after you attack. Combine a snake and bat to draw a card and recover health, or pair a snake and lizard leg to move any fighter up to three spaces. All-Seeing Familiar and Fire Burn And Cauldron Bubble return a card from your discard pile straight into the cauldron to set up spells quickly. Save Something Wicked This Way Comes to cast two different spells without discarding your cauldron ingredients.
**Watch out for:** Casting a spell discards every card currently in the cauldron, so letting too many build up can waste potential ingredients.

### How they lose
- Area damage effects and attacks targeting multiple fighters punish their low health pools.
- Opponents who apply relentless pressure can overwhelm the sisters before they set up their cauldron combos.
- Losing even one sister significantly diminishes their board control and reduces the maximum value of The Stars Align.
- Entering exhaustion is deadly because all three sisters take exhaustion damage simultaneously.

### Key cards
- **Pricking Of My Thumbs**: a defense that deals damage to the attacker equal to the combat damage you took, punishing big hits.
- **Something Wicked This Way Comes**: a scheme that lets you cast any two different spells for free without clearing your cauldron.
- **All-Seeing Familiar**: an attack that returns a card from your discard pile to the cauldron and lets you look at your top deck card if you win.
- **Ward**: a versatile defense that places the opposing fighter in any space within your fighter's zone after combat.
- **The Stars Align**: a versatile whose attack or defense value scales with the number of different zones your fighters occupy.

### Tips
- Keep track of the ingredients in your cauldron so you know which spells are available after an attack.
- Use Unnatural Remedy to discard up to three cards from your cauldron and heal any fighter, including teammates in team modes or opponents if needed.
- Use Curious Familiar to return a card from your cauldron to your hand instead of losing it to a spell discard.
- Avoid letting the game drag into exhaustion, as your multi-hero team will burn through health rapidly.
