## She-Hulk

**In short:** A resilient, high-health melee bruiser who converts cards into unblockable ability damage and pressures the opponent into fatigue while setting up devastating single-card attacks.

### Plan A: Control the zone and pressure with Just Throw Something
**Goal:** Deal steady unblockable damage from range and whittle down your opponent without spending actions.
**How to do it:** At the start of your turn, discard a card to deal damage equal to its boost value to any fighter in your zone. Force opponents to come to you by staying back, or let them approach while you manage your hand. When they try to run or kite you, use **Leap Toward** to close up to four spaces through enemies and gain an action, or **Jennifer Walters, Esq.** to warp to a starting space, draw a card, and gain an action. If they end their turn adjacent, use **Nerve Cluster Strike** immediately to trap them in place so they cannot escape your zone.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who stay out of your zone or use hard zone cutoffs can completely shut down your ability, forcing you to chase or risk running low on cards.

### Plan B: The big finishers
**Goal:** Crack open the opponent's defenses with **Omega-Level Threat** and **The Savage She-Hulk**.
**How to do it:** 
- **Omega-Level Threat** is a 5-value attack. If you deal 2 or more damage—easily done against typical 3-value defenses—your opponent discards 2 random cards, devastating their hand.
- **The Savage She-Hulk** is a 3-value attack that allows you to spend an additional action during combat to make its value a massive 9. Use **Leap Toward** or **Jennifer Walters, Esq.** first to gain an extra action, ensuring you can pull off the 9-value strike. 
- Use **Sensational** (value 4) to win combat and shuffle your best attacks or movement cards from your discard pile back into your deck.

**Watch out for:** Feints cancel the extra action effect on The Savage She-Hulk or waste your Omega-Level Threat's discard effect, so try to bait out cancels with smaller attacks or versatile cards first.

### How they lose
- Highly mobile ranged fighters and elusive opponents (like Darkblade, Sherlock Holmes, or Golden Bat) can kite her and run her out of cards.
- Opponents who manage their positioning to avoid her zone completely negate her special ability.
- High-value defensive cancels and heavy aggressive pressure can overwhelm her lower-value defense cards.

### Key cards
- **The Savage She-Hulk**: A 3-value attack that can jump to 9 if you spend an extra action.
- **Omega-Level Threat**: A 5-value attack that forces 2 random discards if you deal at least 2 damage.
- **Lady Justice**: A 0-value defense that discards the top card of the opponent's deck and increases its value by twice that card's boost value.
- **Nerve Cluster Strike**: A 3-value versatile that immediately traps an adjacent opponent in their space for the turn.
- **Leap Toward**: A scheme that moves her up to four spaces through enemies and grants an action if she ends adjacent to a fighter.

### Tips
- Use **The Defense Rests** when facing big attacks to draw cards equal to the combat damage you took, though be mindful of the exhaustion clock.
- **Cease and Desist** acts as a 4-value attack that cancels effects, making it a great surprise offensive tool, but it only defends for 1.
- **Green Energy** is a reliable 4-value versatile block that deals 1 damage to adjacent fighters if you win combat.
- **Double Jeopardy** lets you draw 2 cards and recover 2 health, pushing her effective health pool even higher, but watch your card count against fatigue matchups.
