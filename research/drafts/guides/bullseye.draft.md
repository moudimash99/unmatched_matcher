## Bullseye

**In short:** An aggressive, fragile ranged fighter who hits from up to 5 spaces away. He wants to attack twice in a turn, winning the first combat to trigger massive effects on his second attack, and relies on hit-and-run movement rather than high blocks to stay alive.

### Plan A: Chain the combo

**Goal:** Win a combat on your first action to unlock devastating effects for your subsequent actions.
**How to do it:** His highest-damage cards and best effects only work if you have already won a combat this turn. You need a reliable "starter" for your first attack. I Never Miss is his best starter because its value of 3 naturally beats a standard 2-value Feint, guaranteeing the win. You can also boost it to beat higher blocks. Arrogant But Effective works as a starter too: its effect means you are considered to have won the combat, even if you take damage.

Once you win that first combat, play an extender or a finisher. Study The Target (2 copies) is your engine. If you play it after winning a combat, it draws 3 cards and gives your action back. With your hand full and an action to spare, drop a finisher: World's Greatest Assassin ignores their defense value entirely, and I'm Better And I'll Prove It hits for 6.
**Watch out for:** Feints ruin this sequence. If your starter attack gets canceled (such as a Feint against the 2-value I Planned To Be Here), you lose the combat, and your finishers sit dead in your hand. Bait out defenses with For My Next Trick before you commit your important cards.

### Plan B: Snipe and flee

**Goal:** Force opponents to waste cards to reach you, then teleport away when they do.
**How to do it:** Bullseye ignores zones and attacks anyone within 5 spaces. Stand exactly 5 spaces away from a melee opponent. Fighters with 2 movement must boost just to get adjacent. When they finally reach you and attack, do not try to block big hits on value—his printed defenses max out at 3. Instead, use Tactical Retreat to place him in a space that shares no zones with his current one. This breaks their turn and ruins any chance they had at a double attack, forcing them to start the chase all over again. Master Strategist also escapes pins by moving him 4 spaces, and it lets you pass through opposing fighters.
**Watch out for:** Master Strategist requires you to move *exactly* 4 spaces. On tight maps, or if you get backed into a corner, you might struggle to find a legal spot to land.

### Plan C: The discard trap

**Goal:** Punish opponents who try to attack you twice in one turn.
**How to do it:** If an opponent starts their turn adjacent to you and has a small hand, you can drain their options. Defend their first attack with Arrogant But Effective. You will likely take damage through its low value, but its effect ensures you "won" the combat. When they attack a second time, block with Right Between The Eyes. Because you already won a combat, they immediately discard a card. They spent two cards to attack you and lost a third to the discard.
**Watch out for:** The sources heavily disagree on Right Between The Eyes. While some recommend this defensive trap to destroy the opponent's hand, another calls the card a trap for Bullseye himself, arguing that you take too much damage setting it up and that it is better used as a blank 3-value block or a 3-value boost for I Never Miss.

### How they lose

​- He only has 14 health, no healing, and his highest blocks are 3s. If a heavy hitter like Bigfoot or Bruce Lee corners him, he dies quickly.
​- He requires double-attack turns to function. Opponents who constantly force him to maneuver leave him with only one action, preventing his combos.
​- Feints shut down his combo starters and his action gains.
​- He runs through his deck rapidly with I Never Miss and Study The Target. He cannot play for exhaustion, because he will run out of cards first.
​- In 2v2 games, he gets pinned far more easily and has to fight through two decks' worth of Feints.

### Key cards

​- **Study The Target**: his only scheme and his best card. Play it strictly after winning a combat to draw 3 and gain an action. Always mulligan for it.
​- **I Never Miss**: his safest combo starter. It beats 2-value cancels, and you can boost it to beat large blocks. If winning combat isn't vital that turn, choose not to boost and draw a card instead.
​- **World's Greatest Assassin**: the ultimate combo finisher. It ignores the opponent's defense value completely if you already won a combat.
​- **Tactical Retreat**: his best defense against double attacks. It teleports you completely out of range after the first hit.
​- **Arrogant But Effective**: a 2-value versatile that guarantees you win the combat. Use it on your first attack to set up your finishers.

### Tips

​- Take turns where you either maneuver twice or attack twice. Maneuvering and then attacking limits you to a single strike, which wastes his combo potential.
​- Ricochet deals 1 damage to "a fighter" in the opposing fighter's zone. Use this against heroes with 1-health sidekicks (like Dracula's Sisters or Sun Wukong's Clones): attack the hero, then bounce the 1 damage onto the sidekick to kill them for free.
​- His 5-space range works against ranged fighters too. You can stand outside of their zone and shoot them, forcing them to spend an action moving before they can shoot back.
​- Don't telegraph your combo starters. For My Next Trick gives you an action back, making it an excellent first attack to bait out the opponent's Feints before you play I Never Miss.
​- Your boost values are excellent. Ten of your cards boost for 3, which can turn I Never Miss into a 6-value attack.
