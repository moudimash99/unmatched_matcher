## Willow

**In short:** A ranged duo that balances high-powered attacks with the chaotic effects of her Dark Willow form. She fights from a distance and uses her sidekick Tara to control her transformations and recover lost cards.

### Plan A: Manage the Dark Willow form

**Goal:** Unleash her strongest effects without destroying your own deck.
**How to do it:** When Willow or Tara is dealt damage, Willow becomes Dark Willow. Her exclusive cards gain powerful but costly effects in this form. *Flayed Alive* (a 4-value attack) and *Black Magic* (a 3-value versatile) force you to blind boost during combat, making them hit hard. *When Good Magic Fails* allows you to teleport to any space in her zone, but you must discard the top card of your deck. To turn back into regular Willow, end your turn with Dark Willow adjacent to Tara, or play *Meditation* (a 5-value defense) which changes her back immediately.
**Watch out for:** The sources strongly disagree on pacing this form. One argues you should minimize your time as Dark Willow because her forced discards and blind boosts throw away important cards. The other suggests staying Dark Willow to hit fast and aggressively before your deck runs out, since regular Willow's cards lack effects entirely and she cannot stall a game out like fighters such as Sherlock or Invisible Man.

### Plan B: Attack and step away

**Goal:** Deal damage from range while keeping enemies at a distance.
**How to do it:** Since both Willow and Tara are ranged fighters, they can poke the opponent from afar. Put Tara between Willow and the enemy to act as a buffer. After attacking, use *Swift Strike* to move your fighter up to 4 spaces, forcing the opponent to spend cards or double-maneuver to reach you. *Rending Shot* lets you push the opposing fighter up to 3 spaces away, ruining their position and preventing them from making a second attack on their turn.
**Watch out for:** Both heroes only have a movement speed of 2. If the opponent closes the distance, it is very difficult for this duo to escape and regain their optimal ranged positioning.

### Plan C: Maximize Tara's utility

**Goal:** Use the sidekick to disrupt the opponent and retrieve discarded cards.
**How to do it:** Tara has exclusive tools that keep the team running. *Revoke* is a 3-value versatile that cancels abilities on the opponent's card—due to early terminology in the Buffy set, this functions exactly like canceling effects, giving your team five total cancels. *Knowledge of the Craft* (a 4-value versatile) lets you pull any card from your discard pile back into your hand if you win the combat. This is perfect for recovering a crucial card lost to a Dark Willow blind boost. Tara will inevitably take hits while shielding Willow, but you can let her fall and play the *Resurrect* scheme to place her back on the board with 3 health, effectively giving the sidekick up to 12 total health for the match.
**Watch out for:** If you burn through your deck recklessly as Dark Willow, you risk blindly discarding *Resurrect*, leaving Tara permanently defeated.

### How they lose

- She exhausts herself. Staying in the Dark Willow form means constantly blind boosting and discarding, which puts her on a very fast clock to fatigue.
- She loses her sidekick early. Without Tara, Willow is stripped of her 3-value cancel and her easiest built-in method to revert to regular Willow.
- *Love and Loss* backfires. When played as Dark Willow, this scheme draws two cards but forces you to discard the top two cards of your deck and deal 3 damage to a sidekick in your zone. If Tara is the only sidekick around, you severely injure your own partner while throwing away deck resources.
- She gets overwhelmed in solo matches. Her low mobility puts her at a distinct disadvantage against aggressive foes in one-on-one games where she cannot easily retreat.

### Key cards

- **Flayed Alive**: Her primary source of raw damage, hitting much harder once she transforms.
- **Meditation**: Her most reliable block, providing excellent defense while acting as an emergency eject button out of the Dark Willow form.
- **Knowledge of the Craft**: Crucial insurance for Tara to fetch back the exact tools lost to random discards.
- **Resurrect**: Gives Tara a second wind to continue shielding Willow and utilizing her exclusive cards.
- **Love and Loss**: A scheme that the sources debate using while transformed—one argues the forced discards and friendly fire are never worth it, while another notes you could theoretically use it to bomb an enemy sidekick in your zone if you accept the cost.

### Tips

- Play the *Hacker* versatile before attacking as Dark Willow. Checking the top card of your deck and choosing whether to keep or bury it lets you control exactly what you are about to blind boost or discard.
- Let Tara take the lead. When she takes a hit, it automatically triggers Willow's transformation, setting you up to launch powerful Dark Willow counterattacks on your next turn.
- In 2v2 team games, *Resurrect* becomes even more potent, as it can target any defeated friendly fighter, allowing you to bring an allied hero or sidekick back into the match.
