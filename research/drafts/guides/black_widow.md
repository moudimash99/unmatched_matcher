## Black Widow

**In short:** A tricky double-ranged team who dictate the flow of the game through unique "mission" schemes. She drains the opponent's hand, deals unavoidable damage across the board, and recycles her best tricks with Maria Hill.

### Plan A: Drain their hand and outlast them

**Goal:** Leave the opponent with few cards to defend with, or win the game through exhaustion.
**How to do it:** *The Kinshasa Directive* forces the opponent to discard two cards, provided Black Widow is in your starting space. Because you start the game there, this is the perfect opening move to cripple their hand immediately. Follow up with *Widow's Bite*, a 4-value attack that strips another card if you win the combat. You can reuse this pressure using Maria Hill's *Acting Director of S.H.I.E.L.D.*, which shuffles a completed scheme from your discard pile back into your deck. Repeated early, this leaves the opponent with no defenses for your attacks. If an aggressive fighter empties your hand instead, play *The Budapest Gambit* to draw five cards and force a random discard, instantly flipping the hand advantage.
**Watch out for:** If you plan to win by exhaustion, be careful when deciding to recycle *The Budapest Gambit*. Playing it repeatedly forces massive card draw, pushing you dangerously close to fatigue.

### Plan B: Auto-damage through positioning

**Goal:** Bypass their blocks by completing damage-dealing missions.
**How to do it:** Against fighters with low-health sidekicks (like Robin Hood or Squirrel Girl), your scheme damage is devastating. *The Madripoor Sanction* deals 2 damage to all of the opponent's fighters anywhere on the map, but Widow must be standing in their starting space to play it. *The Firenze Agenda* deals 2 damage to every enemy in Widow's current zone, but requires her to be adjacent to their hero. To get her into these dangerous spots safely, attack with *Widow's Sting*, using its after-combat effect to move her up to three spaces, or play *Double Identity* to suddenly swap Widow onto the space Maria is holding.
**Watch out for:** Smart opponents will leave a fighter on their starting space to block you from playing *The Madripoor Sanction*. Additionally, hard zone borders protect enemies from *The Firenze Agenda*, because it only strikes fighters sharing Widow's exact zone.

### Plan C: Protect Maria Hill

**Goal:** Keep Maria alive to get maximum uses out of *Acting Director of S.H.I.E.L.D.*
**How to do it:** Maria is not a typical sidekick you throw away to absorb damage. Her *Acting Director of S.H.I.E.L.D.* is your only way to recycle missions. Block for her with *Double Identity*, which lets you swap Widow and Maria after you see the opponent's attack, changing the defender to protect Maria. If she is caught, defend with *Life Model Decoy*. It provides no block value, but immediately resurrects a defeated Maria next to Widow and sets her health to 3.
**Watch out for:** *Life Model Decoy* is an after-combat effect. If Maria's health is low enough that the opponent's raw attack value will kill her, an offensive *Feint* cancels the resurrection. Furthermore, strong after-combat damage (like Little Red's) can strike Maria right after she revives, killing her permanently.

### How they lose

- Acquiring new missions forces you to reveal cards from your deck until you find one. Observant opponents will track this and know exactly when you are holding no *Feints* or defenses.
- Black Widow has only 13 health and no healing. Fighters with unpreventable damage (like Ghost Rider) chip her down quickly.
- Her printed defenses are mostly 3s. Fast, relentless fighters (like T-Rex) can overwhelm her before she can set up her positioning.
- She lacks massive printed attacks. Her highest value is a 5, so she struggles to quickly defeat high-health heroes if her mission setups are repeatedly interrupted.

### Key cards

- **Acting Director of S.H.I.E.L.D.**: Maria's 4-value versatile. It moves both of your fighters up to three spaces through enemies and shuffles a mission back into your deck. This is your most important card.
- **The Budapest Gambit**: Your only reliable card draw. It creates a massive swing in momentum, but pushes you much closer to exhaustion.
- **Fake Out**: A 1-value attack. If you lose the combat, you draw a card and gain an action. It is the perfect bait to safely waste an opponent's high-value block before you follow up with a real attack.
- **Caught in a Web**: A 3-value versatile that cancels *only* after-combat effects. Save it to specifically deny your opponent's card draw, movement, or auto-damage while saving your standard *Feints* for during-combat abilities.
- **Widow's Kiss**: A 4-value versatile that stops the opposing fighter from moving for the rest of the turn.

### Tips

- Widow's ability increases your starting hand to six cards (including *The Moscow Protocol*), giving you early initiative. Attack on your first action, then play this scheme to draw a card, gain an action, and pull a new mission.
- If an opponent camps their starting space to deny you *The Madripoor Sanction*, defend their attack with *Widow's Line* to push them up to two spaces away and claim the spot on your turn.
- If you have no missions left in your deck, do not search for one when a card tells you to. Simply tell your opponent your deck is empty of missions to avoid revealing your remaining cards.
- Play *Widow's Kiss* on defense. Pinning the opponent in your zone gives you a perfect double-attack opportunity on your next turn.
