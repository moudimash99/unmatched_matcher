## Elektra

**In short:** A fighter with a 20-card deck and two distinct lives. She stalls during her first life to build an exhaustion advantage and sculpt her hand, then resurrects alongside her sidekicks to deliver a devastating combo.

### Plan A: Build an exhaustion advantage

**Goal:** Cycle cards into your discard pile before dying, giving you a larger deck than the opponent after the reshuffle.
**How to do it:** Elektra starts with only 20 cards. During her first life, her cards ignore their printed resurrected effects, so focus heavily on survival. Hide behind your four Hand sidekicks. When the opponent attacks Elektra, play Snakeroot Clan to swap her with a Hand, letting the 1-health sidekick take the hit from a massive attack. Use Hands of Red to bring defeated sidekicks back to the board. The longer she survives and plays cards, the more cards go into her discard pile. When she resurrects, that entire pile shuffles back into her deck. Even though she starts with 10 fewer cards than most fighters, a late first death means she will comfortably outlast almost anyone in an exhaustion race.
**Watch out for:** Opponents know this and will aggressively rush to kill her first life early. If she dies before putting at least 10 cards in her discard pile, her deck advantage disappears and she will likely lose the fatigue war.

### Plan B: The resurrection combo

**Goal:** Hoard specific cards to dismantle the opponent in a single turn after she revives.
**How to do it:** During your first life, hold onto both copies of Mesmerize, one The Fist, and one Mystic Assassin. When Elektra resurrects, you place her and the Hand back on the board in different zones. Spend your movement to surround the opponent. Play Mesmerize to look at their hand, force them to discard a defense, and gain an action. Do it again if you have the second copy. Then, attack with The Fist. You may deal 1 damage to your attacking Hand to return The Fist to your hand and gain an action. Repeat this with every adjacent Hand, pummeling the defenseless opponent, before finishing them off with the 6-value Mystic Assassin.
**Watch out for:** Your hand does not reset when you resurrect. If you die with no cards, you revive with no cards and lose your turn. Conversely, reviving with a full hand of seven means you can only draw 13 more cards before taking exhaustion damage.

### How they lose

- Fighters who easily clear 1-health sidekicks (Ghost Rider, Luke Cage, Golden Bat) wipe out her shields and remove her combo pieces.
- Opponents burst down her first life before her discard pile is full, leaving her with too few cards for her second life.
- Unblockable or non-combat damage (Little Red) triggers her death unexpectedly, often while she holds a large hand.
- The Hand have no cancel cards, so opponents attack them to safely trigger their own powerful combat effects without fear of a Feint.
- Black Panther steals her cards into his suit, preventing them from shuffling back into her deck upon resurrection.

### Key cards

- **Mesmerize**: a scheme that replaces its own action. In her second life, it strips a card from their hand. Essential for setting up the combo.
- **The Fist**: a 3-value attack that kills a Hand to refund its action and return to your hand. One copy fuels multiple attacks.
- **Mystic Assassin**: her biggest attack at 6. In her first life, it deals 3 damage to her; in her second, she takes no damage.
- **Snakeroot Clan**: her best tool for wasting the opponent's strongest attacks. Swap places with a Hand and let them take the hit.
- **Hands of Red**: a 4-value attack that brings back a defeated Hand. Play both copies across both lives to generate up to 12 sidekicks in a single game.

### Tips

- Keep a Sai in your hand during your second life. Just revealing it makes Intercept a 5-value defense, and you don't even have to discard it.
- Ninjitsu is the only card the Hand can use to defend, but a 3-value block rarely saves a 1-health sidekick. Play it on attack instead to bait out an opponent's Feint before you commit to The Fist.
- If the opponent refuses to kill your first life when your combo is ready, attack with Mystic Assassin to deal 3 damage to yourself and force the resurrection on your terms.
- Cloaked In Shadow cancels card effects immediately, and in her second life lets her move 3 spaces after combat. Use this to close the gap against ranged attackers who try to kite you.
- When you resurrect, place your fighters to surround the enemy if you have the combo ready. If you are playing for exhaustion instead, place them far away and use your sidekicks to block pathways.
