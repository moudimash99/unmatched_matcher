## Nikola Tesla

**In short:** A ranged combo fighter who uses a fluid resource called coils to gain extra actions, deal area damage, and cancel enemy cards. He hits incredibly hard when fully charged but has a low health pool and burns through his deck very quickly.

### Plan A: Overwhelm them with extra actions

**Goal:** String multiple attacks together in a single turn so the opponent runs out of defensive cards.
**How to do it:** Tesla relies heavily on extra actions to keep the pressure on. **7 Hertz** (3 copies, value 4) is your main engine for this: discharging one coil after combat grants an additional action. Combine this with **Fully Charged** (2 copies), a scheme that immediately fills both coils and provides an action, letting you burst out of nowhere. Follow up with **The Alternating Current** (2 copies, value 5), which hits hard and gives you the choice to charge both coils right back up. Because his ability automatically charges one coil at the end of every turn, you rarely start a round empty.
**Watch out for:** Combo pieces are prime targets for cancel effects. Fortunately, because his card text says you "may" discharge coils, a canceled card means you simply keep your charge for the next attack.

### Plan B: Control the board and punish approaches

**Goal:** Keep melee fighters away and make them pay for sharing your space.
**How to do it:** Tesla’s ability deals 1 automatic damage to adjacent opposing fighters at the start of your turn if both coils are full, pushing them up to one space away. This breaks up enemy positioning and prevents double attacks. You can set up traps using **Remote Control** (2 copies), a scheme that forces all opposing fighters to move up to 2 spaces. Group them together in your zone, then hit them with **Lightning Storm** (3 copies, value 3). For one coil, it pings every opposing fighter in the zone, effortlessly wiping out 1-health sidekicks; for two coils, it deals 2 damage. Because this is effect damage, it ignores block values entirely. If an attacker rushes you, use **Repulsion Blast** (3 copies, value 2) to shove them away and break their action chain.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who spread their sidekicks out. Lightning Storm only hits targets in your current zone, so direct the attack at the fighter whose deck has the fewest movement or cancel effects. For example, aim at Medusa rather than her Harpies, since the Harpies have more tools to dodge the damage.

### Plan C: Ruin their deck and ignore their attacks

**Goal:** Discard their best cards before they draw them, and safely block their biggest hits.
**How to do it:** Tesla has excellent tools for stripping the opponent of resources. **X-Ray Radiation** (3 copies, value 4) reveals the top card of the opponent's deck during combat. If it is a key card, spending one coil discards it permanently. Spending two coils also adds its boost value to your defense. On defense, **Polyphase Coils** (3 copies, value 3) is a perfect counter to massive attacks. You get to see the opponent's card before deciding how many coils to spend. One coil cancels their effects, and a second coil completely ignores their printed value, reducing massive attacks to zero.
**Watch out for:** You cannot afford to spend coils on every defense. Polyphase Coils is powerful but expensive. If you burn all your charge blocking small pokes, you will lack the fuel for your aggressive action chains on your own turn.

### How they lose

- He hits exhaustion very fast. Aside from his schemes, he has no "passes" (actions that do not draw a card). Playing **Intense Experimentation** (3 copies, value 3) late in the game draws cards and accelerates his fatigue.
- Relentless aggression starves him of coils. If an opponent forces him to spend coils just to survive, he cannot build up a charge for his own turn.
- With only 14 health and most defensive values sitting at 2 or 3, big attacks easily chip him down.
- Hand disruption and random discards hurt him deeply, taking away his key combo pieces before he can string them together.

### Key cards

- **X-Ray Radiation**: His best printed block. It provides critical deck knowledge, removes incoming threats, and can swing combat values wildly.
- **7 Hertz**: His primary combo tool. Discharging a coil for an action keeps the aggressive pressure on.
- **Polyphase Coils**: A perfect defensive card if you have the charge. It lets you blank the opponent's effects and value entirely, but only after you see what they played.
- **Intense Experimentation**: A defense that replaces itself by drawing a card. Discharging two coils lets you draw three cards and heal 1 health. It is great in the early game for building a hand, but a liability late.
- **Remote Control**: Moving all opposing fighters lets you clump them up for area damage or push them away to escape pins.

### Tips

- Mulligan for X-Ray Radiation, Lightning Storm, and especially Intense Experimentation. Early card draw helps you find your combos, but using Intense Experimentation near the end of the deck is a major fatigue risk.
- Look for small maps in a draft. Tesla wants to spend his actions attacking, not boosting just to get into range.
- When using **Kinetic Induction** (3 copies, value 2), aim it at 1-health sidekicks. They often will not defend it, allowing you to win the combat and charge both coils for free.
- Polyphase Coils cannot stop defensive immediate effects like Yennenga's Shield Formation. Those effects resolve before your card can cancel them.
- The sources disagree heavily on how to use **Death Ray** (3 copies, value 3). One argues that spending two coils to boost it to a 7 is a great way to punish an opponent for ending their turn in your zone. Another insists it is better used as bait to draw out cancels, arguing that spending two coils for raw damage is too linear and wasteful.
- Play The Alternating Current early to charge your coils rather than saving it for the 2-health recovery. Keeping the pressure high is more important than a small heal.
