## Nikola Tesla

**In short:** A ranged combo fighter who uses a fluid resource called coils to gain extra actions, deal area damage, and cancel enemy cards. He hits incredibly hard when fully charged but has a low health pool and burns through his deck very quickly.

### Plan A: Overwhelm them with extra actions

**Goal:** String multiple attacks together in a single turn so the opponent runs out of defensive cards.
**How to do it:** Tesla relies heavily on extra actions to keep the pressure on. 7 Hertz (3 copies, value 4) is your main engine for this: discharging one coil after combat gives you an action to keep attacking. Combine this with Fully Charged (2 copies), a scheme that immediately charges both coils and gives you an action, allowing you to burst out of nowhere even if you started your turn empty. Follow up with The Alternating Current (2 copies, value 5), which hits hard and charges both coils right back up for your next attack. Because his ability automatically charges one coil at the end of every turn, you rarely start a round with nothing to spend.
**Watch out for:** Your combo cards are prime targets for Feints. However, because his card effects say you "may" discharge coils, a canceled card means you simply keep your coils for the next attack.

### Plan B: Control the board and punish approaches

**Goal:** Keep melee fighters away and make them pay for sharing your space.
**How to do it:** Tesla’s ability deals 1 automatic damage to any adjacent opposing fighter at the start of your turn if both coils are charged, and then moves them away. This breaks up enemy combos and saves you from double attacks. You can set up your own traps using Remote Control (2 copies), which moves all opposing fighters up to 2 spaces. Group them together in your zone, then hit them with Lightning Storm (3 copies, value 3). For one coil, it deals 1 damage to every opposing fighter in the zone, wiping out 1-health sidekicks effortlessly; for two coils, it deals 2 damage. If an attacker rushes you, use Repulsion Blast (3 copies, value 2) to push them away and break their action chain.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who spread their sidekicks into different zones. Lightning Storm only hits fighters in your current zone, so target the fighter whose deck has the fewest movement effects or cancels. For example, aim at Medusa rather than her Harpies, as the Harpies have more cards that can dodge or cancel the damage.

### Plan C: Ruin their deck and ignore their attacks

**Goal:** Discard their best cards before they draw them, and safely block their biggest hits.
**How to do it:** Tesla has excellent tools for stripping the opponent of their resources. X-Ray Radiation (3 copies, value 4) reveals the top card of the opponent's deck during combat. If it is a key card, you can spend one coil to discard it permanently. If you spend two coils, you also add its boost value to your block. On defense, Polyphase Coils (3 copies, value 3) is a perfect counter to massive attacks. You get to see the opponent's card before you decide how many coils to spend. Spending one coil cancels all their effects, and spending a second coil entirely ignores their printed value, effectively turning any massive attack into a zero.
**Watch out for:** You cannot afford to spend coils on every defense. Polyphase Coils is powerful but expensive. If you burn all your coils blocking small pokes, you will have nothing left to fuel your aggressive action chains on your own turn.

### How they lose

​- He hits exhaustion very fast. Aside from his schemes, he has no "passes" (actions that do not draw a card). Playing Intense Experimentation late in the game draws cards and accelerates his fatigue.
​- Relentless aggression starves him of coils. If an opponent forces him to spend coils just to survive their attacks, he cannot build up a charge for his own turn.
​- With only 14 health and most printed defensive values sitting at 2 or 3, big unboosted attacks from melee fighters easily chip him down.
​- Hand disruption and random discards hurt him deeply, taking away his key combo pieces before he can string them together.

### Key cards

​- **X-Ray Radiation**: a 4-value versatile that serves as his best printed block. It provides critical deck knowledge, removes incoming threats, and can swing combat values wildly.
​- **7 Hertz**: his primary combo tool. Spending one coil to gain an action keeps the aggressive pressure on.
​- **Polyphase Coils**: a perfect defensive card if you have the coils. It lets you blank the opponent's effects and value entirely, but only after you see what they played.
​- **Intense Experimentation**: a 3-value defense that draws cards. Spending two coils lets you draw three cards and heal 1 health. It is great in the early game for building a hand, but a liability late in the game.
​- **Remote Control**: a scheme that moves all opposing fighters, letting you clump them up for area damage or push them away to escape pins.

### Tips

​- Mulligan for X-Ray Radiation, Lightning Storm, and especially Intense Experimentation. Drawing Intense Experimentation early gives you the card advantage needed to run your combos, while holding it late is dangerous.
​- Look for small maps in a draft. Tesla wants to spend his cards attacking, not boosting just to get into range.
​- When using Kinetic Induction, try to aim it at 1-health sidekicks. They often will not defend it, allowing you to win the combat and charge both coils for free.
​- Polyphase Coils cannot stop defensive immediate effects like Yennenga's Shield Formation. Their immediate effects will trigger and resolve before your card can cancel them.
​- The sources disagree heavily on how to use Death Ray. One argues that spending two coils to boost it to a 7 is a great way to punish an opponent for standing in your zone. Another insists it is better used as bait to draw out Feints, arguing that spending two coils for raw damage is too linear and wasteful.
​- Play The Alternating Current early to charge your coils rather than saving it for the 2-health recovery. Keeping the pressure high is more important than a small heal.
