## Blackbeard

**In short:** A ranged fighter who runs a unique economy, forcing the opponent to pay for their actions. He hides behind his Sea Dogs, drains the opponent's hand with discards, and starves them of resources to win a long value game.

### Plan A: Starve the treasury

**Goal:** Keep the opponent at zero doubloons so your powerful card effects resolve.
**How to do it:** Blackbeard starts with two doubloons, and the third is in the treasury. Whenever he takes combat damage, he pays a doubloon to the treasury. Opponents can pay doubloons from the treasury to cancel his unique yellow effects, which returns the doubloons to Blackbeard. You want the treasury empty when you play your best cards. Plunder is a scheme that steals a doubloon and repositions your fighters without drawing, making it your best opening play to seize control. When the treasury is empty, hit them with Avast Ye! (a 5-value attack) to force a random discard and deal zone damage, knowing they cannot pay to stop it.
**Watch out for:** His special ability lets you pay a doubloon to gain an action. The sources disagree on using this: some players recommend spending it for massive action-gain combos alongside Give no quarter, while others warn that handing the opponent a free doubloon ruins your defenses, and suggest saving the ability strictly for a final lethal turn.

### Plan B: Body block with the Sea Dogs

**Goal:** Protect Blackbeard's health and his doubloons while gaining card advantage.
**How to do it:** Your two Sea Dogs have 1 health and a melee attack. Place them in bottlenecks to block the paths to Blackbeard. Let the opponent attack them, and simply take the hit without defending. They lose an attack card, and because the special ability specifies Blackbeard himself must take the combat damage, no doubloon goes to the treasury. Bring the Sea Dogs back with Show a leg! or Scourge of the seven seas. Forcing the opponent to waste attacks on respawning 1-health sidekicks drains their hand and pushes them rapidly toward the exhaustion clock.
**Watch out for:** Light the fuse deals 1 damage to all adjacent fighters, not just enemies. Be careful not to blow up your other Sea Dog or accidentally ping Blackbeard.

### Plan C: Rip apart their hand on defense

**Goal:** Remove their best attacks before they can even play them.
**How to do it:** Fearsome and calculating (3 copies) lets you look at their hand and force a chosen discard. Play it on defense when the treasury is empty. Because the effect happens immediately on their turn, you strip away their heaviest attack before they can spend their second action, leaving you a full turn to punish them. Intimidating visage blocks for 4 and heals Blackbeard for 2 if he remains adjacent to the attacker, padding his 13 health into a much larger effective pool. Between chosen discards, random discards, and wasted attacks on Sea Dogs, the opponent runs out of threats.
**Watch out for:** The sources disagree on Queen Anne's revenge (a 7-value versatile that ignores standard cancels but shrinks if the opponent pays doubloons). Some use it strictly on defense to guarantee Blackbeard takes no combat damage and gives up no doubloons, while others prefer to slam it on attack to bypass opponents' defensive cancels.

### How they lose

- Fighters with relentless, high-damage attacks (like the T-Rex) smash through his defenses before he can set up his economy.
- He has terrible card draw. No prey, no pay is his only draw effect, and it requires winning the combat.
- If the opponent manages to horde two or three doubloons in the treasury, they can cancel all of his most important effects and push through damage.
- He fails to find Plunder early in the game, leaving the opponent with too many doubloons to start.

### Key cards

- **Plunder**: His most important card. It steals a doubloon and moves his fighters up to 3 spaces through enemies, taking back control of the economy without advancing his exhaustion clock.
- **Fearsome and calculating**: The core of his defense. A 3-value versatile that forces a chosen discard if they cannot pay.
- **Queen Anne's revenge**: A massive 7-value versatile whose effects cannot be canceled by standard cards like Feint.
- **Intimidating visage**: A 4-value defense that heals him for 2 if he ends up adjacent.
- **Give no quarter**: A 3-value attack that becomes a 5 if a friendly fighter is adjacent to the target, granting an action if unpaid.

### Tips

- Mulligan aggressively for Plunder to empty the treasury on your first turn.
- Never play Fearsome and calculating if the opponent has a doubloon. Guaranteeing the chosen discard is worth taking an undefended hit elsewhere.
- Discard Light the fuse to boost No prey, no pay into a 5-value block, or play it to clear out 1-health enemy sidekicks (like Wukong's clones).
- Scourge of the seven seas gives the opponent three separate effects to pay for. Most opponents will refuse to pay for any of them, letting you heal, summon, and gain an action for free.
- Pick large maps with narrow paths, like Soho or Venice, where you can kite and your Sea Dogs can easily block the opponent's approach.
