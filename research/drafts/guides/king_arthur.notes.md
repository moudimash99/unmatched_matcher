From the first draft:
​- The Gridbeast guide mentions Excalibur deals 4 damage if Feinted. This isn't a special rule; they are just doing the math of a 6-value Excalibur against a standard 2-value Feint (6 - 2 = 4). Excalibur's printed value is 6.
​- One Reddit user (Jimmeu) advocates attacking *only* with boosts and never without them, but almost all other sources consider this a fast track to exhaustion and advise baiting Feints instead. I followed the majority.
​- A video mentions Excalibur is "uncancelable". It just means it has no card text, so a Feint only cancels the attached boost card, not the 6 base value. I clarified this as "cannot be fully canceled."
