## Spider-Man

**In short:** A highly mobile melee fighter who knows the printed value of every incoming attack before defending. He lacks cancels, but relies on perfect blocking, massive card draw, and constant repositioning to outmaneuver the opponent.

### Plan A: Block perfectly with Spider-Sense

**Goal:** Take minimal damage by matching your defenses exactly to their attacks.
**How to do it:** His special ability forces opponents to reveal the printed value of their attack card before you defend, removing most combat mind games. If they declare a 6 or 7 (like Dracula's Beastform or Arthur's Excalibur), play Web Shooters (3 copies, value 3) to cap the damage at 2. If they declare a 4 or higher, play Counter-Attack to deal 2 damage back, or Spider-Sense Tingling! to draw two cards. When they throw a 2 or 3, you can safely block with Snark or Wall Crawler to get a positional or card advantage. Because you know what's coming, you can hold your best defenses in reserve instead of wasting them on weak bait attacks.
**Watch out for:** The ability only reveals the *printed* value. Card effects can change this mid-combat. An opponent might declare a 2 or 3, but reveal a Momentous Shift or a Sinbad Voyage that hits much harder. Spider-Sense also struggles against fighters like Black Panther, Bruce Lee, or Yennenga, who have many different cards with a printed value of 3, keeping you guessing about the actual effects.

### Plan B: Hit, run, and reposition

**Goal:** Control the distance and set up heavy attacks.
**How to do it:** Spider-Man has plenty of effects that let him drop into a space, strike, and vanish. Wall Crawler places him in any space in his zone. Thwip! does the same if you win the combat. Use these to close the gap against ranged fighters or to disengage from melee ones. Moving this way perfectly sets up Momentous Shift (3 copies), which becomes a 5-value attack if you started the turn in a different space. You can also manipulate the enemy's position: Swinging Kick is a 6-value attack that moves the opponent up to the damage dealt. If you hit a 1-health sidekick with it, you calculate movement based on the full damage dealt, letting you throw them far across the board.
**Watch out for:** Card effects that say "place Spider-Man in any space" require him to move to an empty space; you cannot choose the space he is currently standing in.

### Plan C: Deny their card draw

**Goal:** Keep your hand full while starving the opponent's options.
**How to do it:** Right in the Face! (2 copies, value 4) immediately stops the opponent from drawing cards for the rest of the turn. Play it to block their first action, and any card draw from their second attack—or end-of-turn abilities like those of Bigfoot or the T-Rex—is entirely shut down. It also stops fighters like Bloody Mary from drawing to replenish their hand after an evade. While they starve, you draw heavily. Disarming Shot draws cards equal to the damage dealt. Hitting a 1-health sidekick (like Wong) with it draws 4 cards, because over-damage counts.
**Watch out for:** Spider-Man has massive card draw but no cards that let him boost his movement, and only one card that gives an action. If you play too defensively, your hand will fill up fast and you'll be forced to discard.

### How they lose

- He has absolutely no cancels (Feints) in his deck. Fighters with devastating combat effects (Little Red Riding Hood) or who rely on combos rather than pure value can run right over him.
- Opponents who know his deck will bait his Counter-Attacks and Spider-Sense Tingling! cards. Those are weak blocks (value 3 and 2) that guarantee he takes damage against big swings.
- His printed defenses top out at 4, so without Web Shooters in hand, heavy hitters get through easily.

### Key cards

- **Web Shooters**: his only answer to massive attacks. It functions as a pseudo-cancel against raw damage, restricting him to taking no more than 2 combat damage.
- **Swinging Kick**: his biggest attack at 6. It serves as both heavy damage and a way to push opponents away or into corners.
- **Right in the Face!**: a 4-value versatile. His best standard block, and a harsh counter to combo-heavy or card-hungry fighters.
- **Counter-Attack**: punishes opponents for throwing 4s and 5s by dealing 2 guaranteed damage back. Crucial against ranged fighters where Son of a Boxer-style effects wouldn't usually work.
- **With Great Power**: a scheme that gives his attacks +1, draws 1 card, and grants an action. Best used as your first action when already adjacent, allowing two buffed attacks in a row.

### Tips

- Because you know the printed values of incoming attacks, you can afford to play aggressively and operate at a lower hand size, keeping only the exact defenses you need.
- If an opponent attacks with a large number, play Spider-Sense Tingling! to draw cards only if you can afford to take the hit, because its block value is just 2.
- Watch the board zones closely. Thwip!, Wall Crawler, and Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man only let you teleport within your current zone.
- Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man recovers 3 health, which offsets the minor damage you take when intentionally blocking big attacks with Web Shooters or Counter-Attack.
- Disarming Shot combos perfectly with With Great Power to deal 5 damage and potentially draw 5 cards.
