## Alice

**In short:** An aggressive melee fighter who switches between Big (+2 to her attacks) and Small (+1 to her defenses). Because her deck has 15 different cards and three single copies, she plays off the hand she draws, pressing early with Big attacks and the hard-hitting Jabberwock.

### Plan A: Stay Big and time your shifts
**Goal:** Attack while Big, block while Small, and keep constant pressure on the opponent.
**How to do it:** Treat Big as your default state, because +2 on attacks is worth more than +1 on defense and cannot be canceled by Feint. Most sources recommend starting the game Big; one suggests starting Small when going second, or checking your opening hand first since you draw before placing her. You have 11 size-changing cards, including four schemes that shift her without a fight. Chain them to match the turn:
- When Big, attack with The Other Side of the Mushroom (1 copy, value 3) as your second action to hit for 5, move up to 3 spaces away, and turn Small for their turn. O Frabjous Day! (1 copy, value 4) hits for 6 and turns you Small in place.
- When Small, block with Mad as a Hatter (2 copies, value 3) or I'm Late, I'm Late (3 copies, value 2). They block for 4 and 3, move your fighters on the opponent's turn, and turn Alice Big before yours starts.
- Eat Me (2 copies) moves Alice up to 3 spaces and changes her size. Play it as your first action while Small to close in and turn Big for a surprise attack, or as your second action while Big to step back and turn Small.

**Watch out for:** Changing size on these cards is mandatory. Don't play a card that flips you into the wrong form for the next exchange. Without her size bonus, her printed values are low—only five cards in the whole deck have a printed value of 4—so hitting while Small or getting caught Big without a solid defense card hurts.

### Plan B: Pressure with the Jabberwock
**Goal:** Use your 8-health sidekick as a second threat that tears through heroes and clears sidekicks.
**How to do it:** The Jabberwock doesn't get Alice's size bonuses, but its four专属 attacks hit hard on their own:
- Claws That Catch (2 copies, value 3) becomes a 5 against a hero. Never waste it on a sidekick.
- Jaws That Bite (2 copies, value 4) deals 2 damage after combat to any one fighter adjacent to the Jabberwock. Use it for 6 total damage on a hero, or to attack one Medusa Harpy and kill a second adjacent one with the after-combat damage.

Keep the Jabberwock near Alice so it isn't picked off early, or let it do the fighting while Alice hangs back Small. Because opponents expect those four big attacks and want to Feint them, mix in Regroup or Skirmish with the Jabberwock to bait out cancels.
**Watch out for:** With only four Jabberwock-only cards in the deck, you may draw them late or after it dies. Also watch out for defenders with push effects: Jaws That Bite's after-combat damage is mandatory, so if the opponent pushes the Jabberwock away from their fighters and leaves it next to Alice, Alice has to take the 2 damage.

### How they lose
- Both fighters have Move 2 and melee attacks. Faster or ranged fighters can kite her and control when fights happen.
- Her deck is inconsistent. With 15 unique cards and only Drink Me, Looking Glass, and Regroup to draw, key cards or Jabberwock attacks can sit at the bottom of the deck.
- Opponents save an offensive Feint for when Alice drops to 9 health or lower, aiming to cancel Looking Glass.
- If the Jabberwock dies before you draw its four attack cards, those cards become dead draws for combat.
- Opponents who hit hard (5 or more) try to keep her Big where her blocks are weaker, while fighters with many small attacks prefer her Small so she can't race them.

### Key cards
- **Looking Glass**: a 2-value defense (3 when Small) that lets you pick two of three effects: draw 2 cards, heal 3, or place Alice on any space. Wait until you have taken a few hits so the heal isn't wasted, mostly picking the draw and heal unless you need the teleport to escape or chase down a weak enemy.
- **Snicker-Snack**: a 1-copy, 3-value attack. Only play it while Big (for 5) so you win the combat, look at their hand, and discard their best card—such as King Arthur's Excalibur. Use it when their hand is full, and follow up with a Feint next combat since you know what they hold.
- **Manxome Foe**: a 3-value versatile that discards the top card of your deck and adds its boost value. Her boosts average a bit over 2 and four cards have a boost of 4, so while Big it hits for 7 to 9. The sources disagree on whether it's worth the cost: some like it to close out games fast, while one dislikes it because it can mill your single-copy cards and prefers using it on the Jabberwock as Feint bait or saving it for the final blow.
- **Momentous Shift**: a 3-value versatile that becomes 5 if your fighter moved this turn—a 7 when Alice attacks while Big. Save some versatile cards to block for the Jabberwock, since it cannot use Alice's defense cards.
- **Drink Me**: draws 2 cards and changes size. Along with Looking Glass, it's her main way to dig through her inconsistent deck.

### Tips
- Even if an opponent's card cancels or zeroes out your card's printed value, Alice still gets her +2 or +1 from her size (solong as she played a card).
- Pin mobile opponents between Alice and the Jabberwock; don't hesitate to spend a couple of cards boosting movement early to trap them.
- If you start your turn Small right next to an enemy, you can open with O Frabjous Day! (for 4) to turn Big, then hit them a second time with the +2 bonus.
- Regroup is a 3-value attack when Alice is Big, which can win combat for the 2-card draw while bluffing a heavier hit.
