## Angel

**In short:** A straightforward melee fighter wielding powerful strikes but lacking reliable blocks. While his deck hits hard and his ability lets him draw a card when losing a combat on the attack, his sidekick Faith wields some of the duo's most threatening tools.

### Plan A: Hit hard and step away

**Goal:** Deal heavy damage and force the opponent to waste actions reaching you.
**How to do it:** Angel struggles on defense, so positioning is your primary shield. Launch offenses using cards that safely reposition your fighters after the strike. Disengage is a solid attack that relocates the fighter to an empty space in their zone. Faith’s Five by Five is a massive strike that allows her to pass through enemies, serving as both a devastating blow and an escape route. You can also begin a turn by playing the scheme Killer of the Dead for 2 guaranteed damage to someone next to Faith, then follow up with Five by Five to hit them again and retreat.
**Watch out for:** Ranged fighters are largely unfazed if you step away, as long as you land somewhere in their line of sight within the zone.

### Plan B: Bait defenses to draw cards

**Goal:** Trigger your ability by intentionally losing combats to maintain card draw.
**How to do it:** Angel's ability grants a card draw when you attack and lose the combat (ties count as a loss for the attacker). Once you establish pressure with your heavy hitters, opponents will start committing their best blocks. That is the ideal moment to attack with weaker versatile options. Regroup is excellent bait: losing grants one draw from the card and one from the ability, while winning guarantees two draws anyway. Wisdom of Ages provides a safe card draw regardless of the combat result. Brooding punishes successful defenders by dealing them a point of damage if you lose.
**Watch out for:** Triggering this ability is surprisingly difficult. Your massive attacks—Angelus, Scourge of Europe, Five by Five, Momentous Shift, and Cursed with a Soul—comprise a significant portion of your offense and rarely lose. Consequently, your ability triggers less frequently than expected, which can leave you starved for cards.

### How they lose

​- Their defenses are highly vulnerable. The deck relies predominantly on versatile 3s or the sole dedicated defense card, Haunted by the Faces. Opponents wielding massive printed attacks easily break through these weak blocks.
​- Card exhaustion is a major threat. Because their high-value strikes frequently win, the special ability rarely activates, and the deck features very little innate card draw to compensate.
​- Opponents with ranged attacks exploit their hit-and-run tactics, as these repositioning cards create distance but do not provide cover from ranged strikes.

### Key cards

​- **Five by Five**: Faith's premium strike. Because it is so threatening and allows her to escape or corner an opponent, savvy players will often reserve their Feints for Faith rather than Angel.
​- **Disengage**: Vital for dodging counter-attacks by teleporting the attacker to safety after a swing.
​- **The Rogue Slayer**: A versatile card that deals splash damage to all adjacent enemies. Reserve it for defense to effortlessly wipe out swarms of 1-health sidekicks.
​- **Regroup**: Your most dependable tool for card advantage when utilized as an attack.
​- **Angelus, Scourge of Europe**: A blank heavy attack. Since it has no text, it perfectly punishes opponents who blindly play a Feint.

### Tips

​- Reserve Momentous Shift for defending. Since the deck already packs numerous strong attacks, its base block value is generally more crucial than its conditional attack boost.
​- Do not count on Cursed with a Soul for consistent healing. Treat the health recovery as a pleasant bonus if you happen to lose, but anticipate that its high value will win most engagements.
​- When defending with Haunted by the Faces, losing the combat allows you to teleport Angel within his zone. Exploit this to instantly close the distance on an elusive ranged enemy. However, remember that because you are defending, losing this combat will not trigger Angel's innate card draw ability.
