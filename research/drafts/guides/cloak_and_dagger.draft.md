## Cloak and Dagger

**In short:** A volatile duo where one sets up the target and the other goes for the kill. Cloak drains the opponent's hand early in the game, preparing them for Dagger to unleash a rapid chain of unblockable actions. They struggle with low health but make up for it with extreme burst damage.

### Plan A: Drain their hand with Cloak

**Goal:** Empty the opponent's hand and bait out their best defenses before Dagger strikes.
**How to do it:** Cloak's ability, Umbra, forces the opponent to discard a card whenever he deals 2 or more combat damage. This turns every attack into a threat. Lead with Cloak early in the game to force the opponent into tough choices. Darkforce Dimension (2 copies, value 4) guarantees a discard through its effect and moves the enemy, often securing a second discard if it deals enough damage. Play Commanding Impact (3 copies, value 5) with Cloak to hit hard, draw a card, and trigger Umbra to widen your hand advantage. If the opponent refuses to block small attacks, they still lose a card to the ability.
**Watch out for:** Cloak lacks the raw damage to finish high-health enemies on his own. Don't push him so hard that he dies early, and switch to Dagger once the opponent's hand is low.

### Plan B: Chain attacks with Dagger

**Goal:** Unleash a combo of extra actions when the opponent runs out of blocks.
**How to do it:** Wait until the opponent has four or fewer cards in hand. Use Channel the Dark (a 2-value attack) with Cloak to place the enemy next to Dagger and gain an action. Then unload Dagger's cards. Every time she deals 2 combat damage, Refraction gives you another action. Chain multiple attacks together, relying on Perfect Balance (a 6-value attack when both are adjacent) and Commanding Impact to keep the chain going over average blocks. Once the opponent is out of defenses, finish them with Lightforce Barrage.
**Watch out for:** Lightforce Barrage is a 7-value attack that deals its combat damage back to Dagger. Save it for the end of the game or the end of a combo. Throwing it too early can kill her or leave her critically low.

### Plan C: Juggle their health

**Goal:** Keep both fighters alive to maintain your options and board presence.
**How to do it:** They only have 8 health each, and losing one cripples the team. Use Living Shadow on defense to swap Dagger out of danger; it brings Cloak into the combat and boosts the card's value to a 4. The Living Light heals 2 health, but it requires one fighter to play it to heal the other. Chosen Fate lets you deal up to 4 damage to one fighter to heal the other by the exact same amount. Keep both heroes above lethal range by passing health from the healthy fighter to the wounded one.
**Watch out for:** Sources disagree on a trick with Chosen Fate: some argue you can deal 4 damage to a fighter with only 1 health left to heal the other for 4, while others simply advise balancing their health normally to keep both alive. Once Dagger dies, winning is nearly impossible.

### How they lose

**Goal:**

- High-damage attackers burn through their low health pools and weak blocks quickly.
- Fighters with defensive effects that swap or ignore values easily break Dagger's action chain.
- They get kited. With a move of 2 and mostly 1- or 2-value boosts, they struggle to catch fast ranged fighters on large maps.
- Opponents with heavy card draw keep their hands full of blocks, preventing Dagger from ever finding an opening.
- Dagger dies early, leaving Cloak without a reliable way to close out the game.

### Key cards

- **Channel the Dark**: the ultimate combo starter. Move the enemy to Dagger, gain an action, and start attacking. It is an ideal bait for a Feint.
- **Perfect Balance**: a 4-value versatile that becomes a 6 if both Cloak and Dagger are adjacent to the target. Massive on attack to ensure Dagger gets her extra action, and your highest printed block.
- **Darkforce Dimension**: Cloak's 4-value attack. It places the enemy anywhere and guarantees a discard, making it incredible for setting up pins and draining resources.
- **Living Shadow**: a 2-value defense that swaps Dagger with Cloak and becomes a 4. Your best tool to protect Dagger from aggressive enemies.
- **Lightforce Barrage**: Dagger's 7-value finisher. It cannot be canceled, but the self-damage means you must time it perfectly.

### Tips

- Target 1-health sidekicks with Cloak. Even if they have only 1 health, an undefended attack still counts as dealing 2 damage, forcing the opponent to discard a card to Umbra.
- Keep Cloak and Dagger close to each other, but leave an empty space between them to pull the enemy into a pin for Perfect Balance.
- Into Darkness is your best defense against ranged attackers, letting you move a fighter up to 3 spaces after combat to close the gap or escape a second attack.
- Use Into the Void mostly to bait out Feints or big blocks, rather than actually boosting it. You only have four 3-value boosts in the deck, and you usually need them to maneuver.
- If you know your opponent has a strong defense card, attack with your weaker cards first to draw it out before committing Perfect Balance or Lightforce Barrage.
