## Deadpool

**In short:** A chaotic melee fighter who brings a deck of 30 completely unique cards. He has a low maximum health of 10 and few strong blocks, but he constantly recovers health by attacking.

### Plan A: Out-heal the damage

**Goal:** Keep his health pool afloat to survive big hits.
**How to do it:** His ability heals 1 health every time he attacks, which triggers after the attack resolves. To offset his 10-health limit, you need to stay aggressive and attack frequently. He also brings specific healing cards to top up without attacking. Faint (the scheme, not the cancel) gives a quick 2 health and acts as a safe action pass to force the opponent to approach. Gimme Gimme Chimichanga is a 3-value versatile that replaces itself with card draw and heals for 1, making it an excellent defensive play.
**Watch out for:** Be careful with They Have An Amazing Buffet. It recovers 2 health, but if you are at full health afterward, you take 2 damage. If you play it while at 9 health, you will hit 10, trigger the penalty, and end up at 8—less health than you started with. At 8 health, the math simply nets to zero.

### Plan B: Wrangle the unpredictable deck

**Goal:** Find the right answers early and recycle the best ones late.
**How to do it:** With 30 unique cards, your draws are inconsistent. Time out time out time out! lets you search your deck for the exact card you need. Play it early, ideally on defense; when your opponent attacks, you can check your deck and grab a perfect block or a cheap card to throw away, ensuring you don't waste a rare high-value defense on a bait attack. Conversely, if your opponent defends weakly, you can use it on offense to pull Gaze of Stone and deal massive damage.

In the late game, play Dumpster Divin' Deadpool to recover cards from your discard pile. Prioritize returning your best defenses: Super Feint, Feint, and Passwords. This essentially delays your exhaustion clock and concentrates your deck with your strongest tools.
**Watch out for:** Time out time out time out! consumes two cards from your total pool (the card itself plus the one you fetch from your deck). Dumpster Divin' Deadpool is your most important scheme, so beware of fighters who force discards from your hand before you get the chance to play it.

### Plan C: Punish their positioning and hand

**Goal:** Catch the opponent with specific, high-impact tricks.
**How to do it:**

- If the enemy hero hides behind a sidekick, attack the sidekick with Klunkin' Heads. If you win the combat, it deals 3 damage to each other opposing fighter adjacent to the target, bypassing the hero's defenses entirely.
- Cha-Ching! is a 1-value attack that you can boost during combat. If you hold Push to Teleport, you can turn it into a sudden 7-value hit after seeing the opponent's defense.
- If you know what is in their hand—either by guessing or by looking with Non-Retinal Scan Access to Danger Room—play the scheme Holy Mackerel!. Name a card, and they must discard all copies of it. This strips crucial cards from fighters who rely on three copies of the same card, like Bigfoot or Robin Hood.
**Watch out for:** You only have one copy of each combo piece. If your Cha-Ching! gets discarded or your Push to Teleport is at the bottom of your deck, you have to find another way to win.

### How they lose

- Big printed attacks (such as King Arthur's Excalibur or Bigfoot's Larger Than Life) break right through his weak 2- and 3-value blocks. With only 10 health, he dies in a few hits.
- Fast, action-heavy fighters (Raptors, Bruce Lee) bleed him dry. He only has 15 total cards he can block with, and he lacks the card draw to keep his hand full against multiple attacks.
- Discard effects take away his few crucial blocks and cancels.
- He gets pinned easily because he has a move of 2, no sidekicks, and few movement tricks.
- Sherlock Holmes uses Confirm Suspicion. If Sherlock guesses Push to Teleport, Deadpool instantly takes 6 damage—more than half his life bar.

### Key cards

- **Dumpster Divin' Deadpool**: A scheme that heals 1 and shuffles five discarded cards into your deck. Your main tool to survive longer and play your best defenses twice.
- **Time out time out time out!**: A versatile card with no printed value that swaps itself for any card in your deck. Use it on defense to grab the exact block you need.
- **Super Feint**: A 4-value versatile that cannot be canceled. It cancels the opponent's effect and draws a card. His strongest defense.
- **Passwords**: A 5-value defense. It blocks huge hits, but the opponent gets to look at your hand and discard a card of their choice. Try to play it when your hand has nothing vital to lose.
- **Push to Teleport**: A 2-value versatile with a massive boost of 6. Terrible in actual combat, but incredible for moving 8 spaces in a pinch or fueling Cha-Ching!.

### Tips

- Use And For My Next Move... flexibly. Choose the 2 damage to punish a melee attacker, or choose the 3 movement to escape an aggressive ranged fighter.
- Save I Always Get The Last Word for defense. Attacking with it guarantees 1 damage, but leaves you short on blocks.
- Never play Xavier Institute Faculty as a ranged attack. It is your only card that can be played at range, so the opponent will know exactly what it is and block it effortlessly.
- Play Transit Card defensively to reposition out of a pin or to close the gap against a ranged attacker.
- Keep some food on the table to ensure Chimichanga Break! hits as a 5-value card.
