## Sherlock Holmes

**In short:** A methodical fighter who keeps his distance to draw cards, disrupts the opponent's hand, and turns their best cards into direct damage. He uses Watson as a shield and relies on hand knowledge to perfectly counter attacks.

### Plan A: The discard combo

**Goal:** Strip their best cards and deal heavy undefended damage.
**How to do it:** Your best turns revolve around knowing your opponent's hand. Eliminate the Impossible lets you look at their hand and choose one card for them to discard. Play it as your first action, then follow immediately with Confirm Suspicion. Since you just saw their hand, name a value that forces them to discard a card with a high boost value, dealing that much damage to their hero. If they hold two cards with the same value, use Eliminate the Impossible to discard the one with the lower boost, leaving them only the high-boost card to discard for Confirm Suspicion.
**Watch out for:** Confirm Suspicion asks for an attack or defense value, so it cannot target schemes. Use Eliminate the Impossible to pull key schemes from their hand instead.

### Plan B: Punish them on defense

**Goal:** Turn defense into unavoidable damage and canceled attacks.
**How to do it:** Sherlock only has 15 cards he can use to block, and their printed values top out at 3, but their effects make up for it. Counterpunch (3 copies) deals 2 damage to the opposing fighter after combat if Sherlock is adjacent, making melee attackers bleed for every strike. Elementary lets you guess the printed attack value; if you are right, you ignore the value and cancel the effects entirely. Hand knowledge makes this guaranteed, but you can also guess common values based on the matchup (like a 4 or 6 against Bigfoot). Because of your special ability, neither of these Holmes defensive effects can be canceled.
**Watch out for:** Deduce Strategy reduces an attack to its boost value, but it does not lock the value. Cards that increase their value during combat will still buff the attack after your effect drops it. The sources disagree slightly on Elementary's timing: one video notes that "immediately" effects on the attacker's card still trigger before Elementary cancels the rest.

### Plan C: Stall behind Watson

**Goal:** Keep Sherlock safe while you draw your scheme combos.
**How to do it:** Because nine of your cards are schemes that cannot block, you need a large hand to ensure you always hold defenses. Double maneuver often and let Watson stand in the way. He has 8 health, shoots at range, and holds your best printed attack, the uncancelable 5-value Service Revolver. Keep him close to Sherlock: Administer Aid teleports him adjacent to Holmes and heals Sherlock for 1. Once they are adjacent, Fixed Point in a Changing Age heals them both.
**Watch out for:** If Watson dies early, all his cards become dead draws. This clogs your hand when you are desperately looking for blocks against aggressive fighters.

### How they lose

​- Aggressive fighters with extra actions (Raptors, Bruce Lee) force him to defend constantly and run him out of block cards.
​- He has poor card draw. Fighters who keep the pressure on will exhaust his deck or force him to take hits unblocked.
​- Opponents who intentionally keep a small hand give him very few targets for his discard schemes.
​- Melee fighters with high-value printed attacks punch right through his 3-value blocks if he guesses wrong with Elementary.

### Key cards

​- **Confirm Suspicion**: his main source of direct damage. If you guess a value they don't have, you still get to look at their hand.
​- **Eliminate the Impossible**: his most precise discard. It removes their best card and sets up your defensive guesses.
​- **Elementary**: a 3-value defense that completely blanks an attack if you guess the printed value. You play it face up.
​- **Master of Disguise**: swaps Sherlock with the opposing hero and deals 1 damage. Use it to escape a pin, separate a hero from their sidekicks, or drop them next to Watson for a ranged attack.
​- **The Game is Afoot**: a 5-value attack that moves Holmes up to 3 spaces after combat. Your best tool to hit and run.

### Tips

​- Your special ability protects Holmes and Dr. Watson cards from being canceled, but your Any cards (like Feint and Study Methods) can still be canceled.
​- If you use Master of Disguise to swap spaces, remember your opponent now counts as having started their turn in a different space, which can trigger their movement-based attack bonuses.
​- Don't play Counterpunch as an attack unless necessary. The opponent's defensive effect resolves first, so they might move away and dodge the adjacent damage.
​- Education Never Ends draws you 2 cards if you lose the combat. Playing it as a weak defense helps refill your hand without double maneuvering.
​- If you don't know what value to guess for Elementary, guessing higher is often safer so you take less damage if you are wrong.
