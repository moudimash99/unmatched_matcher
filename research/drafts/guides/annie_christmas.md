## Annie Christmas

**In short:** A melee fighter who hits harder when she is injured. She manages her health carefully to trigger her attack boost, uses her ranged sidekick Charlie for positioning and chip damage, and relies on a powerful defense card to survive lethal turns.

### Plan A: Manage health for bigger hits

**Goal:** Trigger her special ability without taking unnecessary risks.
**How to do it:** Annie's passive grants a +2 bonus to her attacks whenever she has less health than the defender. Since her maximum health is 14, many opponents start the game slightly healthier than she is. As the match progresses, the goal is to carefully hover just below the enemy's total. Her primary tool for this is Lagniappe (3 copies, value 5). This attack lets you choose to take up to 2 damage immediately before the fight. If your health is tied or slightly higher, you can deliberately injure yourself to activate the passive, hitting for 7 and drawing cards in the process.
**Watch out for:** Dropping your health too low too early. Staying significantly beneath your opponent's life total throughout the match is incredibly risky and can easily cost you the game.

### Plan B: The one-health wall

**Goal:** Survive huge offensive turns and fight back from the brink.
**How to do it:** Mississippi Queen (3 copies, value 2) is a defense that makes it impossible for her health to drop below 1 for the rest of the turn. This completely blocks lethal combat and effect damage. It is the perfect counter against heroes who unleash strings of attacks in a single turn, like Bruce Lee, Bloody Mary, or the Raptors. You can even intentionally linger at 1 health to ensure your passive is always active, leaning on this card to keep you alive.
**Watch out for:** The protection only lasts for the current turn. Against certain enemies, you might be entirely exposed when they attack again. Play it when you intend to win the game on your following action, or try to keep all three copies in your hand to guarantee survival across multiple turns.

### Plan C: Swap and shoot with Charlie

**Goal:** Bypass her low movement and deal damage from a distance.
**How to do it:** Annie only moves 2 spaces and fights in melee, so catching fleeing ranged adversaries can be a chore. Her ranged sidekick Charlie does not benefit from her passive ability, which might lead opponents to ignore him. You can use him to draw fire. When he gets attacked, play Slick Talker (2 copies, defense 3) to immediately swap their places, forcing Annie to take the hit and landing her right next to the enemy for her upcoming turn. Charlie also provides solid offense with Long Shot (2 copies, versatile 3), which hits for 5 if the target is not adjacent. He can also swing with Bottom Dealing (2 copies, attack 3), which adds the boost value of the bottom card of your deck, routinely hitting for at least 4.
**Watch out for:** Charlie has a modest 8 health. If he is defeated, you lose the ability to play Slick Talker and are stripped of all your ranged pressure.

### How they lose

- She lacks a standard Feint. Quite a Pair is her sole effect-canceling card, and it forces you to hold two identical cards in your hand.
- Her sluggish movement makes it difficult to pin down elusive, ranged enemies if Charlie is out of the picture.
- Players who drain their own health too rapidly can be eliminated before they manage to draw their survival cards.

### Key cards

- **Lagniappe**: Her most threatening attack, offering built-in health manipulation and card draw.
- **Mississippi Queen**: The safety net that lets her endure massive damage spikes.
- **Slick Talker**: An invaluable positioning trick to force engagements or rescue a vulnerable fighter.
- **Quite a Pair** (2 copies, versatile 3): Her unconventional cancel. Revealing the identical cards does not force you to discard them, allowing you to keep the pair for future use.
- **Captain's Orders** (2 copies): A scheme that teleports Annie and another friendly fighter anywhere within Annie's zone, then refunds your action. Perfect for engaging without spending maneuvers.

### Tips

- A Few More Pearls (3 copies) is a scheme that dishes out 2 damage to all enemies adjacent to Annie. It is excellent for securing unblockable damage.
- Striking Beauty (2 copies) is a 1-value versatile that deals extra damage. If used on offense while your passive is active, it hits as a 3, increasing your odds of winning the combat and guaranteeing effect damage.
- Long Shot works beautifully as a defense against ranged heroes, blocking for a solid 5 since you are not adjacent.
- In team formats (2v2 or Co-op Adventures), Better Together (4 copies, versatile 4) and Captain's Orders apply to your allies, enabling you to heal their heroes or teleport them across zones.
- In the cooperative Tales to Amaze mode, villains begin with massive health pools. Send Annie straight at the boss early in the game, as her passive will naturally stay active for a long time.
