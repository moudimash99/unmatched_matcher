## Houdini

**In short:** An explosive, highly mobile melee fighter who boosts cards to trigger powerful extra effects. He converts his cards into massive damage combos, but has weak defenses and struggles in long, grindy matches.

### Plan A: Clear the way for The Big Reveal

**Goal:** Strip away the opponent's defenses and land his strongest attack.
**How to do it:** The Big Reveal lets you boost the attack twice and return one of those boost cards to your hand. You can reach 7 to 9 damage in a single strike. Before you throw it, use Set the Stage's boost effect to look at your opponent's hand and discard their best defense or Feint. When you boost The Big Reveal, use cards with powerful boost effects like Misdirection (to deal 2 extra damage) or Set the Stage again, and return that card to your hand to reuse it later.
**Watch out for:** Hand disruption. If an opponent discards your copies of The Big Reveal, your main win condition is gone. Since he needs to build up cards to combo, he is vulnerable to fighters who attack aggressively and force him to spend his combo pieces on defense.

### Plan B: Bait out their blocks

**Goal:** Force the opponent to waste their good defenses on small attacks, or catch them undefended.
**How to do it:** Sleight of Hand lets you swap it for a different card before combat resolves. Use it to check if the opponent played a defense card. If they play a strong block or a Feint, you return Sleight of Hand and play a weaker card, getting rid of their defense safely. If they play nothing, you can swap in An Illusion of My Own Design. Because they did not play a card, they cannot use its effect to boost their nonexistent defense, leaving them to take a massive hit when you boost yours. You can also use For My Next Trick as a bait attack that replaces itself and gives you an action.
**Watch out for:** One source calls Sleight of Hand his best card, but if the opponent calls your bluff and plays a weak block, you might waste an attack opportunity.

### Plan C: Turn defense into offense and escape

**Goal:** Preserve your cards for attacks and avoid taking hits.
**How to do it:** Houdini only has a few dedicated defense cards, and you usually want to spend them to boost your attacks instead. To survive, hit hard and then use his Escape Artist ability to teleport across the map so melee fighters cannot reach him. Grand Escape also lets you boost to win combat and teleport away. When you inevitably take damage, boost an attack with All Part of the Show to heal 2 health. You can also use Smoke and Mirrors to swap places with Bess, making her take the hit for him.
**Watch out for:** Getting boxed in. If an opponent surrounds him with sidekicks on a tight map, he cannot teleport away without moving through them, forcing him to waste attacks on sidekicks instead of the hero.

### How they lose

​- He lacks strong defenses and card draw for a long game. Fighters who can attack him every turn run him out of cards and health quickly.
​- Discard effects (like Medusa's or the Genie's) drain his hand and snipe his key combo pieces.
​- Opponents who stay aggressive and force him to block with his versatile cards prevent him from using those cards for their powerful boost effects.
​- He gets pinned by sidekicks and takes damage because he cannot maneuver away.

### Key cards

​- **The Big Reveal**: a 2-value attack that boosts twice and returns a boost card to your hand. It is his primary damage dealer.
​- **Set the Stage**: a Bess scheme whose true value is its 2-value boost effect: look at their hand and discard a card. Use it to strip away their Feints before your big attacks.
​- **Misdirection**: a 2-value versatile that cancels all effects. Its boost effect deals 2 damage to any fighter. The sources disagree on how to use it: some save it as a Feint, while others prefer dealing up to 6 automatic damage over the course of the game.
​- **Sleight of Hand**: a 1-value attack that lets you swap it for another card before combat resolves. It is excellent for checking what defense the opponent played.
​- **A Magician Never Reveals His Secrets**: a scheme with a boost of 4 that cancels any effect that looks at your hand. Keep it to stop fighters from stripping your hand, or use it as a massive boost.

### Tips

​- Bess has only 5 health, but her cards work for their boost effects even if she is defeated. Do not waste Houdini's resources trying to keep her alive.
​- A Magician Never Reveals His Secrets stops effects from cards like Buffy's Insight or Dracula's Do My Bidding, but it cannot stop Sherlock Holmes's hero cards, because their effects explicitly cannot be canceled.
​- And the Beautiful Bess! lets you draw a card if an opponent's effect makes you discard it, making it perfect discard fodder against fighters who attack your hand.
​- The sources disagree on All Part of the Show: one says it is better used to heal via its boost effect, while another mentions the threat of its revive effect is powerful enough to make opponents hesitate.
​- Do not boost your movement with 3- or 4-value boost cards. Save them to boost your damage.
