## Ghost Rider

**In short:** A highly mobile melee fighter who manages a unique resource called Hellfire. He spends it to deal unblockable damage by driving through enemies and to fuel his powerful card effects, but struggles on defense when his resource runs dry.

### Plan A: Hit and run

**Goal:** Deal guaranteed damage while staying safely out of reach.
**How to do it:** Ghost Rider starts the game with 5 Hellfire tokens. When you maneuver, you may spend 1 Hellfire to set your movement to 4, move through opposing fighters, and deal 1 damage to each enemy you pass through. Because this damage requires no attack cards, you can steadily wear down the opponent's health or instantly clear out 1-health sidekicks like Medusa's Harpies or Sun Wukong's clones. After driving through them, end your turn a few spaces away. This forces melee opponents to spend their own actions maneuvering just to reach you, throwing off their tempo.
**Watch out for:** You deal 1 damage per fighter, not per pass, so driving back and forth over the same enemy on one turn does not deal extra damage. Also, once you spend the Hellfire, your movement for that maneuver is capped at 4; you cannot discard a card to boost it higher.

### Plan B: Manage the Hellfire economy

**Goal:** Keep enough Hellfire to fuel your defenses without wasting the cards that generate it.
**How to do it:** Ghost Rider does not gain Hellfire passively; he relies entirely on his card effects to refill his supply. Play the scheme Chains of Hellfire (2 copies) when you are at zero to instantly jump back up to 5. Hell Rides With Me also generates 2 Hellfire while refunding the action you spent to play it.
When your Hellfire is high, defend with Control The Demon. It has no printed value, but gains +1 for every Hellfire you hold without forcing you to spend any, giving you a free 4- or 5-value block. When you are completely empty, block with Stoke The Flames. It is only a 2, but it gives you Hellfire equal to the combat damage you take, effectively trading your health to get your engine running again.
**Watch out for:** Be careful with I Brought The Devil With Me. It is a 3-value attack that lets you spend 2 Hellfire for an extra action. Several sources warn that spending nearly half your starting Hellfire for one action is a trap that leaves your defenses too weak for the opponent's next turn.

### Plan C: The mutual destruction finish

**Goal:** End the game with a massive, unblockable zone attack when the opponent is low on health.
**How to do it:** Blaze of Glory is an attack that lets you spend any amount of Hellfire to deal that much damage to every fighter in Ghost Rider's zone, including himself. Once the opponent's hero is at 5 health or less, make sure you share a zone, attack, and spend 5 Hellfire. Even if this damage reduces both you and the opponent to zero health at the same time, the rules state that the active player wins the tie.
**Watch out for:** If the opponent plays a Feint, your Hellfire is wasted, and you are left resolving a basic 2-value attack. Bait out their cancels with your 5-value attacks before attempting this. Because it damages you as well, it only works as a finisher; using it mid-game to clear sidekicks hurts you too much.

### How they lose

​- He runs out of Hellfire. Without it, his printed defense cards mostly sit at values of 2 or 3, making him easy to crush with heavy attacks.
​- Opponents force him to discard cards randomly and hit Chains of Hellfire, permanently breaking his economy.
​- Fighters with strong damage reduction (Luke Cage) or heavy healing (Little Red) outpace his slow, steady movement damage.
​- Fighters with direct, unblockable damage effects (Sherlock Holmes) bypass his Hellfire-scaled blocks.

### Key cards

​- **Chains of Hellfire**: a scheme that sets your Hellfire to 5. The absolute backbone of your resource economy. Use it when you are empty.
​- **Penance Stare**: a 3-value versatile that adds the opponent's boost value to yours. You can spend 2 Hellfire to do it a second time. This is devastating against fighters who rely on large boost values (like Alice or the T-Rex). Against a 4-boost card, spending the Hellfire turns this into an 11-value swing.
​- **Control The Demon**: his best value block, provided you have 4 or 5 Hellfire. It costs no resources to use, but playing it at zero Hellfire leaves it as a complete blank.
​- **Hell Rides With Me**: a scheme that moves Ghost Rider and an adjacent fighter up to 4 spaces, grants 2 Hellfire, and refunds the action. Excellent for dragging an enemy away from your side of the board and topping up your resources.
​- **Blaze of Glory**: your primary game-ending tool.

### Tips

​- Use Spirit Of Vengeance (value 5) to pressure the opponent early. You can spend 1 Hellfire to draw two cards, which helps keep your hand full of defensive options.
​- I Finally Escaped Hell is generally considered his weakest card. Save it to run away after combat, or throw it out as an attack just to draw out an opponent's Feint.
​- Deal With The Devil is a 2-value block that increases by 1 for every Hellfire you spend. Save it for massive incoming attacks; spending Hellfire just to block small hits will leave you drained.
​- The Wicked Will Burn grants 2 Hellfire if you started the turn in a different space. It is much easier to trigger this on your own turn as an attack than hoping to trigger it on defense.
​- You are virtually impossible to pin. As long as you have 1 Hellfire, you can use your maneuver ability to drive straight through the enemies boxing you in.
