## She-Hulk

**In short:** A tough melee fighter who uses her massive health and a zone-based ability to control the tempo. She wins by forcing the opponent to approach, crushing their hand with discards, and trapping them for devastating combo attacks.

### Plan A: Force them to approach

**Goal:** Deal free damage and make the opponent take the initiative.
**How to do it:** Her ability, Just Throw Something, lets you discard a card at the start of your turn to deal its boost value to an enemy in your zone. Because she has 20 health, healing from Double Jeopardy, and cards that drain the opponent's deck, she naturally wins the exhaustion race. Opponents cannot afford to wait, so they have to come to her. Stand in the center of large zones. When they maneuver into your zone to attack, hit them with your ability before taking your actions.
**Watch out for:** Sources disagree on how aggressively to use this ability. Some recommend throwing low-value cards every turn to keep your hand size manageable while you double maneuver, letting the opponent gas out. Others warn that discarding cards too often accelerates your own exhaustion clock and leaves you without the tools needed for big attacks.

### Plan B: The two-action trap

**Goal:** Land a massive 9-value attack.
**How to do it:** The Savage She-Hulk (2 copies, boost 3) has a printed value of 3, but spending an additional action during combat makes it a 9. You need two actions to play it this way, meaning you cannot maneuver into position and attack on the same turn. Set this up with schemes: Leap Toward moves her up to 4 spaces through fighters and grants an action if she ends adjacent, while Jennifer Walters, Esq. places her in any starting space and grants an action. Alternatively, defend with Nerve Cluster Strike (3 copies, value 3). It stops the attacker from leaving their space, ensuring you start your next turn adjacent with both actions ready.
**Watch out for:** Opponents know this attack requires an action, so they will hold their Feints for the first attack of your turn.

### Plan C: Destroy their hand and deck

**Goal:** Strip the opponent of their best cards and advance their exhaustion clock.
**How to do it:** Use the threat of The Savage She-Hulk as bait. When you attack on your first action, the opponent may play a cancel or a weak block, expecting the 9. Instead, hit them with Omega-Level Threat (2 copies, value 5). If it deals 2 or more combat damage, they discard two random cards. Two random discards cripple any fighter. Once their hand is weak, play Sensational (3 copies, value 4) to shuffle an Omega-Level Threat from your discard pile back into your deck, setting up a loop.

On defense, Lady Justice (3 copies) discards the top card of their deck and increases its own value by twice that card's boost. It protects her while permanently removing tools from their deck.
**Watch out for:** Lady Justice relies on the opponent's boost values. Against fighters with many 1-boost cards or effects without printed boost values (like Sinbad), it barely blocks.

### How they lose

​- Mobile ranged fighters stay out of her zone and attack from a distance, forcing her to chase them with a move of 2.
​- Her printed defense values are low. If Lady Justice misses, heavy hitters chew through her health.
​- The Defense Rests (2 copies, value 2) draws a card for every point of combat damage taken. Massive attacks turn this into forced card draw, ruining her exhaustion strategy.
​- Lady Justice's effect happens during combat, so an offensive cancel stops it completely, leaving her vulnerable to the full attack.
​- Her ability requires discarding. Action-heavy, aggressive opponents can empty her hand, preventing her from throwing anything.

### Key cards

​- **The Savage She-Hulk**: Her primary win condition and the only card in her deck with a boost of 3, making it her hardest-hitting throw. Sometimes the threat of it is more valuable than actually playing it.
​- **Omega-Level Threat**: Two random discards can win the game on the spot. Punish opponents who try to value-block it with 3s.
​- **Sensational**: Her recursion. Loop it to bring back attacks, or use it to retrieve Leap Toward if you need mobility.
​- **Green Energy**: A 4-value versatile that deals 1 damage to all adjacent fighters if you win. Her most reliable standard block.
​- **Cease and Desist**: A 1-value versatile cancel. If played on attack, it becomes a 4. Use it aggressively to break through defensive effects.

### Tips

​- Avoid fighting near zone borders. Ranged fighters can stand in adjacent spaces that belong to a different zone, safely ignoring your ability.
​- Sources disagree on Double Jeopardy in the late game: some argue drawing two cards ruins your exhaustion lead, while others say it acts like a free maneuver that heals you.
​- Attack with Legalese (value 2) to deliberately lose the combat. As the loser, you choose whether both players draw or discard. Use it to force the opponent to draw, pushing them closer to exhaustion.
​- In a fatigue race, play Leap Toward but deliberately end your movement away from the opponent. It acts as a safe pass that doesn't draw a card.
