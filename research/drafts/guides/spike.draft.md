## Spike

**In short:** A melee fighter with a deck full of versatile cards who relies on map control. He places shadow tokens to trigger his best card effects, but struggles if the opponent refuses to step into them.

### Plan A: Lure them into the shadows

**Goal:** Place shadow tokens in key paths and trick the opponent when they enter them.
**How to do it:** Spike's strategy revolves around his shadow tokens. His special ability lets you place one in an adjacent space at the start of every turn, even if a fighter is already standing there. Seek the Shadows (4 copies) speeds this up by drawing two cards and placing tokens on Spike's space and every adjacent space. Never stray far from your tokens; double maneuver to force the opponent to approach you through them. When an opponent first steps onto a shadow, attack with Regroup (3 copies, value 1). They will expect a heavy hit and play a Feint. You draw a card and save your real threats. Once they are fighting in the shadows, Let's Dance (3 copies, value 4) lets you boost your combat value, and The Rush (2 copies) becomes a 5-value attack.
**Watch out for:** You only have three shadow tokens. Once they are all on the board, your ability only lets you move one of them to an adjacent space. If you maneuver away from your tokens, opponents will just wait you out.

### Plan B: Pin and punish

**Goal:** Trap an opposing fighter so they cannot escape your shadow effects.
**How to do it:** Savvy opponents will avoid your tokens entirely. To force the issue, use Spike and Drusilla to physically pin an enemy. Once they are trapped, your start-of-turn ability lets you drop a shadow token directly under them. With the opponent pinned on a shadow token, play Drusilla's scheme The Sight (2 copies). It lets you look at their hand and force them to discard two cards of your choice, completely stripping their best defenses. You can then follow up with Bloody Hell! to deal guaranteed post-combat damage for each shadow token adjacent to them.
**Watch out for:** Pinning requires committing both your fighters to the front line. If Drusilla dies, you lose access to The Sight and Always Surprising, her blind-boost versatile that hits for around 5 when she is on a shadow token.

### Plan C: Defend and reposition

**Goal:** Use defense cards to slide enemies into the shadows.
**How to do it:** Twenty-one of his thirty cards are versatiles. This gives him a lot of defensive flexibility when an opponent finally approaches. Defend with Leap Away or Skirmish (both 4-value versatiles). If you win the combat, these cards let you move a fighter. You can push the attacker directly onto a shadow token at the end of their turn, setting them up for an attack on yours. Alternatively, move Spike to the other side of your shadow tokens, forcing the opponent to walk through them again to reach you.
**Watch out for:** These movement effects only happen if you win the combat. His cards are highly dependent on the tokens; without them, most of his deck is just weak printed numbers.

### How they lose

​- Ranged fighters stay back and shoot, never needing to step on a shadow token.
​- Opponents recognize how his deck works and simply refuse to approach, forcing Spike to abandon his shadows to fight.
​- Without shadow tokens, his cards lose their extra text and become easy to defend against.
​- Drusilla dies early, taking away his best forced discard effect.
​- He is highly map-dependent. Open maps allow enemies to walk around the shadows safely, while he needs tight corridors to force engagements.
​- He is widely considered one of the weakest fighters in one-on-one games.

### Key cards

​- **Seek the Shadows**: a scheme that places shadow tokens on Spike and all adjacent spaces. Your best way to gain map control early.
​- **The Sight**: a scheme for Drusilla. If the opponent is on a shadow, you look at their hand and discard two cards of your choice. A devastating hand disruption tool.
​- **Let's Dance**: a 4-value versatile. If either fighter is on a shadow token, you may boost it.
​- **Bloody Hell!**: a 3-value versatile that deals 1 damage after combat, plus 1 more for every shadow token adjacent to the opponent.
​- **Leap Away**: a 4-value versatile that moves either fighter up to 4 spaces if you win the combat. Use it to push enemies into shadows or escape.

### Tips

​- Double maneuver frequently to retreat behind your shadows and force the enemy to burn their cards just to reach you.
​- Use Empathy when Drusilla is *not* on a shadow token. Drawing three cards from its shadow effect often bloats your hand and forces you to discard, so the standard one-card draw is safer.
​- Arrogance is a 4-value attack that becomes a 6 if you discard your hand. It is meant to be intimidating, but discarding your whole hand leaves you defenseless and is rarely worth the cost.
​- Always Surprising doubles its blind boost if Drusilla is on a shadow. Most of your deck's boosts are 2s, making it a 5-value card on average.
​- He performs better in 2v2 team formats, where the board is crowded and shadow tokens are much harder for opponents to avoid.
