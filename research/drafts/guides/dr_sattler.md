## Dr. Sattler

**In short:** A highly defensive fighter who outlasts opponents with massive amounts of healing. She avoids early combat to scatter insight tokens across the map, then cashes them in for heavy attacks or health recovery, relying on Dr. Malcolm to speed up her setup.

### Plan A: Stall and heal to outlast them

**Goal:** Avoid early fights and heal through the opponent's damage.
**How to do it:** Her game revolves around placing all five insight tokens. Moving to a new space drops a token, so a double maneuver with both Sattler and Malcolm places four tokens at once. Run away until the board is full. Once it is, Woman Inherits the Earth can heal Sattler for up to 5 health. Play it on defense: its effect happens immediately, so it resolves before an attacker's Feint can cancel it. Life Finds a Way adds even more healing by forcing the opponent to discard the top card of their deck and healing both of your fighters by its boost value. Together, these cards let her outlive the opponent's best attacks.
**Watch out for:** Woman Inherits the Earth removes all insight from the board. Don't play it if it leaves you with a hand full of cards that need tokens to function, and be prepared to spend your next turn running to replace them.

### Plan B: Damage without winning combats

**Goal:** Punish the opponent with automatic damage and area effects.
**How to do it:** Because her attack values are generally low, she relies on effects. Violently, If Necessary deals 2 damage to every enemy on or adjacent to an insight token after combat. It doesn't remove the tokens, making it highly repeatable area damage that easily clears out multiple sidekicks (like Yennenga's archers). Lock The Doors! deals 2 damage directly to the attacker after combat, regardless of adjacency, which punishes ranged fighters like Robin Hood.
**Watch out for:** Her main attack threat is You Never Had Control, That's the Illusion. With five tokens out, it hits for 7, but it removes all tokens afterward. Opponents will save their Feints for this card, and if canceled, it deals no damage—though your tokens will remain on the board because the removal effect is also canceled.

### Plan C: Maximize Dr. Malcolm's token engine

**Goal:** Keep your sidekick alive to double your token production.
**How to do it:** Malcolm is the key to getting tokens down quickly. While he is alive, every maneuver action generates two tokens if both fighters move to a new space. If he dies, your token production is cut in half, making it much harder to reset after playing your big cards. He also has unique utility cards: the deck's only scheme, The Future Ex-Mrs. Malcolm, provides an action, two cards, and a token, while Chaotician places a token anywhere without needing to move.
**Watch out for:** Opponents know Malcolm powers her engine and enables Life Finds a Way, so they will try to kill him early. With only 7 health and mostly low-value blocks, he is fragile. Use Hey! Hey! Hey! to move him up to 4 spaces out of danger if he gets cornered.

### How they lose

- Malcolm dies early, cutting her insight generation in half and turning off his deck-milling heal.
- Opponents Feint her You Never Had Control, That's the Illusion attacks, leaving her with no way to punch through a solid defense.
- Aggressive fighters (like Bruce Lee or the Raptors) corner her before she gets tokens down, overwhelming her low defense values.
- Her deck relies heavily on four single-copy cards. While they offer unique effects, drawing them inconsistently can disrupt her game plan.
- I Think We're Back In Business has a printed value of zero. If an opponent Feints it, the card provides no defense against massive attacks.

### Key cards

- **Woman Inherits the Earth**: Her best defensive card, which also draws a replacement card. Always play it on defense to dodge offensive cancels.
- **You Never Had Control, That's the Illusion**: Her primary way to deal burst damage. Try to wait until all five tokens are on the board before swinging with it.
- **Life Finds a Way**: Malcolm's signature tool. It reduces the opponent's options by milling a card and provides vital healing for both fighters.
- **I Think We're Back In Business**: A versatile card whose block value equals your hand size. Save it as your fifth or sixth card in hand to block massive attacks from fighters like Mr. Hyde.
- **Sexism in Survival Situations**: A 1-value versatile that jumps to 4 against heroes. After combat, it moves Sattler 1 space, allowing her to safely drop a fresh insight token.

### Tips

- Be deliberate with your maneuvers. Tokens stay where they are dropped until a card removes them, so do not stack multiple tokens on the same space.
- Against ranged fighters, leave insight tokens one space deep into a zone so the enemy has to step adjacent to them, setting up Violently, If Necessary.
- Keep your hand full. It powers up I Think We're Back In Business and deters the opponent from attacking.
- You can place insight tokens on top of other fighters' tokens, such as Muldoon's traps or the Invisible Man's fog.
- Card effects that explicitly say "place" your fighter (like Must Go Faster or The Concept of Attraction) do not trigger her special ability to drop a token, because placing is not considered "moving."
- Use The Concept of Attraction to teleport to any insight token and escape enemy pressure, but remember it becomes a dead card immediately after a board wipe.
