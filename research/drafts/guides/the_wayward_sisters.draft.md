## The Wayward Sisters

**In short:** A trio of low-health melee heroes who control the board by dropping played cards into a cauldron to cast spells. They pin opponents, heal constantly, and punish big attacks with auto-damage.

### Plan A: Pin and pressure with healing

**Goal:** Trap the opponent and maintain an aggressive cycle of attacking, drawing, and healing.
**How to do it:** The Wayward Sisters excel at controlling where the opponent stands. Use your spell that costs a snake and a lizard leg to move any fighter up to 3 spaces, or defend with Ward, to drop the enemy hero squarely between two or three of your sisters. Once they are pinned, attack relentlessly and cast your healing spell (snake and bat) afterward. This draws a card and recovers 1 health, keeping your hand full of options and offsetting the damage they deal when fighting back.
**Watch out for:** You draw a card every time you cast the healing spell. If the game goes to exhaustion, this forced draw deals damage to your fighters and will quickly kill your 6-health heroes.

### Plan B: Chip them down without big attacks

**Goal:** Defeat 1-health sidekicks and wear down heroes without relying on combat damage.
**How to do it:** The Sisters have almost no large printed attack values to break through strong blocks. Instead, use your damage spell (lizard leg and bat) to deal 1 guaranteed damage to any fighter sharing a zone with any of your sisters. This snipes 1-health sidekicks safely and slowly grinds down heroes who lack healing, like Black Widow. On defense, Pricking Of My Thumbs deals damage back equal to the combat damage you took. This turns your opponent's largest attacks into massive unavoidable damage against them.
**Watch out for:** Casting any spell empties your entire cauldron. If you had three or four cards in it, casting a two-ingredient spell discards them all.

### Plan C: Maximize your ingredients

**Goal:** Cast spells without emptying your hand too quickly.
**How to do it:** Throwing three cards into the cauldron just to cast a two-ingredient spell wastes your resources. To build your cauldron efficiently, use cards that add ingredients directly from the discard pile. All-Seeing Familiar and Fire Burn And Cauldron Bubble immediately return a card from your discard pile to your cauldron. This gives you two ingredient symbols for the price of one played card, letting you cast spells out of nowhere. If your cauldron gets too full and you do not want to waste the symbols on a cheap spell, Unnatural Remedy lets you discard up to three of them to heal 1 health per card.
**Watch out for:** Hurly-Burly lets the opponent choose whether to put your top deck card into your hand or your cauldron. They will always pick the option that ruins your current cauldron setup or denies you a useful card. Sources disagree on the value of the action spell (which requires all three symbols): one finds it too expensive and prefers sticking to the two-symbol spells, while another values the flexibility of an extra turn.

### How they lose

​- Opponents with area damage (Little Red) or multi-target attacks hit all three sisters at once, quickly burning through their combined 18 health.
​- Aggressive fighters (Achilles, She-Hulk) focus a single sister down early. Losing a sister removes board presence and permanently weakens The Stars Align.
​- They draw heavy hands of defense cards. With seven purely defensive cards in their deck, their offensive pressure can completely stall.
​- Hand and deck disruption forces them to discard, ruining their carefully planned cauldron management and depriving them of ingredients.
​- Opponents refuse to use their big attacks on Pricking Of My Thumbs, instead forcing the Sisters to block smaller attacks with weak values.

### Key cards

​- **Pricking Of My Thumbs**: a 2-value defense that deals damage to the attacker equal to the damage you took. It makes massive attacks from fighters like Bigfoot or Luke a terrible trade for the opponent.
​- **Something Wicked This Way Comes**: a scheme that casts any two different spells without needing ingredients or emptying the cauldron. Use it to ping a sidekick and move a hero into a pin, or heal and gain an action in the same turn.
​- **All-Seeing Familiar**: a 4-value attack that immediately adds a card from the discard to your cauldron. It is their only printed 4, making it your best tool to punish a Feint while setting up a spell.
​- **Double, Double**: a 3-value attack that gets +1 for each different symbol in the cauldron. It can hit for 6, but heavily telegraphs your attack to the opponent.
​- **The Stars Align**: a 0-value versatile worth the number of different zones your fighters occupy. Play it early while all three sisters are alive to reliably block for 4 or 5.

### Tips

​- Treat your health as a single shared pool. Move heavily damaged sisters to the edges of the board and block with the healthy ones.
​- Remember you can only cast a spell after you attack, not after you defend.
​- Use the movement spell to push opponents onto bad spaces, like the pit on Raptor Paddock, or force them to boost just to reach you.
​- Curious Familiar returns a card from the cauldron to your hand. Use it to grab a second copy of a key card like Pricking Of My Thumbs before it gets discarded by a spell.
​- If an opponent's card effect targets "a hero", a rules update allows you to choose which of your three sisters takes the effect.
​- If the opponent refuses to approach, play Prophecy to stack your deck and drop a needed ingredient into the cauldron.
