## Annie Christmas

**In short:** A melee fighter who hits harder when she is injured. She manages her health carefully to trigger her attack boost, uses her ranged sidekick Charlie for positioning and chip damage, and relies on a powerful defense card to survive lethal turns.

### Plan A: Manage health for bigger hits

**Goal:** Trigger her special ability without taking unnecessary risks.
**How to do it:** Annie's special ability adds 2 to her attacks if her health is lower than the defender's. Because she starts at 14 health, she often begins the game with less health than her opponent. As the game goes on, the trick is keeping her health close to the opponent's. Lagniappe (3 copies, value 5) is her best tool for this. Its effect lets you deal up to 2 damage to yourself immediately, before combat. If your health is tied or slightly higher, you can drop your health to trigger the ability, turning it into a 7-value attack while drawing cards.
**Watch out for:** Dropping your health too low too early. Keeping her health lower than the opponent's at all times is dangerous and can cost you the game.

### Plan B: The one-health wall

**Goal:** Survive huge offensive turns and fight back from the brink.
**How to do it:** Mississippi Queen (3 copies) is a 2-value defense that prevents her health from dropping below 1 for the rest of the turn. This stops both combat damage and effect damage. It shuts down fighters who string together multiple actions in a single turn, like Bruce Lee, the Raptors, the Genie, or Bloody Mary. You can intentionally play at 1 health to guarantee her attack boost, relying on this card to stay alive.
**Watch out for:** It only protects her for that specific turn. Against certain opponents, you end up just as vulnerable on their next turn. Play it when you plan to finish the game on your next action, or hold all three copies to buy multiple turns of survival.

### Plan C: Swap and shoot with Charlie

**Goal:** Bypass her low movement and deal damage from a distance.
**How to do it:** Annie is a melee fighter with only 2 movement, which makes chasing ranged enemies difficult. Charlie is ranged and doesn't get her attack boost, so opponents might ignore him. Use him as bait. When they attack him, play Slick Talker (defense 3) to swap their spaces and make Annie the defender, putting her in melee range for your next turn. On offense, Charlie hits hard with Long Shot, which is a 5 if the fighters aren't adjacent. He also uses Bottom Dealing, an attack that boosts itself with the bottom card of your deck to usually hit for 4 or more.
**Watch out for:** Charlie only has 8 health. If he dies, you lose Slick Talker and your only ranged attacks.

### How they lose

​- She lacks a traditional Feint. Quite a Pair is her only way to cancel effects, and it requires you to hold two cards with the exact same name in your hand.
​- Her low base movement makes it hard to catch ranged kiting fighters if Charlie is dead or out of position.
​- Players who lower her health too aggressively get picked off before they can draw Mississippi Queen.

### Key cards

​- **Lagniappe**: her strongest attack. Take damage to draw cards and trigger her ability immediately, before the opponent defends.
​- **Mississippi Queen**: her ultimate survival tool. It keeps her alive against multi-attack turns and guaranteed effect damage.
​- **Slick Talker**: a 3-value defense that swaps Annie and Charlie. Use it to close the gap or save a dying fighter from a fatal blow.
​- **Quite a Pair**: her unique cancel. You don't discard the revealed pair to use the effect, you just show them to the opponent, so you can keep them for later.
​- **Captain's Orders**: a scheme that places Annie and another friendly fighter anywhere in Annie's zone, then gives you an action. A great way to engage without maneuvering.

### Tips

​- A Few More Pearls deals 2 damage to every adjacent enemy. Use it for guaranteed damage that ignores defense cards.
​- Striking Beauty is a 1-value versatile that deals extra damage. If you attack with it while your ability is active, it becomes a 3-value attack that guarantees at least 1 effect damage.
​- Use Long Shot as a defense against ranged attackers. Because they are not adjacent, it blocks for 5.
​- In team modes (2v2 or Co-op), Better Together and Captain's Orders work on your teammates' heroes as well, letting you heal them or move them into position.
