## Tomoe Gozen

**In short:** A ranged fighter who prefers to fight up close. She uses forced movement to trigger her ping ability, relies on high-value attacks against adjacent targets, and has strong defenses that specifically punish enemy heroes.

### Plan A: Control their movement

**Goal:** Constantly trigger her ability by forcing the opposing hero to leave her zone.
**How to do it:** Her Attack of Opportunity special ability deals 1 damage whenever an opposing hero leaves her zone. This triggers whether they maneuver away or are pushed by a card effect. Stay aggressive to make them run, and use cards that displace them. A Warrior's Way moves the opposing fighter; push them across zone borders to secure the damage, then use the card's effect to teleport adjacent to them. Skirmish (a 4-value versatile) can push the opponent up to 2 spaces if you win the combat. Keep the pressure up by drawing with Piercing Shot so they are forced to spend their turns maneuvering away.
**Watch out for:** The ability only triggers when *they* leave the zone, not when she does. It also strictly affects heroes, meaning opponents can safely move sidekicks in and out of her zone without taking damage. On maps with massive zones, fighters can maneuver freely without ever crossing a border to trigger the ability.

### Plan B: The face-up execution

**Goal:** Land a massive hit with Witness My Last Battle.
**How to do it:** Witness My Last Battle must be played face up and requires Tomoe Gozen to be adjacent. Use A Warrior's Way or Lord Kiso's Final Stand to close the distance. Because it is known before they defend, use it to finish off a low-health hero who has exhausted their strong blocks, or throw it at a high-health sidekick to clear them off the board quickly.
**Watch out for:** If the target survives, they start their turn right next to you, guaranteeing a double attack opportunity since this card instantly ends your turn. Because they see the massive hit coming, they will use their best defense or a punishing tool like a damage-reflecting card.

### Plan C: Choose their discard

**Goal:** Look at their hand and strip away their best card.
**How to do it:** Confront Any Demon or God draws a card, and if you lose the combat, lets you look at their hand and choose a card for them to discard. You want to guarantee you lose the exchange to secure the discard. Against fighters who rely on low-value blocks, attack with it; they will likely defend with a 3, meaning the attacker loses the tie. Against heavy hitters throwing 4- or 5-value attacks, play it on defense to safely lose the combat and rip away their remaining threats.
**Watch out for:** Guessing wrong. If you attack and they play a 2-value block, you win the combat and miss out on the discard completely.

### How they lose

- Her best cards require the opponent to be a hero. Flash of Steel is her only cancel, and A Worthy Opponent is her strongest block, but both only trigger against heroes. Sidekicks hit her with their full effects.
- She has no area damage to clear swarms of 1-health sidekicks, meaning they can easily body-block her and soak her single-target hits.
- Her health pool is only 14. If you have to use the action gain on Lord Kiso's Final Stand instead of its healing option, she can die quickly to unblocked damage.

### Key cards

- **Witness My Last Battle**: A 7-value attack that ends your turn after combat.
- **A Warrior's Way**: A 2-value attack that moves the opponent up to 3 spaces, then places you adjacent to them if they are a hero.
- **Confront Any Demon or God**: A 3-value versatile that triggers the chosen discard.
- **Lord Kiso's Final Stand**: Her only scheme. It moves her up to 3 spaces through sidekicks, then lets you choose between recovering 2 health or gaining 1 action.
- **Flash of Steel**: A 2-value versatile cancel that only works on heroes. If they are adjacent, it deals 1 damage.

### Tips

- Use Fearsome Strength (a 3-value attack) to immediately swap spaces before combat. It breaks pins and secures item tokens.
- Keep track of A Worthy Opponent. It is a 3-value versatile that becomes a 5 against heroes.
- Throw Five Against Thousands at multi-health sidekicks. It is a 4-value attack with no hero requirements.
- The sources completely disagree on Deeds of Valor (a 2-value defense that caps the combat damage you take at 2). One calls it massively valuable for risking little health, while another rates it the worst card in her deck because it does nothing against average 3-value attacks and fails to prevent effect damage. If you find it useless in a matchup, discard it to trigger the return effect of Five Against Thousands.
- Keep Patroclus alive when facing Achilles. If the sidekick dies, Achilles will aggressively attack to draw cards, completely ignoring your zone-exit damage.
