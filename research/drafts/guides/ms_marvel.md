## Ms. Marvel

**In short:** A highly mobile melee fighter who attacks from two spaces away and relies on careful map positioning. She forces opponents to chase her, hits hard with a full hand, and punishes enemies when they stand in different zones.

### Plan A: Control the zones

**Goal:** Attack from a different zone to trigger her best card effects.
**How to do it:** Her Stretchy ability permits a one-space shift at the beginning of your turn, and lets her target enemies up to two spaces away. Combined, you have a five-space threat range without spending actions. Because these still count as melee attacks, they do not trigger ranged-specific defense effects. Use this reach to strike from a space that shares no colors with the defender's space. Doing so allows you to boost Big Wind Up (a base 4 attack) or draw two cards with I'm Not Touching You. For Embiggen, you need to stand in more zones than your opponent, making spaces where three zones meet incredibly powerful. Pick your battleground wisely: look for maps with small zones, sharp boundary cutoffs, or tri-zone spaces, such as Raptor Paddock, Globe Theater, or Sarpedon.
**Watch out for:** Foes who retreat to large, single-colored areas or force you to stand in shared zones. On maps featuring massive continuous zones, like Navy Pier, activating these requirements becomes incredibly frustrating.

### Plan B: Hit hard with a big hand

**Goal:** Keep your hand near the limit to power up your attacks and gain extra effects.
**How to do it:** Various abilities unlock entirely only when you hold at least four cards. Gyro and Fries refunds its action cost so you can heal without sacrificing tempo, while Easy Peasy guarantees adjacent damage. Fangirl's power scales directly with your held cards. A maximum hand of seven yields a 6-value swing, since the card leaves your hand before the numerical check. Use I'm Not Touching You to replenish your options. When aggressive fighters relentlessly attack to empty your hand of defenses, Friends and Family immediately redraws your hand up to seven.
**Watch out for:** Discard effects played during combat, like Ambush. These shrink your hand before Easy Peasy checks your count after the engagement, so try to defend with it while holding five or six cards. Players debate how to optimize Friends and Family: some deploy it strictly for the draw, while others reserve it to boost Big Wind Up for massive damage, as it is her only 4-boost option.

### Plan C: Kite and punish with Momentous Shift

**Goal:** Force them to waste actions catching you, then hit them for 5.
**How to do it:** Taking your free Stretchy step counts as starting the turn in a new space. This guarantees Momentous Shift will hit for 5 on your first action without a maneuver. If an enemy ends their turn next to you, step away for free and use your natural reach to strike them safely. Melee combatants must then waste an action walking back toward you to retaliate, leaving them with just one action to test your relatively weak blocks.
**Watch out for:** Ranged enemies ignore this kiting entirely since they do not need to walk to hit you. Likewise, melee fighters with after-combat movement—such as Sinbad—can close the gap immediately when defending, ready to unleash a double attack on their next turn.

### How they lose

- Most of her blocks cap at 3. If she gets cornered by heavy hitters, her 14 health pool will vanish quickly.
- She relies heavily on map geography; unfavorable layouts neutralize her primary offensive and defensive tools.
- Opponents will anticipate Big Wind Up and play Feint, stripping away the boost.
- Overusing scheme cards or drawing constantly can accelerate deck exhaustion if you fail to balance aggression with patience.

### Key cards

- **Big Wind Up**: Her main offensive threat. Use it when no zones are shared.
- **Momentous Shift**: A versatile card that easily achieves its maximum potential thanks to her start-of-turn shift.
- **Fangirl**: A 0-value printed attack that turns into a massive swing with a stocked hand. It works perfectly to bait out Feints, protecting your boostable attacks without sacrificing damage if canceled.
- **Embiggen**: Her best defensive option. Reaching 6 value when she occupies more zones than the attacker, it is fantastic for camping tri-zone spaces to safely block incoming damage.
- **Slingshot**: A 3-value defense that teleports her anywhere within her current zone, helping set up the perfect distance and zone conditions for the following turn.
- **Friends and Family**: A fantastic reset tool against hand depletion, or a massive combat trick when discarded.

### Tips

- Always take your start-of-turn step to manipulate spacing, even if only retreating to force an enemy to spend a maneuver.
- The ping damage from Easy Peasy hits any adjacent fighter, not just your combat opponent. This is excellent for swatting 1-health sidekicks like Squirrel Girl's squirrels or Robin Hood's outlaws.
- The ping damage is mandatory, so be extremely careful not to accidentally wound your ally in team matches.
- Save Shrink! Shrink! Shrink! to defend against melee attackers. Stripping a card and throwing them three spaces backward often prevents them from reaching you for a second attack.
- Gyro and Fries effectively functions as a pass. By healing 2 and refunding the action, you can stall for time without wasting a turn tossing cards from your hand.
- I'm Not Touching You is an excellent attack to bait a Feint. Because it starts at a 4-value, if they cancel the draw effect with a 2-value block, they still take 2 damage, and you save your bigger tools for later.
