## Pandora

**In short:** A push-your-luck melee fighter who offsets weak printed defenses with huge scaling attacks and relentless healing. She opens a box of miseries every turn to buff her cards, balancing massive potential turns against the risk of self-damage.

### Plan A: Scale up the big turns

**Goal:** Maximize the box's effects without taking unnecessary damage.
**How to do it:** Pandora's Box is a separate deck of seven miseries you reveal at the start of your turn. Three miseries are safe, three have one feather, and Misfortune has two. If you reveal three feathers total, you bust and take a damage for each revealed card. Unless you have a specific reason, flip conservatively, stop at one or two cards, and accept the small buffs.

You only push your luck when you have Hera's Curiosity or Aphrodite's Beauty in hand. Hera's Curiosity adds +1 to its value for every revealed misery, turning a 3-value attack into an 8 or 9. If you do bust, the miseries remain revealed for the turn. You take the damage, but you can immediately attack with Aphrodite's Beauty to heal 1 health for every revealed misery, erasing the penalty while canceling their defense.
**Watch out for:** Feints ruin these massive setups. If your opponent has cancels, bait them out with a normal attack or a blind boost before throwing your fully scaled Hera's Curiosity.

### Plan B: Stay aggressive to hide weak defenses

**Goal:** Pressure the opponent constantly and teleport in for double attacks.
**How to do it:** Her defenses are terrible, relying on 0s and 2s, so you want to dictate the tempo. Position yourself aggressively. Forged by Hephaestus teleports any of your fighters anywhere and gives you an action, letting you drop right next to an enemy for a double attack without spending boosts to maneuver. Zeus's Mischief moves an enemy closer, draws a card for every revealed misery, and refunds the action. Use these scheme cards to stay on top of the opponent and force them to spend their best blocks on normal attacks.
**Watch out for:** Zeus's Mischief can instantly deck you out if you play it with four or five miseries revealed. Watch your deck count closely.

### Plan C: Scrape your health back up

**Goal:** Out-heal the damage you take from bad blocks and the box.
**How to do it:** Because her defense cards like Offering to the Gods are low, you will take hits. Your longevity comes entirely from healing back the damage you take. Guided by the Fates (3 copies) is a versatile that blind boosts and heals Pandora for 2 if you win the combat. Even if a Kakodaemon plays it, Pandora gets the heal.

Once your health drops below 4, you are cut off from using your box aggressively, because busting would kill you. When that happens, play carefully and use Guided by the Fates or a small Aphrodite's Beauty to climb back to a safe health total before gambling again.
**Watch out for:** Blind boosting with Guided by the Fates discards the top card of your deck. You might lose a critical Hera's Curiosity or an important defense.

### How they lose

​- Opponents with high-value defenses and lots of cancels (Bigfoot, Tomoe) block Hera's Curiosity easily and Feint her big heals.
​- She pushes her luck on the box when she doesn't have the hand to capitalize on it, bleeding out from self-damage.
​- She runs out of cards to exhaustion, accelerated by the heavy card draw on Zeus's Mischief.
​- The opponent gets her to low health and she fails to draw her healing, leaving her unable to risk opening the box.
​- Her 0-value defenses get caught by massive printed attacks when she doesn't have a cancel ready.

### Key cards

​- **Hera's Curiosity**: her main threat. With a boost of 4, it's also her best boost fuel if you draw it at the wrong time.
​- **Aphrodite's Beauty**: a 0-value attack that cancels effects and heals her for every revealed misery. It works as a massive heal even on a turn where you busted the box.
​- **Guided by the Fates**: a 2-value versatile that blind boosts and heals 2. The sources disagree on how to use it: one finds it too random on defense and prefers it as an attack to secure the heal, while another treats it as a necessary gamble on defense that punishes the attacker for 2 damage.
​- **Forged by Hephaestus**: a teleport that grants an action. Essential for engaging from across the board or launching multiple attacks.
​- **Zeus's Mischief**: scales with the box for massive card draw and action gain.
​- **Hindsight**: a 3-value attack that puts a discard on top of your deck if it wins. Play it when the Toil misery (+1 to Pandora's attacks) is revealed so it hits for 4.

### Tips

​- Spite deals 1 damage to *each* adjacent fighter. Make sure you don't accidentally kill your own 1-health Kakodaemons.
​- If the Hope misery (look at the top card of the box) is revealed, use the information to decide whether to push for a bigger Hera's Curiosity.
​- When blocking with Offering to the Gods, boost it with your weakest cards rather than throwing away a 3- or 4-boost card.
​- If you are hopelessly behind and going to lose anyway, ignore the math and flip the box as far as it goes to look for a miracle turn.
