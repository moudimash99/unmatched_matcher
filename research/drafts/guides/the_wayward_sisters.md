## The Wayward Sisters

**In short:** A trio of low-health melee heroes who control the board by dropping played cards into a cauldron to cast spells. They pin opponents, heal constantly, and punish big attacks with auto-damage.

### Plan A: Pin and pressure with healing

**Goal:** Trap the opponent and maintain an aggressive cycle of attacking, drawing, and healing.
**How to do it:** The Wayward Sisters excel at controlling where the opponent stands. Use your spell that costs a snake and a lizard leg to move any fighter up to 3 spaces, or play Ward on defense, placing the enemy hero squarely between your sisters. Once they are pinned, attack relentlessly and cast your healing spell (snake and bat) afterward. This recovers 1 health and draws a card, offsetting the damage they deal when fighting back.
**Watch out for:** You draw a card every time you cast the healing spell. If the game goes to exhaustion, this forced draw deals damage to your fighters and will quickly kill your 6-health heroes.

### Plan B: Chip them down without big attacks

**Goal:** Defeat 1-health sidekicks and wear down heroes without relying on combat damage.
**How to do it:** The Sisters have almost no large printed attack values to break through strong blocks. Instead, use your damage spell (lizard leg and bat) to deal 1 guaranteed damage to any fighter sharing a zone with one of your sisters. This snipes 1-health sidekicks safely and slowly grinds down heroes who lack healing, like Black Widow. On defense, Pricking Of My Thumbs turns your opponent's largest attacks into massive unavoidable damage against them.
**Watch out for:** Casting any spell empties your entire cauldron. If you had three or four cards in it, casting a two-ingredient spell discards them all.

### Plan C: Maximize your ingredients

**Goal:** Cast spells without emptying your hand too quickly.
**How to do it:** Throwing three cards into the cauldron just to cast a two-ingredient spell wastes your resources. To build your cauldron efficiently, use cards like All-Seeing Familiar and Fire Burn And Cauldron Bubble to immediately grab an ingredient from the discard pile. This gives you two ingredient symbols for the price of one played card, letting you cast spells out of nowhere. If your cauldron gets too full and you do not want to waste the symbols on a cheap spell, Unnatural Remedy lets you discard up to three of them to heal 1 health per card.
**Watch out for:** Hurly-Burly forces your opponent to choose whether your top deck card goes into your hand, or into the cauldron to boost the attack. They will always pick the option that disrupts your cauldron setup or denies a useful card. Additionally, sources disagree on the value of the action spell (which requires all three symbols): one finds it too expensive and prefers sticking to two-symbol spells, while another values the flexibility of an extra turn.

### How they lose

- Opponents with area damage (Little Red) or multi-target attacks hit all three sisters at once, quickly burning through their combined 18 health.
- Aggressive fighters (Achilles, She-Hulk) focus a single sister down early. Losing a sister removes board presence and permanently weakens The Stars Align.
- They draw heavy hands of defense cards. With seven purely defensive cards in their deck, their offensive pressure can completely stall.
- Hand disruption effects force them to discard. Because discarded cards automatically enter the cauldron, this can overfill it and waste ingredients you were saving for a specific spell.
- Opponents use offensive cancels to shut down cards that scale their values based on ingredients or zones, leaving the Sisters with weak blocks.
- Opponents refuse to use their big attacks on Pricking Of My Thumbs, instead forcing the Sisters to block smaller attacks with weak values.

### Key cards

- **Pricking Of My Thumbs**: a 2-value defense that deals damage to the attacker equal to the damage you took. It makes massive attacks from fighters like Bigfoot or Luke a terrible trade for the opponent.
- **Something Wicked This Way Comes**: a scheme that casts any two different spells without needing ingredients or emptying the cauldron. Use it to ping a sidekick and move a hero into a pin, or heal and gain an action in the same turn.
- **All-Seeing Familiar**: a 4-value attack that immediately adds a card from the discard to your cauldron. It is their only printed 4, making it your best tool to punish a Feint while setting up a spell. If you win combat, it also lets you check the top card of your deck and place it in your hand or cauldron.
- **Double, Double**: a 3-value attack that gets +1 for each different symbol in the cauldron. It can hit for 6, but heavily telegraphs your attack since the opponent can see your available ingredients.
- **The Stars Align**: a 0-value versatile worth the number of different zones your fighters occupy. Play it early while all three sisters are alive to reliably block for 4 or 5.

### Tips

- Treat your health as a single shared pool. Move heavily damaged sisters to the edges of the board and block with the healthy ones.
- Remember you can only cast a spell after you attack, not after you defend.
- Use the movement spell to push opponents onto bad spaces, like the pit on Raptor Paddock, or force them to boost just to reach you.
- Curious Familiar returns a card from the cauldron to your hand. Use it to grab a second copy of a key card before it gets discarded by a spell.
- If an opponent's card effect targets "a hero", a rules update allows you to choose which of your three sisters takes the effect.
- If the opponent refuses to approach, play Prophecy to stack your deck and drop a needed ingredient (the scheme itself) into the cauldron.
