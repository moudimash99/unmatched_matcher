## Ms. Marvel

**In short:** A highly mobile melee fighter who attacks from two spaces away and relies on careful map positioning. She forces opponents to chase her, hits hard with a full hand, and punishes enemies when they stand in different zones.

### Plan A: Control the zones

**Goal:** Attack from a different zone to trigger her best card effects.
**How to do it:** Her Stretchy ability lets her move one space at the start of her turn, and she can attack from up to two spaces away. This gives her an effective threat range of five spaces on her first action. She still counts as a melee fighter, so these strikes benefit from her positioning without counting as ranged attacks. Use that reach to attack fighters while standing in a completely different zone (meaning your space shares no colors with their space). If you pull this off, Big Wind Up lets you boost its base 4 value, and I'm Not Touching You draws two cards. Embiggen requires you to be in more zones than the defender, making spaces that touch three zones highly valuable. Map choice dictates her success: look for maps with sharp zone cutoffs, small zones, or three-zone spaces like Raptor Paddock, Globe Theater, or Sarpedon.
**Watch out for:** Opponents can maneuver to single-zone spaces or retreat to areas where you are forced to share a zone, blanking these effects. On maps with large, continuous zones like Navy Pier, triggering her cards is extremely difficult.

### Plan B: Hit hard with a big hand

**Goal:** Keep your hand near the limit to power up your attacks and gain extra effects.
**How to do it:** Several cards require you to hold four or more cards to unlock their full potential. Gyro and Fries becomes a free action to heal, letting you recover health without losing tempo. Easy Peasy deals guaranteed damage to an adjacent fighter if your hand is large enough. Fangirl's value equals the number of cards in your hand, turning it into a massive attack when your hand is full. Remember that playing a card removes it from your hand, so starting with seven cards makes Fangirl a 6. Play I'm Not Touching You to refill your hand without spending actions on maneuvers. When aggressive opponents try to exhaust you by running you out of cards, Friends and Family lets you draw back up to seven.
**Watch out for:** Discard effects that happen during combat drop your hand size before Easy Peasy checks it after combat. Against discard-heavy opponents, keep your hand at five or six to ensure the effect still triggers. Sources disagree on how to best use Friends and Family: some save it as a scheme to counter aggressive hand depletion, while others prefer to save it strictly to boost Big Wind Up for 8 damage, since it is her only 4-boost card.

### Plan C: Kite and punish with Momentous Shift

**Goal:** Force them to waste actions catching you, then hit them for 5.
**How to do it:** Stretchy's start-of-turn movement counts as starting your turn in a different space. This makes Momentous Shift an easy 5-value attack on your first action, without needing to maneuver first. If an opponent ends their turn next to you, use your free start-of-turn move to step back. Because you can attack from two spaces away, you can immediately strike them safely. To hit you back, melee fighters must spend their first action moving toward you. Because they have to maneuver, they only have one action left to attack your weak defenses.
**Watch out for:** Fighters with after-combat movement, like Sinbad, can close the distance when you attack them, putting them right back in position to double-attack you on their turn.

### How they lose

- Her printed defenses are mostly 3 or lower. If heavy hitters corner her, her 14 health drops fast.
- She is heavily map dependent. A bad map turns off her best damage and defense options.
- Smart opponents anticipate Big Wind Up and cancel it with a Feint, removing the boost and leaving it as a base 4.
- She can quickly run herself into exhaustion by drawing too many cards with I'm Not Touching You and her schemes if she does not pace her aggression.

### Key cards

- **Big Wind Up**: her main damage threat. Boost it when you share no zones.
- **Momentous Shift**: an easy 5-value versatile thanks to her start-of-turn movement. Play it aggressively.
- **Fangirl**: a 0-value attack that becomes a huge threat with a full hand. Play it to bait out Feints before using Big Wind Up, since a canceled Fangirl deals no damage but saves your boostable attack.
- **Embiggen**: her highest potential defense, reaching 6 if you are in more zones than the attacker. Also serves as a heavy attack.
- **Slingshot**: a 3-value defense that teleports her anywhere in her zone, setting up her reach and zone requirements for the next turn.
- **Friends and Family**: her only 4-boost card, or a massive hand refill.

### Tips

- Always use her start-of-turn movement to adjust your position, even if you are just stepping back to force the opponent to move.
- Easy Peasy deals its 1 damage to any adjacent fighter, not just the one in combat. This clears 1-health sidekicks, like Squirrel Girl's squirrels, easily.
- Remember that Easy Peasy damage is not optional, so be careful not to hit your partner in team games.
- Shrink! Shrink! Shrink! is best used defensively against melee fighters. Make them discard, then push them up to three spaces away so they cannot reach you with their second action.
- Gyro and Fries can be used as a pass action. If you play it with four or more cards, you heal 2 and gain the action back, allowing you to stall the board without passing your turn by discarding.
