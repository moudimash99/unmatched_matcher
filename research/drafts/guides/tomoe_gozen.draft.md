## Tomoe Gozen

**In short:** A ranged fighter who prefers to fight up close. She uses forced movement to trigger her ping ability, relies on high-value attacks against adjacent targets, and has strong defenses that specifically punish enemy heroes.

### Plan A: Control their movement

**Goal:** Constantly trigger her ability by forcing the opposing hero to leave her zone.
**How to do it:** Attack of Opportunity deals 1 damage whenever an opposing hero leaves her zone. This triggers whether they maneuver away or are pushed by a card effect. Stay aggressive and use cards that move them. A Warrior's Way (2 copies, value 2) moves the opposing fighter up to 3 spaces; use it to push them across zone borders to trigger the ping, then teleport adjacent to them. Skirmish (3 copies, value 4) can push them up to 2 spaces if you win the combat. Draw cards with Piercing Shot to keep your hand full and maintain pressure, forcing them to spend their turns maneuvering away and taking damage.
**Watch out for:** The ability only triggers when they leave the zone, not when she does. On maps with massive, single-colored zones, opponents can maneuver freely without ever crossing a zone border to trigger the damage.

### Plan B: The face-up execution

**Goal:** Land a massive hit with Witness My Last Battle.
**How to do it:** Witness My Last Battle (2 copies) is a 7-value attack that must be played face up and requires Tomoe Gozen to be adjacent. Use A Warrior's Way or Lord Kiso's Final Stand (her only scheme) to close the distance. Because it cannot be boosted and is known before they defend, use it to finish off a low-health hero who is out of strong blocks, or throw it at a high-health sidekick to clear them off the board quickly.
**Watch out for:** It ends your turn after combat. If the opponent survives, they start their turn right next to you, guaranteeing a double attack opportunity. Since they see the 7 coming, they will use their best defense or a punishing effect like Mirror Image.

### Plan C: Choose their discard

**Goal:** Look at their hand and strip away their best card.
**How to do it:** Confront Any Demon or God (2 copies, value 3) draws a card, and if you lose the combat, you look at their hand and choose a card for them to discard. You want to guarantee you lose the combat to get the discard. Against fighters with low block values, attack with it; they will likely defend with a 3, meaning the attacker loses the tie. Against heavy hitters with 4- or 5-value attacks, play it on defense to safely lose the exchange and rip away their remaining threats.
**Watch out for:** Guessing wrong. If you attack and they play a 2-value block, you win the combat and miss out on the discard entirely.

### How they lose

- Her best cards require the opponent to be a hero. Flash of Steel is her only cancel, and A Worthy Opponent is a 5-value block, but both only trigger against heroes. Sidekicks hit her with their full effects.
- She has no area damage to clear swarms of 1-health sidekicks, meaning they can easily body-block her.
- Her health pool is only 14. If you have to use the action gain on Lord Kiso's Final Stand instead of the 2 healing, she dies quickly to unblocked damage.

### Key cards

- **Witness My Last Battle**: a 7-value attack. It ends your turn and must be played face up.
- **A Warrior's Way**: a 2-value attack that moves the opponent. Her best setup card for triggering zone-exit pings and teleporting adjacent.
- **Confront Any Demon or God**: a 3-value versatile. It replaces itself and gives you a chosen discard if you lose the combat.
- **Lord Kiso's Final Stand**: a scheme that moves her up to 3 spaces through sidekicks. You then choose between 2 health or an extra action.
- **Flash of Steel**: a 2-value versatile. Her only cancel, and it only works on heroes. If they are adjacent, it deals 1 damage.

### Tips

- Use Fearsome Strength to immediately swap spaces before combat. It breaks pins and secures item tokens.
- Keep track of A Worthy Opponent. It is a 3-value versatile that becomes a 5 against heroes, making it her strongest defensive block.
- Throw Five Against Thousands at multi-health sidekicks. It has no hero requirements and hits for 4.
- The sources completely disagree on Deeds of Valor (prevents taking more than 2 combat damage). One calls it massively valuable for risking little health, while another rates it the worst card in her deck because it does nothing against average 3-value attacks and doesn't prevent effect damage. If you find it useless in a matchup, discard it to return Five Against Thousands to your hand.
