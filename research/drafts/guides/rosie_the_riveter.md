## Rosie the Riveter

**In short:** A scaling fighter who builds up power through mech suit upgrades over the game. She manages a queue of four upgrades—which stack their effects as they come online—to gain range, damage, or card draw depending on the matchup.

### Plan A: Control the upgrade line

**Goal:** Set up the best suit abilities for the current opponent.
**How to do it:** You have four upgrade tokens. They activate one by one at the start of your turn, or when triggered by your 4-value We Can Do It! attacks. The order they activate matters. When you deactivate a single upgrade, it moves to the back of the line. Use scheme cards like "E" Award or Rapid Development to deactivate upgrades early, shuffling the ones you want to the front. The draw upgrade is a popular first choice. If you want to avoid exhaustion in a long game, push the draw upgrade back and prioritize damage or range to force melee fighters to approach you.
**Watch out for:** At the end of any turn where all four upgrades are active, they all deactivate. They do this one at a time, left to right, which means they end up in the exact same order they started in.

### Plan B: Capitalize on an active suit

**Goal:** Maximize the value of cards that require active upgrades.
**How to do it:** Once you have several active upgrades, your deck hits harder. Your Full Metal and Technological Superiority cards reach their full potential. You can also play D-Day to immediately trigger your entire suit for a major swing.
**Watch out for:** If your upgrades are already active when you play D-Day, the card also deals 1 damage to the opposing fighter and heals Rosie for 1.

### How they lose

- Leaving the draw upgrade active for too long empties her deck quickly, leading to exhaustion.
- Because she builds power over time as her upgrades come online, she can struggle against aggressive fighters who apply heavy early pressure.

### Key cards

- **D-Day**: A 3-value attack (3 copies) that activates all your upgrades and grants a free action to attack. Save it for big late-game turns.
- **Full Metal**: A 1-value versatile (3 copies) that gains +1 to its value for each active upgrade, potentially reaching a value of 5 with a fully active suit.
- **Hasty Repairs**: A 1-value defense (3 copies) that deactivates an upgrade to heal 3. Deactivating an upgrade this way pushes it to the back of the line, letting you fix your queue while healing.
- **Technological Superiority**: A 3-value versatile (3 copies) that serves as your only cancel, but it only works if you have three or more active upgrades.

### Tips

- Move deactivated upgrades to the far right physically so you always know which one is next in line.
- You can deactivate two upgrades early in the game to ensure your damage and range upgrades come online first.
- Wendy the Welder is a sidekick with 7 health. Use her 3-value Arc Welding versatiles (3 copies) to either advance Rosie's suit or deal 1 damage to each adjacent opposing fighter.
