## Dr. Jill Trent

**In short:** A flexible fighter who excels at the value game. She uses gadgets to manipulate combat math and reveal the opponent's cards, while her durable sidekick Daisy provides huge blocks and recycles cards to win the exhaustion war.

### Plan A: Outlast them with Daisy

**Goal:** Build a massive deck advantage and win in fatigue.
**How to do it:** Daisy is the engine for your late game. Her scheme Helpful Assistant (2 copies) shuffles up to three Jill Trent cards from your discard pile back into your deck and draws a card. Playing both copies gives you six extra cards, making it very hard for the opponent to outlast you. Bring back versatile tools like Utility Belt, your Hypnotist cancels, or Battle of Wits for guaranteed damage. Keep Daisy alive to ensure she can play these schemes. She is incredibly tough on her own: Ace Fighter (2 copies) is a 5-value versatile that draws a card, and because she can play any card marked "Any", she can also block with your 4- and 5-value defenses.
**Watch out for:** Opponents know Daisy holds your recursion and will try to kill her early. If Daisy dies before using her schemes, Jill loses her fatigue advantage and becomes an average melee fighter.

### Plan B: Manipulate combat with gadgets

**Goal:** Turn low-value cards into wins and gather hand knowledge.
**How to do it:** At the start of your turn, you pick an active gadget. Hypnoray Blaster is your default. If your printed value is lower than their defense, it adds the opponent's top deck card's boost to your attack and reveals that card. This turns weak attacks like Caught Red-Handed (value 1) or Laser Pen (value 2) into combat wins, and shows you what the opponent will draw next. Ultrabiotic Tonic heals Jill if her printed value is higher than the defender's. Swap to Tonic later in the game when you know the opponent has run out of high-value blocks. You can also bluff with your gadget choice: pick Hypnoray but attack with a 5-value to bait a low block, or pick Tonic to scare them into wasting a big defense on a weak attack.
**Watch out for:** Hypnotist is your only true cancel, and it only works when Hypnoray Blaster is active. If you swap to Tonic, the opponent knows their card effects will go through. Feints will cancel the boost you get from Hypnoray.

### Plan C: Control their deck

**Goal:** Set up your gadget effects and bury their best tools.
**How to do it:** Insightful Deduction lets you look at the top three cards of the opponent's deck, bury one on the bottom, and stack the rest. Play it defensively so the opponent cannot cancel it. Put a card with a high boost value on top to guarantee a massive swing with Hypnoray Blaster, or to ensure your Battle of Wits deals its 3 effect damage. Take their most threatening card and put it on the bottom of their deck, removing it from the game until the very end.
**Watch out for:** The opponent watches you rearrange the cards and will know their upcoming boost values, so they can plan their blocks accordingly. If the opponent has effects that shuffle their deck (like Wukong), your bottom-deck setup is ruined.

### How they lose

​- Fighters with strong area damage or move-through effects (Ghost Rider, Little Red) can quickly eliminate Daisy, breaking her fatigue game.
​- Opponents who endlessly recycle cards (Daredevil) do not care about her deck size advantage.
​- Aggressive hitters with high printed values (Bigfoot) can smash through her lower defenses if she fails to draw her 5s.
​- Her attacks rely heavily on the opponent's boost values when using Hypnoray; against heroes with mostly 1-boost cards, her damage output drops significantly.
​- Hand disruption forces her to discard her few cancels or Daisy's key schemes before they can be played.

### Key cards

​- **Helpful Assistant**: Daisy's scheme that recycles your key Jill cards. It essentially passes your turn and extends your deck size.
​- **Caught Red-Handed**: a 1-value versatile. It forces the opponent to discard one card, or two if you win the combat. Attack with this while Hypnoray is active to steal a win and drain their hand, especially against fighters who rely on a full hand like the Genie.
​- **Stasis Diffuser**: a 3-value versatile that locks the opponent's card to its printed value. It acts as a specialized cancel against cards that add value during combat, shutting down massive swings like Dracula's Beast Form or Houdini's boosted attacks.
​- **Indestructible Cloth**: a 5-value defense that prevents effect damage. It blocks massive printed attacks while entirely ignoring nasty after-combat damage.
​- **Energizing Spray**: a 5-value attack that gains an action. Since both fighters can play it, you can chain these together for massive pressure in a single turn.

### Tips

​- Remember your special ability triggers at the *start* of your turn. If you go second, you do not have an active gadget when defending during the opponent's first turn.
​- Keep track of the card you put on the bottom of their deck with Insightful Deduction. Knowing their final boost value sets up a perfect Caught Red-Handed or Battle of Wits to close the game.
​- Gyroscopic Jetpack moves you up to 2 spaces through opposing fighters. It is your most reliable way to escape a pin.
​- Utility Belt offers unmatched flexibility. Use the healing to keep Jill healthy, the movement to break out of tight spots, or the draw when you need to find answers.
​- Sisters in Arms requires strict positioning and specific gadget conditions to be useful. It is often your best card to discard for a boost.
