## Ciri

**In short:** A flexible late-game fighter who grows stronger as her discard pile fills with Source cards. She starts slow, using her sidekick to absorb hits, but becomes an unstoppable threat once she reaches seven Source cards and her effects can no longer be canceled.

### Plan A: Build the Source pool early

**Goal:** Fill your discard pile with Source cards while surviving the opponent's early pressure.
**How to do it:** Many of your cards carry a blue Source symbol. Their effects depend on how many Source cards are already in your discard pile. Early in the game, your effects are weak and you are vulnerable. Keep Ihuarraquax between Ciri and the enemy to block paths and absorb attacks. Mulligan for your weakest Source cards, like Pushed to the brink, and play them just to get them into the discard pile. Channel the source is a 2-value attack that lets you discard Source cards from your hand for an attack boost; use it to jumpstart your count quickly, or save it as a surprise late-game finisher. Before your effects are fully active, Blink (3 copies, value 4) is your most reliable versatile block because it allows you to move Ciri through enemies to escape pins without depending on your discard pile.
**Watch out for:** Do not throw away your best cards early. Zireael and The lady of space and time are too valuable to spend before they reach their required thresholds. When defending Ihuarraquax, avoid using premium blocks like Parry; let the sidekick take hits or use its own cards to defend.

### Plan B: Loop the deck

**Goal:** Prevent exhaustion and endlessly reuse your strongest effects.
**How to do it:** The lady of space and time (2 copies, value 2) has two different effects. At 3 Source, it returns a card from your discard pile to the top of your deck. This prevents you from drawing closer to exhaustion and sets up your next turn. You can return Zireael for repeated random discards, Lion cub of Cintra to attack for 7, or your second copy of The lady of space and time to loop indefinitely. Once you reach 7 Source, Ciri's special ability makes this recursion uncancelable.
**Watch out for:** At 8 Source, the card returns to your hand and forces you to draw a card instead. This is a severe drawback because it pushes you toward exhaustion and breaks your deck loop. The ideal state is to stay at exactly 7 Source, where your effects are uncancelable but the forced draw is not yet active.

### Plan C: Auto-damage and safe healing

**Goal:** Win the late game without needing to win combats.
**How to do it:** Child of the Elder Blood deals 1 damage to each other fighter in Ciri's zone at 4 Source, and 3 damage at 9 Source. Because it is a scheme, the opponent cannot defend against it, making it a guaranteed finisher. Unicorn ally recovers 2 health and stays in play, forcing you to draw a card after every combat. If you play Unicorn ally while you have 5 or more cards in hand, the ongoing draw effect is discarded at the end of your turn. This turns the card into a simple heal and a double pass without risking your deck count.
**Watch out for:** Child of the Elder Blood damages your sidekick too. If you play Unicorn ally with a small hand and leave it in play late in the game, aggressive opponents will attack you just to force you to draw cards and die to the exhaustion clock.

### How they lose

- Fighters with high early pressure (like T-Rex or Houdini) overwhelm her before she can build her Source count.
- Unavoidable damage from effects (like Tomoe's) bypasses her blocks and punishes her for playing a slow, defensive game.
- Ihuarraquax dies before using his cards. Bane of the Aen Elle and Searching strike become dead cards in your hand, useful only for their boosts.
- Diligent opponents track her discard pile and carefully manage their cancels, saving them for critical moments before she hits 7 Source.

### Key cards

- **The lady of space and time**: Her main utility tool. It controls exhaustion and recurs your best effects, but loses its deck-building power at 8 Source.
- **Zireael**: A 2-value versatile that becomes a 4-value block at 3 Source, and additionally forces a random discard at 5 Source. Her best defensive tool.
- **Lion cub of Cintra**: Her main attack. It jumps from a 2 to a 4 at 1 Source, and to a massive 7 at 5 Source.
- **Blink**: Her most consistent early block and a reliable way to kite opponents.
- **Parry**: A 3-value defense that cancels effects. Her only cancel, and it only works on defense. Save it for heavy hits.

### Tips

- Keep careful track of the exact number of Source cards in your discard pile. The effects change at 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, and 9.
- Zireael’s discard effect triggers during combat. This means you can force the opponent to discard a card before their after-combat effects can resolve.
- Attack with Lion cub of Cintra at 1 Source early in the game to push 4 damage and get a Source card into the pile.
- Searching strike rarely wins combat because average defenses will block its value of 3. Use it to bait out blocks, or boost with it once the sidekick dies.
- Bane of the Aen Elle moves Ihuarraquax through fighters for 1 damage. Use it to safely finish off 1-health sidekicks.
- At exactly 7 Source, your opponent's defensive cancels become dead cards. This forces them to use them early while your attacks are still relatively weak.
