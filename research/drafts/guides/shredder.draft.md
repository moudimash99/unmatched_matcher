## Shredder

**In short:** A tactical fighter who uses Foot soldiers to extend his reach and control the board. He wins either by building a stationary fortress of soldiers for a single massive turn, or by harassing the opponent from a distance.

### Plan A: Build the Foot soldier fortress

**Goal:** Camp on a highly connected space, protect your Foot soldiers, and unleash a massive turn.
**How to do it:** Shredder's special ability lets him deploy a Foot soldier to a path adjacent to a friendly fighter at the start of every turn. Usually, an opponent removes these soldiers by boosting their maneuver and moving through them. However, enemies cannot move through Shredder himself. If you position Shredder on a space that connects to four or five paths, you can surround him with soldiers that melee opponents cannot reach. Use Bebop & Rocksteady to body block any open paths. Gruff Escort (2 copies, value 4) helps set this up quickly by deploying two soldiers anywhere and moving Shredder up to two spaces. Once the fortress is built, play Obedient Subjects (2 copies). This scheme grants an action for each Foot soldier adjacent to Shredder, potentially giving you three to five extra actions. Spend them attacking repeatedly with Swarming Strike (3 copies), which adds +1 to its printed value of 5 for every adjacent soldier.
**Watch out for:** Fighters with forced movement effects, such as Leonardo. If they push Shredder out of his central space, they can easily boost through the empty spot and clear out your clustered soldiers.

### Plan B: Hit and run from a distance

**Goal:** Attack safely through Foot soldiers and force the opponent to waste boosts clearing them.
**How to do it:** You do not have to stay near the enemy. The sources disagree on his best approach: while some advocate the stationary fortress strategy, others prefer a mobile, hit-and-run style. Deploy a Foot soldier near the opponent, maneuver to safety, and attack the opponent through the soldier. Several cards reward this distance. Long Shot (3 copies) is a 3-value versatile that becomes a 5 if you are not adjacent to the target. Savagery (3 copies, value 4) deals 1 damage to all fighters adjacent to your attacking fighter if you win the combat; attacking from a distance ensures you do not accidentally damage Bebop & Rocksteady. Gang Up (2 copies) jumps from a 3 to a 6 if the opposing fighter is adjacent to two or more Foot soldiers. To stop these distant attacks, the opponent must boost their maneuver to clear the tokens, costing them cards and leaving them in awkward map positions.
**Watch out for:** Losing Bebop & Rocksteady early. Without your sidekick to apply pressure, draw attacks, and play Gruff Escort, Shredder takes all the heat and quickly runs out of defensive options.

### How they lose

- Aggressive, high-tempo opponents overwhelm him before he can deploy enough Foot soldiers to establish control.
- His defenses are generally weak. He only has 14 defensive cards in total, and most block for values of 2 or 3.
- Action gain or heavy discard from the opponent drains his hand and breaks his setup.
- Big printed attack values crush him. Disrupting Strike (2 copies, value 3) locks the opponent's card to its printed value, which neatly counters floating modifiers like Alice's size changes or Donatello's inventions, but does nothing to stop a high base number.
- If Bebop & Rocksteady are defeated and he does not draw Back to work! to revive them, Shredder loses his best tools for deploying soldiers quickly and absorbing hits.

### Key cards

- **Swarming Strike**: his heavy hitter. Keep Shredder surrounded by soldiers to swing for 8 or 9 damage.
- **Obedient Subjects**: the engine for his biggest turns. It easily generates three or four actions if Shredder is well positioned.
- **All According to Plan**: a 3-value defense that gives the opponent an action, but forces them to discard two cards at the end of the turn unless they play a scheme. This can force an awkward scheme play or severely punish fighters who lack schemes entirely, like Buffy.
- **Masterful Defense**: a 2-value defense that heals Shredder for each adjacent Foot soldier. When camped in a fortress, this recovers significant health to offset the damage he takes through the weak block.
- **Back to work!**: a scheme that revives Bebop & Rocksteady with 3 health, even if they are defeated, and places them in a space as close as possible to Shredder.

### Tips

- Pick maps with highly connected spaces. Five connections are common, but look for spaces that allow maximum Foot soldier placement.
- Use spare Foot soldier tokens to track your remaining actions after playing Obedient Subjects so you do not lose count during a massive turn.
- Play Think Hard (2 copies, value 3) on defense to draw a card for each Foot soldier adjacent to the attacker.
- Play Long Shot on defense against ranged attackers, as it becomes a reliable 5-value block when the enemy shoots from afar.
- Master of the Foot deals 1 damage to every opposing fighter adjacent to a Foot soldier. It hits the whole group, but a fighter only takes 1 damage even if they are surrounded by multiple soldiers.
- Do not fixate on the secondary effect of Perplexing Tactics. Getting 10 Foot soldiers on the board to ignore the opponent's value is nearly impossible against an opponent who actively clears them.
