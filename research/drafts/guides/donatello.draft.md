## Donatello

**In short:** A methodical melee fighter who manipulates the bottom of his deck with his maneuver ability. He sets up permanent buffs through his unique Inventions, relies on Metalhead for heavy hits, and recurs his best cards for late-game damage.

### Plan A: Set up your Inventions early

**Goal:** Deploy your three Inventions to secure permanent buffs before you start attacking.
**How to do it:** Donatello’s maneuver ability provides serious card advantage: you draw two cards and place one from your hand on the bottom of your deck. Use this to dig for Electro grenade (1 copy, value 2, boost 3). Play it as early as possible to tuck it and activate its permanent +1 bonus to all your attacks. Keep weaker cards like Shift focus (3 copies, value 2) and Quick strike (3 copies, value 3) in your hand to survive the early game, and hide your highest-boost cards at the bottom of the deck. Self defense grid (1 copy, value 2) provides a permanent +1 to your defenses and is your only healing. Wait to play it until you have taken at least 4 damage, and remove versatile or defense cards from your discard pile to maximize the health recovery.
**Watch out for:** Boosting with your Inventions wastes them permanently. If a discard effect takes them from your hand, bring them back immediately with Donatello does machines (2 copies, boost 3).

### Plan B: The Metalhead payload

**Goal:** Hit for massive, unpredictable damage using your curated deck bottom.
**How to do it:** Untested enhancements (3 copies, value 3) is a Metalhead versatile that adds the boost value of the bottom card of your deck. Since you spent the early game sending 3-boost cards there during maneuvers, this reliably hits for 6. Turtle power! (2 copies, value 3) works similarly, but uses a boost directly from your hand. You can also stack the deck bottom by attacking weak sidekicks with Thinking ahead (3 copies, value 3). If you win the combat, you can put a spent card with a high boost value on the bottom to fuel Metalhead's next strike.
**Watch out for:** Untested enhancements is mandatory. If the defender plays a card that ignores value changes, your attack stays at 3 and you still discard your carefully placed bottom card. Protect Metalhead early; if he dies, you lose access to half your damage potential.

### Plan C: The scheme action engine

**Goal:** Chain schemes together to deal damage without taking an action.
**How to do it:** Once Smoke bomb (1 copy, scheme) is tucked, every scheme you play refunds its own action cost. Play Party wagon! (2 copies) to move through an enemy for 2 unblockable damage, then use the refunded action to attack them. You can loop this damage: use Thinking ahead to return Party wagon! to your deck, and Donatello does machines to return Thinking ahead to your hand.
**Watch out for:** The sources strongly disagree on how to play Smoke bomb. Some players hold it until they have four schemes in their discard pile to gain four actions at once for a massive finishing turn. Others argue this is a trap: Donatello draws cards so quickly that taking extra actions pushes him rapidly toward the exhaustion clock. They prefer playing Smoke bomb as a pass with zero targets, simply to secure the permanent scheme buff without accelerating exhaustion.

### How they lose

​- Short circuit (3 copies, value 3) is his only cancel, and it only stops after-combat effects. He has no way to cancel immediate or during-combat effects, which leaves him vulnerable to cards that alter values or trigger massive damage (like Dracula's Beast Form).
​- He burns through his deck quickly. His maneuver ability and cards that draw force him through his deck faster than the opponent, meaning he usually loses the race against exhaustion.
​- Opponents with deck-shuffling effects scramble the bottom of his deck, ruining his setup for Untested enhancements.
​- If Metalhead dies early, Donatello loses access to his strongest attacks and deck-scouting schemes.
​- Smart opponents stay out of his current zone or use hard boundaries to avoid the movement effect of Shift focus.

### Key cards

​- **Electro grenade**: His most important card. Play it as early as possible to buff all future attacks, even if you cannot remove many cards from your discard pile.
​- **Self defense grid**: His only source of health recovery. Fuel it with discarded versatile cards so you don't remove your valuable printed defenses from the game.
​- **Untested enhancements**: Metalhead's big swing. Never play this on defense; it wastes your carefully placed bottom card just to block.
​- **Heroes in a half shell**: A printed 5-value defense with no effect (2 copies). Save this for massive attacks with during-combat effects that Short circuit cannot stop.
​- **Party wagon!**: A scheme that moves a fighter 5 spaces and deals 2 damage to one enemy they pass through. It ignores pins and provides reliable, action-free damage once Smoke bomb is active.

### Tips

​- You rarely want to attack twice in a row early in the game. Poke, run, and double maneuver to stack your deck bottom until your attack buff is active.
​- Play Shift focus on defense against your opponent's last action. You can push them away to disrupt their positioning for next turn, or pull Metalhead closer to set up your own attack.
​- If the opponent lacks dangerous after-combat effects, use Short circuit as a 2-value boost instead of holding it in your hand.
​- Bo staff (2 copies, value 3) deals 1 damage to an adjacent enemy after combat. Combine it with the +1 defense buff to create a solid 4-value block that punishes melee attackers.
​- The future of Ninjutsu (2 copies, scheme) lets you look at the top card of any deck. Use it on yourself to check your next draw, and put it on the bottom if Metalhead needs a high-boost card for an upcoming attack.
