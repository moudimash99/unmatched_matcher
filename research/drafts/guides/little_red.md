## Little Red Riding Hood

**In short:** A tricky hybrid fighter whose cards do more when their symbol matches the top of her discard pile (her "basket"). She plans one card ahead, deals damage through effects on her turn and on yours, and heals back up with the Huntsman.

### Plan A: Chain the basket
**Goal:** Trigger the extra effect on most of the cards you play.
**How to do it:** Each card's effect has a symbol: pelt, knives or wolfsbane. It only works if the card on top of your discard pile shows the same symbol. The basket starts the game as a wild, so your first effect always works. After that, every card you play sets up the next one. What's That In My Basket? (4 copies, value 4) is a wild that resets the chain. Play it on whichever turn needs a symbol rather than saving it for a perfect moment. When you boost a maneuver, pick the boost card for its symbol, not just its value. The three copies of Into the Woods each carry a different symbol, so check which one you drew.
**Watch out for:** Don't twist your turn to trigger every symbol. Sometimes the right play is a card without its effect, or taking a hit to keep a key card for later. Blocking with Stones in the Belly as a plain 2 wastes it.

### Plan B: Damage through effects
**Goal:** Wear the opponent down with damage that ignores card values.
**How to do it:** With knives on top:
- Once Upon a Time deals 3 damage after combat even if they block. Use it as an attack.
- Never Leave the Path deals 2 damage to every opposing fighter in her zone. It hits the whole zone, not just adjacent spaces, so use it when it hits two or more fighters or a 1-health sidekick.
- Stones in the Belly makes them discard a random card and adds its boost to your value. It is the Huntsman's best defense.

On defense with pelt on top, What a Terrible Big Mouth You Have hits back for the printed value of their attack. That's brutal against big printed attacks (Bigfoot's 6s, Excalibur), but weak against attacks that get their damage from effects or boosts.
**Watch out for:** Experienced opponents Feint her attacks rather than wait, because her defensive effects go off on their turn, when they can't respond.

### Plan C: Outlast them with the Huntsman
**Goal:** Turn 14 health into much more.
**How to do it:** A Grimm Tale (Huntsman only, 2 copies) heals 2, or 4 with pelt on top. Both copies at 4 give her an effective 22 health. What Large Hands You Have with wolfsbane on top can return a Grimm Tale from the discard pile for a third heal. Keep the Huntsman (9 health, ranged) near Red but away from the enemy. Long Have I Sought You is worth 6 when he's adjacent, which punishes anyone who rushes him.
**Watch out for:** Opponents know this and go after the Huntsman first. The sources disagree on how often you actually get to play A Grimm Tale: one rates it her best card, another says it gets played rarely and works mostly as a threat.

### How they lose
- Opponents with many Feints or cancels (Bigfoot has five) blank the effects she sets up.
- Discard effects, random ones especially, take her planned cards and scramble the basket. Medusa is the classic example.
- The Huntsman dies before A Grimm Tale is played, so she loses her healing.
- She gets pinned. Never Leave the Path with pelt (place her anywhere) is her only sure way out.
- Fast, action-heavy fighters such as the Raptors overwhelm her 2-value defenses before her effects add up.

### Key cards
- **What's That In My Basket?**: a 4-value versatile wild. Her most reliable block, and the card that fixes a broken chain.
- **What Big Eyes You Have**: a 3-value versatile that cancels their effects; with wolfsbane it also ignores their value. The sources disagree on when to play it: some save it for defense, where it stops an attack completely; one prefers it on attack, for 3 damage they can't block.
- **What Large Hands You Have**: a 2-value attack that returns to your hand, so you can attack every turn and pick off 1-health sidekicks. With wolfsbane it returns the top card of your discard pile instead. That only works on wolfsbane cards: A Grimm Tale, Stones in the Belly, Once Upon a Time, What's That In My Basket? and one Into the Woods. Don't waste it on Into the Woods.
- **Never Leave the Path**: three effects in one card. Teleport plus an action with pelt, area damage with knives, draw 3 with wolfsbane (only when you're desperate for cards).
- **What Big Ears You Have**: a 4-value attack that can be played on defense with knives on top. It has no other effect, which makes it ideal bait for a Feint.

### Tips
- Attack with your 4-value cards that have no effect (Basket, Big Ears) to draw out Feints before your important effects.
- Keep the second What Big Eyes in hand. While it's in there, the opponent has to fear wolfsbane on top.
- The Wolf's Skin (pelt: draw 2) is your main card draw. Once or twice a game is usually enough.
- Long Have I Sought You still hits for 4 at range, which kills Outlaws and other small sidekicks.
- If you can reach the enemy's starting zone with your first maneuver, Never Leave the Path with knives can hit their hero and every sidekick at once.
- Keep your discard pile in order. Both players need to see the top card.
