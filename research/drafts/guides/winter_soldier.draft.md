## Winter Soldier

**In short:** A ranged fighter who hits hard and defends well, but suffers from mandatory negative card effects. He filters the bad cards out of his deck and endlessly recycles his strongest options to win long games.

### Plan A: Build an infinite deck of good cards

**Goal:** Trash the harmful cards and keep reusing the best ones until the opponent runs out.
**How to do it:** His special ability makes his card effects uncancelable. This means Reprogram (3 copies) always resolves. Play it to shuffle three cards from your discard pile back into your deck. Always choose another Reprogram as one of the three so the loop never breaks. For the other two, pick what you need: Without Remorse for damage, Complete the Mission for defense, or Feint to stop enemy effects. Before starting the loop, use the maneuver action to boost away his worst cards, such as Programmed to Kill and Marksman, leaving only the good ones to draw. Because he can loop forever, he thrives in long games where the opponent eventually takes exhaustion damage.
**Watch out for:** Finding the time to play Reprogram. It is only a 2-value versatile, so using it to attack gives the opponent a break, and using it to block takes heavy damage. If you lose your last Reprogram to a random discard effect, the loop ends.

### Plan B: Manage the Red Room effects

**Goal:** Attack with massive values without letting the negative text ruin your game.
**How to do it:** Five of his cards have a Red Room effect that hurts him or helps the opponent, and his ability means these happen every time. A Boy Named Bucky (2 copies) is a scheme with a boost of 3 that ignores these effects for the turn and gives an action. Play it before attacking with Bionic Arm (a 6-value attack when adjacent) so the opponent does not get to move their fighters up to 5 spaces away. You can also use it to safely play Reflex Memories on attack without discarding two random cards.
**Watch out for:** You cannot use A Boy Named Bucky on the opponent's turn. Reflex Memories is a 5-value versatile, making it your best defense, but blocking with it always forces the random discard. Play it when your hand is nearly empty, or trust your Reprogram loop to recover whatever important cards you lose.

### Plan C: Punish them for attacking

**Goal:** Turn his 15 health into an advantage by trading damage efficiently.
**How to do it:** Complete the Mission is a 3-value defense that deals damage back to the attacker equal to what you took. It also lets you boost it. If you need to survive, boost it with a 3-value scheme to block for up to 6. If you want to race the opponent, do not boost it: let a big attack hit you, and they will take the same damage. Since he loops cards and has printed 6-value attacks, he often wins a pure damage race.
**Watch out for:** Some fighters want to trade health. Do not take unnecessary damage early in the game before your deck is fully curated, because you lack reliable healing to recover.

### How they lose

​- Fast, action-heavy fighters like Houdini or the Raptors overwhelm his 15 health before he can set up his deck loop.
​- He lacks reliable card draw on his combat cards, so he easily loses hand size against opponents who attack often and drain his options.
​- He has no effects that move him after combat, making him easy to pin. Bionic Arm's normal effect even gives the opponent free movement to trap him further.
​- Opponents who force discards might snipe his Reprograms before he can play them.

### Key cards

​- **Reprogram**: the core of his strategy. A 2-value versatile that brings back three cards. Never leave the discard pile without returning one of these.
​- **Complete the Mission**: a flexible 3-value defense. Boost it to block huge hits, or take the hit to deal unavoidable damage back.
​- **A Boy Named Bucky**: a scheme that cancels his Red Room effects and replaces the action.
​- **Manipulation**: a scheme with a 3-boost that draws until you have five cards. The Red Room effect lets opponents do the same, which is dangerous against aggressive fighters but fine against opponents trying to stall.
​- **Without Remorse**: a 6-value ranged attack. The opponent gets to draw a card, but the massive damage usually makes it worth playing.

### Tips

​- Play A Boy Named Bucky before Manipulation. This lowers your hand size first, allowing you to draw more cards while completely denying the opponent their Red Room draw.
​- Never play Programmed to Kill. It is a 4-value attack that deals 2 damage to you if you win. Use it strictly as a 2-value boost card to maneuver.
​- Marksman is usually weak, but it ignores secret passages. On maps with passages, it can reach massive values by counting the physical spaces across the board.
​- The sources disagree on Born in the Barracks (heals 2 if you win the combat). Most say a 3-value card rarely wins, but one notes it works well on defense if the opponent uses a low-value bait card, or if you stand on a map item token to raise its combat value.
​- If you just need to stall for exhaustion, play Reprogram and shuffle back your other Complete the Missions and Reprograms to build an impenetrable deck.
