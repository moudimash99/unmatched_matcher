## Eredin Bréacc Glas

**In short:** A melee fighter who sacrifices his four Red Riders to become enraged. He transitions from managing his sidekicks early to hitting with high values and upgraded card effects once they are gone.

### Plan A: Feed the Red Riders to the hunt

**Goal:** Empty the board of sidekicks to trigger King of the Wild Hunt.
**How to do it:** Eredin becomes enraged only when all Red Riders are defeated. Use their deaths to pay for your card effects. Foul purpose returns to your hand if you defeat a Red Rider after combat. Icy guile ignores the opponent's card value if you defeat a Red Rider during combat. You can also play Portal defense to swap Eredin with an adjacent Red Rider, forcing the sidekick to take the hit.
**Watch out for:** The sources disagree on pacing. Several players argue you should kill the sidekicks as fast as possible to unlock Eredin's power immediately. Others say you should use them first to drain the opponent's defensive cards, bringing them back with Unyielding hordes to keep up the early pressure.

### Plan B: Batter them with enraged attacks

**Goal:** Overwhelm the opponent using his passive bonuses and upgraded card text.
**How to do it:** When enraged, Eredin adds +1 to all his combat cards and moves 3 spaces. This upgrades his standard deck into a heavy-hitting arsenal. Skirmish becomes a 5-value versatile. Backhand hits for 5 and forces the opponent to discard a random card. Implacable becomes an attack you can boost; discarding Close for the kill (a 4-value boost) creates an 8-value strike. Wild hunt hits for 4 and deals 2 damage to adjacent enemies. His reach extends too: an enraged Foul purpose can target a fighter in any space.
**Watch out for:** Opponents will try to stop these effects with Feints. Punish them with Brutal strike, an uncancelable 2-value attack that ignores the opponent's value if their card has an IMMEDIATELY effect.

### How they lose

- If the map is open and the opponent is a ranged fighter, he struggles to close the distance early while his movement is only 2.
- He lacks strong early offense and can run out of attacks in the first half of the game if his hand is not managed carefully.
- He easily gets caught holding Portal defense when no Red Riders are adjacent, leaving him with a dead 0-value block.

### Key cards

- **Foul purpose**: Sacrifice a Red Rider to recycle this attack early, or use it to strike across the board once enraged.
- **Icy guile**: A 2-value versatile. When Eredin is enraged, it cancels all effects on the opponent's card. If you are not enraged, you can instead defeat a Red Rider to ignore their value.
- **Portal defense**: Once enraged, it draws 2 cards and blocks for 1, which can be worthwhile if you have a massive health lead and can afford to absorb the damage.
- **Brutal strike**: Play this when you anticipate a Feint, as its effects cannot be canceled and it punishes cards with IMMEDIATELY text by ignoring their value.
- **Might of the Aen Elle**: A scheme that makes Eredin immune to all damage except combat damage and exhaustion. Play it against fighters who rely on non-combat damage, but remember you must attack that turn to keep the card in play.

### Tips

- You cannot use Icy guile to both cancel effects and ignore values. The cancel checks first, requiring Eredin to already be enraged; defeating your last Red Rider during that same combat happens too late to trigger it.
- Always keep at least one Red Rider adjacent to Eredin as long as they are alive, so Portal defense does not become a dead card.
- Save Backhand for the end of the game when Eredin is enraged, ensuring the random card is discarded rather than placed on the deck.
- Unyielding hordes adds +1 to its 1-value for each friendly fighter adjacent to the target, so surround the enemy before playing it.
