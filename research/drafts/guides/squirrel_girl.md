## Squirrel Girl

**In short:** A setup-heavy melee fighter who uses a swarm of small sidekicks to bypass blockers and trigger powerful conditional effects. She starts weak, but becomes dangerous once she surrounds the opponent with squirrels.

### Plan A: Build and spread the swarm

**Goal:** Flood the board with sidekicks to control zones and access enemy targets.
**How to do it:** Her special ability adds one squirrel a turn, which is too slow to build a threat. Dig for your summoning cards early. Get 'Em Tippy-Toe! summons two squirrels in any space, and Eat Nuts heals her and summons one nearby. Squirrels follow the rules for small fighters: they can share spaces with other fighters, and opposing fighters can move through them. This lets your sidekicks bypass enemy sidekick walls, such as Elektra's Hand or Wukong's clones, and walk straight to the enemy hero. Spread them across the board and, on Marvel maps, share spaces with item tokens to steal them from camping opponents. Keep four squirrels in the enemy's zone to turn Bite of Steel into a 5-value attack.
**Watch out for:** If a squirrel takes damage, every squirrel in that same space is defeated instantly. Avoid stacking them in a single space so you do not lose several sidekicks to a single area-of-effect attack, but remember you will eventually need to group them to get maximum damage out of Kick Butts.

### Plan B: Surround them for conditional effects

**Goal:** Catch the opponent adjacent to four or more squirrels to trigger your strongest cards.
**How to do it:** Her best effects only activate when the enemy is surrounded. If four or more squirrels are adjacent to the target, Unbeatable Squirrel Girl cancels their card and ignores its value. Kick Butts gains +1 value for each adjacent squirrel, reaching up to a 9. Horde of Squirrels deals 2 automatic damage, and Nutwork of Spies forces them to discard two cards instead of one. To set this up without wasting actions on movement, defend with Get 'Em Tippy-Toe! to drop two squirrels next to the attacker on their turn. On your turn, use Fuzzball Special first: it moves every squirrel up to 3 spaces, shifting your swarm into position for a second action.
**Watch out for:** Kick Butts and Bite of Steel have no built-in cancels and fail against a Feint. Play Nutwork of Spies first to strip the opponent's defensive cancels before committing your biggest attacks.

### Plan C: Outmaneuver with sidekicks

**Goal:** Use the scattered swarm to cross the board quickly.
**How to do it:** Squirrel Girl has a base movement of 2, and all of her cards have a boost value of 1 or 2. This caps her standard boosted movement at 4, making it hard to catch ranged fighters who kite her. Squirgility solves this: after combat, she may move up to 3 spaces, move through sidekicks, and ignore any spaces containing them. If your squirrels are spread out between you and the opponent, she can leapfrog over them to cover 10 or more spaces in a single action. Squirmish also helps close the distance by placing her in any space in her zone after combat.
**Watch out for:** This mobility depends entirely on keeping your squirrels alive and positioned well. If the opponent clears the board, you are forced to walk slowly.

### How they lose

- Area-of-effect damage clears her squirrels instantly. Fighters who deal damage to adjacent spaces (like Buffy) or multiple sidekicks wipe out her board presence.
- She starts the game with no sidekicks and low health. Aggressive fighters can rush her down before her swarm is ready.
- She is highly vulnerable to exhaustion. She has weak card draw, and kiting opponents force her to discard cards for low-value boosts just to chase them.
- Her squirrels only have one defense card (Call of the Mild) and cannot play Feint. Opponents easily farm them to trigger "after combat" effects safely.
- Her printed defenses top out at 3 (except for Squirmish and conditional effects), so heavy hitters deal damage through her blocks consistently.

### Key cards

- **Get 'Em Tippy-Toe!**: a 3-value versatile. It is your best tool to quickly rebuild the swarm or trap an enemy on their turn.
- **Squirgility**: a 3-value defense. With a well-placed swarm, this card allows her to cross the entire board in a single action.
- **Unbeatable Squirrel Girl**: a 4-value versatile. Use it as an ultimate defense against big effects, or as an attack to push through 4 unpreventable damage if they do not play a cancel.
- **Nutwork of Spies**: a scheme that removes a card from their hand. Use it to strip their Feints before committing to a big attack.
- **Kick Butts**: a 1-value attack that scales with adjacent squirrels. It offers her highest damage potential if you can swarm the target.

### Tips

- Bite of Steel is an attack card, so you cannot save it to block. Play it aggressively to force the opponent to waste a good defense on a sidekick to avoid a 5-value hit.
- Fuzzball Special moves each squirrel individually. Use it to reshape the board and surround an enemy all at once.
- Keep your Feints for defense. You only have two copies, and your sidekicks cannot play them.
- Play Horde of Squirrels only when you have the four squirrels in position. Two automatic damage is crucial when your health is only 13.
- Call of the Mild is the only defense card your sidekicks can play. If its 4-value block wins the combat, you draw two cards.
