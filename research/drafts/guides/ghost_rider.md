## Ghost Rider

**In short:** A highly mobile melee fighter who manages a unique resource called Hellfire. He spends it to deal unblockable damage by driving his motorcycle through enemies and to fuel his powerful card effects, but struggles on defense when his supply runs dry.

### Plan A: Hit and run

**Goal:** Deal guaranteed damage while staying safely out of reach.
**How to do it:** Ghost Rider starts the game with 5 Hellfire. When you maneuver, you can spend 1 Hellfire to increase your move value to 4, pass through opposing fighters, and deal 1 damage to each enemy you drive over. Since this deals damage without playing an attack card, you can steadily wear down the opponent's health or effortlessly clear out 1-health sidekicks (like Medusa's Harpies or Sun Wukong's clones). After passing through them, end your turn a few spaces away so melee opponents must waste their own actions catching up.
**Watch out for:** You deal 1 damage per fighter, not per pass, so crossing the same enemy multiple times on one turn yields no extra damage. Also, spending Hellfire hard-caps that maneuver's movement at 4; you cannot discard a card to boost it further.

### Plan B: Manage the Hellfire economy

**Goal:** Keep enough Hellfire to fuel your defenses without wasting the cards that generate it.
**How to do it:** Ghost Rider does not gain Hellfire passively; he relies entirely on card effects to refill his supply. Play the scheme Chains of Hellfire (2 copies) when you are empty to instantly reset your resource to 5. Hell Rides With Me also generates 2 Hellfire while refunding the action spent to play it.
When your Hellfire is high, defend with Control The Demon. It is a versatile card with no printed value that gains +1 for every Hellfire you hold without forcing you to spend any, providing a free 4- or 5-value block. If you are entirely empty, block with Stoke The Flames. It is only a 2, but it awards Hellfire equal to the combat damage you take, trading health to restart your engine. You can also chain your action-gaining effects to create huge turns: playing Hell Rides With Me followed by the attack I Brought The Devil With Me (which lets you spend 2 Hellfire to gain an action) allows you to take up to four actions in a single turn.
**Watch out for:** Spending Hellfire on extra actions or attacks can be a trap. Burning through your resources on your turn leaves you with weak 2- or 3-value defenses for the opponent's counterattack. Ensure the extra aggression is worth the vulnerability.

### Plan C: The mutual destruction finish

**Goal:** End the game with a massive, unblockable zone attack when the opponent is low on health.
**How to do it:** Blaze of Glory is an attack that lets you spend any amount of Hellfire to deal equal damage to every fighter in your zone, yourself included. Once the opposing hero drops to 5 health or lower, move into their zone, play this attack, and spend 5 Hellfire. Even if the blast reduces both of you to zero health simultaneously, Unmatched rules dictate that the active player wins the tie.
**Watch out for:** If the opponent cancels the card with a Feint, your Hellfire is wasted, and you just resolve a basic 2-value attack. Bait out their cancels using your other high-value cards first. Because this severely damages you, reserve it purely as a finisher rather than using it mid-game to clear sidekicks.

### How they lose

- He runs out of Hellfire. Without it, his printed defense cards mostly sit at values of 2 or 3, leaving him highly vulnerable to heavy attacks.
- Opponents force him to randomly discard his hand and snipe Chains of Hellfire, permanently breaking his resource economy.
- Opponents with heavy healing (like Little Red) or strong damage reduction (like Luke Cage) outpace his slow, steady movement damage.
- Fighters with direct, unblockable damage effects (like Sherlock Holmes) bypass his Hellfire-scaled blocks.

### Key cards

- **Penance Stare**: A 3-value versatile that adds the opponent's boost value to yours. You can spend 2 Hellfire to trigger this addition a second time. Against a 4-boost card (like those from Alice or the T-Rex), spending the Hellfire turns this into a devastating 11-value swing.
- **Spirit Of Vengeance**: A 5-value attack that lets you spend 1 Hellfire to draw two cards. This is your best tool to pressure the opponent early while maintaining your hand size.
- **Deal With The Devil**: A 2-value defense that increases by 1 for every Hellfire you spend. Save it to survive massive incoming attacks, as spending Hellfire to block small hits drains you too quickly.
- **The Wicked Will Burn**: A 3-value versatile that grants 2 Hellfire if you started the turn in a different space.

### Tips

- I Finally Escaped Hell is widely considered his weakest card. Use it to retreat after combat, or throw it out as an attack just to bait an opponent's Feint.
- It is much easier to trigger The Wicked Will Burn's bonus on your own turn as an attack, rather than hoping you get attacked after moving on defense.
- You are virtually impossible to pin. As long as you have 1 Hellfire, you can use your maneuver ability to drive straight through the enemies boxing you in.
- Ghost Rider excels against fighters relying on 1-health sidekicks (like Robin Hood), as he can effortlessly run them over without wasting attack cards.
