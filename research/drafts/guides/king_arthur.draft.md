## King Arthur

**In short:** A high-health melee fighter who uses his ranged wizard sidekick to control the board. His ability lets him boost his attacks for massive damage, but it burns through cards quickly and requires careful planning to bait out defensive cancels.

### Plan A: Shield the wizard

**Goal:** Deal damage safely from a distance and keep Merlin alive to use his powerful schemes.
**How to do it:** Treat Arthur as a meat shield. With 18 health, he can afford to block choke points and take hits while Merlin (7 health, ranged) safely attacks from afar. Use Arthur to body-block paths so enemy melee fighters cannot reach Merlin. Merlin's schemes dictate the board: Command the Storms moves everyone to set up perfect ranges, and Restless Spirits deals unblockable area damage that clears out 1-health sidekicks. Once Merlin is defeated, all of his remaining cards become excellent 2-value boosts for Arthur's attacks.
**Watch out for:** Opponents know Merlin is the real threat and will try to bypass Arthur to kill him early. Merlin's only defense card, Bewilderment, prevents all damage, but its effect happens during combat. If the opponent plays a Feint against it, the zero-value card gets canceled and Merlin takes the full hit.

### Plan B: Bait the Feints

**Goal:** Drain the opponent's cancels so your big attacks land.
**How to do it:** King Arthur's ability lets him boost attacks, but he must play the boost card face down before the opponent defends. If the opponent plays a Feint, the attack's effects are canceled and your boost card is discarded for nothing, costing you two cards to their one. To prevent this, bait their Feints by boosting weaker cards like Regroup or Skirmish. Once you know their Feints are in the discard pile, start boosting your heavy hitters.
**Watch out for:** Boosting rapidly empties your hand. If you attack and boost too often, you will run out of cards to defend yourself and accelerate your exhaustion clock. Pass turns by double maneuvering to keep your hand near the maximum limit.

### Plan C: Swing Excalibur twice

**Goal:** Maximize the single best attack in his deck.
**How to do it:** Excalibur is a 6-value attack with no text. Always boost it, preferably with a 3-value boost to hit for a massive 9. Even if the opponent plays a Feint and cancels the boost, a 6-value base attack will still push heavy damage through most defenses. The Lady of the Lake lets you search your deck or discard pile for Excalibur. Only play The Lady of the Lake after Excalibur is already in your discard pile, ensuring you get to play your best card twice in the game.
**Watch out for:** If you play The Lady of the Lake to pull Excalibur from your deck, you will only ever get to swing it once. Because there is only one copy of Excalibur, Arthur is highly vulnerable to random discard effects; if it gets discarded from your hand, your primary win condition is gone.

### How they lose

​- Opponents hold their Feints specifically for his boosted attacks, destroying his card advantage and rushing him to an early exhaustion.
​- He uses his boost cards to increase his 2-value movement to chase faster or ranged fighters, leaving him with no cards for combat.
​- Fighters with forced discard effects (like Medusa) catch Excalibur or The Lady of the Lake in his hand.
​- Opponents track his health and hit him with a massive attack when he nears 5 health, killing him before he can trigger The Holy Grail.

### Key cards

​- **Excalibur**: his main threat. A 6-value attack with no text. It cannot be fully canceled, making it the safest card to boost.
​- **The Lady of the Lake**: brings Excalibur back to your hand. Save it until Excalibur is in the discard pile.
​- **Noble Sacrifice**: a 2-value attack that can be boosted twice. Most sources consider attacking with it a trap, as committing three cards to one easily-Feinted attack is devastating. Instead, use its 3 boost value to boost your other attacks.
​- **Bewilderment**: a 0-value defense that prevents all damage and teleports Merlin. Play it early; if you wait until Merlin is at 1 health, the opponent will guess you are playing it and cancel it with a Feint.
​- **The Holy Grail**: a 1-value defense that heals Arthur back to 8 health if he survives combat with 4 or less health.

### Tips

​- Do not boost attacks that have good printed effects, like The Aid of Morgana or Momentous Shift. Boosting them just tempts the opponent to play a cancel and erase the card draw or bonus damage you wanted.
​- Use Command the Storms to move enemies into Merlin's zone, then hit them with Restless Spirits for unblockable damage.
​- With 2 movement, use Swift Strike and Divine Intervention to hit and retreat, forcing the opponent to spend their own actions walking back to you.
​- Play Prophecy when your hand drops below 5 cards to safely reload your options without blind-drawing.
​- Use Regroup to bait out big blocks or Feints. If you boost it with another Regroup and actually win the combat, you draw two cards and replace what you spent.
