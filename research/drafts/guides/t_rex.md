## T. Rex

**In short:** A massive melee fighter with a 27 health pool and incredible reach. She draws a card at the end of every turn, creating a ticking exhaustion clock that forces her to play aggressively, burn her own health with self-damaging attacks, and constantly boost her maneuvers to hunt down her prey.

### Plan A: Close the distance and end it fast

**Goal:** Catch fleeing opponents and kill them before exhaustion does.
**How to do it:** With only a base movement of 1 and a mandatory card draw every turn, you cannot sit back. You must relentlessly boost your maneuvers to cross the board. Use her large fighter ability to attack from up to two spaces away, which allows her to strike over intervening sidekicks. Closer Than She Appears acts as a free step forward, replacing the action used to play it while drawing a card. Ripples in the Water instantly teleports her to any space in her current zone to help close the gap.
**Watch out for:** Map features impact her heavily. She ignores one-way arrows, easily navigating enclosures like the Raptor Paddock pit, but her size prevents her from using secret passages, making her vulnerable to keep-away tactics on Baskerville Manor. When placing her with Ripples in the Water, remember her base occupies two spaces; you can place one half in her current zone and let the other overhang into an adjacent zone to get closer to a target.

### Plan B: Trade health for unavoidable damage

**Goal:** Burn through her massive health to guarantee damage against strong defenses.
**How to do it:** While she lacks any cards to cancel opposing effects, she possesses attacks that bypass the opponent's block. 15,000 Pounds of Muscle ignores the defender's printed value entirely. Reckless Lunge circumvents shields by dealing its damage through an after-combat effect. When Dinosaurs Ruled the Earth allows you to boost its low base value mid-combat, paying you back with an action and a card if you win.
**Watch out for:** Self-damage adds up rapidly. Playing every copy of these three cards would inflict 21 damage to yourself. Since Reckless Lunge boasts a massive boost value of 4, you should almost always save it in your hand to supercharge your maneuvers rather than taking the 3 self-damage to attack with it. For When Dinosaurs Ruled the Earth, only spend the boost if you are confident the resulting hit will deal at least 2 damage; otherwise, the mandatory 2 self-damage results in a negative health trade.

### Plan C: Control the board state

**Goal:** Pin down mobile fighters and clear out sidekick swarms.
**How to do it:** Terrifying Roar freezes fighters in their spaces for the remainder of the turn. If played defensively, the defender's effect resolves first, which completely shuts down the evasive after-combat movement of fighters like Robin Hood or Bruce Lee and traps them in range for your own double attack next turn. To stop sidekicks from body-blocking your path, play Thrash and boost it to secure the combat win, letting its effect deal 1 damage to every opposing fighter in your zone.
**Watch out for:** Ripples in the Water forces opponents to discard a random card, but only if they are adjacent to you. This conflicts slightly with her natural preference to stay two spaces away and kite melee attackers, so you must carefully weigh your positioning before teleporting.

### How they lose

- The mandatory card draw means opponents who commit entirely to running away and defending can easily wait her out until she dies of exhaustion.
- She only has 10 cards that can be played on defense. If forced onto the back foot, she runs out of defensive options very quickly.
- Because she has no cancels, fighters capable of stringing together massive action-gain sequences (like the Raptors) or executing huge late-game combos (like Elektra) can safely hoard their cards and unleash their full damage without fear of interruption.
- Ranged fighters and large maps force her to constantly boost her movement just to get within striking distance, rapidly draining her hand.

### Key cards

- **15,000 Pounds of Muscle**: An attack that ignores the opponent's defense value. It reliably punches through heavy blocks, keeping you ahead on the damage trade even after suffering the self-damage.
- **Terrifying Roar**: Your best countermeasure against hit-and-run tactics, ensuring evasive targets cannot step away.
- **Commanding Impact**: A printed 5-value attack that draws a card. Because its damage comes directly from its base value rather than a combat effect, it serves as her safest and most punishing strike against opponents holding defensive cancels.
- **You're Just Making Her Angry**: A defense that returns to your hand if you win the combat by boosting it. The community is split on its utility: some praise it for offering a recurring shield, while others argue it dangerously consumes the high-boost cards you desperately need for movement and attacks.
- **65 Million Years of Gut Instinct**: A scheme that retrieves any card from your discard pile other than itself. Use it to grab Ripples in the Water for an extra teleport, or recycle Terrifying Roar to freeze them again.

### Tips

- Avoid taking two maneuver actions in a single turn. Combined with her automatic end-of-turn draw, this forces you to draw three cards at once, burning through your deck far too rapidly.
- Occupying two spaces means you can be targeted from either. On maps with adjoining sections, spreading across boundaries can put you in three zones simultaneously, opening you up to ranged attacks from multiple angles.
- Kite other melee fighters by maneuvering just one space away and attacking from a distance. If they spend an action to move adjacent and attack you, you can unleash a devastating double attack on your subsequent turn.
