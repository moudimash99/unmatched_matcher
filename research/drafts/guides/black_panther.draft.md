## Black Panther

**In short:** An aggressive hybrid duo that strips cards from the opponent's deck to fuel massive attacks. He turns enemy resources against them, but mandatory card draw from his boosts puts him on a dangerous exhaustion clock.

### Plan A: Lock away their deck

**Goal:** Pull cards straight from their deck into the Vibranium Suit to deny them resources and build a stockpile of boosts.
**How to do it:** Cards stored in the suit never enter the opponent's hand or discard pile. Shuri's scheme, Tactical Remote Scanning (2 copies, boost 3), is your strongest tool because it steals the top two cards without risking combat. Try to play it early. Analyze and Adjust (3 copies, value 3) secures one card after combat regardless of who wins. Since Shuri is ranged, she can throw it from safety to force the opponent to react to her. Ancestral Insight (3 copies, value 4) requires you to win the combat to get the steal, so play it on defense against weak bait attacks, or against predictable setups to guarantee the trigger.
**Watch out for:** The sources disagree heavily on Shuri's overall value: one claims she is crucial and losing her before she schemes usually loses you the game, while another finds her highly overrated and says you can win comfortably without her. She only has 6 health, so aggressive opponents will try to eliminate her immediately. Furthermore, stealing is random. You might grab their best attacks, or you might hit low-value defensive cards that offer little boost power.

### Plan B: Explode with massive boosts

**Goal:** Overwhelm the opponent's defenses by adding stolen boost values to your own combat values.
**How to do it:** His special ability forces you to draw a card every time you boost, whether on a maneuver or a card effect. By boosting with the cards trapped in your Vibranium Suit rather than your hand, you actually grow your hand size, keeping the pressure high. Wakanda Forever! (3 copies, value 3) is his main finisher because it lets you boost up to twice. Adding two stolen 3-boost or 4-boost cards pushes it to 9 or more, breaking through almost any block. For unpredictable trades, Anti-Metal Claws (2 copies, value 1) automatically adds the opponent's boost value to itself, and then lets you boost it further. This creates huge swings against fighters with high-boost cards, like Dracula or Yennenga. On defense, Microweave Mesh (2 copies, value 2) lets you see the incoming attack before deciding whether to boost, ensuring you only spend resources when necessary.
**Watch out for:** Feint. If the opponent cancels Wakanda Forever! or Anti-Metal Claws, you waste the boost cards and still take the mandatory draw. Use smaller boostable attacks like Vibranium Shockwave to bait out their cancels first.

### Plan C: Starve recursion fighters

**Goal:** Trap key cards permanently so fighters who rely on their discard pile run out of options.
**How to do it:** Because stored cards technically do not exist in the discard pile, effects that recycle cards cannot target them. If you steal a vital piece of recursion from fighters like Elektra, Daredevil, or Sinbad, simply leave it in your suit for the entire game instead of spending it on a maneuver or attack. They will play the rest of the match with a permanent hole in their deck and strategy. With up to ten possible steals in your deck, you can severely limit the options of opponents who run smaller 20-card or 22-card decks.
**Watch out for:** You draw through your deck much faster than they do. You cannot simply sit back and wait for them to exhaust from missing cards, because your mandatory draws mean you will likely empty your deck first. You still have to end the game with combat damage.

### How they lose

- He burns through his 30-card deck too fast. Every boost forces a draw, making him highly vulnerable to late-game exhaustion if he cannot close out the fight.
- The opponent kills Shuri on the first or second turn before she can use her Tactical Remote Scanning schemes, denying him four potential stolen cards.
- He suffers from bad luck on his steals, grabbing weak 1-boost cards instead of high-value targets, leaving his own attacks underpowered.
- Heavy cancel decks strip the boosts off his big attacks, leaving him with low base values and a shrinking deck.
- He struggles on large maps. Because he only moves 2, chasing a kiting opponent forces him to boost his maneuvers, which accelerates his exhaustion.

### Key cards

- **Tactical Remote Scanning**: the best steal effect. Two copies guarantee four cards removed from the opponent without any combat risk.
- **Wakanda Forever!**: his main damage engine. Boost it twice for huge hits and draw two cards to refill your hand.
- **Anti-Metal Claws**: punishes opponents for playing high-boost defenses, and scales up incredibly high.
- **Analyze and Adjust**: an attack that steals a card after combat whether it wins or not. It has a boost of 3, so save it for the effect rather than tossing it for movement.
- **Stalking Panther**: moves both fighters up to 3 spaces and gains an action. A great way to close distance for a double attack without triggering a mandatory draw from a maneuver boost.

### Tips

- Always try to boost using the opponent's cards from the suit rather than your own, so you do not deplete your strongest tools.
- Keep Shuri out of reach. Her defenses are weak, and her Nanotriage Processor healing is usually too minor to save her from a focused assault. Cat-Like Reflexes is a good 3-value defense to throw out if you just need to keep her alive.
- Attack with Regroup or unboosted smaller attacks first to clear out the opponent's Feints before committing to Wakanda Forever!.
- If you draw Tactical Remote Scanning in your opening hand, use your first actions to maneuver Shuri into position and play it immediately.
- Pick smaller maps with hiding spots for Shuri. The less you have to maneuver, the less you have to boost and draw.
