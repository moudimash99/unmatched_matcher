## Spike

**In short:** A melee fighter with a deck full of versatile cards who relies on map control. He places shadow tokens to trigger his best card effects, but struggles if the opponent refuses to step into them.

### Plan A: Lure them into the shadows

**Goal:** Place shadow tokens in key paths and trick the opponent when they enter them.
**How to do it:** Spike's strategy revolves around his shadow tokens. His special ability lets you place one in a space adjacent to Spike or Drusilla at the start of every turn, even if a fighter is already standing there. Seek the Shadows (4 copies) speeds this up by drawing two cards and placing tokens on Spike's space and every adjacent space. Never stray far from your tokens; double maneuver to force the opponent to approach you through them. When an opponent first steps onto a shadow, attack with Regroup (3 copies, value 1). They will expect a heavy hit and play a Feint. You will draw a card (or two if you win the combat) and save your real threats. Once they are fighting in the shadows, Let's Dance (3 copies, value 4) lets you boost your combat value, and The Rush (2 copies) becomes a 5-value attack.
**Watch out for:** You only have three shadow tokens. Once they are all on the board, your ability only lets you move one of them to an adjacent space. If you maneuver away from your tokens, opponents will just wait you out.

### Plan B: Pin and punish

**Goal:** Trap an opposing fighter so they cannot escape your shadow effects.
**How to do it:** Savvy opponents will avoid your tokens entirely. To force the issue, use Spike and Drusilla to physically pin an enemy. Once they are trapped, your start-of-turn ability lets you drop a shadow token directly under them. With the opponent pinned on a shadow, play Drusilla's scheme The Sight (2 copies). It lets you look at their hand and force them to discard a card, or two cards of your choice if any of their fighters are on a shadow, completely stripping their best defenses. You can then follow up with Bloody Hell! (3 copies, value 3) to deal one guaranteed post-combat damage, plus another for each shadow token adjacent to them.
**Watch out for:** If Drusilla dies, you lose access to The Sight and Always Surprising (3 copies, value 1). The latter is a versatile that blind-boosts, doubling the boost if Drusilla is on a shadow token; since most of your deck's boosts are 2s, this averages a 5-value hit you will sorely miss.

### Plan C: Defend and reposition

**Goal:** Use defense cards to slide enemies into the shadows.
**How to do it:** Twenty-one of his thirty cards are versatiles. This gives him a lot of defensive flexibility when an opponent finally approaches. Defend with Leap Away or Skirmish (2 copies each, value 4). If you win the combat, these cards let you move a fighter up to 4 or 2 spaces, respectively. You can push the attacker directly onto a shadow token at the end of their turn, setting them up for an attack on yours. Alternatively, move Spike to the other side of your shadow tokens, forcing the opponent to walk through them again to reach you.
**Watch out for:** These movement effects only happen if you win the combat. His cards are highly dependent on the tokens; without them, most of his deck is just weak printed numbers.

### How they lose

- Ranged fighters stay back and shoot, never needing to step on a shadow token.
- Opponents recognize how his deck works and simply refuse to approach, forcing Spike to abandon his shadows to fight.
- Without shadow tokens, his cards lose their extra text and become easy to defend against.
- Drusilla dies early, taking away his best forced discard effect.
- He is highly map-dependent. Some maps restrict how many tokens you can place at once with Seek the Shadows because of adjacency limitations.

### Key cards

- **Seek the Shadows**: A scheme that draws two cards and places shadow tokens on Spike and all adjacent spaces. Your best way to gain map control early.
- **The Sight**: A scheme for Drusilla. You look at the opponent's hand and discard one card, or two cards if they have a fighter on a shadow token. A devastating hand disruption tool.
- **Let's Dance**: A versatile attack. If either fighter is on a shadow token, you may boost it.
- **Bloody Hell!**: A versatile that deals 1 damage after combat, plus 1 more for every shadow token adjacent to the opponent.
- **Leap Away**: A versatile that moves either fighter up to 4 spaces if you win the combat. Use it to push enemies into shadows or escape.

### Tips

- Pay close attention to your cards: some require Spike to be on a shadow token, others require Drusilla, and others require the opposing fighter.
- Use Empathy (2 copies, value 3) when the *opposing fighter* is not on a shadow token. Drawing three cards from its shadow effect often bloats your hand and forces you to discard, so the standard one-card draw is safer.
- Seek the Shadows has a boost value of 2, making it excellent for boosting your maneuvers to extend the reach of your shadows, or for pairing with Let's Dance.
- Arrogance (1 copy, value 4) becomes a 6 if you discard your hand. It is meant to be intimidating, but discarding your whole hand leaves you defenseless and is rarely worth the cost.
- He performs better in 2v2 team formats, where the board is crowded and shadow tokens are much harder for opponents to avoid.
