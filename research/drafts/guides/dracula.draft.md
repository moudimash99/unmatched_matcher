## Dracula

**In short:** A patient, calculating melee fighter who slowly drains his opponent's health and hand. He uses his Sisters to block paths and deal chip damage, baits out defensive cancels, and wins the game with one massive, boosted attack.

### Plan A: Set up the Beastform finisher

**Goal:** Chip the opponent into lethal range and land a huge attack they cannot survive.
**How to do it:** Beastform (2 copies) is a 6-value attack that adds +1 for every card you discard from your hand. Your goal is to keep your hand near the 7-card limit by double maneuvering or passing with schemes, building a massive resource advantage. Once the opponent is hovering around 8 to 10 health, play Mistform. This scheme places Dracula in any space and grants an action, letting you teleport adjacent to the enemy and immediately drop Beastform. By discarding a full hand, this attack can reach a value of 13 or 14.
**Watch out for:** Mistform into Beastform is incredibly predictable, so an opponent will usually hold their best defense for it. The sources disagree on how to handle the two copies: some recommend dumping your hand on the first one to guarantee a kill, while others suggest using the first Beastform with few or no discards just to deal a solid 4 to 6 damage and draw out a cancel, saving the massive discard for the final blow.

### Plan B: Strip their defenses and cancels

**Goal:** Remove the opponent's Feints and high-value blocks before you commit to Beastform.
**How to do it:** Because Beastform requires you to discard your hand, a Feint on a fully boosted Beastform is disastrous. You must clear their cancels first. Do My Bidding (2 copies) is a 3-value defense that returns the opponent's attack card to their hand, then lets you look at their hand and force them to play an attack or versatile card instead. Use this to rip a Feint out of their hand and force them to waste it as an attack. Ambush forces a random discard and adds its boost value to your attack, further punishing their defensive options. You can also use weaker cards like Dash or Exploit on attack to bait out their remaining cancels.
**Watch out for:** Do My Bidding only triggers if the opponent attacks you. Opponents who realize your plan may play defensively and refuse to attack, denying you the chance to pick apart their hand.

### Plan C: Chip damage and Sister positioning

**Goal:** Deal undefended damage to soften the enemy without risking combat.
**How to do it:** Dracula wants to avoid direct combat until the end. His start-of-turn ability deals 1 damage to an adjacent fighter and draws a card. Use Thirst for Sustenance to pin an opponent next to Dracula; if they fail to escape on their turn, they take the ping damage and give you card advantage. The Sisters bring Ravening Seduction, a scheme that moves any fighter up to 2 spaces and deals 1 damage for each Sister adjacent to them. Positioning the Sisters properly before playing this can guarantee up to 3 unblockable damage.
**Watch out for:** The Sisters have very few defensive options. Their best block is Exploit (a 4-value versatile), so once opponents realize how fragile they are, they will aggressively clear them off the board. Feeding Frenzy and Ravening Seduction become worthless if the Sisters are dead.

### How they lose

​- He has a low base health of 13 and a severe lack of pure defense cards. With nine schemes in his deck, he frequently draws hands with no blocks, leaving him exposed to aggressive, action-heavy fighters.
​- Ranged attackers easily kite him. They refuse to end their turn adjacent to Dracula, denying him the card draw and free damage from his ability.
​- Hand disruption and random discard effects ruin his Beastform setup by constantly lowering his hand size.
​- His Beastforms sit at the bottom of the deck, leaving him treading water and taking damage while he waits for his win condition.
​- Area-of-effect damage easily wipes out the 1-health Sisters, removing his board presence.

### Key cards

​- **Beastform**: his game-winning attack. Keep your hand full to maximize the discard damage, and make sure their cancels are gone before you play it.
​- **Mistform**: places him anywhere and grants an action. The perfect setup for a surprise Beastform, or an emergency escape when pinned.
​- **Do My Bidding**: his best defense. It provides perfect hand knowledge and lets you force the opponent to waste a crucial cancel or heavy attack.
​- **Ravening Seduction**: the primary reason to keep the Sisters alive. It deals up to 3 undefended damage and can force an opponent into a pin so Dracula can use his ability.
​- **Baptism of Blood**: a scheme that heals 2 health and revives a Sister. Play it to rebuild your board presence before using Ravening Seduction.

### Tips

​- Dracula's start-of-turn ability is optional. If your deck is running empty, skip the damage so you do not take fatal exhaustion damage from being forced to draw a card.
​- His ability can target *any* adjacent fighter. In a pinch, you can deal 1 damage to your own Sister to kill her and draw a card if you desperately need an answer.
​- Prey Upon is an excellent counter to opponents with multiple sidekicks. If you catch several fighters adjacent to Dracula, you deal damage to all of them and recover health for each.
​- Look Into My Eyes is a highly risky 1-value defense because many strong attacks have very low boost values. Use it only when you have no other options.
​- Don't waste your few good blocks on the Sisters. It is often better to let a Sister die and revive her later with Baptism of Blood than to leave Dracula defenseless.
