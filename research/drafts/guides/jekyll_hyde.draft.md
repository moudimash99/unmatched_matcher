## Jekyll & Hyde

**In short:** A form-switching solo fighter who stays as Dr. Jekyll to defend and draw cards, then transforms to Mr. Hyde to deliver massive attacks. Hyde hurts himself when maneuvering, so he relies on card effects to let the opponent come to him.

### Plan A: Build a massive Forever Hyde

**Goal:** End the game with a single, heavily boosted attack.
**How to do it:** Forever Hyde (2 copies) is a 5-value attack that lets you discard Dr. Jekyll cards to add 2 to its value for each. To fuel it, stay as Dr. Jekyll for about 75% of the game. Draw cards with Scientific Method, a 2-value defense that draws cards equal to the damage you take. Since you draw after combat, on the opponent's turn, this can push your hand above the 7-card limit and set up a huge attack for your next turn. Save your weakest Jekyll cards, Distracted Triage and Succumb to Compulsion, purely as discard fodder for Forever Hyde.
**Watch out for:** Feints cancel the boost, leaving the attack at a flat 5. Discarding too many defensive cards early in the game leaves you helpless later, so balance your hand and don't throw away your best blocks.

### Plan B: Move Hyde without maneuvering

**Goal:** Close the distance as Mr. Hyde without taking his damage penalty.
**How to do it:** Hyde takes 1 damage every time he maneuvers. To avoid this, force the opponent to come to you. If they maneuver and attack, they end their turn adjacent to you. You can then transform into Hyde at the start of your turn and attack twice. If you must move, use Pure Evil, a scheme that places Hyde anywhere in his current zone and deals 2 damage to all adjacent fighters. Alternatively, defend an attack as Jekyll with With Haste! to move 4 spaces toward them, putting you in position for a Hyde turn.
**Watch out for:** Pure Evil only places him within his current zone. Opponents who watch the board zones can position themselves just out of reach, denying you the effect. If you have to maneuver as Hyde, it is often worth discarding a card to boost your movement so you close the gap in a single action.

### Plan C: Don't get caught defending as Hyde

**Goal:** Switch back to Dr. Jekyll before the opponent swings back.
**How to do it:** Hyde has no dedicated defense cards. If you end your turn next to the opponent as Hyde, they will punish you. Recoiling Blow hits for 5, places him anywhere in his zone, and transforms him to Jekyll. Play it as your last action. On the opponent's turn, Madness Relents is his best defense: it blocks for 4 and transforms you back to Jekyll, protecting you from their second action.
**Watch out for:** Opponents know Recoiling Blow is your escape route and will often Feint it. If they do, you still deal 3 damage, but you are stuck as Hyde. Enemy cards that move you after combat can also strand Hyde far away, forcing a painful maneuver on your next turn.

### How they lose

​- Aggressive opponents force Jekyll to play his defense cards early, starving Forever Hyde of fuel.
​- They get stranded as Hyde with no cards to transform back, taking heavy damage from weak blocks or from their own maneuver penalty.
​- Their turns are predictable. Jekyll's attacks are weak and Hyde's are dangerous, so opponents know exactly when to take the hit and when to spend their Feints and strong blocks.
​- Low mobility makes it hard to chase down ranged kiters, bleeding their health as they repeatedly maneuver as Hyde.
​- They play Calming Research to draw cards and are forced to bury their best attacks at the bottom of the deck.

### Key cards

​- **Forever Hyde**: the deck's win condition. A 5-value attack that scales with discarded Jekyll cards.
​- **Duality of Man**: an Any versatile. It is a 6 on defense for Jekyll, and a 6 on attack for Hyde. Never use it as a 3-value attack with Jekyll or a 3-value defense with Hyde.
​- **Recoiling Blow**: a 5-value Hyde attack that resets your position and your form.
​- **Scientific Method**: a 2-value Jekyll defense that turns taken damage into drawn cards. Play it against big attacks to fill your hand.
​- **Calming Research**: a Jekyll scheme that heals 2 and lets you look at up to 3 cards. You keep one, and the rest go to the bottom of the deck. The sources suggest drawing just 1 to avoid burying key cards, or 0 if you have a full hand or are nearing exhaustion.
​- **With Haste!**: a 4-value Jekyll defense that moves him 4 spaces. Essential for repositioning without taking damage.

### Tips

​- When With Haste! is played on defense, its movement resolves before the attacker's after-combat effects. This allows you to step away and break the attack chains of aggressive fighters.
​- Keep your Skirmish cards exclusively to defend as Hyde. He needs the 4-value blocks more than Jekyll does.
​- Use Pure Evil to get adjacent to the opponent, then attack with Skirmish. Since they expect a massive Hyde attack, this often baits out their Feint, clearing the way for Forever Hyde later.
​- Strange Case deals damage equal to the boost value of your top card, but it reveals the card to the opponent. Be prepared for them to know what you are holding.
​- If you have an early lead on health, aggressively take damage on Scientific Method to build a massive hand.
