## Muhammad Ali

**In short:** A complex, defensive fighter who plays for exhaustion while looking for the perfect opening. He switches between Float Like a Butterfly for reach and tricky defenses, and Sting Like a Bee for heavy damage combos.

### Plan A: Play for exhaustion with defensive value

**Goal:** Outlast the opponent through healing, card recursion, and avoiding damage.
**How to do it:** Stay in Float Like a Butterfly during the opponent's turn. It grants access to your best defensive effects. Rope-a-dope heals 3 if you lose the combat, turning a low block into a massive health swing. Ali Shuffle lets you shuffle a non-scheme card from your discard pile into your deck when you win. Use it to recur Rope-a-dope for more healing, or to keep your deck thicker than your opponent's. Stick and move in Butterfly locks the opponent's card to its printed value, stopping fighters who boost their attacks (like Achilles or Houdini). Fancy footwork moves you immediately; if you land in a space sharing no zones with the attacker, you ignore their card's value completely.
**Watch out for:** Getting stuck in Sting Like a Bee. His printed defenses are weak in Bee, turning cards like Rope-a-dope into blank 2-value blocks. If you must end your turn in Bee, ensure you have a strong defense ready.

### Plan B: Set up a combo knockout

**Goal:** Unleash a string of high-damage attacks when the opponent's hand is weak.
**How to do it:** Look at their hand using Hard to be humble or Stronger than the skill. When you see they lack 4-value defenses or cancels, strike. The scheme Answer the bell is your ideal starter: it moves you 3 spaces and gives an action back, letting you double-attack without spending a maneuver to approach. Start with Momentous Shift. Because Answer the bell moved you, its value becomes 5, and Sting Like a Bee adds another 2, making it a 7-value attack. Follow up with The greatest. If you dealt 6 or more combat damage this turn, it grants two extra actions to keep hitting.
**Watch out for:** Forcing The greatest. The sources disagree on this card: some build their entire strategy around triggering its extra actions, while others call it a trap that rarely works. Cancels completely stop the action gain even if you hit the damage threshold.

### Plan C: Drain their hand

**Goal:** Strip away their best defenses to make your attacks land.
**How to do it:** Stronger than the skill is a 3-value attack that lets you look at their hand and, if you win, shuffle one of their cards into their deck. Play it in Bee so it hits for 5, guaranteeing a win against most blocks. Use this to remove their best defense. Follow it up with Louisville lip. This scheme draws you two cards, heals one, and if you won a combat this turn, forces a random discard. Jab is a perfect fake-out to draw defenses out early. It draws a card and gains an action. Throw a Jab pretending it's a big attack, eat their block, then launch your real strike.
**Watch out for:** Louisville lip requires you to have won a combat *this turn*. Do not play it first or after a tied combat.

### How they lose

​- Opponents with numerous cancels (Feints) stop his stance-dependent effects and ruin his combo turns.
​- Aggressive fighters who catch him in Sting Like a Bee tear through his weak defenses.
​- Opponents with strong movement effects after combat (Bruce Lee, Houdini) escape his range before he can launch his second attack.
​- High-value uncancelable attacks or offensive cancels (like Invisible Man's 5-value attacks) punch through his tricky defensive movement.

### Key cards

​- **Momentous Shift**: his hardest-hitting attack. Cards like Answer the bell, Fancy footwork, and Jab move him to make this a 5 before Bee's bonus.
​- **Ali Shuffle**: the engine for his exhaustion game. It recycles healing and defensive tools while keeping his deck count high.
​- **Fancy footwork**: a 1-value versatile. It can dodge massive attacks defensively, or guarantee a win offensively if you position carefully to share no zones.
​- **Close and clinch**: a 3-value versatile that places Ali adjacent to the opposing fighter and locks both in their space. Use it to stop opponents from running away, or to drag ranged fighters into a brawl.
​- **Answer the bell**: a scheme that gives movement, healing, and an action. His most reliable way to start a double-attack.

### Tips

​- Always use the stance token. It is easy to forget which stance he is in.
​- Save Champion of the world for defense. It is a 4-value versatile and his only reliable big block that doesn't rely on stance or positioning.
​- When you attack with a Jab in Butterfly, use its movement to set up the perfect distance for your next attack.
​- Against fighters with sidekicks, aim The greatest at a 1-health sidekick. They are often left undefended, guaranteeing your 6 damage and extra actions.
​- Take your time. He requires careful planning and precise positioning to get the most out of his cards. Do not play aggressively just because he is a boxer.
