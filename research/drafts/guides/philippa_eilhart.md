## Philippa Eilhart

**In short:** A ranged fighter who leverages an optional card draw ability to sustain relentless aggression or a distant stalling game. She attacks with lower-value cards, forces difficult discard choices, and uses hand knowledge to dictate the match.

### Plan A: Relentless aggression

**Goal:** Attack every turn to wear the opponent down, using her ability to replace the cards you spend.
**How to do it:** Her ability, TWO STEPS AHEAD, lets you draw until you have four cards at the end of your turn. This makes boosting maneuvers early in the game painless, as you simply replace the spent cards. Attack constantly with her weaker cards. The opponent feels bad spending a strong block on them, but taking the hits undefended quickly adds up. Use Lightning bolt to deal 1 extra damage to every enemy in her zone, provided you have not maneuvered. End your turn with Owlform, discarding your remaining hand to boost its attack value, then immediately use your ability to refill your hand. This makes going all-in far less risky than similar mechanics on other fighters.
**Watch out for:** Fighters with strong card draw (like Dr. Strange) or who gain advantages through their deck over time (like Ciri) can weather the storm and punish you. Going too fast also empties your deck, leaving you vulnerable to exhaustion damage.

### Plan B: Hand disruption and stall

**Goal:** Starve the opponent of valuable cards and avoid combat.
**How to do it:** Keep your distance and force the opponent to throw away their best options. Spymaster's ruse makes them reveal four cards, letting you discard two of your choice before they draw replacements. Redanian plot and Cunning give them a choice: discard a card, or suffer a negative effect (ignoring their card's value or taking 2 damage). When they attack, use Do my bidding to look at their hand and force them to play a less optimal card. To keep them away, Polymorphy lets you place Philippa anywhere and gives her a move value of 5. Place Dijkstra in their path so they have to spend actions moving around him instead of attacking her. Spellbreaker immediately cancels effects and ends the turn, making it a safe first action to stall the game when you have a good hand.
**Watch out for:** If Dijkstra dies before you use Spymaster's ruse, your stall game suffers heavily. Additionally, running away with Polymorphy means you will have to discard it at the end of the turn, as it only stays in play if you share a zone with an enemy. Finally, stalling is dangerous against fighters who become stronger as the game goes on; you will need to pivot to aggression against them.

### How they lose

- She only has 12 health and her defenses (like Blinding dust and Paralyzing fetters) are mostly low values. High-damage attackers can kill her quickly if she is caught.
- She runs out of cards and dies to fatigue if she leans too hard into her aggressive tools without finishing the opponent.
- Fighters who can comfortably maintain hand advantage easily brush off her barrage of small attacks.

### Key cards

- **Owlform**: Your main damage card. Emptying your hand to boost it is easily mitigated by your character ability.
- **Do my bidding**: A defense that returns their card and forces them to play a card of your choice from their hand. Beyond defense, it gives you perfect hand knowledge to safely launch an Owlform on your next turn.
- **Spellbreaker**: A versatile cancel that belongs only to Philippa, meaning Dijkstra cannot play it.
- **Polymorphy**: A scheme that grants incredible mobility, but requires you to end your turn in a zone with an opposing fighter to keep the movement boost active.
- **Backup plan**: A scheme that can set Philippa's health to 5, which is useful when she is near death. Alternatively, you can use it to draw 3 cards to fuel a massive Owlform attack.

### Tips

- TWO STEPS AHEAD is optional, which allows you to avoid exhaustion damage late in the game. However, if you choose to use it, you must draw completely up to four cards; you cannot choose to partially draw to save your deck.
- Paralyzing fetters locks an opponent's card to its printed value. This stops all value changes, including character abilities (like Alice's size) and map rules (like high ground).
- Chain lightning targets a single zone Philippa is in. If she stands on a space with multiple colors, you must pick just one of those zones.
- Match your map to your plan: a small map helps you engage constantly for aggression, while a large map gives you space to utilize Polymorphy's high movement.
- Regicide jumps to a massive 6 value against heroes. Even if the effect is canceled, its base value of 4 is high enough to force damage through most blocks.
