## Eredin Bréacc Glas

**In short:** A melee fighter who sacrifices his four Red Riders to become enraged. He transitions from managing his sidekicks early to hitting with high values and upgraded card effects once they are gone.

### Plan A: Feed the Red Riders to the hunt

**Goal:** Empty the board of sidekicks to trigger King of the Wild Hunt.
**How to do it:** Eredin becomes enraged only when all Red Riders are defeated. Use their deaths to pay for your card effects. Foul purpose returns to your hand if you defeat a Red Rider after combat. Icy guile ignores the opponent's card value if you defeat a Red Rider during combat. You can also play Portal defense to swap Eredin with an adjacent Red Rider, forcing the sidekick to take a massive hit for you.
**Watch out for:** The sources disagree on pacing. Several players argue you should kill the sidekicks as fast as possible to unlock Eredin's power immediately. Others say you should use them first to drain the opponent's defensive cards, bringing them back with Unyielding hordes to keep up the early pressure.

### Plan B: Batter them with enraged attacks

**Goal:** Overwhelm the opponent using his passive bonuses and upgraded card text.
**How to do it:** When enraged, Eredin adds +1 to all his combat cards and moves 3 spaces. This turns his standard deck into a heavy-hitting arsenal. Skirmish becomes a 5-value versatile. Backhand hits for 5 and forces the opponent to discard a random card instead of placing it on their deck. Implacable becomes a boostable 4-value attack. Wild hunt hits for 4 and deals 2 damage to adjacent enemies. His reach extends too: an enraged Foul purpose can target a fighter in any space, giving him an attack with unlimited range.
**Watch out for:** Opponents will try to stop these heavy hits with Feints. Punish them with Brutal strike, an uncancelable attack that ignores the opponent's value if their card has an IMMEDIATELY effect.

### How they lose

- If the map is open and the opponent is a ranged fighter, he struggles to close the distance while his movement is still 2.
- He lacks early offensive options and can run out of attacks in the first half of the game if his hand is not managed carefully.
- He gets caught holding only Portal defense when no Red Riders are adjacent, leaving him with a 0-value block.

### Key cards

- **Foul purpose**: a 3-value attack. Sacrifice a Red Rider to recycle it early, or use it to strike across the board once enraged.
- **Icy guile**: a 2-value versatile. When Eredin is enraged, it cancels all effects on the opponent's card. If he is not, you can defeat a Red Rider to ignore the opponent's value instead.
- **Portal defense**: a 0-value defense that swaps Eredin with an adjacent friendly fighter. Once enraged, it draws 2 cards and blocks for 1, which helps if you have a massive health lead and can afford to absorb the damage.
- **Brutal strike**: a 2-value attack that ignores defensive cancels.
- **Might of the Aen Elle**: a scheme that makes Eredin immune to effect damage. Play it against fighters who rely on non-combat damage, but remember you must attack that turn to keep the card in play.

### Tips

- You cannot use Icy guile to both cancel effects and ignore values at the same time. The cancel checks if you are already enraged; defeating your last Red Rider during that combat happens too late to trigger it.
- Always keep at least one Red Rider adjacent to Eredin as long as they are alive, so Portal defense does not become a dead card.
- Save Backhand for the end of the game when Eredin is enraged, making the random discard hurt the most.
- Unyielding hordes adds +1 for each friendly fighter adjacent to the target, so surround the enemy before playing it.
