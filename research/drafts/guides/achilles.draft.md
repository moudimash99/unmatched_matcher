## Achilles

**In short:** A high-health melee fighter who turns into an offensive powerhouse when his sidekick dies. He hits hard and refills his hand constantly, but must manage a ticking exhaustion clock and limited mobility.

### Plan A: Plan Patroclus's death

**Goal:** Trigger the sidekick's ability on your terms without crippling your hand.
**How to do it:** When Patroclus is defeated, you must discard two random cards. You can force his death using his self-damaging attacks, Battle Frenzy and The Day of Your Doom. If the opponent avoids hitting him, play Under Achilles' Helm (value 2) on defense to swap places and make him take the hit instead. Prepare your hand size before he falls: stay near the maximum limit so you still have options left after the penalty, or empty your hand completely so you discard nothing.
**Watch out for:** Sources disagree on when Patroclus should die. Some prefer rushing his death immediately to start dealing massive damage. Others advise keeping him alive longer to chew through enemy sidekicks (like Sun Wukong's clones) or to delay exhaustion against evasive fighters. Be careful not to lose key cards to the random discard. If you play Battle Hardened as a defense card when Patroclus takes lethal damage, the discard happens during combat, letting you immediately return one of those lost cards to your hand.

### Plan B: Unleash angry Achilles

**Goal:** Overwhelm the opponent with boosted attacks and sustained pressure.
**How to do it:** While Patroclus is dead, Achilles adds +2 to the value of all his attacks and draws a card whenever he wins a combat. This turns standard versatile cards into massive threats. Brothers In Arms becomes a 6-value log. Test For Weakness creates a mind game: a 3-value initial attack followed by a 5-value bonus attack. If the opponent uses a big block to stop the first hit, they might take heavy damage from the second. Because you constantly replace your spent cards, you can attack repeatedly without losing card advantage.
**Watch out for:** The draw effect triggers on defense as well. Winning defensive combats speeds up your exhaustion clock, forcing you to end the game quickly before fatigue damage kills you.

### Plan C: Catch them and pass the turn

**Goal:** Close the distance and deal damage without drawing cards.
**How to do it:** Because Achilles only has a move value of 2, opponents will try to kite him and force him to waste cards on maneuvers. Keep Under Achilles' Helm in your hand after Patroclus dies; it becomes a weak block, but its boost value of 4 lets you sprint across the board. Once you reach the opponent, use Spear Throw. It deals 2 unblockable damage to anyone in your zone. Because it is a scheme, it does not trigger a combat, making it a safe way to pass your turn and deal damage without pulling a card from your shrinking deck.
**Watch out for:** Boosting with Test For Weakness gives you 3 extra movement, but costs you your trickiest offensive tool.

### How they lose

​- He exhausts too fast. Every combat won draws a card, meaning late-game defenses pull him closer to taking 2 fatigue damage per draw.
​- Kiting opponents stay out of range, forcing him to boost his maneuvers and depleting his hand.
​- Sidekick walls absorb his massive attacks, wasting his best cards before he can reach the enemy hero.
​- The random discard from Patroclus's death takes away essential late-game tools, such as Battle Hardened or his schemes.
​- Opponents intentionally attack with small values late in the game, knowing Achilles will win the combat and draw closer to exhaustion.

### Key cards

​- **Under Achilles' Helm**: a 2-value defense with a 4 boost. Early on, it forces damage onto Patroclus. Later, it provides the movement needed to chase down enemies.
​- **Test For Weakness**: a 1-value attack that triggers a 3-value bonus hit. It punishes opponents who spend high-value defenses early.
​- **Battle Hardened**: returns any card from your discard pile. Use it to loop itself against fighters with few cancels, or to retrieve a scheme for guaranteed damage.
​- **Brothers In Arms**: a 4-value attack. Play it early with Patroclus to gain an action, or save it for an unblockable 6-value swing late.
​- **Achilles' Heel**: a 4-value defense. If you lose the combat, the opponent gains an action, which accelerates your exhaustion by giving them more turns to pressure you.

### Tips

​- Use The Day of Your Doom to bait out Feints early, as opponents want to stop the attack from becoming a 5.
​- Blessed By Hermes moves your fighters up to 3 spaces after combat. Play it defensively against ranged fighters to end your turn adjacent to them.
​- Wily Fighting deals 1 damage to adjacent fighters after combat. Use it as a block against melee attackers; since the defender resolves first, the damage triggers before they can move away.
​- Do not recur Achilles' Heel or Brothers In Arms over Skirmish. Skirmish reaches the same 6 value on attack but can still be used as a safe 4-value block.
