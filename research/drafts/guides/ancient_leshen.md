## Ancient Leshen

**In short:** A slow, ranged fighter who relies on fast sidekicks to block the board and harass the enemy. He heals constantly while hiding, wears down the opponent's hand, and saves his massive +3 attack ability for a late-game finisher.

### Plan A: Harass with the wolves

**Goal:** Drain their health and cards without risking the hero.
**How to do it:** Leshen's ability limits him to moving 1 space, but his wolves have a move value of 3. Send them ahead to pressure the enemy. Attack with the 4-value **Command the forest** to force a discard if you win, or the 4-value versatile **Planted feet** to draw a card if your wolf hasn't left its space this turn. When the opponent hits back, defend the wolves with **Harrying strike**. This 2-value versatile adds +2 for every friendly fighter adjacent to the attacker; against a melee strike, the defending wolf is already adjacent, making it a reliable 4-value block. Forcing the opponent to spend extra cards just to clear 1-health sidekicks gives you a massive hand advantage. Replenish your own hand with the 2-value attack **Nature abounds**, drawing a card for every friendly fighter next to the target.
**Watch out for:** Do not overcommit trying to trigger Leshen's +3 ability early in the game. Opponents have plenty of resources to evade the second attack, and aggressively chasing them leaves you exposed. Stay patient and poke with the wolves.

### Plan B: Hide and heal

**Goal:** Recover his low 13 health and outlast the opponent.
**How to do it:** **Strength of the pack** (three copies) is your main survival scheme. It summons a wolf into Leshen's zone and heals him for 1 health at the start of every turn, as long as a wolf remains in that zone. Play it early and hide Leshen behind his sidekicks. Over a long game, this infinite healing can slowly erase massive amounts of damage. If an enemy breaks through your wall of wolves, play **Vanish into murder**. This scheme deals 1 damage to every opposing fighter in Leshen's zone, removes him from the board, and lets him drop into any space on your next turn with a bonus card draw. It clears out pesky 1-health sidekicks and perfectly escapes pins.
**Watch out for:** Opponents will prioritize killing your wolves to shut down the healing. Bring them right back with the 4-value attack **Primeval slam**, which summons a wolf into Leshen's zone and then lets you move each wolf up to 3 spaces to rebuild your defenses.

### Plan C: Set up the double attack

**Goal:** Land a massive hit in the late game when their defenses are low.
**How to do it:** His Heart of the Forest ability adds +3 to Leshen's attack if he already attacked this turn. The most consistent way to land this is by opening with the 5-value attack **Flock of birds**. After combat, it lets you place Leshen in any space, guaranteeing you can warp right next to the target for your +3 second attack even if they played a movement defense. Alternatively, you can pull off a three-action combo: attack with Leshen first, then have a wolf attack with **Disturbing howls** (a 1-value attack that moves the enemy up to 2 spaces and grants an action) to push them into Leshen's zone. You can then use the gained action to land Leshen's massive second hit.
**Watch out for:** An opponent who expects the combo will save a Feint or a high-value defense. You can subvert this by using Flock of birds as your final action, throwing a 5-value attack and warping safely away instead of pursuing the double hit.

### How they lose

- He has only 9 defense cards in his deck. If an opponent bypasses the wolves and chains extra actions together (like Bruce Lee), Leshen cannot block everything.
- Opponents who can shuffle their discard pile back into their deck (like Elektra) can completely ignore his exhaustion and discard tactics.
- His best defense, the 5-value **Primeval guardian**, forces the opponent to discard the top card of their deck and adds its boost value to their attack. Against fighters with naturally high boost values, this block breaks easily.
- His 1 movement makes it a struggle to catch opponents who stay far away and refuse to approach his zone.

### Key cards

- **Strength of the pack**: Your primary survival tool that ensures constant healing.
- **Vanish into murder**: An escape tool, guaranteed area damage, and card draw all in one. Save it for when you are heavily pressured or to confirm a lethal hit.
- **Flock of birds**: Your most versatile printed attack. Use it to hit and run safely, or to chase down an enemy to guarantee your ability.
- **Primeval guardian**: While risky against high boosts, the top-deck discard mills their deck for exhaustion, and the after-combat effect safely moves your wolves to set up your next turn.
- **Command the forest**: Let the wolves play this to safely pressure the opponent's hand from across the board.

### Tips

- Mulligan aggressively for attacks and Strength of the pack. You want to start by pressuring the opponent and establishing your healing; do not keep defenses in your opening hand.
- Defend Leshen with the 3-value versatile **Wily Fighting**. After combat, it deals 1 damage to each opposing fighter adjacent to you, heavily punishing melee attackers who finally reach your hero.
- Primeval guardian is great against attacks that set a fixed value (like Shift), because the value resets back to its fixed number after the top-deck boost is added.
- Expect savvy opponents to play offensive Feints against Leshen to cancel Primeval Guardian's top-deck discard or Wily Fighting's auto-damage.
- Draft maps with choke points or item tokens. His 1-movement hero and blocking sidekicks thrive in tight passages.
