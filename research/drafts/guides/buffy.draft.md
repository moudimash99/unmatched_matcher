## Buffy

**In short:** A highly mobile melee fighter who easily escapes pins because she can move through enemies. Before the match begins, she chooses between two sidekicks: Giles for hand disruption and card draw, or Xander for heavy attacks.

### Plan A: Pick the right sidekick

**Goal:** Tailor your deck to exploit the opponent's weaknesses.
**How to do it:** You choose your sidekick after the opponent reveals their fighter. Your choice replaces five cards in your deck.

- **Giles** brings the only scheme cards to Buffy's deck. Insight (3 copies) lets you look at the opponent's hand and force them to discard a card of your choice. This strips their best defense or Feint before you attack. Training (2 copies) draws 3 cards, which fixes Buffy's lack of natural card draw.
- **Xander** brings aggression. Military Knowledge (3 copies) is a 4-value attack that replaces itself by drawing a card. Right-hand Man (2 copies) is a versatile that hits for 6 when Xander is adjacent to Buffy.

**Watch out for:** Each choice leaves a gap. With Xander, Buffy struggles to maintain a large hand size. With Giles, she lacks heavy attacks and must keep him alive until his five schemes are played.

### Plan B: Deal damage after combat

**Goal:** Punish attackers and chip away health without relying on high attack values.
**How to do it:** Buffy uses defensive and versatile cards to deal unblockable damage while repositioning. Cartwheel Kick is a 2-value defense that lets her move up to 3 spaces after combat, then deals 1 damage to each adjacent opposing fighter. Stay just within the opponent's range to bait an attack, play Cartwheel Kick to mitigate the hit, then slide away and deal a damage.

Slayer's Strength is a 4-value versatile that lets you move all fighters adjacent to Buffy to another space in their zone, then deals 1 damage to each fighter moved. Played defensively, this ruins a melee opponent's positioning and deals unexpected damage.
**Watch out for:** Fighters with many cancels (Bigfoot, Black Widow) will Feint these cards. If Cartwheel Kick is canceled, Buffy is stuck taking a hit against a weak 2-value block with no movement or damage payoff.

### Plan C: Safe, heavy attacks

**Goal:** Land big hits that opponents cannot cancel.
**How to do it:** Opponents will want to save their Feints for Buffy's movement and damage effects. Punish this with Mr. Pointy (2 copies), a printed 5-value attack with no text. It cannot be canceled, meaning a Feint played against it is entirely wasted.

Follow up with Daring Strike, a 4-value attack that draws 2 cards if you win the combat. Because Buffy has limited card draw (especially without Giles), winning with Daring Strike is crucial for keeping your options open.
**Watch out for:** If you lose the combat with Daring Strike, you take 1 damage. With only 14 health, Buffy cannot afford to lose combats on her own turn too often. Only play it when you are confident the opponent has weak defenses in hand.

### How they lose

- Her health is low (14), making her vulnerable to large printed attacks if her maneuvers fail.
- Opponents with plenty of Feints shut down her crucial after-combat damage and movement effects.
- If she drafts Xander, she can easily run out of cards and lose the war of attrition.
- She relies heavily on her sidekick for either draw or damage; if the opponent hunts the sidekick down early, her game plan falls apart.

### Key cards

- **Insight**: A Giles scheme. Look at the opponent's hand and force them to discard a card of your choice. Her best tool for breaking enemy combos or stripping cancels.
- **Slayer's Strength**: A 4-value versatile that moves adjacent fighters within their zone and damages them. Excellent for defensive damage.
- **Mr. Pointy**: A 5-value attack with no effect. A clean, heavy hit that ignores Feints.
- **Right-hand Man**: A Xander 2-value versatile that becomes a 6 when he is adjacent to Buffy. Usually best played on offense, as savvy opponents will rarely attack a target that can defend with a 6.
- **Cartwheel Kick**: A 2-value defense that moves Buffy away and damages adjacent opponents.

### Tips

- Buffy's special ability allows her to move through opposing fighters. This makes her incredibly strong against heroes who rely on body-blocking or pinning to win, such as the Raptors, Dracula, or Spike.
- There is no map where Buffy cannot maneuver. Use her base movement of 3 and her ability to ensure you never end your turn backed into a corner.
- When playing Xander, use him offensively. Right-hand Man requires adjacency to Buffy, so move them as a pair.
- When playing Giles, keep him completely out of the fight until he has safely played his schemes.
- Use Slayer's Strength against fighters with 1-health sidekicks to clear multiple targets at once without spending separate attack actions.
