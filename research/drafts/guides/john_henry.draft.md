## John Henry

**In short:** A tough melee fighter who lays a network of track tokens across the board. He uses free movement on his tracks to build momentum and power up his heavy attacks.

### Plan A: Build momentum on the tracks

**Goal:** Move extensively to trigger the bonus effects on his Hammer cards.
**How to do it:** His Steel-Driving Man ability adds a track token to his zone at the start of every turn. When he maneuvers or moves, spaces with track tokens do not count toward his movement limit. This free movement helps him meet the requirement on his Hammer cards, which need him to have used at least 4 different paths this turn. When he meets this condition, Eighteen-pound Hammer (2 copies, value 6) cancels all effects on the opponent's card. Twelve-pound Hammer lets you place a fighter in the combat onto any track token. Nine-pound Hammer deals 2 damage to each opposing fighter in his zone.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who fight away from his tracks or pin him down stop him from getting the movement he needs for his best effects.

### Plan B: Hit harder in the late game

**Goal:** Turn spent cards into raw attack power.
**How to do it:** Bring Down The Mountain (2 copies) is a 3-value versatile that adds 1 to its value for each Hammer card in your discard pile. With seven Hammer cards in his deck, this card grows into a heavy hitter late in the game after he has spent his early attacks.
**Watch out for:** Playing it early in the game as a weak versatile wastes its potential. Wait until your discard pile has several Hammers in it.

### How they lose

- He relies on movement to enable his Hammer effects. Fighters who trap him prevent him from using enough paths.
- His printed defenses are weak. His highest dedicated defense is Deeds Of Valor, which is only a 2, though it caps the combat damage he can take at 2.
- He takes heavy hits often. Instead of blocking large attacks completely, he relies on Cool Drink Of Water (0 value) to heal back 3 health after the fact.

### Key cards

- **Eighteen-pound Hammer**: his biggest attack. With the movement condition met, it hits for 6 and cancels the opponent's defense effect.
- **Cool Drink Of Water**: a 0-value defense that recovers 3 health and draws 1 card. This is his main way to survive heavy damage.
- **Striking Fire**: a scheme that moves him up to 4 spaces and grants an action. It sets up his required movement perfectly before he attacks.
- **Bring Down The Mountain**: a versatile card that scales up late in the game based on the Hammer cards in his discard pile.

### Tips

- Momentous Shift (3 copies) hits for 5 if you started the turn in a different space. His free movement on tracks makes this very easy to trigger.
- Play Hear That Cold Steel Ring to draw a card, move a space, and gain a free attack action. This keeps your turn going and helps build more paths.
- Use Knock Them Silly (a 4-value versatile) on defense to cancel the opponent's after-combat effects, provided your 4 is enough to win the combat.
- Power Through moves a fighter in the combat up to 2 spaces without counting track tokens, letting you slide enemies across your network.
