## Willow

**In short:** A ranged duo that balances high-powered attacks with the chaotic effects of her Dark Willow form. She fights from a distance and uses her sidekick Tara to control her transformations and recover lost cards.

### Plan A: Manage the Dark Willow form

**Goal:** Unleash her strongest effects without destroying your own deck.
**How to do it:** Taking damage turns Willow into Dark Willow. When she is Dark, her exclusive cards gain powerful but costly effects. Flayed Alive (3 copies, value 4) and Black Magic (3 copies, value 3) force you to blind boost them, making them hit very hard. When Good Magic Fails adds teleportation to any space in her zone, but you must discard the top card of your deck. To turn back into regular Willow, end your turn with Dark Willow adjacent to Tara, or play Meditation on defense.
**Watch out for:** The sources strongly disagree on how to play this form. One argues you should minimize your time as Dark Willow because her random discards and blind boosts waste important cards. The other argues you should stay Dark Willow, hit fast, and try to win before your deck runs out, because regular Willow's attacks lack effects.

### Plan B: Attack and step away

**Goal:** Deal damage from range while keeping enemies at a distance.
**How to do it:** Willow and Tara are both ranged, so they can poke the enemy from afar. Put Tara between Willow and the opponent to act as a buffer. Attack with Swift Strike to move your fighter up to 4 spaces away after combat, forcing the opponent to waste cards to reach you. Rending Shot pushes the opposing fighter up to 3 spaces away, which ruins their positioning and prevents them from making a second attack on their turn.
**Watch out for:** Both fighters only have 2 movement. If a fast melee fighter corners them, it is difficult to escape.

### Plan C: Maximize Tara's utility

**Goal:** Use the sidekick to disrupt the opponent and retrieve discarded cards.
**How to do it:** Tara has excellent exclusive cards that keep the team running. Revoke is a 3-value versatile that cancels abilities, working effectively as a Feint. Knowledge of the Craft (2 copies, value 4) lets you retrieve any card from your discard pile if you win the combat. This is perfect for recovering a key card you lost to a Dark Willow blind boost. Since Tara will inevitably take hits while standing in front of Willow, let her die and then play Resurrect to bring her back to the board with 3 health.
**Watch out for:** If you play too recklessly as Dark Willow, you might discard Resurrect as a blind boost, leaving Tara dead for the rest of the game.

### How they lose

- She exhausts herself. Playing aggressively as Dark Willow forces you to blind boost and discard constantly, putting you on a fast exhaustion clock.
- She loses her sidekick early. Without Tara, Willow loses access to her cancel (Revoke) and her main way to revert from Dark Willow.
- Love and Loss backfires. Playing this scheme as Dark Willow forces you to discard two cards and deal 3 damage to a sidekick in her zone. If only Tara is around, you hurt your own team for no gain.
- She gets pinned. With low movement and no move-through effects, she struggles when trapped against the edge of the map.

### Key cards

- **Flayed Alive**: a 4-value attack that blind boosts when you are Dark Willow. Her main source of damage.
- **Meditation**: a 5-value defense. Her best block, and it instantly turns Dark Willow back into regular Willow.
- **Knowledge of the Craft**: a 4-value versatile for Tara. Crucial for getting back cards lost to random discards.
- **Resurrect**: a scheme that brings a defeated friendly fighter back with 3 health.
- **Love and Loss**: a scheme to draw 2 cards. The sources disagree on playing it as Dark Willow: one says never do it because it hurts your own sidekick and discards cards, while the other suggests using it to damage an enemy sidekick if you accept the discards.

### Tips

- Play Hacker before attacking as Dark Willow. Looking at the top card of your deck lets you know exactly what you are about to blind boost or discard.
- Let Tara take the lead. When she takes damage, Willow transforms, allowing you to counterattack hard with Dark Willow on your next turn.
- Keep Tara adjacent to Willow at the end of your turn to guarantee you revert to regular Willow.
- In 2v2 team games, Resurrect can target a defeated allied hero, bringing them back into the match.
