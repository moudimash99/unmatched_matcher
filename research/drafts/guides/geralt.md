## Geralt of Rivia

**In short:** A flexible melee fighter who chooses his key equipment before the game begins. He hits hard and disrupts his opponent's hand, but relies on his fragile sidekick and struggles with mobility.

### Plan A: Draft the perfect gear

**Goal:** Select the right Sword, Armor, and Potion to counter the opponent's game plan.
**How to do it:** Before the deck is shuffled, you add two copies each of an attack, a defense, and a scheme gear card.

- **Sword**: Sword of silver (value 4) ignores defense cards with a printed value of 4 or higher, bypassing heavy blocks like Bigfoot's. Sword of steel grants extra actions for a burst turn, potentially generating two actions against enemies with 8 or more health.
- **Armor**: Wolf Medallion (value 3) cancels effects and ignores the opponent's card value if they are not adjacent, crippling ranged attackers. Armor of the Forgotten Wolf (value 2) ends the turn and gains value for every adjacent fighter; the turn-ending effect halts hit-and-run tactics and non-combat damage like the Sisters' cauldron, while the value boost punishes swarms like the Raptors.
- **Potion**: Both schemes stay active on the board until you end a turn without attacking. Blizzard draws a card, moves Geralt up to 3 spaces, and prevents opponents from gaining actions. Tawny Owl draws two cards, provides an action, and stops discard effects.

Use Witcher Senses to find your gear early.
**Watch out for:** Tawny Owl is often considered the stronger overall option because its bonus action can be used to boost a maneuver, effectively replicating Blizzard's mobility while leaving you with more cards. However, Blizzard remains vital for shutting down enemies who rely on action-gain loops.

### Plan B: Mix up your heavy hits

**Goal:** Break through defenses by keeping the opponent guessing.
**How to do it:** Geralt has multiple tools to bypass a standard block. If you anticipate a strong defense, Sword of silver neutralizes it. If they play a weaker defense, Rend (value 3) lets you boost your attack value during combat; feed it a 4-boost card like Damn, you're ugly to hit for 7. If you expect a cancel, Igni has a base value of 0 but guarantees 2 post-combat damage. Use Dandelion's Plot twist to look at their hand, strip away their best defense, and pick the perfect attack for what remains.
**Watch out for:** Igni deals 1 collateral damage to *each other* adjacent fighter. This will hit Dandelion, so ensure your sidekick is clear of the blast zone before casting it.

### Plan C: Cash in Dandelion early

**Goal:** Get value from your sidekick before he is eliminated.
**How to do it:** Geralt's deck contains no ANY cards. Dandelion can only play his own six exclusive cards: three copies of Plot twist (to strip hands and draw) and three of Annoying tune (to push the opponent away). Burn through these quickly to disrupt the enemy and cycle your deck.
**Watch out for:** Dandelion only has 5 health and is easily picked off. Once he dies, his remaining cards become dead draws that can only be used to boost maneuvers or Rend.

### How they lose

- He bleeds value through movement. With a base move of 2 and no passive mobility tools outside of his potions, he must frequently burn cards to boost toward ranged kiters, draining his hand.
- He cannibalizes his resources. His most powerful cards are versatiles. If heavy pressure forces him to spend Damn, you're ugly or Disciplined Duelist on defense, his offensive threat drops significantly.
- He starts with a sturdy 16 health, but his deck contains zero healing. Every point of damage he takes is permanent.
- Losing his sidekick early deprives him of his primary hand-disruption and scouting tool.

### Key cards

- **Witcher Senses**: This scheme pulls a gear card from your deck or discard pile. If you search your deck, it shuffles back in, meaning you can play it early to set up and draw it again later to recycle discarded gear.
- **Damn, you're ugly**: A versatile 5 that hits incredibly hard on its own or provides a massive 4-value boost.
- **Disciplined Duelist**: A versatile 4 that punishes specific defense values: playing against a 2 lets you draw a card, a 3 deals 1 damage, and a 4 forces the opponent to discard.

### Tips

- Armor of the Forgotten Wolf gains +1 for *each* adjacent fighter. Parking Dandelion next to Geralt adds to its defensive value.
- Use Yrden to freeze an opponent in their space. This either allows Geralt to step away safely without eating a second attack, or pins a ranged fighter so they cannot flee on their next turn.
- If you use Rend defensively, remember that the defender's effects resolve first. You must commit to boosting before the attacker decides whether to trigger any boost effects of their own.
