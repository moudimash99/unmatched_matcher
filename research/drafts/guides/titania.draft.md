## Titania

**In short:** A tricky ranged fighter with a separate deck of six passive abilities called glamours. She cycles through them to power up her attacks, chain free schemes together, and set up an invincible exhaustion game, but she must protect her incredibly low starting health.

### Plan A: Cycle the glamours

**Goal:** Find the right passive ability for the moment and build up your discard pile to strengthen your cards.
**How to do it:** You start the game with no glamour. At the start of your turn, you flip the top card of your glamour deck face-up, and its effect stays active until discarded. Several of your versatile and defense cards require you to discard your active glamour to trigger their best effects. For example, Fairy Song jumps from a 3 to a 5, and Protection Of The Fairy Woods becomes a cancel. Because her main attack, Queen Of The Fairies, gains +1 value for every glamour in your discard pile, you are heavily incentivized to cycle through them. If you draw a great glamour at the wrong time, discard it anyway; you can use The Moon Looks Down to put it safely on the bottom of your deck for later.
**Watch out for:** If you discard your active glamour on your own turn to boost an attack, you will not reveal a new one until the start of your next turn. During the opponent's turn, you have no passive ability and cannot pay the discard cost to cancel effects with Protection Of The Fairy Woods. Experienced opponents will look for this window to hit you with their best attacks.

### Plan B: The scheme combo turn

**Goal:** Chain free actions together for massive damage and disruption.
**How to do it:** Glamour of Rhyme gives you an action back whenever you play a scheme. If you line your hand up, you can play several in one massive turn. Start with Gift Of The Fair Folk to draw cards and move your fighters into position. Follow up with What Fools These Mortals Be to look at the opponent's hand and shuffle away their best defense. Finally, drop A Momentary Glance to deal 2 damage. Because these schemes refunded your actions, you can now attack twice with Met By Moonlight or a fully charged Queen Of The Fairies against a defenseless opponent.
**Watch out for:** A Momentary Glance deals 2 damage to *any* fighter in Titania's zone. If the enemy maneuvers away and leaves Titania alone, you will have to deal the damage to yourself unless you are protected by Glamour of Sleep. Also, do not hold Glamour of Rhyme too long waiting for the perfect hand; it offers no defensive benefits while it is face-up.

### Plan C: Sleep out the clock

**Goal:** Survive into exhaustion where you take no damage.
**How to do it:** Glamour of Sleep prevents your fighters from taking any damage other than combat damage. This completely ignores effect damage from fighters like Tesla or Medusa, and it nullifies fatigue damage when your deck is empty. If you reach the bottom of your deck with Sleep active, you can maneuver freely while the opponent takes exhaustion damage. It also breaks the symmetry of Parting Gift: you deal 2 damage to every fighter in the combat, but take zero yourself. Use The Moon Looks Down to place Sleep at the very bottom of your glamour deck if you reveal it early in the match.
**Watch out for:** Opponents know this trick and will try to feint The Moon Looks Down to leave Sleep stranded in your discard pile. If you reach fatigue, they will try to play card effects that force you to discard your active glamour, stripping away your immunity.

### How they lose

- 12 health is one of the lowest starting hero health pools in the game. Aggressive melee fighters (like Bigfoot) rush her down before she can cycle to her defensive glamours.
- She actually only has 16 real combat cards, because But A Dream returns the opponent's card to their hand without going to the discard pile. Discard effects drain her small hand quickly.
- Oberon dies early. He holds her three 5-value attacks (Met By Moonlight) and serves as a crucial body block.
- Opponents save their cancels specifically for her big Queen Of The Fairies attacks and The Moon Looks Down.

### Key cards

- **But A Dream**: a 0-value defense that cancels all effects, ignores the printed value, and returns the attack to their hand. You draw a card. While they keep the card, you waste their entire action, which ruins fighters who rely on chained combo turns.
- **Queen Of The Fairies**: a 2-value attack that gains +1 for each discarded glamour. A massive late-game threat that can hit for up to 8.
- **Met By Moonlight**: a 5-value attack for Oberon that places him adjacent to Titania after combat. Send him in to hit hard, then pull him back to safety so the opponent has to waste movement to reach him.
- **The Moon Looks Down**: a 3-value versatile that returns a discarded glamour to the bottom of the deck. Crucial for saving Glamour of Sleep for the end game, or getting a second use out of a strong passive.
- **Protection Of The Fairy Woods**: a 2-value defense. Her only true cancel, but it costs your active glamour.

### Tips

- Glamour of Jealousy lets Titania and Oberon swap spaces when one is attacked, *before* cards are played. This is a guessing game: the opponent might poke with a weak attack to bait the swap, or swing hard expecting you to put Titania in harm's way.
- Glamour of Love lets you move an opposing fighter 1 space when you maneuver. Use it to pull hiding enemies into your zone, or push body-blocking sidekicks out of the way so you don't have to boost to reach your target.
- The sources disagree slightly on when to play Queen Of The Fairies: one recommends waiting until you have 4 or 5 glamours discarded to end the game, while another suggests taking a solid 5 or 6 value mid-game rather than holding it too long.
- If Oberon is at 1 health and about to die anyway, attack with Parting Gift. It will kill him, but it guarantees 2 damage to the opponent.
- Don't be afraid to discard a highly situational passive, like Glamour of Invisibility, just to cycle closer to the ones you actually need.
