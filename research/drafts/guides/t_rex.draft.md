## T. Rex

**In short:** A massive melee fighter with a 27 health pool and unprecedented reach. She draws a card at the end of every turn, creating a ticking exhaustion clock that forces her to play aggressively, burn her own health with self-damaging attacks, and boost her movement to hunt down her prey.

### Plan A: Close the distance and end it fast

**Goal:** Catch fleeing opponents and kill them before exhaustion does.
**How to do it:** With only 1 movement and a forced card draw every turn, she cannot sit back. You must boost your maneuvers constantly to cross the board. Use her large fighter ability to attack from up to two spaces away, which lets you reach over sidekicks. Closer Than She Appears (3 copies) acts as a free maneuver because it refunds the action you spent to play it. Ripples in the Water places her anywhere in her current zone, helping her close gaps instantly.
**Watch out for:** Some maps heavily punish her size. She ignores one-way arrows, making Raptor Paddock a strong choice, but she cannot use secret passages, leaving her easily kited on Baskerville Manor. When placing her with Ripples in the Water, remember she occupies two spaces, so part of her base can hang into an adjacent zone to get closer to the enemy.

### Plan B: Trade health for unavoidable damage

**Goal:** Burn through her massive health to guarantee damage against strong defenses.
**How to do it:** She has no Feints, but she has attacks that don't care about the opponent's block. 15,000 Pounds of Muscle (2 copies, value 3) ignores their printed value completely. Reckless Lunge (3 copies, value 3) deals 3 damage via its effect. When Dinosaurs Ruled the Earth (4 copies, value 2) can be boosted to a 5 or 6, refunding the card and action if you win.
**Watch out for:** Self-damage adds up fast. Playing every copy of these three cards deals 21 damage to yourself. Because Reckless Lunge has a boost value of 4, you should usually keep it in your hand to boost maneuvers rather than playing it as an attack. For When Dinosaurs Ruled the Earth, only boost it if you know the hit will deal at least 2 damage to the opponent; otherwise, you lose the health trade.

### Plan C: Control the board state

**Goal:** Pin down mobile fighters and clear out sidekick swarms.
**How to do it:** Terrifying Roar (2 copies, value 3) locks fighters in their space for the rest of the turn. Played on defense, the defender resolves first, which completely shuts down after-combat movement abilities from fighters like Bruce Lee or Robin Hood, and sets up a double attack for your next turn. To stop sidekicks from body-blocking you, play Thrash and boost it to win the combat, dealing 1 damage to every opposing fighter in her zone.
**Watch out for:** Ripples in the Water forces a random discard from the opponent, but only if they are adjacent to her. This clashes slightly with her preference to kite and attack from two spaces away, so position carefully before playing it.

### How they lose

​- The forced card draw means opponents who do nothing but run away and defend will easily win by exhaustion.
​- She only has 10 cards that can block. If she gets caught on the defensive, she runs out of options fast.
​- Fighters with strong action-gain combos (like the Raptors) or massive late-game damage (Elektra) can safely build their hands and unload on her, knowing she has no cancels to stop them.
​- Large maps and ranged kiting fighters (Robin Hood, Muldoon) force her to discard her best attacks just to boost movement.

### Key cards

​- **15,000 Pounds of Muscle**: a 3-value attack that ignores the opponent's defense value. Great for punching through heavy blocks, and you end up ahead on the health trade even after taking the 2 self-damage.
​- **Terrifying Roar**: your best tool against hit-and-run tactics. Stops them from leaving the space, guaranteeing they stay in range.
​- **Commanding Impact**: a 5-value attack that draws a card. Her best punish against an opponent throwing a Feint, since it doesn't rely on a combat effect for its damage.
​- **You're Just Making Her Angry**: a 1-value defense that returns to your hand if you win the combat by boosting it. The sources disagree on this card: one player praises it for providing infinite defense, while another argues it wastes the high boost values of your best attacks just to save a block.
​- **65 Million Years of Gut Instinct**: a scheme that returns any card from your discard pile except itself. Grab Ripples in the Water for another teleport, or Terrifying Roar to freeze them again.

### Tips

​- Never double maneuver. You already draw a card every turn, so taking two draw actions burns through your deck far too quickly.
​- You occupy two spaces and can be attacked from either. On maps with adjoining zones, you can sometimes occupy three zones at once, which makes you vulnerable to ranged attacks from multiple angles.
​- Kite melee fighters. Maneuver one space away and attack from range. If they move in to hit back on their turn, you get to double attack on your next turn.
​- Don't bother tracking your opponent's Feints. You don't have any of your own, and your best attacks either ignore defense entirely or have no combat effects to cancel.
