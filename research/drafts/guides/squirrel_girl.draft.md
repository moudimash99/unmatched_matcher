## Squirrel Girl

**In short:** A setup-heavy melee fighter who uses a swarm of small sidekicks to bypass blockers and trigger powerful conditional effects. She starts weak, but becomes dangerous once she surrounds the opponent with squirrels.

### Plan A: Build and spread the swarm

**Goal:** Flood the board with sidekicks to control zones and access enemy targets.
**How to do it:** Her special ability adds one squirrel a turn, which is too slow to build a threat. Dig for your summoning cards early. Get 'Em Tippy-Toe! (3 copies) summons two squirrels in any space on the board, and Eat Nuts heals her and summons one nearby. Squirrels follow the rules for small fighters: they can share spaces with other fighters, and both you and the opponent can move through them. This lets your sidekicks bypass enemy sidekick walls, such as Elektra's Hand or Wukong's clones, and walk straight to the enemy hero. Spread them across the board to body block routes and, on Marvel maps, share spaces with item tokens to steal them from camping opponents. Keep four squirrels in the enemy's zone to turn Bite of Steel into a 5-value attack.
**Watch out for:** If a squirrel takes damage, every squirrel in that same space is defeated instantly. The sources disagree on how to position them: most advise keeping them in separate spaces to avoid losing several to a single attack, while one points out you must stack them occasionally to get the maximum value out of Kick Butts.

### Plan B: Surround them for conditional effects

**Goal:** Catch the opponent adjacent to four or more squirrels to trigger your strongest cards.
**How to do it:** Her best effects only activate when the enemy is surrounded. If four or more squirrels are adjacent to the target, Unbeatable Squirrel Girl cancels their card and ignores its value completely. Kick Butts gains +1 value for each adjacent squirrel, reaching up to a 9. Horde of Squirrels deals 2 automatic damage, and Nutwork of Spies forces them to discard two cards instead of one. To set this up without wasting actions on movement, defend with Get 'Em Tippy-Toe! to drop two squirrels next to the attacker on their turn. On your turn, use Fuzzball Special first: it moves every squirrel up to 3 spaces, shifting your whole swarm into position for a second action.
**Watch out for:** Kick Butts and Bite of Steel have no built-in cancels and fail against a Feint. Play Nutwork of Spies first to strip the opponent's defensive cancels before committing your biggest attacks.

### Plan C: Outmaneuver with sidekicks

**Goal:** Use the scattered swarm to cross the board quickly.
**How to do it:** Squirrel Girl has a base movement of 2, and all of her cards have a boost value of 1 or 2. This caps her standard boosted movement at 4, making it hard to catch ranged fighters who kite her. Squirgility (3 copies, value 3) solves this: after combat, she may move up to 3 spaces, move through sidekicks, and ignore any spaces containing them. If your squirrels are spread out between you and the opponent, she can leapfrog over them to cover 10 or more spaces in a single action. Squirmish also helps close the distance by placing her in any space in her zone after combat.
**Watch out for:** This mobility depends entirely on keeping your squirrels alive and positioned well. If the opponent clears the board, you are forced to walk slowly.

### How they lose

​- Area-of-effect damage clears her squirrels instantly. Fighters who deal damage to adjacent spaces (Buffy) or multiple sidekicks wipe out her board presence.
​- She starts the game with no sidekicks and low health. Aggressive fighters can rush her down before her swarm is ready.
​- She is highly vulnerable to exhaustion. She has weak card draw, and kiting opponents force her to discard cards for low-value boosts just to chase them.
​- Her squirrels only have one defense card (Call of the Mild) and cannot play Feint. Opponents easily farm them to trigger "after combat" effects safely.
​- Her printed defenses top out at 3 (except for squirmish and conditional effects), so heavy hitters deal damage through her blocks consistently.

### Key cards

​- **Get 'Em Tippy-Toe!**: a 3-value versatile that summons two squirrels anywhere. Your best tool to rebuild the swarm or trap an enemy on their turn.
​- **Squirgility**: a 3-value defense that moves her through sidekicks without counting those spaces. With a spread-out swarm, she can cross the entire board.
​- **Unbeatable Squirrel Girl**: a 4-value versatile. If four squirrels are adjacent to the enemy, it blanks their card and value. Use it as an Ultimate defense, or as an attack for 4 damage they cannot prevent.
​- **Nutwork of Spies**: a scheme that takes a card from their hand, or two if they are adjacent to four squirrels. Use it to clear their faints before attacking.
​- **Kick Butts**: a 1-value attack that scales with adjacent squirrels. Her highest damage potential if you can get enough sidekicks close.

### Tips

​- Attack with Bite of Steel rather than saving it to block. It forces the opponent to spend a good defense on a 5-value attack or take heavy damage from a sidekick.
​- Fuzzball Special moves each squirrel individually. Use it to reshape the board, surround an enemy, and block narrow paths all at once.
​- Keep your Feints for defense. You only have two copies, and your sidekicks cannot play them.
​- Play Horde of Squirrels only when you have the four squirrels in position. Two automatic damage is crucial when your health is only 13.
​- If you have to block an attack aimed at a squirrel, use Call of the Mild. If the 4-value block wins the combat, you draw two cards.
