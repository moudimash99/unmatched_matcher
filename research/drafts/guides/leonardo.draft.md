## Leonardo

**In short:** A tactical melee fighter who controls the board by moving anyone for free. He dictates where fights happen to deal unblockable damage, sets up big attacks with his sidekick Splinter, and spends cards from his hand to ignore massive enemy hits.

### Plan A: Control the map and zones

**Goal:** Force the opponent into bad positions to trigger auto-damage and take map advantages.
**How to do it:** His Team Tactics ability moves any fighter one space at the start of your turn. You can move Leonardo, Splinter, the enemy hero, or an enemy sidekick. Use this to pull an opponent off a map item, high ground, or a secret passage, then walk in and take the space yourself. Position Leonardo and Splinter on spaces that touch multiple colored zones, while pushing the enemy into spaces that belong to fewer zones. This activates your auto-damage versatiles: Wise beyond his years (3 copies, value 2) deals 2 damage, and Spatial awareness (Splinter only, 3 copies, value 3) deals 1 damage and draws a card.
**Watch out for:** Moving fighters without a clear purpose. Always plan your free movement to set up an attack, secure a zone advantage, or ruin the opponent's formation.

### Plan B: Mind games with heavy hits

**Goal:** Bait out defensive cancels so your biggest attacks land cleanly.
**How to do it:** You have three heavy hitters. Katana (2 copies) is a blank 6-value attack. Turtle power! (2 copies, value 3) lets you boost its value during combat. Fearless leader (3 copies, value 3) gains +2 when your other fighter is adjacent to the defender, capping at a 5-value attack in a standard game. Opponents expect Fearless leader when you maneuver both Leonardo and Splinter next to them. Use that predictable setup, but attack with Katana instead to punish the Feint they play. When they stop trying to cancel your setups, hit them with Turtle power! boosted by Leonardo leads. Leonardo leads is a scheme with a boost of 4, making Turtle power! a 7-value hit.
**Watch out for:** Cancels on Turtle power!. If the opponent cancels its effect, you cannot boost it, and it remains a simple 3. Watch their discard pile to track their remaining cancels before you commit.

### Plan C: Spend cards to break the rules

**Goal:** Discard from your hand to blank huge attacks and manipulate the board.
**How to do it:**

- I have a plan (3 copies, value 2) lets you discard a card to cancel their effects and completely ignore their value. Because you see their attack before you choose to discard, this blanks massive attacks while dodging the damage completely. Played on offense, it acts as an unblockable 2-damage finisher if it isn't canceled, because ignoring their defense value means they take the printed 2.
- For Sensei (2 copies, value 4) moves both fighters up to 1 space, and lets you discard a card to gain an action. Use the movement to separate the fighters safely, preventing a counter-attack.
- Eat, sleep, and breath ninjutsu (3 copies, value 3) places every adjacent fighter in another space in their zone. Use it to trap an enemy between Leo and Splinter on a single path, break up enemy sidekick walls, or even teleport Splinter across a large zone to reach a hiding opponent.

**Watch out for:** Running out of cards. You must discard to boost, to cancel, and for extra actions. Do not use For Sensei's action gain if you are low on cards or defenses.

### How they lose

- He needs to discard often to fuel his best effects. Opponents with discard effects drain his hand even faster, leaving him without defenses.
- He tries to play a fatigue game. Sources disagree on his playstyle: some consider him a pure defensive value fighter, but another argues that Quick strike, his schemes, and Spatial awareness draw too many cards for him to successfully outlast a patient opponent.
- Splinter dies early. This costs Leonardo the +2 bonus for Fearless leader, the auto-damage from Spatial awareness, and access to his healing scheme.
- Area damage punishes him for keeping Splinter adjacent to the enemy for his attack setups.

### Key cards

- **I have a plan**: Your only cancel, but it ignores the attack's value entirely. Essential for surviving big hits.
- **Heroes in a half shell**: A plain 5-value defense. Great for blocking strong attacks when you do not want to discard a card for I have a plan.
- **Eat, sleep, and breath ninjutsu**: A versatile that shifts adjacent fighters anywhere in their zone. Excellent for trapping enemies, escaping, or breaking up formations.
- **Leonardo leads**: A scheme that moves friendly fighters 3 spaces. In a standard game with only Splinter, it is weak as an action. Keep it primarily as a 4-value boost card for Turtle power! or a large maneuver.
- **Protective father**: A Splinter scheme that swaps his place with a friendly fighter and heals them both for 2. Use it to save Leonardo when he is cornered and gain an action in the process.

### Tips

- Do not blindly discard high-boost cards for movement. Save Leonardo leads and cards belonging to a defeated Splinter specifically to boost Turtle power!.
- If you play I have a plan against a weak bait attack like Regroup, simply choose not to discard. You keep your hand and still get a 2-value block.
- On maps with special item tokens or secret passages, use your start-of-turn ability to pull the opponent off those spaces before you take your actions.
- When an opponent chases Leonardo, run Splinter in the opposite direction. You can then play Protective father to swap them, bringing Leonardo to safety while healing them both.
