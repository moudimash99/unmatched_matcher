## Buffy

**In short:** A highly mobile melee fighter who easily escapes pins because she can move through enemies. Before the match begins, she chooses between two sidekicks: Giles for hand disruption and card draw, or Xander for heavy attacks.

### Plan A: Pick the right sidekick

**Goal:** Tailor your deck to exploit the opponent's weaknesses.
**How to do it:** You choose your sidekick after the opponent reveals their fighter. Your choice replaces five cards in your deck.

- **Giles** brings the only scheme cards to Buffy's deck. Insight (3 copies) lets you look at the opponent's hand and force them to discard a card of your choice. This strips their best defense or cancel before you attack. Training (2 copies) draws 3 cards, which fixes Buffy's lack of natural card draw.
- **Xander** brings aggression. Military Knowledge (3 copies) is a 4-value attack that replaces itself by drawing a card. Right-hand Man (2 copies) is a 2-value versatile that hits for 6 when Xander is adjacent to Buffy.

**Watch out for:** Each choice leaves a gap. With Xander, Buffy struggles to maintain a large hand size. With Giles, she lacks heavy attacks and you must keep him alive until his five schemes are safely played.

### Plan B: Deal damage after combat

**Goal:** Punish attackers and chip away health without relying on high attack values.
**How to do it:** Buffy uses defensive and versatile cards to deal unblockable damage while repositioning. Cartwheel Kick (2 copies) is a 2-value defense that lets her move up to 3 spaces after combat, then deals 1 damage to each adjacent opposing fighter. Stay within three spaces of the opponent to bait an attack, play this to mitigate the hit, then slide away and deal a damage.

Slayer's Strength (3 copies) is a 4-value versatile that lets you move all fighters adjacent to Buffy to another space in their zone, then deals 1 damage to each fighter moved. Played defensively, this ruins a melee opponent's positioning and dishes out unexpected damage.
**Watch out for:** Fighters with many cancels (Bigfoot, Black Widow) will hold them for these effects. If your defense is canceled, Buffy is stuck taking a massive hit against a weak 2-value block with no movement or damage payoff.

### Plan C: Safe, heavy attacks

**Goal:** Land big hits that opponents cannot cancel.
**How to do it:** Opponents will want to save their cancels for Buffy's movement and damage effects. Punish this with Mr. Pointy (2 copies), a printed 5-value attack with no text. It cannot be canceled, meaning a defensive cancel played against it is entirely wasted.

This attack serves as the perfect follow-up to the defensive maneuver from Plan B. Once you have baited an attack and moved away with Cartwheel Kick, use your first action to step back in and strike with Mr. Pointy. If you have an action left, finish your turn with Slayer's Strength to deal another point of unblockable damage and push the opponent away again.
**Watch out for:** Buffy thrives when she controls the distance. If you launch a heavy attack without a way to maneuver away afterward, you risk ending your turn next to the enemy and inviting a devastating counterattack.

### How they lose

- Her health is low (14), making her vulnerable to large printed attacks if her maneuvers fail.
- Opponents with plenty of cancels shut down her crucial after-combat damage and movement effects.
- If she drafts Xander, she lacks natural card draw and can lose the war of attrition.
- She relies heavily on Giles for draw and disruption; if the opponent hunts him down early, her game plan falls apart.

### Key cards

- **Insight**: A Giles scheme. Her best tool for looking at the opponent's hand, breaking enemy combos, and stripping away cancels.
- **Slayer's Strength**: Excellent for dealing defensive damage and disrupting a melee opponent's positioning.
- **Mr. Pointy**: A clean, heavy hit that wastes enemy cancels.
- **Right-hand Man**: A Xander versatile that hits for a massive 6 when he fights beside Buffy. Usually best played on offense, as savvy opponents will rarely attack a target that can defend with a 6.
- **Cartwheel Kick**: A defense that moves Buffy out of danger while dealing unblockable damage to adjacent opponents.

### Tips

- Buffy's special ability allows her to pass through opposing fighters. She easily escapes heroes who rely on body-blocking or trapping to win, such as the Raptors, Dracula, or Spike.
- There is no map where she cannot maneuver. Use her base movement of 3 and her special ability to ensure you never end your turn backed into a corner.
- When playing Xander, use him offensively. Right-hand Man requires adjacency to Buffy, so move them as a pair.
- When playing Giles, keep him completely out of the fight until he has safely played his schemes.
- Position carefully when defending with Cartwheel Kick to catch multiple enemy sidekicks in the blast radius and eliminate them simultaneously.
