## Titania

**In short:** A tricky ranged fighter with a separate deck of six passive abilities called glamours. She cycles through them to power up her attacks, chain free schemes together, and set up an invincible exhaustion game, but she must protect her incredibly low starting health.

### Plan A: Cycle the glamours

**Goal:** Find the right passive ability for the moment and build up your discard pile to strengthen your cards.
**How to do it:** You start the game with no active glamour. At the start of your turn, you flip the top card of your glamour deck face-up, and its effect stays active until discarded. Several of your versatile and defense cards let you discard your active glamour to trigger stronger effects. For example, Fairy Song jumps from a 3-value to a 5, and Protection Of The Fairy Woods becomes a cancel. Because Titania's Queen Of The Fairies attack gains +1 for every glamour in your discard pile, you are heavily incentivized to cycle through them. If you draw a great glamour at the wrong time, you can use The Moon Looks Down to put it safely on the bottom of your deck to use later.
**Watch out for:** If you discard your active glamour on your turn to trigger a card effect, you will not reveal a new one until the start of your next turn. During the opponent's turn, you will have no passive ability and cannot pay the discard cost to cancel effects with Protection Of The Fairy Woods. Experienced opponents will look for this window to hit you with their best attacks.

### Plan B: The scheme combo turn

**Goal:** Chain free actions together for massive damage and disruption.
**How to do it:** Glamour of Rhyme gives you an action back whenever you play a scheme. Start with Gift Of The Fair Folk to draw cards and move your fighters into position. Follow up with What Fools These Mortals Be to look at the opponent's hand and shuffle away their best defense. Finally, drop A Momentary Glance to deal 2 damage. Because these schemes refunded your actions, you can now attack twice with Met By Moonlight or a charged Queen Of The Fairies against a defenseless opponent.
**Watch out for:** A Momentary Glance deals 2 damage to *any* fighter in Titania's zone. If the enemy maneuvers away and leaves Titania alone, you will have to deal the damage to yourself (or Oberon) unless you are protected by Glamour of Sleep. Also, do not hold Glamour of Rhyme too long waiting for the perfect combo; play the schemes you have and discard the glamour, as it offers no defensive benefits while face-up.

### Plan C: Sleep out the clock

**Goal:** Survive into exhaustion where you take no damage.
**How to do it:** Glamour of Sleep prevents your fighters from taking any damage other than combat damage. This completely ignores effect damage, and it nullifies fatigue damage when your deck is empty. If you reach the bottom of your deck with Sleep active, you can maneuver freely while the opponent takes exhaustion damage. It also breaks the symmetry of Parting Gift: you deal 2 damage to every fighter in the combat, but take zero yourself. Use The Moon Looks Down to place Sleep at the very bottom of your glamour deck if you reveal it early in the match.
**Watch out for:** Opponents know this trick and will try to feint The Moon Looks Down to leave Sleep stranded in your discard pile. If you reach fatigue, they will play attacks with devastating effects to force you into a terrible choice: take the hit, or voluntarily discard your active glamour to cancel it with Protection Of The Fairy Woods, stripping away your immunity.

### How they lose

- Her 12 health is extremely low for a solo hero. Aggressive melee fighters (like Bigfoot) can rush her down before she sets up her defenses.
- Although her deck size is standard, But A Dream returns the opponent's card to their hand instead of the discard pile. Opponent discard effects (like those from Medusa) drain her small hand quickly, leaving her vulnerable.
- Oberon dies early. He holds her three powerful 5-value attacks (Met By Moonlight) and serves as a crucial body block, severely limiting your damage output if he falls prematurely.
- Opponents will save their cancels specifically for her big Queen Of The Fairies attacks and The Moon Looks Down.

### Key cards

- **But A Dream**: A 0-value defense that cancels all effects, ignores the printed value, and returns the attack to the opponent's hand. You draw a card. While they keep their card, you waste their entire action, which ruins fighters who rely on chained combo turns (like Bloody Mary or Tesla).
- **Queen Of The Fairies**: A 2-value attack that scales with discarded glamours. A massive late-game threat that can reach up to 8 value.
- **Met By Moonlight**: A 5-value attack for Oberon that places him adjacent to Titania after combat. Send him in to hit hard, then automatically pull him back to safety so the opponent has to waste actions to reach him again.
- **The Moon Looks Down**: A 3-value versatile that returns a discarded glamour to the bottom of the deck. Essential for saving Glamour of Sleep for the end game, or getting a second use out of a strong passive.
- **Protection Of The Fairy Woods**: A 2-value defense. This cancel requires you to discard your active glamour to trigger, making it costly to use at the wrong time.

### Tips

- Glamour of Jealousy lets Titania and Oberon swap spaces when one is attacked, *before* cards are played. This creates a guessing game: the opponent might poke with a weak attack to bait the swap, or swing hard expecting you to put Titania in harm's way.
- Glamour of Love lets you move an opposing fighter 1 space when you maneuver. Use it to pull hiding enemies into your zone, or push body-blocking sidekicks out of the way so you don't have to boost to reach your target.
- Glamour of Greed allows you to draw a card at the end of your turn, making it incredibly useful early in the game to maintain hand advantage and pressure.
- Glamour of Invisibility lets you move through opposing fighters. Use it on maps with secret passages (like Baskerville Manor) to move through occupied spaces, or to easily escape from being pinned by multiple sidekicks.
- Do not wait too long to play Queen Of The Fairies. Rather than holding it for maximum value and clogging your hand, playing it as a 5 or 6 mid-game is often enough to secure a solid advantage.
- If Oberon is at 1 health and about to die, attack with Parting Gift. It will kill him, but guarantees 2 damage to the opponent regardless of their defense.
- Don't be afraid to discard a highly situational passive just to cycle closer to the ones you actually need.
