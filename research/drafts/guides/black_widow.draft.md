## Black Widow

**In short:** A tricky double-ranged team who dictate the flow of the game through unique "mission" schemes. She drains the opponent's hand, deals unavoidable damage across the board, and recycles her best tricks with Maria Hill.

### Plan A: Drain their hand and outlast them

**Goal:** Leave the opponent with few cards to defend with, or win the game through exhaustion.
**How to do it:** The Kinshasa Directive (1 copy, value None) forces the opponent to discard 2 cards, as long as Black Widow is in your starting space. Because you start the game there, this is the perfect opening move to cripple their hand immediately. Follow up with Widow's Bite (3 copies), a 4-value attack that discards another card if you win the combat. Use Maria Hill's Acting Director of S.H.I.E.L.D. to recycle The Kinshasa Directive from your discard pile. Repeated early, this leaves the opponent with no defenses for your attacks. If an aggressive fighter empties your hand instead, play The Budapest Gambit to draw 5 cards and force a random discard, instantly flipping the hand advantage.
**Watch out for:** Every time you complete a mission, you reveal cards from your deck until you find a new one. This thins your deck quickly. If you plan to win by exhaustion, recycle The Kinshasa Directive instead of The Budapest Gambit to avoid drawing yourself to death.

### Plan B: Auto-damage through positioning

**Goal:** Bypass their blocks by completing damage-dealing missions.
**How to do it:** Against fighters with 1-health sidekicks (like Robin Hood or Squirrel Girl), your scheme damage is devastating. The Madripoor Sanction deals 2 damage to all of the opponent's fighters anywhere on the map, but Widow must be standing in their starting space to play it. The Firenze Agenda deals 2 damage to every enemy in Widow's zone, but she must be adjacent to their hero. To get her into these dangerous spots safely, attack with Widow's Sting (5 copies) and use its after-combat effect to move her 3 spaces, or play Double Identity to suddenly swap Widow onto the space Maria was holding.
**Watch out for:** Smart opponents will leave a sidekick on their starting space to prevent you from playing The Madripoor Sanction. Hard zone borders protect enemies from The Firenze Agenda, because it only hits fighters in her current zone.

### Plan C: Protect Maria Hill

**Goal:** Keep Maria alive to get maximum uses out of Acting Director of S.H.I.E.L.D.
**How to do it:** Maria is not a typical sidekick you throw away to absorb damage. Her Acting Director of S.H.I.E.L.D. is your only way to recycle missions. Block for her with Double Identity, which lets you swap Widow and Maria after you see the opponent's attack, changing the defender. If Maria is caught, defend with Life Model Decoy. It is a blank defense, but if Maria is defeated, it places her next to Widow and sets her health to 3.
**Watch out for:** Life Model Decoy is an after-combat effect. If Maria's health is low enough that the opponent's raw attack value will kill her, an offensive Feint cancels the resurrection and she stays dead.

### How they lose

​- Finding new missions forces you to reveal cards from your deck. Observant opponents will track this and know exactly when you are holding no Feints or defenses.
​- Black Widow has only 13 health and no healing. Fighters with unpreventable damage (Ghost Rider) chip her down quickly.
​- Her printed defenses are mostly 3s, and her only 4-value block is Widow's Kiss. Fast, aggressive fighters (T-Rex) overwhelm her before she can set up her missions.
​- She lacks massive printed attacks. Her highest value is the 5 on Widow's Sting, so she struggles to burst down high-health heroes if her missions are interrupted.

### Key cards

​- **Acting Director of S.H.I.E.L.D.**: Maria's 4-value versatile. It moves both of your fighters 3 spaces through enemies and shuffles a mission back into your deck. The most important card you have.
​- **The Budapest Gambit**: your only reliable card draw. A massive swing in momentum, but it pushes you much closer to exhaustion.
​- **Fake Out**: a 1-value attack. If you lose the combat, you draw a card and gain an action. It is the perfect bait to waste an opponent's high-value block before you follow up with a real attack.
​- **Caught in a Web**: a 3-value versatile that cancels *only* after-combat effects. Save it to deny your opponent's card draw, movement, or auto-damage, and use your standard Feints for during-combat effects.
​- **Widow's Kiss**: a 4-value versatile. Her highest block, which stops the opposing fighter from moving for the rest of the turn.

### Tips

​- You always start the game with The Moscow Protocol in hand. Attack on your first action, then play this scheme to draw a card, gain an action, and get a better mission.
​- If an opponent camps their starting space to deny you The Madripoor Sanction, attack them and defend with Widow's Line to move them 2 spaces away.
​- If you have no missions left in your deck, you do not search for one when a card tells you to. Simply tell your opponent your deck is empty of missions so you don't reveal your remaining cards.
​- Play Widow's Kiss on defense. Pinning the opponent in your zone gives you a perfect double-attack opportunity on your next turn.
