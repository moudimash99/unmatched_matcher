## Raptors

**In short:** Three fast, aggressive melee fighters who each have 7 health. They use their high movement to surround enemies, boost their attacks, and chain extra actions, but struggle with low defense and zero card draw.

### Plan A: Pin and surround

**Goal:** Trap the opponent and maximize your passive ability.
**How to do it:** Their ability adds 1 to an attack's value for each of your *other* Raptors adjacent to the defender, up to a maximum of 2 extra damage. To guarantee this, surround the opponent so they cannot physically move out of their space. Working Things Out (2 copies) is an ideal setup scheme: it moves all your fighters up to 3 spaces—even through opposing fighters—and grants an action. Once the enemy is pinned, unleash Pack Hunters (2 copies). This 4-value attack deals 1 damage for every Raptor adjacent to the defender if you win combat, hitting for up to 9 damage with the full passive boost.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who can move through fighters, like Bigfoot or Buffy, easily slip out of your pins. Defensive cards that allow post-combat movement will break the surround and cancel the bonus damage on Pack Hunters if you have not blocked all escape routes.

### Plan B: Chain extra actions

**Goal:** Bleed the opponent of defense cards and hit them when they are defenseless.
**How to do it:** The deck features seven attacks that grant an extra action: They Remember (4 copies, 2-value) and Clever Girl (3 copies, 3-value). Lead with They Remember to force the opponent to spend a block, then spend the gained action to strike again. By stringing multiple attacks together, you punish fighters with small hand sizes. When you suspect they have run out of blocks, drop Eviscerate (2 copies), a 5-value attack that reaches a massive 7 with a full surround. If their hand is low, use Ambush (3 copies, 2-value) to force a random discard and add its boost number to your attack value.
**Watch out for:** Feint cancels the action gain on your attacks, ending your turn immediately. Test the waters by attacking with a solo Raptor first using attacks without crucial effects, like Disengage, saving your action-gainers for when you are confident the opponent lacks cancels.

### Plan C: Hide and rotate

**Goal:** Keep all three Raptors alive to maintain your damage output.
**How to do it:** Opponents will often try to focus their damage on a single Raptor to pick her off early. Losing just one fighter permanently lowers your maximum passive bonus and makes trapping enemies much harder. If a Raptor takes heavy damage, retreat her to the edge of a zone where she cannot be easily targeted. Disengage (2 copies, 4-value) is perfect for this, letting the attacker instantly place herself in an empty space in her zone. Once an injured Raptor is safely out of the fray, use your healthy fighters to double maneuver, rebuilding your hand before setting up the next ambush.
**Watch out for:** The deck has no card draw effects whatsoever. Double maneuvering is the only way to refill your hand, but this downtime allows the opponent to draw cards and recover. Also, be extremely wary of opponent cards that target or damage "a hero," as these effects will hit all three Raptors simultaneously.

### How they lose

- The opponent focuses on one Raptor. Once one falls, they lose the bodies needed to surround targets and maximize their ability.
- They run out of defenses. With only 10 cards that can block, they are highly vulnerable to sustained aggression.
- They waste actions chasing. Foes with strong defensive movement can easily kite the pack if they fail to lock down a tight pin.
- Cards that deal damage to all fighters in a zone heavily punish the Raptors for clumping together.

### Key cards

- **Pack Hunters**: The highest potential damage dealer. Only play it when you have the enemy firmly trapped with multiple Raptors.
- **Working Things Out**: Acts as a free maneuver with extended reach, letting you set up a surround without wasting your first action of the turn.
- **Decoy**: A 3-value defense that instantly places another undefeated Raptor adjacent to the attacker. It disrupts ranged opponents and immediately sets up a surround for your next turn.
- **Clever Girl**: Grants an action if any of your Raptors remain adjacent to the opponent after combat. A staple for extending your attack chains.
- **Eviscerate**: A plain attack with high base damage. Use it to bait out cancels, or save it for a devastating hit when the opponent is fully surrounded.

### Tips

- Save Eaten Alive (3 copies) strictly for defense. It is your only 4-value block, and you need it to survive major hits.
- Against fighters with 1-health sidekicks, attack the minions with They Remember. You clear their board presence and still receive an action to hit the hero or retreat.
- Against opponents who lack cancels, like T. Rex, hoard your action-gain cards until you have several in hand, then unleash an unstoppable combo turn.
- Use Decoy against ranged attackers to immediately close the distance on their turn, allowing you to go on the offensive when your turn begins.
- Coordinated Attack Pattern (2 copies) is best used as a surprise trap. Leave one Raptor exposed, and when the opponent moves in to attack, play this scheme to instantly pull the rest of the pack into the zone.
