## Krang

**In short:** A chaotic melee fighter whose mobility and power depend on his Doomsday machines. He rolls a unique die for massive effects, and deactivates machines to reroll when luck turns against him.

### Plan A: Keep the machines running

**Goal:** Maintain high movement and a safety net of rerolls.
**How to do it:** Krang starts with a printed move of 1, but he adds 1 to his move for each active Doomsday machine. With one active at the start, he moves 2. Cards like Molecular Amplification Unit and Welcome to the Technodrome! turn more machines on. An active machine is also your reroll token: if you roll the Die of Ultimate Destruction and get a bad number, deactivate a machine to roll again. The die has faces from 1 to 5, plus an X that counts as a 0. You can reroll multiple times on a single effect if you have enough active machines to spend.
**Watch out for:** Running out of machines. Without them, you cannot reroll bad results, and his move drops back to 1. A move of 1 makes it very easy for opponents to pin him.

### Plan B: Push your luck in combat

**Goal:** Turn low-value cards into massive hits after seeing their defense.
**How to do it:** Both Molecular Amplification Unit and Android arms: Missiles add the die roll to their value during combat. This timing means the opponent plays their card first, so you see exactly what number you need to beat. If they play a weak block, roll to crush it. If you roll an X or a 1, deactivate a machine and try again.
**Watch out for:** Cancels. If the opponent plays a card that cancels effects, it stops the "during combat" step entirely. You do not get to roll. Also, do not waste machines rerolling if they played a massive defense that your highest roll cannot beat anyway.

### Plan C: Disrupt their hand and deck

**Goal:** Drain their resources with strange effects.
**How to do it:** Krang wins card comparisons thanks to his long names. Warlord of Dimension X reveals the top cards of both decks and lets you draw yours if the name is longer. Because many of his cards start with "Android arms:", he usually wins. Android arms: Chain Flail forces them to discard two cards if their defense value is lower than your die roll. Pan-dimensional Portal places random cards from both players' hands on the bottom of their decks.
**Watch out for:** The chaos of Pan-dimensional Portal hurts you too. It pulls cards from your hand completely at random.

### How they lose

​- He burns all his machines on rerolls and gets pinned with a move of 1.
​- Feints shut down his "during combat" die rolls on his main attacks.
​- He rolls too high on IQ of 968's card draw and rushes himself to exhaustion.
​- He defends with Minimizer against an attack that sets its value "during combat." Minimizer immediately lowers their card by 4, but the attacker's effect then sets the value to a new high number (like Casey Jones's Slapshot setting to 6), ignoring the reduction.

### Key cards

​- **IQ of 968**: a scheme that activates all three machines. Then you roll the die separately to draw cards and recover health. You can reroll a low health result while keeping a low draw result to avoid exhaustion.
​- **Molecular Amplification Unit**: a 2-value versatile that activates a machine immediately, then rolls to increase its own value.
​- **Welcome to the Technodrome!**: a 2-value versatile that acts as his Feint. If the opponent plays a card with a weak effect, you can choose to activate a machine instead of canceling it.
​- **Minimizer**: a 0-value defense that immediately decreases the opponent's value by 4. Against a standard attack, it acts like a 4-value block and ends the turn.
​- **Android arms: Missiles**: his only ranged attack. It must be played face up to use the ranged effect.

### Tips

​- Warlord of Dimension X counts every single letter, number, character, and space in the card's name.
​- Android arms: Wings moves Krang through opposing fighters up to the die roll, dealing 1 damage to each. It also grants an action, making it great for escaping or clearing 1-health sidekicks.
​- Android arms: Powerbomb can move the opposing fighter up to the damage dealt. Since it is a 3-value attack you can boost, you can push them far out of position.
