## Krang

**In short:** A chaotic brawler who relies on his Doomsday machines for movement and consistency. He rolls a unique die for massive card effects, shutting down his own machines to reroll when the odds are against him.

### Plan A: Keep the machines running

**Goal:** Maintain mobility and a pool of rerolls.
**How to do it:** Krang starts with a base move of 1, but gains an extra step for every active Doomsday machine. With one online at the start, his baseline movement is 2. Versatile cards like Molecular Amplification Unit and Welcome to the Technodrome! bring additional machines online. These active devices double as reroll tokens. If you get a terrible result on the Die of Ultimate Destruction—which features numbers 1 through 5 plus an X that counts as zero—you can deactivate a machine to roll again. You are even allowed to reroll a single effect multiple times in a row, provided you can pay the machine cost.
**Watch out for:** Exhausting your machines entirely. Without them, your movement plummets back to 1, leaving you incredibly vulnerable to being pinned, and you lose the ability to fix disastrous die rolls.

### Plan B: Push your luck in combat

**Goal:** Amplify modest attacks into crushing blows after checking the defender's response.
**How to do it:** Krang's Molecular Amplification Unit and Android arms: Missiles both add the die result to their strength "during combat." Because of this timing, you get to see exactly what defense the opponent played before rolling. If they commit a weak block, you can roll to overwhelm it. If the die lands on a 1 or an X, power down a machine to try for a better number.
**Watch out for:** Effect cancellation. If the defender plays a Feint, it skips the "during combat" step entirely, meaning you do not get to roll. Additionally, conserve your machines: do not reroll if the opponent played a defense so high that even a maximum roll of 5 wouldn't break through.

### Plan C: Disrupt their hand and deck

**Goal:** Bleed their resources through bizarre card abilities.
**How to do it:** Krang excels at winning card-reveal comparisons due to his incredibly lengthy titles. Warlord of Dimension X lets you draw the revealed card if its name is longer than the opponent's top card. The recurring "Android arms:" prefix on many of his cards ensures he wins these matchups frequently. Android arms: Chain Flail strips two cards from the opponent's hand if they don't defend at all, or if their block's printed value is lower than your die result. Pan-dimensional Portal creates chaos by shuffling a random number of cards from each player's hand to the bottom of their decks.
**Watch out for:** Pan-dimensional Portal's indiscriminate nature. The random hand drain affects you as well, potentially trashing your most valuable cards.

### How they lose

- He burns through all his active machines chasing rerolls and gets cornered with a move of 1.
- Opponents save their cancels for his main "during combat" dice-roll attacks.
- He accidentally rolls too high on the card draw for IQ of 968, accelerating his own fatigue.
- He defends with Minimizer against attacks that set their value "during combat." Minimizer drops the attack's value immediately, but a card like Casey Jones's Slapshot will then set its value to a flat 6, completely bypassing the reduction.

### Key cards

- **IQ of 968**: A scheme that turns on all three Doomsday machines at once. You then roll the die twice—once to draw cards and once to heal. Because these are separate steps, you can safely reroll a terrible heal result without losing a low card-draw result.
- **Molecular Amplification Unit**: A 2-value versatile card that instantly turns on a machine, then rolls to boost its own attack or defense value.
- **Welcome to the Technodrome!**: A versatile option that functions as his cancel. However, if the opponent's effect is harmless, you can opt to activate a machine instead of wiping their text.
- **Minimizer**: A 0-value block that instantly chops 4 off the incoming attack's value, then ends the turn. Against regular strikes, it essentially blocks 4 damage.
- **Android arms: Missiles**: His singular ranged option. You must reveal it face up to attack from afar, but doing so does not protect its "during combat" die roll from being canceled by a Feint.

### Tips

- Warlord of Dimension X measures every single character in the title, counting spaces, punctuation, and numbers alongside the letters.
- Android arms: Wings allows Krang to pass through enemy fighters up to the rolled distance, pinging each one for 1 damage. It refunds its action, making it excellent for repositioning or wiping out fragile sidekicks.
- Android arms: Powerbomb is a 3-value attack that lets you push the defender up to the amount of damage they take. Because it can be boosted, you can launch an opponent entirely out of the fight.
