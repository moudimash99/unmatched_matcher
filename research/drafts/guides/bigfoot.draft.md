## Bigfoot

**In short:** A highly mobile, hard-hitting melee fighter who draws cards by ending turns alone. He relies on his sidekick for unblockable damage and wears opponents down with high-value defenses and plenty of cancels.

### Plan A: Puppeteer the Jackalope

**Goal:** Use the Jackalope for aggressive damage while Bigfoot safely draws cards.
**How to do it:** Bigfoot's ability lets him draw a card at the end of his turn if there are no opposing fighters in his zone. Keep Bigfoot far away in an empty zone, and send the Jackalope to fight alone. Jackalope Horns (3 copies) moves the sidekick up to 5 spaces through enemies and deals 2 damage to an adjacent fighter. Play a Horns for guaranteed damage, then follow up with an attack like Momentous Shift. The Jackalope can use all of Bigfoot's versatile and defense cards, making it a surprisingly tough 6-health fighter that can harass the opponent relentlessly while your hand size grows.
**Watch out for:** Once the Jackalope dies, you lose its unblockable scheme damage and a valuable body on the board. Protect it until you play all three Horns; after that, it becomes expendable, and opponents will often stop attacking it anyway.

### Plan B: Hit and run

**Goal:** Strike with massive attacks, then leave the zone to trigger your card draw.
**How to do it:** Bigfoot boasts extreme mobility and huge attack values. Larger Than Life (3 copies) is a printed 6-value attack that punches through most blocks. Play your big attacks, then use your movement effects to escape, forcing the opponent to spend actions approaching you. Crash Through the Trees (2 copies) moves Bigfoot up to 5 spaces through opposing fighters, letting him slip out of danger or bypass sidekicks to ambush a hero. Disengage moves you to an empty space in your zone after combat. Use it on the Jackalope to pull it back after a strike, saving it for Bigfoot only when you have no other options.
**Watch out for:** Disengage keeps you in the same zone, so it rarely helps Bigfoot trigger his ability. Fast fighters or those with ranged attacks can chase you down and stay in your zone, turning off your extra card draw.

### Plan C: Bait and punish with raw value

**Goal:** Win the long game by trading high-value cards and canceling their best effects.
**How to do it:** Bigfoot has five cancel cards: three Feints and two copies of It's Just Your Imagination, a 3-value defense. This lets you freely cancel the opponent's tricks without having to carefully hoard your Feints. Because opponents are terrified of Larger Than Life, they will often throw their best blocks at your first attack. Exploit this by attacking with Regroup. If they waste a Feint or a high-value defense on your 1-value card, you draw a card and keep your heavy hitters for later.
**Watch out for:** Bigfoot relies entirely on his ability and Regroup for card draw. If an opponent corners him and turns off his ability, he can struggle to keep his hand full.

### How they lose

- Fighters with many 1-health sidekicks (Robin Hood, Medusa, Sun Wukong) use them to physically block Bigfoot's path to the hero.
- Constant aggressive pressure forces him to burn through his 11 strong defensive cards (his 4-values and cancels). Once those are gone, he has to take damage.
- He has no healing. Every hit he takes is permanent.
- Discard effects cripple him because nearly every card in his deck is valuable, and he has no weak cards to throw away safely.
- Fighters with cards that punish high-value attacks (Little Red, Beowulf) turn Larger Than Life against him.

### Key cards

- **Larger Than Life**: his signature 6-value attack. Save it for the enemy hero, not their sidekicks.
- **Jackalope Horns**: a scheme that moves the sidekick and deals 2 damage. The main reason to keep the Jackalope alive.
- **It's Just Your Imagination**: a 3-value defense that cancels effects. It functions as a better Feint on defense, freeing up your actual Feints for offense or more flexible blocking.
- **Savagery**: a 4-value attack that deals 1 damage to each adjacent fighter if you win. His best tool for clearing out clumps of 1-health sidekicks.
- **Hoax**: a 4-value versatile that moves you up to 5 spaces after combat. Best saved for defense to step away and avoid a second attack.

### Tips

- Be careful with Savagery: the 1 damage hits *each* adjacent fighter, including the Jackalope. Also, the effect checks for adjacency after combat, so if the defender moves away during the combat, they dodge the extra damage.
- Maneuver and discard Crash Through the Trees for its 3 boost to move 6 spaces and draw a card, rather than playing the scheme as an action to move 5 without drawing.
- Save Hoax and Skirmish for defense to preserve your high-value blocks.
- Remember to use his special ability at the end of every turn it applies. Sources note it is one of the most frequently forgotten abilities. It is optional, so you can decline the draw if your hand is full or your deck is low.
- Look for maps with sharp zone cut-offs (Baskerville Manor, Point Pleasant) rather than large open zones (Marmoreal), to make your card draw ability easier to trigger.
