## Raptors

**In short:** Three fast, aggressive melee fighters who share 21 health. They use their high movement to surround enemies, boost their attacks, and chain extra actions, but struggle with low defense and zero card draw.

### Plan A: Pin and surround

**Goal:** Trap the opponent and maximize your passive ability.
**How to do it:** Their ability adds 1 to their attack value for each of your *other* Raptors adjacent to the defender, for a maximum of 2 extra damage. To get this consistently, you must surround the opponent so they physically cannot move out of their space. Working Things Out (2 copies) is your best setup tool: it moves all your Raptors up to 3 spaces, ignoring opposing fighters, and gives you an action. Once the opponent is pinned, play Pack Hunters. If it wins combat, it deals 1 damage for every Raptor adjacent to the enemy, hitting for up to 9 total damage with the passive boost.
**Watch out for:** Opponents who can move through fighters (Bigfoot, Buffy) easily escape your pins. Defensive cards that allow movement (Leap Away, Skirmish) will break the surround and cancel Pack Hunters' bonus damage if you haven't blocked all their escape routes.

### Plan B: Chain extra actions

**Goal:** Bleed the opponent of defense cards and hit them when they are defenseless.
**How to do it:** The deck has seven cards that grant an extra action: They Remember (4 copies) and Clever Girl (3 copies). Play They Remember first. It is only a 2-value attack, but it forces the opponent to spend a block. Then use the extra action to attack again. By stringing multiple attacks together, you punish fighters who rely on small hand sizes. When you suspect they are out of defenses, play Eviscerate, a 5-value attack with no effect that becomes a 7 with a full surround. If they are low on cards, use Ambush to force a random discard and boost your attack with it.
**Watch out for:** Feint cancels the action gain on They Remember, ending your turn immediately. Play your action-gain cards carefully if the opponent still holds Feints, or test them by attacking with a lone Raptor first.

### Plan C: Hide and rotate

**Goal:** Keep all three Raptors alive to maintain your damage output.
**How to do it:** The opponent's best strategy is to focus all their attacks on one Raptor. Losing a single Raptor permanently drops your maximum passive bonus and makes pinning much harder. When a Raptor takes heavy damage, retreat her to the back or stand at the edge of zones where ranged attackers cannot safely target her. Disengage is a 4-value attack that lets the attacker instantly move to an empty space in her zone, pulling an injured Raptor out of danger after she strikes. Double maneuver with your healthy Raptors to build your hand back up, then set up a new attack.
**Watch out for:** You have zero card draw effects. Double maneuvering is your only way to refill your hand, which gives the opponent time to draw their own cards and recover.

### How they lose

- The opponent focuses all attacks on a single Raptor. Once one falls, their passive ability is halved and they lose the bodies needed to pin.
- They run out of defenses. With only 10 cards that can block (Decoy, Eaten Alive, Feint), fast opponents catch them empty-handed.
- They waste actions chasing. Opponents with strong defensive movement kite them easily if they fail to lock down a complete pin.
- Attacks that deal damage to multiple fighters in a zone punish them for standing together.

### Key cards

- **Pack Hunters**: Your highest potential damage card. Only play it when you have the enemy pinned with multiple Raptors.
- **Working Things Out**: A scheme that acts as a free maneuver with better reach, letting you set up a surround without wasting your first action.
- **Decoy**: A 3-value defense that immediately places another undefeated Raptor adjacent to the attacker. It disrupts ranged attackers and sets you up for a surround on your next turn.
- **Clever Girl**: A 3-value attack that grants an action if you keep adjacency after combat. A staple for your action chains.
- **Eviscerate**: A plain 5-value attack. Use it to bait out Feints, or save it for a massive 7-value hit when the opponent is fully surrounded.

### Tips

- Save Eaten Alive entirely for defense. It is your only 4-value block, and you need it to survive big hits.
- Against fighters with 1-health sidekicks (Muldoon's InGen Workers), attack the sidekicks with They Remember. You clear the board presence and still get your action to attack the hero or run away.
- Against T. Rex, who has no Feints, save your action-gain cards until you have several in hand, then unleash them all in one massive turn she cannot interrupt.
- Decoy's movement happens immediately, before cards are revealed. Use it to place a healthy Raptor in the way of a follow-up attack.
- Coordinated Attack Pattern requires the chosen Raptor to be in a good position already. Use it as a surprise trap if the opponent steps into a zone with a lone Raptor.
