## Spider-Man

**In short:** A highly mobile melee fighter who knows the printed value of every incoming attack before defending. He lacks cancels, but relies on perfect blocking, massive card draw, and constant repositioning to outmaneuver the opponent.

### Plan A: Block perfectly with Spider-Sense

**Goal:** Take minimal damage by matching your defenses exactly to their attacks.
**How to do it:** His special ability forces opponents to reveal the printed value of their attack card before you defend, removing most combat mind games. If they declare a 6 or 7 (like Dracula's Beastform or Arthur's Excalibur), play Web Shooters (3 copies, value 3) to restrict the damage you take to 2. If they declare a 4 or higher, play Counter-Attack (3 copies, value 3) to hit them back for 2 damage, or Spider-Sense Tingling! (3 copies, value 2) to draw two cards. When they throw a 2 or 3, you can safely block with Snark or Wall Crawler to secure a positional or hand advantage. Because you know what's coming, you can hold your optimal defenses in reserve instead of wasting them on weak bait attacks. This ability also completely shuts down the guessing game against fighters like Sun Wukong, letting you easily distinguish between his 7-value and 0-value cards.
**Watch out for:** The ability only reveals the printed value. Card effects can change this mid-combat. An opponent might declare a 2 or 3, but reveal a Momentous Shift or a Sinbad Voyage that ultimately hits much harder. Spider-Sense is also less informative against fighters like Black Panther, Bruce Lee, or Yennenga, who feature many different cards that share a printed value of 3, keeping you guessing about the actual effects.

### Plan B: Hit, run, and reposition

**Goal:** Control the distance and set up heavy attacks.
**How to do it:** Spider-Man has plenty of effects that let him drop into a space, strike, and vanish. Wall Crawler (2 copies, value 3) places him in any space in his zone. Thwip! (3 copies, value 4) does the same if you win the combat. Use these to close the gap against ranged opponents or to safely disengage. Moving this way perfectly sets up Momentous Shift (3 copies, value 3), which hits for 5 if you started the turn in a different space. You can also manipulate the enemy's position: Swinging Kick (3 copies, value 6) is an attack that pushes the opponent up to the damage dealt. If you land this on a 1-health sidekick, you calculate the knockback based on the full damage dealt, letting you throw them far across the board.
**Watch out for:** Card effects that place Spider-Man in any space require him to move to an empty space; you cannot choose to stay in his current spot.

### Plan C: Deny their card draw

**Goal:** Keep your hand full while starving the opponent's options.
**How to do it:** Right in the Face! (2 copies, value 4) immediately stops the opponent from drawing cards for the rest of the turn. Play it to block their first action, and any card draw from their second attack—or end-of-turn abilities like those of Bigfoot or the T-Rex—is completely shut down. It also prevents fighters like Bloody Mary from replenishing their hand after playing an evade. While they starve, you can stock up. Disarming Shot (2 copies, value 4) draws cards equal to the damage dealt. Hitting a 1-health sidekick (like Wong) with it nets you 4 cards, because over-damage counts.
**Watch out for:** Spider-Man has massive card draw but lacks effects that grant extra movement, and only has one card that gives an action. He cannot quickly spend a massive hand, so use that draw to fuel constant aggressive strikes and operate on a leaner hand size, rather than hoarding cards defensively.

### How they lose

- He has absolutely no cancels in his deck. Fighters with devastating combat effects (like Little Red Riding Hood) pose a massive threat since he cannot reliably stop their card text.
- Opponents who know his deck will try to bait Counter-Attack and Spider-Sense Tingling!. Since these only block for 3 and 2, misreading an opponent’s modified attack guarantees he takes a heavy hit.
- His printed defenses top out at 4, meaning heavy hitters will break through if Web Shooters isn't ready.

### Key cards

- **Web Shooters**: His best answer to massive attacks. It functions as a pseudo-cancel against raw damage.
- **Swinging Kick**: His biggest attack. It serves as heavy damage and a reliable way to push opponents away or into corners.
- **Right in the Face!**: A versatile card that serves as his best standard block, and a harsh counter to combo-heavy or card-hungry fighters.
- **Counter-Attack**: Punishes opponents for throwing 4s and 5s by dealing guaranteed damage back. Crucial against ranged fighters since the return damage doesn't require adjacency.
- **With Great Power**: A scheme that gives his attacks +1, draws a card, and grants an action. Best used as your first action when already adjacent, allowing two buffed attacks in a row.

### Tips

- Because you know the printed values of incoming attacks, you can afford to play aggressively and operate at a lower hand size, keeping only the exact defenses you need.
- If an opponent attacks with a large number, play Spider-Sense Tingling! only if you can afford to take the hit, as its block value is low.
- Watch the board zones closely. Thwip!, Wall Crawler, and Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man only let you teleport within your current zone.
- Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man recovers 3 health, which helps offset the minor damage you take when strategically blocking big attacks with Web Shooters or Counter-Attack.
- Disarming Shot combos perfectly with With Great Power to deal 5 damage and potentially draw 5 cards.
