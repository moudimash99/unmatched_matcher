## Leonardo

**In short:** A tactical melee fighter who controls the board by moving anyone for free. He dictates where fights happen to deal unblockable damage, sets up big attacks with his sidekick Splinter, and spends cards from his hand to ignore massive enemy hits.

### Plan A: Control the map and zones

**Goal:** Force the opponent into bad positions to trigger auto-damage and take map advantages.
**How to do it:** His Team Tactics ability lets you move any fighter up to one space at the start of your turn. You can shift Leonardo, Splinter, the enemy hero, or an enemy sidekick. Use this to pull an opponent off a map item, high ground, or a secret passage, then walk in and take the space yourself. Position your fighters on spaces that touch multiple colored zones, while pushing the enemy into spaces that belong to fewer zones. This setup activates your auto-damage versatiles: Wise beyond his years deals 2 damage, and the Splinter-exclusive Spatial awareness deals 1 damage while drawing a card.
**Watch out for:** Moving fighters without a clear purpose. Always plan your free movement to set up an attack, secure a zone advantage, or ruin the opponent's formation (such as denying them a one-directional attack bonus).

### Plan B: Mind games with heavy hits

**Goal:** Bait out defensive cancels so your biggest attacks land cleanly.
**How to do it:** You have three heavy hitters: Katana is a blank 6-value attack, Turtle power! is a 3-value attack that you can boost during combat, and Fearless leader gains +2 value when your other fighter is adjacent to the defender (capping at a 5-value attack in a standard 1v1 game). Opponents expect Fearless leader when you maneuver both Leonardo and Splinter next to them. Use that highly telegraphed setup, but attack with Katana instead to severely punish their defensive cancel. Once they stop trying to cancel your setups, hit them with Turtle power! boosted by your Leonardo leads scheme (which has a boost value of 4) to deliver a 7-value strike.
**Watch out for:** Faints and cancels on Turtle power!. If the opponent cancels its effect, you cannot boost it, leaving it at a mere 3-value. Track their used cancels before committing your most valuable boost cards.

### Plan C: Spend cards to break the rules

**Goal:** Discard from your hand to blank huge attacks and manipulate the board.
**How to do it:**

- I have a plan lets you immediately discard a card to cancel enemy card effects and completely ignore their value. Because you see the attack before choosing to discard, you can blank massive incoming hits without taking a scratch. Played on offense, it acts as an unblockable 2-damage finisher if uncancelled, since ignoring their defense value forces them to take the printed damage.
- For Sensei moves both fighters in the combat up to 1 space, and lets you discard a card to gain an action. Use the movement to safely separate the fighters, preventing a counter-attack.
- Eat, sleep, and breath ninjutsu places every adjacent fighter in another space in their zone. Use it to trap an enemy between Leo and Splinter on a single path, break up enemy sidekick walls, or teleport a friendly fighter across a large zone to reach a hiding opponent.

**Watch out for:** Running out of cards. You must discard to boost, to cancel, and for extra actions. The action gain from For Sensei can be a trap; do not use it if you are low on cards or defenses.

### How they lose

- He needs to discard often to fuel his best effects. Opponents with discard effects drain his hand even faster, leaving him vulnerable.
- He accidentally fatigues himself. His deck forces heavy card draw through Quick strike, Leonardo leads, and Spatial awareness, making it extremely difficult to outlast a patient opponent trying to win by exhaustion.
- Splinter dies early. This costs Leonardo the +2 bonus for Fearless leader, the auto-damage from Spatial awareness, and access to his healing scheme.

### Key cards

- **I have a plan**: Your only cancel. Though costly, ignoring the attack's value entirely is essential for surviving massive hits.
- **Heroes in a half shell**: A plain 5-value defense. Great for blocking strong attacks when you want to save your hand instead of discarding for I have a plan.
- **Eat, sleep, and breath ninjutsu**: A versatile card that shifts adjacent fighters anywhere in their zone. Excellent for trapping enemies, escaping, or breaking up sidekick formations.
- **Leonardo leads**: A scheme that moves friendly fighters 3 spaces. In a standard 1v1 game, it is inefficient as an action. Keep it primarily for its 4-value boost to power up Turtle power! or a large maneuver.
- **Protective father**: A Splinter scheme that swaps his place with a friendly fighter and heals them both for 2, while gaining an action. Use it to rescue a cornered Leonardo.

### Tips

- Do not blindly discard high-boost cards for movement. Save Leonardo leads and any leftover cards from a defeated Splinter specifically to boost Turtle power!.
- If you play I have a plan against a weak bait attack like Regroup, simply choose not to discard. You keep your hand and still get a 2-value block.
- On maps with special item tokens (like Marvel sets) or one-directional attack bonuses (like Hanging Gardens), use your start-of-turn ability to constantly pull the opponent off those advantageous spaces.
- When an opponent chases Leonardo, run Splinter in the opposite direction. You can then play Protective father to swap them, bringing Leonardo to safety while healing both fighters.
