## Philippa Eilhart

**In short:** A ranged fighter who leverages a unique card draw ability to sustain relentless aggression or a distant stalling game. She attacks with low-value cards, forces difficult discard choices, and uses hand knowledge to dictate the match.

### Plan A: Relentless aggression

**Goal:** Attack every turn to wear the opponent down, using her ability to replace the cards you spend.
**How to do it:** Her ability, TWO STEPS AHEAD, lets you draw until you have four cards at the end of your turn. This makes boosting maneuvers early in the game painless, as you simply draw the spent cards right back. Attack constantly with her lower-value cards. The opponent feels bad spending a strong defense on them, but taking the hits quickly adds up. Use Lightning bolt when you haven't maneuvered to deal 1 extra damage to every enemy in her zone. End your turn with Owlform (3 copies, value 3) and discard your remaining hand to add to its value, then immediately draw four new cards.
**Watch out for:** Fighters with strong card draw, like Ciri or Dr. Strange, can maintain their hand size and weather the storm. Going too fast empties your deck, leaving you vulnerable to exhaustion.

### Plan B: Hand disruption and stall

**Goal:** Starve the opponent of valuable cards and avoid combat.
**How to do it:** Keep your distance and force the opponent to discard. Spymaster's ruse makes them reveal four cards and discard two of your choice. Redanian plot and Cunning give them a choice: discard a card, or suffer a negative effect. Use Do my bidding to look at their hand and force them to play a weak card. When they finally close the distance, use Polymorphy to teleport away and gain a move value of 5. Place Dijkstra in their path so they have to spend actions moving around him instead of attacking Philippa. Spellbreaker (3 copies, value 2) cancels effects and immediately ends the turn, making it a safe first action to stall the game.
**Watch out for:** The sources disagree on her best strategy: most players find aggression more effective, but some insist stalling works better against fighters who run out of steam early. If Dijkstra dies before you use Spymaster's ruse, your stall game suffers heavily.

### How they lose

​- She only has 12 health and her defenses (like Blinding dust and Paralyzing fetters) are mostly 2s. High-damage attackers can kill her quickly if they catch her.
​- She runs out of cards if she leans too hard into her aggressive tools without finishing the opponent.
​- Fighters who can comfortably maintain hand advantage easily brush off her barrage of small attacks.
​- Her defenses depend on their card text. Fighters with Feints cancel the effects on Paralyzing fetters or Blinding dust and land their full damage.

### Key cards

​- **Owlform**: her main damage card. Discard your hand to boost it, then refill with TWO STEPS AHEAD. Use it when you know they don't have a high-value block.
​- **Do my bidding**: a 3-value defense that returns their card, lets you look at their hand, and forces them to play a card you choose. Great for stripping their best defense before you launch an attack on your next turn.
​- **Spellbreaker**: a versatile that cancels effects and ends the turn. It belongs only to Philippa, so Dijkstra cannot play it.
​- **Polymorphy**: a scheme that places Philippa anywhere and increases her move to 5. You must discard it at the end of your turn if you don't share a zone with an enemy, which rewards staying close if you want to keep the movement boost.
​- **Backup plan**: a scheme that can set Philippa's health to 5. Play it late in the game when she is near death, but don't wait so long that she dies before using it.

### Tips

​- TWO STEPS AHEAD is optional. You don't have to draw if you want to avoid exhaustion damage late in the game.
​- Paralyzing fetters stops all value changes, including character abilities like Alice's size and map rules like high ground.
​- Chain lightning targets a single zone Philippa is in. If she stands on a space with multiple colors, you must pick just one of those zones.
​- Match your map to your plan: a small map helps you engage constantly for aggression, while a large map gives you space to abuse Polymorphy's 5 movement.
​- Regicide hits for 6 against heroes. Even if canceled, it is a big enough number to force damage through most blocks.
