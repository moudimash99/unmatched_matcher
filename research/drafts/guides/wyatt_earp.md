## Wyatt Earp

**In short:** A fast, aggressive ranged fighter who chains multiple attacks together to drain the opponent's hand. He applies immense pressure with his Showdown ability, but operates as a glass cannon with weak defensive cards and no healing.

### Plan A: Drain their defenses with constant pressure

**Goal:** Empty the opponent's hand so your biggest attacks land unblocked.
**How to do it:** Wyatt Earp holds the initiative by throwing out a relentless string of attacks. His ability, Showdown, triggers whenever you attack and win combat. It lets you choose to draw a card, gain a free action to attack, or move a space (though you cannot pick the same effect twice in one turn). Start by tossing out bait attacks like *Fan The Hammer* or *A Marshal And An Outlaw* to force the opponent to spend their blocks. If you win combat, use the free attack action to immediately swing again. Once you suspect they are out of defensive cards, drop *You Just Gonna Stand There And Bleed?* to hit them with an undefended 5.
**Watch out for:** He has low health and his deck lacks high-value defenses. Going all-in on offense leaves you vulnerable if the opponent survives your barrage and counterattacks while your hand is empty.

### Plan B: Extract Doc Holliday's value early

**Goal:** Get Doc's powerful card effects into play before he gets eliminated.
**How to do it:** Doc Holliday brings a massive amount of damage and card draw to the team, so you want him in the action immediately. Mulligan your starting hand to find his specific cards. *You're A Daisy If You Do* is a 4-value versatile card that draws two cards whether you win or lose the combat, generating a huge card advantage. *I Have Two Guns, One For Each Of You* is a 4-value attack that punishes opponents for playing high-value defenses: if they block, it deals that printed value to a different opposing fighter in Doc's zone. This makes it devastating against players trying to keep their sidekicks alive.
**Watch out for:** Doc has only 8 health. Smart opponents will focus him down quickly to remove his best cards from the game. If he dies, his remaining cards become weak boost fodder. When setting up *I Have Two Guns, One For Each Of You*, ensure both the enemy hero and their sidekick are in Doc's zone, otherwise the effect does nothing.

### Plan C: Pivot to the exhaustion game

**Goal:** Punish opponents who overreact to your aggression by letting them deck out.
**How to do it:** Wyatt's aggressive play pattern often panics opponents, causing them to double maneuver repeatedly to keep their hands full of defenses. If you sense you are pulling ahead on the exhaustion clock, stop attacking and play for value. Both *In Vino Veritas* and *You Die First* are schemes that grant a free action to attack, but you are not forced to use that action. You can play them simply as safe passes to skip your turn. *Fan The Hammer* also supports this value game: if it defeats a 1-health sidekick, it returns to your hand, saving you a card in the long run.
**Watch out for:** You can only switch to a value game if you have a comfortable lead in the deck count. If you wait too long to pivot, Wyatt's weak defensive cards will not hold up in the late game.

### How they lose

- He is a glass cannon. With 15 health, no healing, and mostly low-value defenses, big printed attacks easily break through.
- Doc gets killed before playing his cards, completely crippling the deck's damage output and card draw.
- Fast aggro fighters simply outpace his weak deck before he can set up his offensive turns.
- Opponents with offensive cancels target *Bring 'Em To Justice*, reducing it to a 0-value defense and dealing massive damage.
- Characters that ignore combat values (like Doctor Strange) completely blank his vital 5-value attacks.

### Key cards

- **You Just Gonna Stand There And Bleed?**: His biggest hit. With three copies at a value of 5, it is your most reliable way to win combat and trigger Showdown.
- **Second Shot**: A 2-value attack that you may boost after seeing the opponent's defense. Throw a 3-boost card (like *You Die First* or *Fan The Hammer*) into it if you know it will win combat. Don't waste the boost if they play a 5-value block.
- **In Vino Veritas**: The engine of his aggressive turns. Attack until your hand is nearly empty, then play this scheme to draw back up to four cards and gain another free attack.
- **Better Swear Me In**: A 2-value cancel that lets you look at the top card of the opponent's deck and optionally put it on the bottom. Against double attacks, leave a high-boost card on top to power up *Bring 'Em To Justice* for their second swing.
- **Bring 'Em To Justice**: His only dedicated defense card. It starts at 0 and adds twice the boost value of the opponent's top deck card. Extremely risky against fighters whose decks contain 0-boost cards.
- **Gunfight At The O.K. Corral**: A versatile card that causes both players to deal 2 automatic damage across the board. Excellent as a bait attack to slip past high-value blocks, but terrible on defense because the opponent will almost certainly assign their 2 automatic counter-damage to Wyatt.

### Tips

- Because Showdown only triggers when you *win* combat, you should count the opponent's discards to track their cancels and high blocks. Sequence your attacks for when you hold the mathematical advantage.
- You cannot choose the same Showdown effect twice in a turn. Plan your sequence carefully: you might draw a card on the first win, move a space to kite on the second, and take the free attack on the third.
- Use *You Die First* defensively. Instead of pulling an enemy closer, use the movement effect to push an adjacent attacker one space away, opening up an escape route.
- Because both fighters are ranged, keep them in central zones that touch multiple areas. This gives you massive map control and leaves the opponent with nowhere to hide.
