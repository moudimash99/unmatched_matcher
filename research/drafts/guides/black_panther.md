## Black Panther

**In short:** An aggressive hybrid duo that strips cards from the opponent's deck to fuel massive attacks. He turns enemy resources against them, but mandatory card draw from his boosts puts him on a dangerous exhaustion clock.

### Plan A: Lock away their deck

**Goal:** Pull cards straight from their deck into the Vibranium Suit to deny them resources and build a stockpile of boosts.
**How to do it:** Cards stored in the suit never enter the opponent's hand or discard pile. Shuri's scheme, Tactical Remote Scanning, is your strongest tool because it bypasses combat entirely to steal the top two cards. Try to play it early. The attack Analyze and Adjust secures one card after combat regardless of who wins. Since Shuri is ranged, she can throw it from safety to force the opponent to react. Ancestral Insight requires you to win the combat to get the steal, so play this 4-value defense against weak bait attacks or predictable setups to guarantee the trigger.
**Watch out for:** The sources disagree heavily on Shuri's overall value: one claims she is crucial and losing her before she schemes usually loses you the game, while another finds her highly overrated. She only has 6 health, so aggressive opponents will try to eliminate her immediately. Furthermore, stealing is random. You might grab their best attacks, or you might hit low-value cards that offer little boost power. If you steal cards without a printed boost value, you cannot use them to boost at all, leaving them stuck in your suit permanently.

### Plan B: Explode with massive boosts

**Goal:** Overwhelm the opponent's defenses by adding stolen boost values to your own combat values.
**How to do it:** His special ability forces you to draw a card every time you boost, whether on a maneuver or a card effect. By boosting with the cards trapped in your Vibranium Suit rather than your hand, you actually grow your hand size, keeping the pressure high. Wakanda Forever! is his main finisher because it lets you boost the 3-value attack up to two times. Adding two stolen 3-boost or 4-boost cards pushes it to 9 or more, breaking through almost any block. For unpredictable trades, the 1-value Anti-Metal Claws automatically adds the opponent's boost value to itself, and then lets you boost it further. This creates huge swings against fighters with high-boost cards, like Dracula or Yennenga. On defense, Microweave Mesh lets you see the incoming attack before deciding whether to boost, ensuring you only spend resources when necessary.
**Watch out for:** Cancels. If the opponent plays a Feint against Wakanda Forever!, your boosting effects are cancelled, leaving you with a weak base attack. Attack with your Regroup cards or unboosted smaller attacks like Vibranium Shockwave first to bait out their cancels before committing to your finisher.

### Plan C: Starve recursion fighters

**Goal:** Trap key cards permanently so fighters who rely on their discard pile run out of options.
**How to do it:** Because stored cards technically do not exist in the discard pile, effects that recycle cards cannot target them. If you steal a vital piece of recursion from fighters like Elektra, Daredevil, or Sinbad, simply leave it in your suit for the entire game instead of spending it on a maneuver or attack. They will play the rest of the match with a permanent hole in their deck and strategy. With up to ten possible steals in your deck, you can severely limit the options of opponents who run smaller 20-card or 22-card decks.
**Watch out for:** You draw through your deck much faster than they do. You cannot simply sit back and wait for them to exhaust from missing cards, because your mandatory draws mean you will likely empty your deck first. You still have to end the game with combat damage.

### How they lose

- He burns through his 30-card deck too fast. Every boost forces a draw, making him highly vulnerable to late-game exhaustion if he cannot close out the fight.
- The opponent kills Shuri on the first or second turn before she can scheme, denying him four potential stolen cards.
- He suffers from bad luck on his steals, grabbing weak 1-boost cards instead of high-value targets, leaving his own attacks underpowered.
- Heavy cancel decks strip the effects off his big attacks, leaving him with low base values and a shrinking deck.
- He struggles on large maps. Because he only moves 2, chasing a kiting opponent forces him to boost his maneuvers, which accelerates his exhaustion.

### Key cards

- **Tactical Remote Scanning**: His highest-yield stealing tool. Guaranteeing cards are removed without combat risk makes Shuri a primary target.
- **Wakanda Forever!**: His main damage engine. Boosting it twice triggers two mandatory card draws to rapidly refill your hand.
- **Anti-Metal Claws**: Punishes opponents for playing high-boost defenses.
- **Analyze and Adjust**: Its 3-boost value makes it one of your best native boosting tools if you fail to steal good enemy cards, but try to save it for its attack effect.
- **Stalking Panther**: Moves both fighters up to 3 spaces and gains an action. A great way to close distance for a double attack without triggering a mandatory draw from a maneuver boost.

### Tips

- Always try to boost using the opponent's cards from the suit rather than your own, so you do not deplete your strongest tools.
- Keep Shuri out of reach. Her defenses are weak, and her Nanotriage Processor healing only recovers 1 health, which is usually too minor to save her from a focused assault. The 3-value Cat-Like Reflexes is a good defense to throw out if you just need to keep her alive.
- If you draw Tactical Remote Scanning in your opening hand, use your first actions to maneuver Shuri into position and play it immediately.
- Pick smaller maps with hiding spots for Shuri. The less you have to maneuver, the less you have to boost and draw. Maps with scheme item tokens, like the Sanctum, let you play her scheme as a free action for immediate value.
