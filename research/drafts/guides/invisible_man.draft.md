## The Invisible Man

**In short:** A defensive, elusive solo fighter who uses fog tokens to teleport and boost his blocks. He relies on unblockable scheme damage and running his opponent out of cards.

### Plan A: Outlast them

**Goal:** Win by exhaustion while staying completely out of reach.
**How to do it:** Spread your three fog tokens as far apart as possible, especially on large maps. This forces the opponent to boost their maneuvers just to reach you. Always try to end your turn standing on a fog token, which adds 1 to the value of all your defense cards. When the opponent has a full hand, use Vanish (3-boost scheme) as your first action. This removes you from the board entirely, leaving them with nothing to attack. They will likely have to double maneuver and discard cards at the end of their turn, accelerating their exhaustion.
**Watch out for:** Drawing cards speeds up your own exhaustion clock. Lurking, Covert Preparation, and Coded Notes all force you to draw. Sources disagree on Coded Notes: some call it a trap because of the forced draw, while others consider it essential for hiding your best cards on top of your deck to protect them from discard effects.

### Plan B: Damage without combat

**Goal:** Deal heavy damage using schemes to bypass the opponent's defenses.
**How to do it:** Because his attacks rarely deal enough damage to win a game, he relies on his schemes. Reign of Terror (2 copies) deals 2 damage to any opposing fighter on the board, as long as you are standing on a fog token. Step Lightly (2 copies) deals 3 damage to an adjacent fighter if you are on a fog. To land this safely against a ranged fighter, use Rolling Fog to move a token next to them, gain an action, and then play Step Lightly. Between these cards, you can deal 10 damage without ever initiating combat.
**Watch out for:** Step Lightly allows the opponent to move a fog token up to 2 spaces. If you play it as your second action, they might move the token you are standing on, leaving you adjacent to them without your defense bonus for their entire turn.

### Plan C: Timed strikes

**Goal:** Land your few high-value attacks and immediately retreat.
**How to do it:** He has no standard cancels for defense, so Surprise Attack (value 5) is your only way to cancel an opponent's card effects. Emerge From Mist hits for 5 if you start your turn on a fog token. Mix these in with weaker attacks like Covert Preparation to bait out the opponent's Feints. Slip Away (3 copies) is your primary escape tool: you can use it to attack and immediately teleport a fog token and yourself to a safe space.
**Watch out for:** Surprise Attack forces you to move a fog token if you are standing on one. If you play it as your second action while on a fog token, you will push the token away and end your turn exposed.

### How they lose

​- His guaranteed damage output is limited, so fighters with large health pools or heavy healing (Alice, Little Red) can survive everything he throws at them.
​- Opponents with multiple sidekicks body-block his fog tokens. He cannot afford to spend his high-value attacks or scheme damage clearing sidekicks.
​- Hand disruption and discard effects strip away his critical schemes and big attacks.
​- Ranged fighters do not need to stand on or near his fog tokens to hit him.
​- Small, tight maps prevent him from effectively spreading his fog tokens and running away.
​- Aggressive fighters with multiple actions per turn (Bruce Lee) quickly burn through his defenses.

### Key cards

​- **Vanish**: a scheme that heals 1 and removes him from the board until his next turn. Crucial for forcing the opponent to burn cards.
​- **Impossible to See**: a 2-value versatile that reduces the opponent's value to 0, and prevents effects from changing it. This is his best defense against massive printed attacks or boost-driven attacks (Beastform, Forever Hyde), though it does not cancel after-combat effects.
​- **Into Thin Air**: a 4-value defense that becomes a 5 when on a fog token. The downside is that the opponent gets to move a fog token up to 3 spaces.
​- **Surprise Attack**: a 5-value attack that cancels all opponent card effects.
​- **Confound**: a 3-value versatile that forces the opponent to discard a card, or else lets you move every fog token to any space. Play it when their hand is small to make the choice difficult.

### Tips

​- When Covert Preparation allows the opponent to move a different fog token, first choose to move the token you are already standing on by zero spaces. This protects your current token, as the opponent must pick a "different" one to move.
​- Dreaming of Revenge deals 1 damage to all opposing fighters on fog tokens. Use this to clear out 1-health sidekicks trying to camp on your escape routes.
​- Use your schemes that don't draw cards to pass actions. This delays your own deck exhaustion.
​- Treat the Harpies or other multi-sidekicks as a lost cause; if you burn your damage on them, you won't have enough left to defeat the hero.
​- Do not be afraid to use Covert Preparation as an attack to bait out defensive cancels.
