## Ancient Leshen

**In short:** A slow, ranged fighter who relies on fast sidekicks to block the board and harass the enemy. He heals constantly while hiding, wears down the opponent's hand, and saves his massive +3 attack ability for a late-game finisher.

### Plan A: Harass with the wolves

**Goal:** Drain their health and cards without risking the hero.
**How to do it:** Leshen only moves 1 space, but the wolves move 3. Send the wolves ahead to attack with Command the forest to force discards, or Planted feet to draw cards. When the opponent hits back, defend the wolves with Harrying strike. It adds +2 for every friendly fighter adjacent to the enemy, so a melee attack makes it a 4-value block. Protecting the wolves forces the opponent to spend extra cards just to clear the path to Leshen. Keep your hand full with Nature abounds so you always have threats to throw.
**Watch out for:** The sources disagree on how aggressively to pursue Leshen's +3 ability early in the game. One recommends using his cards to chase and land the double attack, while another warns that forcing the ability early leaves you overcommitted and exposed.

### Plan B: Hide and heal

**Goal:** Recover his low 13 health and outlast the opponent.
**How to do it:** Strength of the pack summons a wolf into Leshen's zone and heals him for 1 health at the start of every turn, as long as a wolf stays in his zone. Play it early and put Leshen behind the wolves. Over a long game, this infinite healing can recover massive amounts of health. If an enemy breaks through, Vanish into murder deals 1 damage to their zone, removes Leshen from the board, and lets him drop into any space on your next turn. This escapes pins and clears out 1-health sidekicks instantly.
**Watch out for:** The opponent will try to kill the wolves to end the healing. You have three copies of Strength of the pack and three of Primeval slam to bring wolves back, so replace them as soon as they fall.

### Plan C: Set up the double attack

**Goal:** Land a massive hit in the late game when their defenses are low.
**How to do it:** His Heart of the Forest ability adds +3 to his attack if he already attacked this turn. Set this up without spending Leshen's movement. Disturbing howls is a 1-value wolf attack that moves the enemy up to 2 spaces and gains an action. Use it to push them into Leshen's zone, then attack twice with Leshen. Alternatively, attack with Flock of birds first. It hits for 5 and lets you place Leshen in any space, guaranteeing you can warp next to them for the +3 second attack even if they try to run.
**Watch out for:** An opponent who expects the second attack will save a Feint or a high-value defense for it.

### How they lose

- He has only 9 defense cards in his deck. If an opponent bypasses the wolves and gets extra actions (Bruce Lee, Geralt), he cannot block everything.
- Fighters who recycle their decks (Daredevil, Winter Soldier, Elektra) do not care about his exhaustion tactics or forced discards.
- His best defense, Primeval guardian, adds the opponent's top deck boost to their attack. Against fighters with high boost values (Houdini), it breaks easily.
- He struggles to catch opponents who stay away, force him to approach, and refuse to enter his zone.

### Key cards

- **Strength of the pack**: your main survival tool. It revives a wolf and provides constant healing.
- **Vanish into murder**: an escape tool, guaranteed area damage, and card draw all in one. Save it for when you are pressured or to confirm a lethal hit.
- **Flock of birds**: a 5-value attack that teleports Leshen. Use it to hit and run, or to chase an enemy for your ability.
- **Primeval guardian**: a 5-value Leshen defense that discards their top card. The discard mills their deck for exhaustion, and the card moves your wolves up to 3 spaces after combat to set up your next turn.
- **Command the forest**: a 4-value attack that makes them discard a card if you win. Let the wolves play this to pressure their hand.

### Tips

- Mulligan for attacks and Strength of the pack. You want to start aggressive and get your healing running; do not keep defenses in your opening hand.
- Defend Leshen with Wily Fighting. It deals 1 damage to adjacent fighters after combat, punishing melee attackers who finally reach you.
- Primeval guardian is great against cards that set a fixed value (like Shift), because the value resets after the top deck boost is added.
- Draft maps with choke points or item tokens. His 1-movement hero and blocking sidekicks thrive in tight spaces.
