## Angel

**In short:** A simple melee fighter with heavy attacks but weak defenses. His deck hits hard, and his ability draws a card when he loses a combat on his turn, but Faith brings some of his most dangerous tools.

### Plan A: Hit hard and step away

**Goal:** Deal heavy damage and force the opponent to waste actions reaching you.
**How to do it:** Angel lacks strong defensive cards, so positioning is your main shield. Attack with cards that move your fighters to safety after the strike. Disengage (3 copies) is a 4-value attack that places the fighter in an empty space in their zone. Faith’s Five by Five (2 copies) is a massive 5-value attack that lets her move up to 5 spaces through enemies, working as both a heavy blow and an escape route. You can also start a turn with Killer of the Dead for 2 guaranteed damage to someone next to Faith, then use Five by Five to hit them again and retreat.
**Watch out for:** Ranged fighters don't care if you step away, as long as you land in their zone.

### Plan B: Bait defenses to draw cards

**Goal:** Keep your hand full by losing combats on purpose.
**How to do it:** Angel's ability draws one card when you attack and lose the combat. Ties count as a loss for the attacker. After you establish pressure with your heavy 4s and 5s, the opponent will start blocking with their best cards. That is the time to attack with weak versatile cards. Regroup is the perfect bait: if you lose, you draw one from Regroup and one from the ability. If you win, Regroup draws two anyway. Wisdom of Ages draws a card regardless of the outcome. Brooding deals 1 damage to the opponent if you lose, making them pay for successfully defending.
**Watch out for:** It is surprisingly hard to lose combats with this deck. Your big attacks—Angelus, Scourge of Europe, Five by Five, and Cursed with a Soul—make up a large chunk of your offense and rarely lose. This means your ability triggers less often than you might expect, leaving you starved for cards.

### How they lose

​- His defenses are very weak. He relies mostly on versatile 3s or his only true defense, Haunted by the Faces. Opponents with big printed attacks crush these blocks.
​- He runs out of cards. Because his high-value attacks usually win combats, his ability rarely triggers, and the deck has very little other card draw.
​- Ranged attackers pick him apart because his hit-and-run cards only create distance, not cover.
​- He is fundamentally weak. Sources consider him one of the weakest fighters in the game, noting he is mostly used to teach beginners or offer them a handicap.

### Key cards

​- **Five by Five**: Faith's best attack. A massive 5 that lets her escape or box an opponent in. Because it is so strong, experienced opponents will often save their Feints for Faith instead of Angel.
​- **Disengage**: an attack that teleports the fighter anywhere in their zone. Crucial for avoiding a counter-attack.
​- **The Rogue Slayer**: a 3-value versatile that deals 1 damage to all adjacent enemies. Save it for defense to clear out swarms of 1-health sidekicks.
​- **Regroup**: your most reliable way to maintain card advantage. Use it as an attack to guarantee two drawn cards.
​- **Angelus, Scourge of Europe**: a blank 5-value attack. It has no effect, making it the perfect way to punish an opponent who tries to Feint you.

### Tips

​- Save Momentous Shift for defense. With enough big printed attacks in the deck, you need its 3-value block more than its conditional 5-value attack.
​- Don't rely on Cursed with a Soul to heal you. Treat the health recovery as a nice bonus if you happen to lose, but expect its 4 value to win most combats.
​- If you must block with Haunted by the Faces, losing the combat lets you place Angel anywhere in his zone. Use this to suddenly close the gap on an enemy who is trying to stay away.
