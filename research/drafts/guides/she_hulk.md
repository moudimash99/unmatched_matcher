## She-Hulk

**In short:** A tough melee fighter who uses her massive health and a zone-based ability to control the tempo. She wins by forcing the opponent to approach, crushing their hand with discards, and trapping them for devastating combo attacks.

### Plan A: Force them to approach

**Goal:** Deal free damage and force the opponent to take the initiative.
**How to do it:** Her Just Throw Something ability allows you to discard a card at the start of your turn to deal its boost value as damage to an enemy in your zone. Combined with her massive 20 health, deck-depleting cards, and the healing from Double Jeopardy, she is heavily favored to win fatigue battles. Because opponents will naturally lose a waiting game, they must come to you. Position yourself centrally in large zones so enemies cannot safely stand on the borders. When they maneuver into your zone to attack, hit them with your ability.
**Watch out for:** It can be risky to over-use the throw ability. Discarding cards excessively against highly mobile ranged fighters empties your hand and speeds up your own exhaustion clock, leaving you without necessary attack options.

### Plan B: The two-action trap

**Goal:** Land a massive 9-value attack.
**How to do it:** The Savage She-Hulk (2 copies, 3 value, 3 boost) allows you to spend an extra action during combat to swing for 9. Because this requires two actions, you cannot use a standard maneuver to reach your target and still trigger the effect. Instead, set it up using your schemes. Leap Toward moves you up to 4 spaces, ignoring opposing fighters, and grants an action if you end adjacent. Jennifer Walters, Esq. teleports you to any starting space, draws a card, and gives an action. Defensively, you can catch aggressive enemies with Nerve Cluster Strike (3 copies, 3 value). If you are adjacent, it immediately stops the attacker from leaving their space for the turn, leaving you perfectly positioned to double-attack on your next turn.
**Watch out for:** Opponents anticipate the two-action requirement and will often save their Feint effects for your first attack. Also, Nerve Cluster Strike only stops the opponent from moving—if they play a card that forcefully moves She-Hulk away, the trap fails. Astute players will also avoid ending their turns near starting spaces to deny your teleport setup.

### Plan C: Destroy their hand and deck

**Goal:** Strip the opponent of their best cards and advance their exhaustion clock.
**How to do it:** Leverage the threat of a 9-value hit as bait. When you swing on your first action, opponents are likely to waste a cancel or play a weak block. Instead, hit them with Omega-Level Threat (2 copies, 5 value). If this attack deals at least 2 combat damage, the defender discards two random cards, devastating their hand. Once their defensive options are low, use Sensational (3 copies, 4 value) to shuffle an Omega-Level Threat back into your deck upon winning combat, creating a loop.

On defense, Lady Justice (3 copies, no printed value) discards the top card of the attacker's deck, increasing its own block value by double that card's boost. This continuously removes future threats from their deck while you defend.
**Watch out for:** Lady Justice is entirely dependent on the opponent's deck composition. Against fighters with many 1-boost cards or those lacking boost values entirely, like Sinbad, it offers very little protection. Additionally, if the opponent's deck is empty, Lady Justice provides zero block and has no effect.

### How they lose

- Fast ranged opponents can easily stay outside her zone, forcing her to rely on her sluggish base move of 2.
- Her standard defensive values are weak. If Lady Justice pulls a low boost, heavy attackers can rapidly deplete her health pool.
- Massive attacks played into The Defense Rests (2 copies, 2 value) force her to draw cards equal to the damage taken, completely wrecking her fatigue advantage.
- Because Lady Justice resolves during combat, an offensive cancel will shut it down entirely, forcing her to take the full hit undefended.
- Pressure-heavy fighters can rapidly deplete her hand, leaving her with no cards to fuel her throw ability.

### Key cards

- **The Savage She-Hulk**: Her primary win condition and the only card in the deck with a 3-boost, making it the strongest projectile for her ability. The looming threat of this attack often influences opponent behavior more than actually playing it.
- **Omega-Level Threat**: Forcing two random discards can completely tilt the match in your favor. It severely punishes foes who attempt to mitigate damage with standard 3-value blocks.
- **Sensational**: Your primary recursion engine. Use it to repeatedly loop your best attacks, or recover Leap Toward if you desperately need mobility.
- **Green Energy**: A 4-value versatile card that pings all adjacent fighters for 1 damage if you win combat. It acts as her most consistent and reliable standard block.
- **Cease and Desist**: A 1-value versatile cancel. While it works defensively, playing it as an attack turns it into a 4-value strike, making it an excellent tool to aggressively punch through defensive abilities.

### Tips

- Avoid fighting near zone borders where ranged opponents can safely attack from adjacent spaces in a different zone, completely ignoring your throw ability.
- Attack with Legalese (3 copies, 2 value) hoping to deliberately lose the combat. As the loser, you get to choose whether both players draw or discard a card. Choose to draw, pushing the opponent closer to exhaustion.
- In a pure fatigue race, Leap Toward can be played as a safe pass. Simply move without ending adjacent to anyone, allowing you to stall a turn without drawing a card.
- There is debate on using Double Jeopardy late in the game: drawing two cards can hurt your exhaustion lead, but it essentially replaces a maneuver action while granting 2 health.
